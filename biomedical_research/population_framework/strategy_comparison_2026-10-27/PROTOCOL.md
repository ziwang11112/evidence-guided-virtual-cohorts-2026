# Framework strategy comparison: frozen development pilot

Date: 27 October 2026. User objective: optimize the reusable generation/revision
procedure across cases, without coordinator-chosen case-specific coefficients.

## Question and design

Does a fixed evidence-linked policy improve generated target populations over a
capable ordinary iterative modeling policy? Two cases, two policies, two stages:
FIT/MI to FIReWoRk women, later Han joint evidence; STRRIDE-PD to Queen's exercise
participants, later DREW joint evidence. These are two transfer tasks, not a random
sample of studies. All real target cohorts and historical splits have previously
been exposed to this research project. This is retrospective development, not
fresh independent confirmation, clinical validation or a significance contest.

Four fresh builders receive no conversational history or prior generators. Each
builder constructs initial and revised versions for one case/policy. Each receives
the same evidence as its counterpart, same fixed contexts, schema, numerical
source/baseline observations, generation N/seeds and one initial plus one revision
submission. Ordinary iteration can optimize, redesign structure and use all tools
permitted to the structured policy. Common decision fields are output plumbing;
the structured policy adds an explicit evidence contract, alternatives and localized
revision diagnosis. It is a whole-policy pilot, not an isolated component ablation.

Model calls use collaboration handoff. Exact backend/version/token use are not
available. We match submission count and information, NOT actual tokens or wall
time. Supplied-input calculations before submission are unrestricted in both arms;
their compute is not measured. Independent Agent repetitions are absent. Two
sampling seeds quantify simulation behavior, not independent Agent replicates.

No target outcome information in the initial stage. At revision, both policies get
the same new paper and FOUR target development summaries: mean and sample SD of
percentage weight change and absolute waist change, pooled over the frozen eligible
development members. Include denominator and definitions. No correlations, joint
probabilities, conditional/subgroup outcomes or check summaries are delivered.
Compute these once only after all initial models are committed; retain exact packet
hashes. This predeclared marginal calibration is not added after evaluating models.
It changes the setting to limited target feedback and cannot isolate the contribution
of new paper evidence from that feedback. Numerical source/baseline diagnostics also
come from the same engine. Retain every
submitted failure. No post-submission repair beyond the scheduled revision; a failed
initial can be repaired in that revision. A valid generated target can be evaluated
even if source checks fail, with failure status reported. Missing final target
output is a failure, not an excluded favorable task. Both policies retain the same
fundamental interface, reproducibility and evidence-accounting checks.

## Cases and evidence limits

Copy audited selected packets without enriching either arm. FIT initial contains
occasion moments, not all information obtainable from its paper; unreported paired
variation is unidentified here. This is a controlled partial-evidence task, not an
exhaustive-paper extraction demonstration. FIT target is women only. Exercise
nominal arm matching does not resolve dose, measurement site, sex or population
differences. DREW's pooled correlation does not identify within-arm target correlation.
Baseline summaries use all eligible target covariates: target-informed/transductive,
not zero-target-information generation. Cases use their frozen eligibility and
historical splits without outcome-based reselection.

## Frozen evaluation (not delivered to builders)

Primary: historical-check joint response four-cell total variation, defined by
weight change percent <= -5 and waist change cm <= -5. The 5 cm threshold is an
analytic cutoff, not a clinical minimal important difference. FIT has one pooled
female stratum (expected check N107). Exercise has LVLI/HVLI/HVHI strata (expected
check total N49); average their TVs equally. Pool equal-size samples from two
generation seeds to estimate synthetic probabilities. Report cell counts/probabilities.
The main contrast is structured final TV minus ordinary final TV (negative favors
structured). Also report initial TVs and difference in changes: (structured final
- initial) - (ordinary final - initial). The final contrast is whole workflow;
different initial models confound causal attribution specifically to revision.
An equal-case mean is descriptive only. Do not declare superiority on two tasks.

Mandatory secondary diagnostics in native units: absolute errors of both marginal
response probabilities, q10/q50/q90 of weight-percent and waist-cm changes, and
correlation/OLS waist-on-weight-percent relationships where defined. Report every
case, policy and stage, not only improved metrics. Improvement confined to supplied
moments or accompanied by material marginal degradation is not broad generation
improvement. No single uncalibrated composite combines these disparate units.
Historical development split results are secondary descriptive checks, not selection
criteria; their four disclosed moments are calibration information and their other
relationships are unprovided. Apart from the predeclared development marginal export,
target evaluation occurs only after all eight submissions and evaluator code are
committed. No subsequent model tuning under this protocol.

Use 1,000 paired target-participant bootstrap resamples, stratified by declared
exercise arm, retaining each stratum's N. Compare both policies/stages in the same
resample. Percentile intervals describe target-estimator sampling uncertainty
conditional on these fixed generators. They exclude Agent, source-study, selection
and transport uncertainty. Undefined estimates are explicit, never replaced by zero.

## Interpretation and stopping rule

Claim an encouraging development signal only if the structured final improves joint
TV on both tasks without a clear deterioration in mandatory marginal diagnostics.
Otherwise report mixed/no advantage and locate failure in the generic strategy,
identification or transport assumptions. Do not hand-tune either case, search for
favorable thresholds, or launch larger experiments to hide failure. A next version
would require a new predeclared policy and additional cases. No promise of publishable
novelty or of replacing patient data collection follows from this pilot.
