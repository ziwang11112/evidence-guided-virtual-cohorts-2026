# Shared generation and revision policy: completed pilot

**Result: the structured policy did not outperform capable ordinary iteration.**
Its final joint-response error was higher in both tasks. The frozen success rule
was not met. Retain this as a negative development result; do not promote this
policy as an accuracy improvement or repair individual cases after evaluation.

The reusable execution, evidence delivery, immutable submissions and independent
evaluation worked. They remain useful infrastructure. Passing their checks did
not guarantee accurate target populations.

## What was implemented and run

Four builders started without prior conversation or generator artifacts: one per
case/policy. Each submitted an initial generator and one revision, producing eight
committed programs. The same ordinary policy served both cases, as did the same
structured policy. The coordinator supplied no case-specific modeling coefficients
and changed no submitted generator.

The structured policy required an evidence contract, consideration of alternative
joint models, inspectable marginal/dependence/transport components, an explicit
revision diagnosis and numerical checks of claimed effects. Ordinary iteration
could freely select models, optimize, decompose, test and revise; it received the
same machine-readable interface and essential checks. This was a whole-policy
comparison, not an ablation identifying which instruction caused a difference.

The shared runner now accepts a versioned request template and snapshots it into
each staged input lock. A generic evaluation program verifies all eight commitments
before loading outcomes. Sampling budgets were the same within each task/policy:
12,000 target records per seed, two fixed seeds; 40,000 records for each native-source
context. These are Monte Carlo samples, not independent experimental participants.

Initial construction used selected published source evidence and target baseline
summaries. After all four initial models were frozen, both policies received the
same new paper evidence and exactly four historical-development statistics: mean
and sample SD of percentage weight change and waist change in cm. The denominators
were 211 women for FIReWoRk and 42 exercise participants for Queen's. No target joint
probability, correlation, conditional outcome or check statistic was supplied.

The revised programs were all committed before the new comparative evaluation.
The FIT target horizon is six months; the exercise horizon is 24 weeks. Both
cohorts and their historical splits were previously exposed to this project.
This is retrospective, target-informed development with limited marginal feedback,
not zero-target-data synthesis or new external confirmation.

## Prespecified primary result

Four-cell joint-response total variation (TV), lower is better. The cells use
weight change <= -5% and waist change <= -5 cm; these are analytic cutoffs, not a
clinical equivalence threshold. Exercise scores average its three declared arms
equally. Every case and candidate is retained.

| Historical check task | Real N | Ordinary initial -> revised | Structured initial -> revised | Revised structured minus ordinary |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| FIT/Han -> FIReWoRk women | 107 | 0.0862 -> 0.0646 | 0.0554 -> 0.0775 | +0.0128 |
| STRRIDE/DREW -> Queen's exercise | 49 | 0.4418 -> 0.1499 | 0.4917 -> 0.2382 | +0.0883 |

In FIT/Han, the structured initial model had lower joint TV, but its revision made
that metric worse. In exercise, both revisions improved markedly, but the ordinary
revision ended lower. A large initial-to-final gain alone would therefore have
misrepresented the comparative result.

The conditional paired-participant bootstrap 95% intervals for the final contrasts
were [-0.0029, 0.0128] for FIT/Han and [0.0131, 0.1055] for exercise. These intervals
exclude Agent variability, source-study uncertainty and transport uncertainty. They
must not be used as evidence of population-wide superiority from two selected tasks.
One independent builder per policy/task is insufficient to separate strategy from
Agent-run variation.

## What the other metrics show

The outcome is not explained by a failed program. All eight candidates passed
their source/baseline checks; final revisions passed 31 applications per FIT policy
and 28 per exercise policy. All four final programs also passed independent
numerical review. No unit, individual-SD-versus-SE or hidden generator outcome-access
defect was found. Unproven transport assumptions remained explicit.

The mandatory diagnostics reveal a more specific limitation:

- **FIT/Han:** both final generators reproduced the check-sample forward slope
  closely: about 0.665 and 0.661 versus the observed 0.671 cm per percentage point.
  Nevertheless, generated correlations were about 0.559 and 0.557 versus 0.734 in
  that sample. A close regression slope does not establish the correct joint
  distribution; correlation also depends on marginal scales. Waist quantile MAE
  worsened after revision in both policies (ordinary 1.170 -> 1.600 cm; structured
  1.006 -> 1.643 cm), while weight quantiles improved. Calibration was not uniformly
  beneficial across the reserved distributional checks.
- **Exercise:** structured final weight-response probability error was slightly
  lower (0.0352 versus 0.0405), yet joint TV was much higher. Its generated arm-wise
  correlations were 0.211-0.233, versus 0.477-0.502 for ordinary and 0.612-0.901 in
  the small real check strata. Both underrepresented observed concordance; the
  structured version did so more. Correct pooled means/SDs did not identify which
  people would change in both outcomes together.
- **Historical development:** the final structured joint TV was also higher in
  both tasks (0.0891 versus 0.0775; 0.2145 versus 0.1605). These are development
  diagnostics, not new replication, and no model was selected using them.

The exercise structured program transported the reported female pooled association
through a mixture observation rule and retained an unvalidated independent male
completion. Ordinary iteration used a different explicit dependence prior and
qualified evidence blend. Those choices help describe the numerical contrast;
this pilot does not causally establish that any particular instruction or
conservative choice produced the poorer result. Neither default is validated as
a general biological law.

Both revised policies received new paper evidence and four target statistics at
the same time. There is no ablation separating their contributions. Improvements
shared by both methods cannot be credited specifically to the added structured
procedure, and they do not prove that generated rows add value beyond numerical
prediction without records.

## Audit trail and framework lessons

The core has 22 passing tests, the evaluator 18, and the feedback exporter one
artificial-data test: 41 in total. Numerical evaluation checks include exact
four-cell arithmetic, paired bootstrap ordering, missing-seed/arm failure handling
and refusal to evaluate uncommitted models. The exporter test confirms that check
outcome values are not parsed when extracting development summaries.

Independent review corrected two minor issues before model locking: a six-month
versus 24-week label and verification of the revision-preparation lock. A separate
historical instruction conflict in the evidence packet received the same explicit
clarification in all four builder contexts. Original staged inputs were retained.
Future adapters should separate historical instructions from evidence objects.

All 27 locked files of the older FIT paper-evidence experiment and all 10 locked
files of the exercise preparation remain unchanged. New runs, source packets,
feedback, generated samples, engine snapshots, decisions and hashes are retained.
The generator-import checks and read restrictions are workflow aids, not an
adversarial filesystem sandbox. Actual token use and internal fitting compute
were unavailable; this was information/submission matched, not token matched.

## Consequence for the next framework version

Keep the common execution and audit infrastructure. **Freeze this scientific policy
version as unsuccessful; do not expand it as an accuracy-proven method.** The next
candidate mechanism should address selection among multiple evidence-compatible
joint models, rather than merely requesting a longer explanation of one model.

A bounded next experiment could retain several plausible dependency/transport
completions, identify the later queries on which they disagree, and use a fixed
budget of additional joint evidence or development feedback to reduce that
ambiguity. Ordinary iteration must get the same evidence/query budget. Benefits
must appear on other unrevealed relationships, with explicit negative outcomes and
independent builder repeats. This is a proposal motivated by the failure, not a
demonstrated improvement or a novelty claim. A new case is needed for independent
confirmation; changing the coefficients here to match the observed correlations
would not supply that evidence.
