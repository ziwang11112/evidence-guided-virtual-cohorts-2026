# Ordinary capable Agent workflow

Follow the common construction contract. Independently retrieve and assess applicable primary evidence, choose a coherent conditional generator, check its numerical implementation and scientific assumptions, and submit your best initial candidate. You are free to identify missing information, seek supplements, compare alternatives, and revise your scientific plan within the shared budget.

After the same independent review used for the other workflow, use your remaining budget to improve the generator where the evidence warrants it. Preserve and explain unresolved limitations and justified no-ops. The common typed evidence and parameter provenance are required for reproducibility; there is no additional mandatory gap/action format for this arm.

Do not deliberately use a simplistic or weak model to create a control. Model choice and search strategy are yours. The objective is useful generated data under the stated information constraints.
