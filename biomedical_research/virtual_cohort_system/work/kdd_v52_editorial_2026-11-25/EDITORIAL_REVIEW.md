The editorial practices adopted here are an explicit link from each claim to its experiment, one concrete example shared by the opening and lead figure, a short account of the nearest method, and captions that state what was measured. Scope qualifications and informative negative comparisons belong wherever omitting them would change the interpretation. An exact grid bound, a pair of feasible scenarios and an independent population estimate each require a separate label. The project licenses its original material under CC BY 4.0, credits MIT-licensed base material and exempts published paper excerpts from the CC license. No exemplar prose was reused.
