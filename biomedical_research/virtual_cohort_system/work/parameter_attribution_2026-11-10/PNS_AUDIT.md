It does not establish exact reproduction of the requested age/sex-adjusted regressions, and it does not identify all target discrepancy as transfer error. Multiplication by 5 gives **3.8770 and 2.9835**, with source SE 0.1795 and 0.1285. These SEs quantify source-estimate sampling precision, not residual SD or transport uncertainty. The requested [PNS operator](D:/AIR-014_ECRC_Paper_Clean_2026-08-16/biomedical_research/virtual_cohort_system/work/next_case_validation_2026-11-09/PROTOCOL.md:23) uses the common design `[1,(age−50)/10,female,(BMI−25)/5]`, equal-weight generated data and `peso_lab`-weighted reference data. It omits the additional source covariates and changes population, selection, weighting and measurement context.

The Beijing Table 1 SD identity gives raw r=0.545126 among 791 participants aged 65+ (SDs 20.98,11.22,17.59 for SBP,DBP,PP), and raw r=0.534443 overall. These are derived from rounded unadjusted SDs, assuming common paired observations and variance convention; they are not reported adjusted residual correlations. All authors explicitly leave conditional dependence unidentified and choose rho. A05 also cites the overall raw correlation as context. No source residual-r sampling interval was supplied.

**Why the declared parameters do not identify the requested outputs.** All five laws have nonlinear age terms, age-dependent residual scales and feasibility selection. The requested linear projection does not include the nonlinear age terms or interactions. Selection can change conditional means and covariance. Even before selection, pooling heteroscedastic innovations can change correlation from a fixed-covariate rho, and omitted mean structure can enter the linear-regression residuals. These are mathematically relevant differences, not a quantified decomposition of this batch’s error.

**Claim assessment.** “All copied coefficients were exactly reproduced” is defensible only for literal placement of the two source numbers in the model specifications. It is unsupported for the final requested regressions, and it does not apply to rho, which was assumed. “All error is transfer error” is not established: adjustment/operator mismatch, population transfer, model completion, nonlinear projection, selection, finite draws and uncertain reference estimates remain entangled. Existing independent numerical agreement supports reproducibility of the calculations; it does not isolate their scientific discrepancy. Agreement across authors is descriptive repeatability under shared inputs and conventions, not proof of correctness or calibrated uncertainty.
