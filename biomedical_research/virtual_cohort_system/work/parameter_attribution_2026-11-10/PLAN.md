# Retrospective parameter-to-analysis attribution audit

Date: 2026-11-10. Status at creation: in progress.

## Question

Does the existing frozen record support a storyline in which Agent-authored models accurately execute evidence and assumptions, while analysis-specific discrepancies arise at identifiable evidence-to-model and model-to-population interfaces?

This is a bounded retrospective audit, not a new clinical validation, benchmark, generation campaign, prospective prediction of reliability, or manuscript revision. Revealed reference values are available to the auditors. No authors will regenerate data against these values.

## Scope and permitted inputs

- PNS: all five final authors and three requested analyses, using frozen specifications/programs and existing aggregate results.
- Lipids: Q5 L01-L03 frozen laws and the previously completed algebra/result audit. Other lipid batches are contextual replication, not independent target cohorts.
- Iron: both final laws, source contingency-table algebra and existing aggregate results.
- Main CHNS/ENSANUT study: all twenty Agent constructions, documenting how relational evidence was actually used and whether the proposed provenance taxonomy fits.
- Existing public summary reports, evidence extractions and review records. The PNS auditor additionally checked the already-local INTERSALT p322 and specified Beijing primary-text passages; its report names the exact files and inspected scope. Other primary-source facts remain attributed to existing extraction/audit artifacts, not represented as a fresh fulltext review.

No patient or synthetic row files, new data acquisition, model/API calls for scientific construction, generator executions, target analyses, frozen edits or retrospective replacement of receipts.

## Attribution model

For each analysis retain five separate objects:

1. Source statistic with its population, measurement and analysis operator.
2. Adopted model parameters and their recorded origins.
3. Model-implied requested estimand, when derivable; otherwise explicitly unavailable.
4. Finite generated-data estimate, with within-law seed variation.
5. Real-sample reference estimate and available uncertainty.

REPORTED, DERIVED and ASSUMED describe parameter origins. They are not an ordinal credibility score. One analysis can depend on all three. Source applicability, unreported joint dependence, measurement bridges and transport assumptions must be recorded separately. A copied conditional parameter need not equal a differently adjusted regression projection after nonlinear modeling or selection.

## Deliverables and decision

Create original audit tables/notes with exact artifact pointers, a compact integrated attribution table and a conclusion distinguishing established findings, model-algebra deductions and untested explanations. Include counterexamples and unavailable quantities. Do not rank heterogeneous raw correlation, odds ratio and standard-deviation errors on one ratio scale.

Independent numerical/semantic checking follows table assembly. The audit can motivate an analysis-support card and future cross-provider or prospective study; it cannot establish that provenance predicts error or that all discrepancies are transport errors. Preserve manuscript v0.27 and frozen experiments unchanged.
