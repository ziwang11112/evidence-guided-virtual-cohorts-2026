The frozen lipid and iron examples support **faithful implementation of an assumption-explicit model**, rather than a uniquely faithful reconstruction of the evidence. Reported inputs, mathematical derivations, author assumptions, model-population estimands, finite generated estimates and measured-reference estimates must remain separate. Code can implement the chosen law correctly while its scientific translation remains underidentified.

This audit inspected existing frozen generator/specification text, admitted source extractions and aggregate summaries. It did not execute or import generators, read patient or generated rows, retrieve new evidence, modify frozen artifacts, or run a new experiment. Only this report and its [machine-readable attribution record](D:/AIR-014_ECRC_Paper_Clean_2026-08-16/biomedical_research/virtual_cohort_system/work/parameter_attribution_2026-11-10/LIPID_IRON_AUDIT.json) were created. The JSON contains 15 requested-analysis records tagged `main_analysis` (nine lipid, six iron) and six SD diagnostics tagged `supplementary_dispersion`. The source-to-model mapping explains why direct “source versus generated” comparisons are often invalid. Obirikorang supplies rural, community-pooled, **unadjusted Spearman** correlations BMI–TC .212, BMI–HDL −.100 and TC–HDL .106. These are not age/sex-adjusted raw slopes or age/sex/BMI-residual Pearson coefficients. Agongo supplies arithmetic means and individual marginal SDs in selected rural Ghana adults aged 40–60. Greiner supplies selected rural Malawi sex-specific geometric means and age quartiles; its unresolved transformed regression coefficients were not substituted for raw slopes. The source statistics also come from different populations and assays. [Obirikorang operator and population](D:/AIR-014_ECRC_Paper_Clean_2026-08-16/evcUseL03/sources/curated/OBIRIKORANG_2015_CURATOR_EXTRACTION.json:10), [Agongo moments](D:/AIR-014_ECRC_Paper_Clean_2026-08-16/evcUseL03/sources/curated/AGONGO_2018_CURATOR_EXTRACTION.json:101), [Greiner operators](D:/AIR-014_ECRC_Paper_Clean_2026-08-16/evcUseL03/sources/curated/GREINER_2021_CURATOR_EXTRACTION.json:151).

All three lipid generators convert reported ranks to latent Gaussian correlations using `2 sin(pi*rS/6)`, assuming a continuous Gaussian copula. They then **assume** that source pooled ranks can describe dependence within sex. The margins are lognormal, source sex labels are mapped to F/M, and the synthetic sex mixture is balanced. L01 additionally assumes latent age correlations .10/.15/.05 with BMI/TC/HDL; L02 uses .12/.25/.05. L03 instead assumes age independent of the biomarker vector conditional on sex. Its biomarkers remain dependent. These are scientific construction choices, not mechanically recovered facts.

L03's conditional age independence makes the exact projection transparent. Write `Wjk = sum_s p_s Cov(Yj,Yk | sex=s)`. The raw BMI slope for outcome Y is `W_BY/W_BB`, and the lipid residual covariance is `W_TH − W_BT*W_BH/W_BB`, with analogous residual variances. Sex-specific lognormal covariance is `m_j,s*m_k,s*[exp(r_jk*sigma_j*sigma_k)−1]`. Together these yield the audited L03 values. This demonstrates internal model coherence; it does not show that the corresponding adjusted source relations were observed. [L03 assumptions](D:/AIR-014_ECRC_Paper_Clean_2026-08-16/evcUseL03/candidates/initial/model_spec.json:80), [implemented construction](D:/AIR-014_ECRC_Paper_Clean_2026-08-16/evcUseL03/candidates/initial/generator.py:35).

For A, let `P_w` be the source joint law after weighting depleted ferritin by 2/3 and normalizing. The final law is `P_A = .8 P_w + .2(P_w,Hb × P_w,ferritin)`. The weighting changes margins but preserves the threshold OR; the independent redraw preserves those reweighted margins while reducing dependence. This exactly explains the model OR change 6.653137→4.645566 and joint prevalence change .076735→.067348. At fixed continuous margins, the same mixture gives population Spearman_after=.8*Spearman_before; no numerical pre-redraw coefficient was calculated here. These are identifiable consequences of an author-chosen structural operator, not a direct copying of literature. The claim that all discrepancies are “transfer” is defensible only if transfer is defined so broadly that it includes every conditioning bridge, distributional family, invented covariate law, structural transport operator and measurement assumption. That terminology does not identify a cause. More narrowly, the present comparisons cannot distinguish population/assay nontransportability from inadequate author reconstruction, source sampling uncertainty, target selection/sampling uncertainty or finite generated realization. In particular, it is invalid to subtract pooled Spearman .106 from residual Pearson .132 or .512 and call the difference a transport error between equivalent estimands.

A defensible description is: **the system preserves selected evidence types and values, makes the additional construction assumptions inspectable, and can verify that execution matches the declared law; target utility remains specific to the analysis and depends on assumptions that the source summaries do not identify.** The evidence does not establish that all assumptions are unavoidable, that the resulting construction is optimal, or that population nontransportability uniquely explains the residual discrepancy. No new generator changes or target tuning follow from this audit.
