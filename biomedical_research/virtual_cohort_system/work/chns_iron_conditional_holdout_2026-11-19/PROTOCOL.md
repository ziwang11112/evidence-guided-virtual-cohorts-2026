# Can conditional iron evidence improve an analysis on held-out communities?

19 November 2026. This protocol concerns whole-cohort generation for a specified scientific analysis. Choose the existing 2009 China Health and Nutrition Survey (CHNS) on measurement definitions and data access. The study family was exposed in glycemic experiments, and early codebook work exposed some marginal summaries. The exposure registry and iron experiments document no prior estimate of this new partition's hemoglobin–ferritin rank correlation. This is one within-study, geographically separated validation. It does not constitute a new external cohort. The previous Burkina Faso and Freetown references stay closed.

For same-population evidence, construct a **study-style supplement** using only A. Within a fine Hb band and depletion status, permuting ferritin values preserves all tabulated summaries including the new contrast, while changing overall ranks. ## Outcomes and interpretation

The primary scientific endpoint is unweighted whole-sample Spearman correlation of Hb and ferritin with average ranks for ties. The guard analysis is the crude natural log odds ratio of Hb<12 comparing ferritin<15 to ferritin≥15. Always run the same functions on real and virtual rows. Prespecify all three comparisons per constructor: initial versus evidence revision, initial versus revision with no new evidence, and the **primary incremental contrast** |rho_control − rho_B| − |rho_evidence − rho_B|. Positive favors supplying the additional relationship. Also report the conditional GM ratio in each output as a source-uptake check. A closer fit to A alone does not count as held-out improvement.

For exploratory cohort planning, a reduction ≥0.05 in absolute rank error is material, and final absolute error ≤0.10 is the working usefulness margin. At correlations 0.30 and 0.35 the independent-person Fisher-z planning formula changes the implied sample requirement by roughly one quarter. These are study-specific conventions. For the guard, require final OR within a factor of 1.50 of B and evidence/control ORs within a factor of 1.20 of each other. Retain the guard as indeterminate if any B crude cell contains fewer than 10 participants. Obtain 95% percentile intervals by 2,000 province-stratified whole-community bootstraps, holding frozen models fixed and using the same B replicates for all arms. Report at the arm level the median of paired constructor contrasts, the counts meeting each criterion and all individual results. A claim of a case-level material benefit for an arm requires all three sibling pairs, median incremental improvement ≥0.05 with bootstrap lower endpoint >0, at least two constructors meeting the final rank tolerance and the two guard conditions, and a B rank interval with half-width ≤0.10. The precision interval conditions on these construction sessions. A skill advantage, multiple-population transport or clinical equivalence would require further evidence.
