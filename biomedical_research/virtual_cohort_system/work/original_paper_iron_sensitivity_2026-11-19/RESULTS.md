# One paper supports a threshold association and leaves continuous ranks uncertain

Three independent GPT-6.1 Sol sessions each read Zhu et al.'s original article, reconstructed its crude counts and wrote a program to draw complete hemoglobin and ferritin records. The same two analyses were run on every generated cohort. The source threshold association was stable. Continuous rank correlation changed in every completed primary–alternative pair, including one reversal of sign. The external survey had 44 women with complete measurements and no woman below the ferritin threshold. It supplied an imprecise continuous association and no estimable threshold odds ratio. [Original article](https://doi.org/10.1024/0300-9831/a000014) · [Independent survey and codebook](https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/5CXCLX)

## What the constructors received

The full article, a target description of nonpregnant, nonlactating adult mothers in rural Burkina Faso, the two scientific questions and an executable table contract. The paper describes a selected population of women in Hebei. Each pair of alternative requests shared one sealed primary, model configuration and article. A separate statistical construction assumed independent ranks within the published cells. The nine author calls had no target measurements, scores, benchmarks, or web retrieval. The experiment measures interpretation and construction from an assigned paper. The proportions for the four cells were reconstructed from eight source strata and sum to 1,671 women. The crude odds ratio is (173 × 1,082)/(85 × 331) = 6.653. The source also reports geometric means and depletion proportions in three hemoglobin bands. ## External comparison

The admission order was recorded before outcome inspection. The first candidate, the rural Burkina Faso iron survey, qualified. Its codebook specifies hemoglobin in g/dL and ferritin in µg/L. Among 51 eligible women, seven lacked a ferritin measurement. The 44 complete cases covered 23 villages in two provinces. The unweighted four cells in source order were 0, 18, 0 and 26. The public depletion and anemia flags agreed with the recomputed thresholds in all 44 cases. With no measured iron-depleted women, the sample cannot estimate the anemia odds ratio comparing depleted and replete groups. In particular, the absent odds ratio is not zero. The weighted Spearman estimate was 0.069 with a 95% within-province village-bootstrap interval of −0.198 to 0.389. The unweighted estimate was 0.038. The interval describes uncertainty in the measured sample. Agreement on the sensitivity label does not establish accuracy in a new population. ## Interface and cost

Nine calls cost an estimated USD 1.36649 using recorded token usage and tariffs, including both incomplete calls. The output allowance was 12,288 tokens per call. Seven calls returned a complete Python program and JSON. All seven put their parameters one level deeper than the original runner expected. Their original 21 isolated attempts failed before producing rows. We registered a common missing-key correction after examining the three primary artifacts and before executing any study generator or opening either kind of outcome. The correction supplies the exact submitted inner parameter object, with the code, scientific values, task and seeds preserved. The raw statistical comparator ran through the original interface. All 21 corrected agent draws succeeded. This distinguishes raw end-to-end reliability from the utility of executable scientific models after the recorded mechanical correction.
