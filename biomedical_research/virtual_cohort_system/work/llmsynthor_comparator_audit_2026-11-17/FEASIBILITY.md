# LLMSynthor comparison feasibility for the KDD paper

17 November 2026, America/Chicago.

**Disposition.** Cite LLMSynthor as a close aggregate synthesis method. Its published description supports an adaptation for frequency tables, including the iron threshold table. A comparison on the current full continuous biomedical tasks would depend on additional frequency distributions, within-bin laws, or a new discrepancy engine. The author repository offers no executable implementation at the inspected revision. Carry the existing quantitative comparisons forward and keep this distinction in the manuscript.

## Test case compatibility

### Iron can share a threshold-frequency task

Zhu et al. Table IV can be collapsed to counts 173, 85, 331 and 1,082 for the four Hb/ferritin threshold cells. Their margins agree exactly with the admitted Table I counts. These frequencies and their coherent two-way operator fit the mechanism in Sections 3.2–3.3. The joint frequency gives the source crude OR 6.653 and both-low prevalence 10.353%. These are supplied-source alignment endpoints. For example, a calibrated method recovering the four source cell frequencies also recovers that OR by algebra. They remain estimates from selected postpartum, IUD-using women in China. Applying all four frequencies to the U.S. analytical population requires a transfer assumption. [Zhu et al.](https://doi.org/10.1024/0300-9831/a000014).

The original continuous task also includes ferritin geometric mean 17.4 µg/L and a requested continuous Spearman correlation. A geometric mean supplies an expected log concentration. It does not specify bin frequencies or determine a range within a threshold cell. Open-ended bins also need a within-range sampling rule before native finite-range proposals can produce continuous values.

The local arithmetic check holds the four exact cell probabilities and **both complete continuous marginals** fixed, including the ferritin geometric mean. One construction draws ranks independently *within* each admitted joint cell and gives Spearman **0.171**. The second rearranges intervals of rank within the same cell masses and gives **0.985**. These are two possible laws that agree on all admitted numerical summaries. Their values are illustrative examples rather than bounds or empirical estimates. This is a consequence of the available information, independent of which model samples from it. Details and the exact arithmetic checks are in [the script](check_aggregate_compatibility.py) and [its output](AGGREGATE_COMPATIBILITY.json).

### Blood pressure would require stronger assumptions or a different discrepancy

The Burkina Faso study and the curated additions supply means/SDs and an unadjusted Pearson block. The full paper also reports regressions with a larger adjustment set than the PNS task. Those statistics can support some projections and assumptions. They do not supply the frequency tables on which the native LLMSynthor correction operates. Converting the moments into quantile counts via a Gaussian family, for example, would impose the adapter's law as input. Feeding it PNS-derived bins would change the allowed-information setting. Adding regression and covariance discrepancies would be a new adaptation. [Burkina Faso source, Tables 2–6](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2024-089374).

The arithmetic check constructs two weighted discrete laws with the same source mean vector, the same reported BP/BMI SDs and all six published Pearson coefficients. Both use the same illustrative age SD and respect positive values, age at least 40, and SBP above DBP. The laws have different SBP frequency at or above an example cutoff of 140 mmHg (50% and 0%). The check concerns the supplied moment block. The examples are mathematical distributions and do not reconstruct the additionally adjusted regressions or describe patients. The result illustrates why a shared frequency input cannot be taken directly from the moment block.

A narrow paper-based pilot is feasible if the paper needs a comparison of synthesis from threshold frequencies. Share the frozen Zhu counts, population/measurement definitions and query with both methods. Keep dependence selection and cumulative discrepancy-guided batch allocation in the LLMSynthor adaptation. Share any added within-bin constraints and mark them as assumptions, or compare binary outcomes alone. Hold fixed the backbone, row count, cost ceiling and evaluator. Record what is copied from the source and what the adapter chooses. Source OR and joint-low prevalence should be labeled input recovery. Any independent continuous endpoint would need separate data support and separate interpretation. A binary-only run would remove the continuous analyses that are central to the existing tasks. The present audit closes with no paid head-to-head run and no performance estimate against LLMSynthor.
