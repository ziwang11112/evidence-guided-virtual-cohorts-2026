# From a research request to a sourced virtual cohort

This integration run starts with a research request about paired corneal thickness measurements. The scientific agent receives no starting citation, known paper title, reference population rows, fitted relationship or scoring key. The question was chosen before searching for studies. It concerns a different measurement from the prior paired-pressure development case. Feasibility of retrieving, using and generating from published evidence is evaluated in a single run. The newly selected study is not automatically an independent external validation cohort.

The search agent proposes Europe PMC queries, reads hits, opens original JATS articles, and chooses a primary paper and optionally one supporting paper. The primary population and measurement pair must be explicit. Additional papers require explicit attention to population, instrument and protocol differences. Work within three query actions, three opens and up to eight search-model decisions, then stop on a selection or inability to proceed. Only screened text/tables and captions from papers the agent opened can reach the constructor. Exclude participant workbooks/individual records and do not digitize scatterplots. The research team does not supplement unsuccessful searches or select papers from an answer key. Preserve failed requests and rejected articles.

Use fresh GPT sessions with the same configured model, medium reasoning. Budget $1.25 for search decisions plus $1.75 for the design/build/review sequence. In the modeling session hand over the original question and the frozen, identified selected texts. The model chooses all distributions, parameters and functions, and may keep, revise or abstain after trial-row feedback. The runner evaluates the frozen final program at three existing seeds, 10,000 virtual eyes per table. This is a sequential handoff; the final author cannot quietly add external sources after the handoff.

Record the search terms and available hits, papers opened/selected, proof of exact source delivery, evidence locators and case-relevant numerical extraction, model choices and independent computation of both estimands on raw virtual paired measurements. Preserve missing mappings, execution failures and all scenarios. Review the article for directly reported or algebraically derivable findings and uncertainty about the threshold; report discrepancy with actual reported comparable study statistics where possible. In the absence of a measured threshold reference, the generated percentage is a model estimate. Different scenarios alone do not demonstrate that all are compatible with the paper. When same-study paired public measurements can be verified without overlap or ambiguous mapping, compare after model closure and label them accordingly. Otherwise explicitly report what can be tested. No tuning to any revealed outcome.
