# Source-role audit and evidence-composition probe

Started 15 November and completed 16 November 2026 (America/Chicago). The retrospective audit is complete. The new controlled probe has prepared scientific inputs and passed mathematical preflight; no Agent experiment has been run for it.

## Finding and decision

The broad statement “Agents know evidence is inappropriate but copy it anyway” does not adequately describe these records. The delivered generators use several distinct statistical operations, usually accompanied by explicit assumptions. The practical unresolved issue is how those assumptions constrain the analyses that a virtual cohort can support.

Retain the virtual-data generation objective. Do not start a large evidence-harm study or adopt a reject-all applicability gate. The next useful diagnostic is whether an Agent can **compose complementary study summaries into the joint relationships needed by a requested analysis**, while separating identified results from scenario choices. This is a refinement of the proposed mechanism pilot, not a claim that the system already improves generation quality.

These are **not twenty errors**: the original task expressly permits an assumption-completed scenario. Nor is this a reproduction of the proposed “17/18 copying” count, whose selection rule remains unavailable.

All twenty programs hold their scientific model parameters fixed across individual sampling draws. They do not sample source-estimation or transport uncertainty across alternative laws. More rows or seeds can reduce Monte Carlo variation within a scenario; they do not test whether that scenario's missing relationships are correct. Fixed scenarios remain a valid output for explicitly conditional simulation uses.

## Three concrete distinctions

### Rejecting substitution does not identify the missing relation

DeepSeek lipid C1 averages two source ranks and assumes residual rho 0.14. Sol lipid C1 explicitly rejects the rank-to-residual substitution, but chooses factor loadings 0.5 and 0.3, implying rho 0.15. Thus a literal-copying label is not a sufficient account of the modeling decision. The missing conditional information remains missing in both routes.

### A source-derived value can remain assumption-dependent

Three blood-pressure laws derive covariance from reported SBP, DBP and pulse-pressure variation, then subtract variation explained by a chosen mean/covariate model. The identity is useful and algebraically legitimate. The source does not supply every needed target component; moving from that identity to the requested residual quantity still requires assumptions. An applicability check should preserve useful derivation while exposing its conditions.

Similarly, Claude lipid C2's zero HDL/non-HDL error correlation is not zero TC/HDL correlation. The implemented component sum implies nonzero TC/HDL dependence. Comparing a configuration number to an analysis result without retaining the operator would falsely diagnose an implementation mismatch.

### A specific final-output claim is not justified

DeepSeek lipid C2 redraws outcome pairs until positive, with a finite-attempt floor fallback. It nevertheless declares structural slopes and proposal rho to equal final population OLS quantities by exogeneity. Acceptance depends on covariates and outcomes, so the stated identity does not follow after selection. The prior audit had already identified this; this pass verifies the original code and claim rather than counting a new discovery.

A separate exact algebraic counterexample illustrates the issue without running or retuning that clinical model. With X equally likely 0 or 1, propose Y=X+N(0,1) and redraw Y until positive, retaining X. The proposal slope is 1; the final binary-X regression slope is about **0.489715**. This establishes that the generic preservation argument is invalid. It does not estimate the numerical impact in the old lipid candidate.

The two Claude decisions also describe differently defined source quantities as conflicting. Their own notes acknowledge the bridging assumptions. The comparison requires a common estimand before an empirical conflict can be asserted; it should not be counted as evidence of concealment or indiscriminate copying.

## Prepared diagnostic: complementary evidence rather than presumed harm

The previous proposed four-way manipulation labeled sources applicable/inapplicable in advance. The present audit supports a more precise first task: **common metadata only; an anchor summary; a complementary bridge summary; both**. Both summaries are truthful within each explicitly artificial population. They inform different parts of the joint law. The analysis can reveal inappropriate direct substitution without assuming all indirect evidence is useless.

| Family | Requested primary analysis | Anchor record | Complementary record |
|---|---|---|---|
| Covariate adjustment | Correlation of X and Y after linear adjustment for Z | Marginal X-Y Pearson correlation | X-Z and Y-Z Pearson correlations |
| Repeated measurements | Correlation of within-person X and Y changes | Correlation between stable person-specific components | Same-visit X-Y correlation |

The common records supply exact nuisance moments and, for the repeated-measure task, the stated component/visit assumptions needed for the algebra. These are controlled mathematical records, **not invented publications or clinical evidence**. Agents remain responsible for choosing the sampling model and completing unreported relationships. The record schema does not insert a clinical target coefficient.

The intended scale is two families × four evidence conditions × three fresh constructors = **24 unexecuted session slots**, using one economical existing GPT configuration. Combined-record ordering is balanced across the six combined slots. This is an information-composition diagnostic, not an end-to-end workflow superiority comparison and not a cross-model benchmark.

### What mathematical preflight established

Nineteen checks passed: all six witness covariance matrices are positive definite; independent projection/change calculations agree; each single-record condition admits different primary answers; combined records identify the requested primary functional; the selection counterexample changes its slope.

- Marginal X-Y correlation 0.5 is compatible with adjusted residual correlations **0.21875** and **0.479167** in two different valid worlds.
- Keeping both covariate correlations at 0.6 instead permits adjusted residual correlations **0.21875** and **0.53125** when the missing marginal relation differs.
- The same between-person summary permits change correlations **−0.6** and **+0.6**. The same visit-level summary permits **−0.6** and **+0.8** under different valid component completions.
- Combining each family's two records identifies the base world's primary quantity through covariance algebra. These witnesses show nonidentification; they are not complete identification intervals or a calibrated posterior.

## Evaluation and launch boundary

In the combined condition, evaluate whether the generated law/data satisfies both source operators and supports the requested derived analysis. In partial-information conditions, score the known source constraints and whether the Agent distinguishes identified from assumed quantities. **Do not score disagreement with one hidden completion as an error when the same inputs also support another completion.** An honest point scenario is allowed by the task.

Report executable delivery, source-operator consistency, requested analytical quantities, claimed identification and costs separately. Extra scenarios are sensitivity analyses unless probabilistic weights have a justified meaning. A model that refuses all generation or discards useful evidence must not appear superior merely by avoiding wrong numerical claims.

The scientific payloads and algebra have been prepared; **the API launch is not frozen or started**. Remaining implementation is the response/table contract, shared scorer, linkage to the existing isolated execution path, exact model/runtime and tariff reservation, followed by an immutable launch manifest. Current inputs should be reviewed together with that implementation before calling the first model. There is no new scheduler or automatic 384-construction queue.

## Implication for the paper

The positive objective remains analyzable virtual data from incomplete evidence. A potentially useful methodological contribution is preserving the meaning of a source statistic through numerical construction, including when an analysis depends on additional assumptions. This audit supplies a taxonomy and traceable examples; it does not prove an improved method, a causal harm mechanism, or calibrated analysis-confidence labels.

The proposed diagnostic is useful only if it informs those generation decisions. Evidence composition that improves final analyses would support the practical contribution; transparent uncertainty under incomplete information would delimit its uses. If ordinary Agents already compose the evidence correctly, that is a positive capability result and a reason to keep the system simple, not to add a compulsory gate.
