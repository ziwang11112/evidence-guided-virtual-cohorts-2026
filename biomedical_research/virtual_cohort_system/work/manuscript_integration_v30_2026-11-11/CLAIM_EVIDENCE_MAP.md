## Claims intentionally not made

The manuscript does not claim first-ever literature-to-medical-data synthesis, universal superiority to ordinary Agent or statistical methods, a general analytical gain from structured checking, learned biological mechanisms, safe replacement of measured biomarkers, calibrated provenance confidence, an independent clinical validation from reused references, or a causal evidence-indirectness explanation for all discrepancies. It does not multiply clinical evidence by counting synthetic rows or seeds.

The new figures and sections sharpen the three application contributions already supported. They do not convert the optional indirectness review into an experiment, a result or the title of the paper.
