No model calls, new author contexts, API calls, browser activity, downloads, generation or automation are performed here. ## Question and case choice

Do authors supplied the same incomplete evidence choose similar unreported parameters or shared completion assumptions across provider families, and where do they disagree? This tests declared model choices under controlled information. It does not test general generation superiority or whether agreement identifies a correct population model.

Use **PNS blood pressure** and **Q5 Turkana lipids**. PNS separates reported BMI coefficients from unsupported innovation dependence, age curves, scale heterogeneity, covariate distributions and selection. Lipids expose the bridge from pooled rank/marginal statistics to conditional joint structure, sex mixtures and raw-scale analysis. Neither packet prescribes a joint family, positive direction, residual rho, tail parameter or independence default. Do not add old fitted models as examples.

PNS retains age20–79, the existing selected weighted analytical-population description and measurement limitations, variables `row_id,age,female,bmi,sbp,dbp`, and the two BMI slopes plus paired residual Pearson correlation after `[1,(age−50)/10,female,(BMI−25)/5]`. Lipids retains its admitted adult population metadata, age/sex/BMI/TC/HDL schema, the two age/sex-adjusted BMI slopes and residual Pearson correlation after the same full design. Delivered lipid units remain mmol/L; requested slopes are mg/dL per5kg/m² using the existing 38.67 conversion. Keep the scientific questions visible; supply no desired answers.

## Twelve slots and comparable opportunity

Freeze three distinct provider/developer families and one actual model/mode per family before outputs. Three aliases or reasoning modes from one family do not meet the design. Use two fresh chats per case/family, without cloned histories, project memory or prior responses; record any setting that cannot be disabled or verified. Counterbalance case order across families and contexts and record real times. Two contexts within a family are repeated construction opportunities, not independently trained models.

Each context gets one initial response (30-minute deadline) and at most one 10-minute mechanical continuation/repair. Repairs may address missing files, schema/ABI errors or a definite code/spec mismatch. They may not introduce wanted effects, target comparisons or new numerical assumptions. Freeze and retain the initial scientific choices before any repair; retain changed choices separately, never overwrite them. No automated regenerate, replacement author or favorable-response selection. An interrupted/failed attempt consumes its slot; a logged continuation uses the same second-turn allowance.

Identical packet, turn and time rules provide comparable opportunity, **not matched computation**. Browser token/compute usage is recorded when exposed and otherwise null with a reason. Hidden prompts and unavailable exact snapshots are limitations, not fabricated controls. No new paid API or new account is proposed.

## What each author returns

Ask for `model_spec.json`, a minimal `generator.py` implementing the existing `generate(observed,spec,seed)` ABI with `observed=None`, and a concise source/assumption decision table. Require the existing schema and n_rows=3000; no generated table is requested from the web chat. Each scientific parameter must state its actual JSON path, statistical operator, conditioning, scale, unit, source pointer or derivation, and unverified assumptions. An author may explicitly leave a quantity unidentified; no common rho or tail parameter is forced into unrelated model families.

The common request should say: “Construct a joint model for the supplied population and requested analyses using only this packet. Choose and justify the statistical structure. Distinguish reported statistics, algebraic conversion and assumptions. Do not equate a literal model coefficient with a differently adjusted requested analysis. Return a specification, minimal generator and decision table. If an exact requested population estimand is not derived, leave it unavailable; do not substitute a sampled result or source coefficient.” The full final payload, including source and task content, must be frozen before launch.

A prose-only or specification-only response can support **declared-choice observations only**. It cannot support executable-model or generation-quality claims. Preserve such outcomes in the denominator.

## Primary coding and descriptive comparison

Code initial choices before repair using the same form across all slots. Preserve source population/measurement/adjustment; source operator and value; literal parameter operator/value/unit; reported/derived/assumed or mixed dependencies; bridge assumptions; requested estimand; and exact model-implied requested value **only if proved**, otherwise null. Provider-blind coding is preferred where practicable; reconcile disagreements against artifacts rather than target results.

| Case | Prespecified choice dimensions |
|---|---|
| PNS | Conditional innovation dependence, nonlinear age/interactions, residual family/heterogeneity, covariate distributions, eligibility/selection and measurement transport. |
| Lipids | Pooled-rank-to-conditional bridge, raw/log/rank scale and joint family, sex mixture/age structure, marginal variance reconstruction, support/selection and transport. |

Show all six choices per case, within-family pair agreement and cross-family modal counts with explicit denominators. For parameters with genuinely matching operators/conditioning/scales, show values, range, median absolute deviation and within-family pair differences; perform only justified unit conversions. Otherwise mark not comparable. A latent copula correlation, innovation rho, raw Pearson, adjusted residual Pearson and Spearman are not one numerical variable. Literal regression coefficients are not automatically the requested OLS projections.

Do not pool incomparable quantities, impute omissions as zero, choose a convergence tolerance after seeing values or infer a universal prior from a small range. Six contexts per case and three families are too few for universal convergence, causal provider comparisons, calibrated error prediction or cross-model superiority. Shared choices may reflect the shared packet/prompt rather than an independent common prior.

## Executability check and stopping rule

At a later authorized execution, after initial choices are captured and final code/specs frozen, allow at most **one schema-only run per construction**, seed31001, using the existing approved WSL/bubblewrap worker and declared n_rows=3000. The maximum is12 runs,60seconds each. Check completion, schema, count, finite values and code/spec traceability only. No requested clinical analysis, source-feedback fitting, reference lookup or accuracy comparison is part of this stage. Record all failures and no seed retries. Do not display generated rows in the report. If the approved worker is unavailable, mark execution unverified; no unrestricted local import/run fallback.

Completion means accounting for all12 slots and initial/repair/failure artifacts. A practical **go** threshold for a separately designed larger choice study is ≥10/12 complete, traceable code/spec submissions with schema-only executable receipts and ≥1success in each of the6case-by-family cells. This is operational feasibility, not a scientific convergence threshold. Otherwise stop this pilot, report the gaps and design any repair as a separate version; do not add replacement contexts. If only prose/spec capture succeeds, report that narrower outcome explicitly.

No full clinical evaluation is planned here. A future clinical or prospective reliability-card study would require its own frozen estimands, reference-exposure accounting, calibration design and authorization. This pilot cannot resolve whether earlier disagreement is wholly transfer error or whether agreement implies correctness.
