# Scientific-use review of the v0.30 examples

11 November 2026. This unit reviews existing aggregate evidence for the application's main question: what specified scientific analyses can the final virtual data support? It does not launch an evidence-indirectness benchmark or a new generator experiment.

## Scope and method

Two complementary examples were selected for biomedical interpretation, not to estimate a project-wide success rate: all two ENSANUT constructions and all six recent lipid constructions. Every primary analysis and constructor in these examples is retained. The eight initial laws produced 24 saved tables and 72 primary seed estimates. Two completed repair branches from one lipid law add six dependent tables and 18 primary estimates; they are not new independent authors. Both clinical references were already exposed during development.

An independent numerical audit reconciled saved aggregate results, seed-level estimates, reference definitions and repair lineage without reading patient or synthetic individual records. It passed 587 checks. A separate biological interpretation review checked three primary/methodological sources, including the original Turkana supplementary measurement protocol. Neither review is a completed human biomedical evaluation.

## Findings relevant to the main contribution

| Analysis/use | Existing evidence | Supported interpretation and unresolved requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Change in the BMI–HbA1c association after glucose adjustment | Both ENSANUT generators and all six draws preserve attenuation. Mean coefficients change from 0.09165 to 0.07509 and 0.07140 to 0.04733 HbA1c percentage points per 5 kg/m². | The same generated data support a meaningful conditional-association comparison. This is not a causal mediation result. |
| Age association and HbA1c variability | Age coefficients exceed the reference point. HbA1c SD is about 0.362/0.406 versus 0.527; residual RMS about 0.295/0.324 versus 0.488. | The usable adjustment pattern does not establish calibrated power calculations, prediction intervals or individual heterogeneity. Those are different uses requiring their own evidence. |
| Joint lipid analyses | All six constructor means have lower residual TC–HDL correlation and HDL SD than the saved sample reference. All primary slopes and both spread diagnostics are shown. | Several associations are computable and interpretable, but joint/variation-sensitive uses are not yet supported by agreement across these measures. |
| Direction near zero | Workflow 2 HDL slopes are +0.0312, −0.1489 and +0.0829 mg/dL per 5 kg/m²; their mean is −0.0116. | A mean direction is not stable preservation across draws. Retain the signed results, not only the mean. |
| Completed mathematical repair | Both repair policies corrected the same Ordinary 2 divergent ideal TC moments. The TC slope moved closer to the reference point; residual dependence barely changed and spread agreement did not improve. | Checks can improve mathematical validity without establishing a general scientific-quality benefit. Report both repaired branches rather than presenting the initial law as still unrepaired. |

Real-sample values remain uncertain references, not known population truths. ENSANUT uses the saved equal-eight-cell analysis and empirical cluster-resampling stability bands. Lipid slopes have saved HC3 conditional-sensitivity intervals, not design-based population confidence intervals. No residual-correlation interval was invented. The TC slope reference interval includes zero.

Source/measurement review distinguishes an ideal positive lipid-component model from a model of measured assay outputs. The retained HDL-above-TC row is not removed or justified by an assumed assay-error rate. The original Turkana study's measurement protocol supplies context, not evidence that our generated assay process is valid.

## Decision and next action

Keep the application story: Agent-authored virtual data can support specified analyses, with inspectable evidence and assumptions. Do not promote these selected patterns into blanket scientific validity, overall workflow superiority or a universal failure theory. The workflow's source/assumption records help explain use boundaries; they are not validated confidence scores.

The packet is ready for a biomedical collaborator to assess the concrete use decisions. All response fields remain blank; no person was contacted. Human review is not retrospectively claimed and is not a substitute for independent empirical validation. Its most useful input is whether a particular claim/use needs restriction or a specific additional source/measurement check. Such feedback can guide a focused next experiment; no new batch is scheduled by this unit.

The manuscript v0.30 and frozen experiments are unchanged. This unit made no API calls, generated no new data, fitted no new patient references, acquired no new dataset, and performed no public GitHub synchronization.
