# Scale of the measured comparisons and a handgrip subgroup replay

This is a retrospective follow-up to v0.46. All three target handgrip samples and their standardized whole-sample estimates have already been opened. The following analysis choices were recorded before calculating new subgroup effects or threshold curves for this release.

## Scientific question

Does the lower error of the agent's coupled hand model relative to random pairing extend to women and men aged 50–59 and 60–69 within the measured populations? How often is the resulting mean absolute right–left difference also within 1 kg of the measured estimate? The previously registered 1 kg planning margin applied to the standardized main estimand. Its application to each subgroup here is descriptive. Estimate both closeness to the reference and improvement over independent pairing.

## Inputs and analysis

- Use all four Australian source strata and the three frozen seeds. Use the realized marginal-only agent law shared by all four marginal-only author sessions. Verify every archived permutation digest against the sealed prediction record. Keep the exact independent pairing expectation as the marginal-only comparator. As a secondary comparison report all four joint-evidence authors and the source-calibrated statistical rule, which both had access to the additional German evidence.
- Read the original eligible sample with the archived loader, extraction rules, design variables and weights. Both US NHANES and rural South African HAALSI used the first two measurements per hand. Report all eight sex-by-decade comparisons at these two principal sites. Separately report all four Brazilian EDOC subgroups, which used maximal-trial grip. Subgroups are nested within studies. No simulations or model revisions are needed.
- The primary display reports mean absolute hand difference in kilograms. Report pointwise 95% intervals for the measured subgroup means under the original stratified delete-one-cluster jackknife. Also report each site's full threshold profile for an absolute difference of at least 0, 1, …, 12 kg, giving each source stratum equal weight. At 5 kg, compare both measured and virtual estimates to the earlier pooled analysis. The profiles are descriptive and the thresholds are nested.
- For a measured point or interval, distinguish a reduction in absolute error relative to independence from an absolute discrepancy small enough for the intended use. Keep results for every cell, including weak or reversed results. Use the frozen 1 kg as a descriptive ruler. No cellwise hypothesis tests or claim about simultaneous coverage.
- Verify reconstruction of the previously released pooled virtual and measured means, 5 kg screening rates and measured confidence limits. Cross-check the new subgroup means and their jackknife intervals from design-unit sufficient statistics. Publish aggregate estimates and code. Participant-level and generated records stay in their existing local locations.
