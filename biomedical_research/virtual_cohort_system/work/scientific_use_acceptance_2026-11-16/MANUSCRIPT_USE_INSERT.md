# Proposed scientific-use clarification for the next manuscript revision

This is an insertion proposal, not a new manuscript version or experiment. DRAFT_v0_32.md and its compiled artifacts remain unchanged.

## Evaluation objective

We evaluate analysis-specific utility: whether a virtual cohort supports a stated scientific analysis under the same eligibility rules, adjustment set, transformations, weighting and estimator as the real-data reference. The reference is an estimate from an observed sample, rather than known population truth. We distinguish reconstruction of supplied source findings, developmental comparisons against exposed external references, and controlled tasks with mathematically identified parameters. Synthetic sample size and repeated draws do not constitute additional clinical evidence.

## What the present results establish

The lifestyle example reconstructs the urban BMI pattern: the urban–pastoralist contrast is 1.700–2.251 kg/m² across six virtual tables, compared with 1.882 in the reference. Complementary joint evidence improves PNS adjusted SBP–DBP dependence, with mean absolute reference difference decreasing from 0.17962 to 0.01277 across the two three-constructor arms. This is a targeted use improvement; the SBP slope and dispersion become less close, while rural lipid contrasts and several variation measures remain discrepant. The ENSANUT example additionally preserves attenuation of the BMI–HbA1c coefficient after glucose adjustment in both constructors and all six draws. These results support specified association uses, not uniform numerical adequacy, causal conclusions, individual measurement recovery or unrestricted reuse.

## Acceptable differences and the remaining validation

No biomedical equivalence margin was prespecified in these experiments. We report raw-unit discrepancies and reference uncertainty rather than assigning a post-hoc pass threshold. A scientific tolerance must be justified for the intended use, independently of the result being judged. Separate confidence-interval overlap and nonsignificance do not establish equivalence; synthetic-only standard errors also omit source, structural and transport uncertainty. The prioritized follow-up is one matched-size real–real versus virtual–real empirical sampling diagnostic on the existing lifestyle case. Its purpose is to contextualize ordinary sampling and sample-availability effects, not to create independent validation cohorts or to retune completed generators. This diagnostic is planned and has not been run.
