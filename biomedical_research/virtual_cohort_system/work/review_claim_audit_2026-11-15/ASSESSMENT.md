# Assessment of the proposed evidence-indirectness paper

15 November 2026. Root-led read-only scientific audit plus aggregate arithmetic. No new model calls, generation, patient refits, manuscript revision, automation or GitHub publication. This note adds to the [11 November audit](../evidence_indirectness_review_2026-11-11/REVIEW_ASSESSMENT.md); it does not overwrite its findings or restart its proposed pilot.

## Decision

The review identifies a useful experimental question, but it overstates both the established phenomenon and its novelty. Keep the practical objective: **Agents construct virtual biomedical data that can support specified scientific analyses**. Investigate evidence applicability as a possible determinant of that quality, rather than replace the objective with a preselected claim that Agents knowingly misuse evidence.

The most defensible question is:

> When an Agent converts study-qualified statistics into a joint generator, which uses are directly supported, which require a valid transformation, and which depend on an unverified assumption? Can improving those decisions improve the requested analyses or appropriately represent unresolved alternatives?

“Flagged-but-adopted” is an observable candidate behavior, not automatically a modeling error. Acknowledging a population gap while using a weak prior or an explicitly conditional scenario can be reasonable. The failure to test is an unsupported numerical or scientific claim: for example, presenting an unadjusted rank statistic as an identified adjusted Pearson correlation, or reporting uncertainty that never reaches generated analyses.

## A concrete counterexample changes the proposed explanation

The original [DeepSeek decision](../cross_model_api_pilot_2026-11-10/candidates/LIPID_DS_PRO_C1/decision_record.json) at lines 316–317 states the midpoint-and-rounding operation and the Ghana/Turkana, unadjusted/adjusted and Spearman/Pearson gaps. Its [program](../cross_model_api_pilot_2026-11-10/candidates/LIPID_DS_PRO_C1/generator.py) uses the resulting rho in a shared-factor residual law.

The original [Sol decision](../cross_model_api_pilot_2026-11-10/candidates/LIPID_OA_SOL_C1/decision_record.json) at lines 342 and 353 explicitly derives 0.5 × 0.3 = 0.15 while rejecting the source rank-to-residual substitution. Its [program](../cross_model_api_pilot_2026-11-10/candidates/LIPID_OA_SOL_C1/generator.py) implements those factors. Their generated means are approximately 0.151 and 0.139, versus the existing sample reference 0.512.

Thus low dependence has at least two documented construction paths. Refusing direct substitution alone need not solve the data-quality problem. Also, the original task explicitly permits assumptions and requests acknowledgment of mismatches. A disclaimer can reflect instructed transparency; it does not independently establish spontaneous recognition followed by knowingly invalid action.

## Novelty and terminology

Primary sources rechecked on 15 November:

- [TruthTrap, Findings EACL 2026](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-eacl.155/) studies factually correct but misleading hints. Its abstract and official publication record were inspected here.
- [MisleadQA, arXiv 2610.24851](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.24851) directly studies true but out-of-scope context, alone and with correct context. Its scope-recognition probe is a **separate request** linked to answering behavior. Relevant abstract and scope-method passages were inspected here; this is a preprint, not a newly verified conference acceptance.
- [GRADE Book: indirectness](https://book.gradepro.org/guideline/indirectness) states that imperfect PICO matching alone is not enough to downgrade evidence: there must be a reason to expect meaningful effect differences. The direct app required JavaScript; the indexed primary chapter text was inspected. The proposed five modeling dimensions are our extension, not GRADE's official five-item rule.

Consequently, neither “true evidence can mislead” nor “recognition need not prevent misuse” is an untouched general phenomenon. The narrower opportunity is to connect **source definition → same-session numerical commitment → executable joint model → multiple downstream scientific analyses**. Whether this yields a distinct method or empirical contribution remains to be tested. This was a focused overlap check, not an exhaustive novelty search.

## Corrections needed before any randomized experiment

1. **Separate compatibility labels from outcome error.** Applicability labels come from source population, measurement and estimand definitions plus justified bridges. Target microdata supply uncertain analytical references, not an answer key for which external study is applicable. Close-to-reference evidence is not automatically the most applicable.
2. **Use a relative condition name.** “No focal relationship evidence” is more accurate than “no evidence”: task descriptions, nuisance information and model pretraining remain. Separate removal of evidence from an instruction to ignore it.
3. **Separate epistemic and numerical outcomes.** Record directly supported use, exact transformation, disclosed borrowing, unidentified completion and unsupported direct claims. Numerical equality alone misses transformed copying and falsely counts valid conversions as failures.
4. **Use evidence conditions for the information question.** None/direct/indirect/both with one fixed Agent tests effects of supplied information. It does not replace the existing end-to-end comparison where both policies start from a request and retrieve sources. A later review-policy comparison should hold its sources, tool access and budget constant.
5. **Randomization has a defined scope.** It can identify assigned-information effects within frozen tasks. It does not undo retrospective task selection, earlier target-informed design decisions or model training exposure. Previously revealed references do not become new-population confirmation.
6. **Do not make interval inclusion the definition of usable data.** Report effect estimates in scientific units, dispersion and joint relations, reference uncertainty and complete analysis profiles. Equivalence needs a justified scientific margin, not whichever width a real sample CI happens to have.
7. **Do not build a reject-all gate.** Exact conversion, defensible borrowing, further retrieval and explicit scenario generation can all be legitimate. Pooling differently adjusted coefficients does not repair their incompatibility. Report loss of useful information and delivery/abstention as well as incorrect direct use.
8. **Count authors, tasks and calls separately.** The proposed 384 is a constructor count, not necessarily 384 API requests. The observed request-only study used 331 calls for twelve constructors. Simple same-process arithmetic gives about USD127.53 for 384 constructors before extra review; this is not a forecast. Short supplied-packet calls could cost less. Neither USD50 nor eight repetitions per cell is justified without a priced/powered design.
9. **Avoid fragile inference.** Multiple endpoints from one cohort are dependent. Six task labels do not guarantee enough independent families for a stable random-effects estimate. Start with task-level effects and their uncertainty; use controlled randomization for the assigned-condition contrast. Generated rows and seeds do not enlarge the number of model constructors.
10. **Use a meaningful stopping rule.** An underpowered nonsignificant harm contrast does not establish no harm. If the manipulation changes neither numerical decisions nor outcomes, diagnose the probe. If disclosed borrowing is legitimate or a proposed intervention only changes wording, do not expand that mechanism claim. None of these outcomes by itself invalidates the practical generation project.

## Recommended next unit, still proposed

First complete a narrowly specified decision ledger on the already closed two-round roster. A row is a numerical evidence-use decision for a requested analysis, nested within its constructor. It needs source definition, intended use, acknowledgment, actual code use, required bridge and propagated uncertainty. Preserve delivery failures and dependent review versions separately. Judge source-role validity without using target errors; obtain human biomedical/statistical adjudication for ambiguous transfers before claiming a validated prevalence. The existing reviewer packages contain zero human responses.

If that audit establishes recurring unsupported numerical commitments, use the already proposed **24-constructor controlled pilot**, not a new 384-constructor queue: two operator families × four evidence conditions × three fresh constructors, using one economical GPT configuration. Candidate families are marginal versus adjusted dependence and between-person versus same-visit dependence. Artificial evidence must be labeled artificial; no fabricated publications. Include valid conversions and legitimate indirect borrowing. Multiple compatible hidden laws should test ambiguity representation rather than reward guessing an unidentified truth. This demonstrates mechanism, not patient-data validation.

Only then test a targeted source-role intervention against equally resourced ordinary review. Its success must involve the generated data or useful uncertainty/scenario outputs, not just better notes. Broaden tasks/models only if that small unit changes the scientific judgment.

## Paper implication

Use the practical generation result to open the paper, then explain why scientific usefulness differs by analysis and evidence support. Source-rich reconstruction, external transfer and uncalibrated analysis reuse answer different questions and all can be retained. The quality-control results show genuine repairs; the policy comparisons show no stable advantage; the source traces expose assumptions. These are compatible findings.

A focused methods story is possible: **evidence-guided generation for scientific use, with explicit handling of the gap between reported statistics and required joint structure**. A stronger reliability-paper story would require the controlled findings; it is an option, not an established pivot. Do not claim “scientific Agents no longer invent numbers,” “the workflow works by increasing literature dependence,” or “adding evidence causes harm” from the current observations alone.

Current disposition: no new batch queued; no change to v0.31, frozen methods or historical results. The next decision is whether the trace audit justifies a mechanism pilot that improves the central data-generation objective.
