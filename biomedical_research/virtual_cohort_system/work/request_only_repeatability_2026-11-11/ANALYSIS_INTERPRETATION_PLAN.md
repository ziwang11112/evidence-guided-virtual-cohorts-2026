# Interpretation fixed before the repeatability batch

This document fixes the reporting questions for the newly authorized twelve-session unit. It does not change either Agent's research request or scientific inputs. Existing clinical aggregate references are already exposed at project level. New author contexts provide independent construction opportunities, not new clinical validation cohorts.

## Primary purpose

Assess whether repeated, autonomous request-to-generator construction produces usable scientific-analysis profiles under the current implementation. The ordinary Agent is a valid method, with the same retrieval, submission, execution and checking opportunities. Workflow superiority is an empirical possibility, not a prerequisite or intended outcome.

## Denominators and units

- Twelve preassigned sessions: two requests, two policies, three constructors per cell. Preserve all assigned sessions, including inability, incomplete output, operational failure and normal resource exhaustion.
- At most twelve selected scientific laws and thirty-six final tables. Three prescribed seeds per selected law are conditional simulation repeats, not additional constructors, studies or patients.
- All thirty-six planned final slots remain in the output ledger. Missing/unexecutable slots are unavailable with an explicit reason; they are not zero-valued coefficients or silently discarded attempts.
- Do not replace an unsuccessful author, select a favorable seed, or import a prior pilot author as a planned replicate.
- The first four-session block is an implementation/accounting check. It does not expose clinical-comparison results or decide continuation using scientific agreement. All twelve sessions must be terminal before the comparison dashboard is produced.

## Scientific analyses

For blood pressure, retain both age/sex-adjusted BMI slopes per 5 kg/m^2 and SBP–DBP residual Pearson correlation after age/sex/BMI adjustment. For lipids, retain the corresponding total-cholesterol and HDL slopes and their residual correlation. Keep the frozen unit conversions and estimator implementations. Include every prespecified distribution, validity and execution diagnostic; do not introduce a favorable composite score.

Report each constructor's mean across the three final seeds together with the individual seed estimates/range and the saved clinical sample reference. Use native units, show signed and absolute differences, and state the reference's actual uncertainty convention. A seed range measures Monte Carlo variation conditional on one selected law. It is not a confidence interval for transport, model choice, source validity or a target-population truth.

Where a saved reference interval exists, display it with its original limitations. Crossing or overlapping an interval is not a newly defined equivalence test. Do not manufacture an interval where none is saved. No scientific-use tolerance or win threshold will be selected retrospectively from known reference values. No broad superiority test will treat six estimands or thirty-six tables as independent clinical datasets.

## What the Agent actually did

For every assigned session, report the requested and returned model identity, number of model/tool calls, retrieved sources and versions, candidate submissions, executed trials, final selection or reason for nonselection, elapsed time and accounted model cost. Separate code/interface repairs from scientific changes. A retained candidate is a valid outcome; no revision benefit is implied by the presence of a feedback opportunity.

Traceability concerns observable artifacts: source locator and statistic, its population/measurement/adjustment conditions, adopted quantity, conversion or assumption, implemented law and affected analysis. It does not reveal private reasoning or certify source extraction, transportability or clinical suitability. Missing fields and unresolved source conflicts remain visible. Post hoc source audits may explain a discrepancy but cannot turn it into a prospectively predicted failure.

## Interpretation and next-step decision

Show results for both policies together, all constructors and both tasks. If a policy performs better on one analysis and worse on another, describe that profile. Repeated agreement on a subset supports those uses in these developmental contexts; persistent discrepancy limits those uses. Constructor agreement is not proof of correctness, and stochastic row count is not additional external evidence.

The current batch can strengthen repeatability, delivery, cost and analysis-specific utility claims. It cannot establish general superiority, prospective transport to an unseen cohort, causality, savings in actual specimen collection, or a validated confidence score. Optional new-model or glycemia experiments require a subsequent decision after this complete batch; neither is triggered automatically by a favorable result.

## Budget interpretation

The newly authorized cap is USD4 per session and USD48 for this twelve-session unit, including conservative reservations for calls whose usage is unknown. It is a ceiling rather than expected spend or a verified account balance. Current official pricing requires a long-context multiplier above 272,000 input tokens. This unit corrects the cost/reservation arithmetic accordingly; scientific policies, byte/call ceilings and analysis operators remain unchanged. A conservative reservation may deny a very large call before the nominal byte ceiling. Historical pilot receipts are unchanged, and their observed input counts were all below that surcharge threshold.

Model charges are estimates from returned usage and the frozen tariff, not provider invoices, human review cost or the full research project's cost. No API retry, fallback provider or extra author is granted merely because an output is unfavorable.
