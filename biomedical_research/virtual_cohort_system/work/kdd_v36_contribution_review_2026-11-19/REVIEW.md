# Which results can we credit to the Agent?

Technical review of the 19 November 2026 KDD working paper at commit `368aa30`. **The strongest evidence concerns authorship of scientific models.** The Agent interpreted published tables, wrote sampling laws and made choices whose effects can be checked on the same virtual records. The open empirical question is whether those choices help a researcher obtain a useful answer to a new analysis. ## A statistical check on the interpretation of Figure 4

I used the three existing statistical-comparator draws, totaling 60,000 synthetic records. I held every hemoglobin value fixed and permuted the existing ferritin values. Pairing increasing hemoglobin ranks with increasing or decreasing ferritin ranks yields the largest or smallest possible Spearman correlation for that particular partition and those empirical marginals. This follows from the rearrangement inequality applied to the products of the global centered ranks within each cell.

| Permitted operations on the frozen draws | Smallest Spearman | Largest Spearman | Crude odds ratio |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Original statistical pairing | 0.3114 | 0.3114 | 6.5641 |
| Permute only within the same hemoglobin band **and the same ferritin-depletion status** | −0.0972 | 0.7159 | 6.5641 |
| Permute only within the same hemoglobin band | −0.3509 | 0.9700 | 6.5641 |

All four rearrangements retained the 16 original source-check estimates to absolute tolerance $10^{-12}$. The stricter permutation also retained each person's binary ferritin status. Reading the hash-verified historical draws and replaying the published comparator in memory gave identical reported endpoints. The frozen scorer and a separate calculation on rank products agreed. This is a retrospective deterministic illustration of the argument already in the appendix. The endpoints are mathematical extremes of a fixed empirical sample. Biological plausibility, coverage of a new population and sharp bounds over every source-compatible law remain open. Two implementation details change the attribution. All three iron primary authors classified the threshold association as derivable and the continuous one as sensitive before disclosure. Each alternative saw its parent's full sealed artifact, including both judgments. Seven agreeing output records therefore represent three independent initial judgments and four completed follow-ons. The largest shift came from the ordinary alternative, which assumed a within-band Gaussian copula correlation of −0.75. The paper supplies no biological estimate for that parameter. Only one parent completed both alternative requests. Of nine scheduled calls, two hit the output limit and all seven complete programs initially failed the parameter-binding contract. The recorded binding correction recovered all seven scientific programs before external disclosure while preserving their code and constants. These are distinct findings about interpretation, prompting and end-to-end execution. ## The highest-value KDD validation

**Can the Agent use a further piece of conditional evidence to improve a held-out scientific analysis in the same population, while preserving an analysis that was already supported?** This would distinguish useful scientific modeling from the ability to produce different answers on demand. The current controlled comparisons already show that the specifically informed discrepancy improved in both domains and all four companion discrepancies increased. A particularly informative next test keeps the endpoint being scored out of the added evidence and holds population and measurement constant. KDD Research weighs originality and technical merit alongside execution, potential impact and reproducibility. The proposed experiment is our judgment about this manuscript's evidential gap. [Controlled results in the manuscript](../../manuscript/kdd_draft_v36_2026-11-19/main.tex) · [KDD Research evaluation](https://kdd2027.kdd.org/research-track-call-for-papers/)

1. **Select on design and precision.** Prefer a genuinely independent sample from the same population, with the same assay and enough paired measurements for both analyses. Verify that the source article excludes the evaluation participants and the held-out answer. For a split of one study, split at its sampling unit, recompute every exposed summary on the evidence portion, mask overlapping full-study results and label the exercise a reconstruction of the article. Fix practical error tolerances from the intended research use before disclosure. Estimate sampling variability using the study design. A sparse or uninformative reference leaves the result inconclusive.
2. **Give two arms the same paper, scientific request, model, tools and budget.** Compare an ordinary coding Agent with the proposed workflow. Neither receives a prepared extraction from the study team. Freeze each initial program, its primary estimate and the assumptions it identifies. Give both arms one prespecified, equally available conditional summary from the evidence portion and one opportunity to revise. For iron, a possible addition is a ferritin geometric-mean contrast between finer hemoglobin strata within one broad band. First verify analytically that the added contrast informs dependence and neither reports nor determines the held-out whole-sample Spearman correlation. The crude odds ratio serves as a stability check.
3. **Score what a user would do with the data.** Freeze all programs before evaluating the sealed portion. Use the identical analysis, units and weighting throughout. The primary contrast is the change in absolute error of each prespecified primary law for the held-out endpoint before and after receiving the extra summary. Report both arms, their paired or blocked difference across construction replicates, uncertainty from the measured sample, and whether the change reaches the predeclared practical margin. Check that the stable analysis stays within its tolerance. A blinded biomedical reviewer can flag unsupported parameter values or implausible joint patterns before disclosure. Retain failed programs in the usability denominator. Report sensitivity ranges separately from accuracy of the nominated law.

Improvement in both arms would support the practical value of generating analyzable cohorts from the added evidence. A reproducible difference between matched arms would support a workflow-specific claim. A wider range of model answers without better held-out performance would support sensitivity analysis as the demonstrated use. A single source family establishes a case-level result.
