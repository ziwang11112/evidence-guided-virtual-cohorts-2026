# CHNS statistical comparator for the two iron analyses

**Decision fixed on 19 November 2026 (America/Chicago).** The earlier CHNS B assessment was complete and its results were public to the analyst designing this comparison. This is a retrospective comparator for one archived case. The new protocol locks a single statistical construction and its scoring before evaluating its draws. It cannot restore blinding or support a new prospective validation claim.

**Question.** Using the evidence available to the six original-evidence CHNS constructors, how well does a conventional model with independent measurements within each reported hemoglobin band reproduce the two requested analyses? Does removing only the within-band pairing in the agent outputs give a similar answer?

## Construction and information

Use the original Zhu article (Hebei women, DOI 10.1024/0300-9831/a000014) for the scientific setting and the previously supplied CHNS A tables for numerical constraints. Give the target-population A summaries precedence wherever the sources overlap. The table already contains an exact crude odds ratio. Do not add the supplemental contrast, seek further publications or access participant-level A or B data. The six original-evidence controls did not receive that contrast. This comparison starts at the statistical modeling step, with the aggregates already extracted. The baseline is a specified statistical algorithm, without an LLM call.

1. Draw the Hb band with probability equal to its reported A count divided by the A total. Fit a beta by moments on [0,12) or [12,14) for the two bounded bands, and a gamma by moments on the excess over 14 g/dL for the upper band. Treat the reported sample moments as estimates of population moments. The support is defined by positivity and by the band cutoffs.
2. For log ferritin fit the maximum differential entropy density on the real line with the reported log mean, log variance, and probability below log(15). Its two pieces are one underlying normal truncated on the two sides of log(15) and reweighted to the observed threshold mass. Solve its two continuous moment equations separately per band. The log scale is the scale of the supplied geometric summaries. Use this one density. An infeasible moment system stops the run.
3. Sample Hb and ferritin independently conditional on band. The three distinct ferritin distributions can induce a positive marginal rank association even with zero within-band dependence. This is a transparent reference assumption. It neither identifies the true dependence nor spans all conventional statistical models.
4. Generate 12,000 records for each of the three original seeds (191901, 191903, 191909), one law, no retries with scientific changes. Use the pooled 36,000 records for point estimates, as in the original study. Seed results describe Monte Carlo variation. Keep all individual generated records local.
