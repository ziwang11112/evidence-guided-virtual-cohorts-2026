# What does age pairing add to the published lifestyle contrasts?

This is a retrospective attribution check of the six already frozen Turkana tables (two agent programs, three draws each). The nine real effect estimates and the earlier matched-size summaries are known. The test will change only the pairing of adult age with the three generated outcomes. Use all tables, all three biomarkers, and all three age- and sex-adjusted group comparisons. Do not tune a generator, use real participant rows, rank constructors by success or treat this as a new cohort.

For each published additive contrast, compute its original raw-unit OLS coefficient on the whole virtual table. Permute the entire vector of three outcomes jointly within each lifestyle-by-sex cell while keeping the design rows, measurements and the cells' joint outcomes fixed. This destroys within-cell pairing to age while preserving the effects already encoded in group means and sex composition. The coefficient is linear in the outcome for a fixed full-rank design. Its expectation over all allowed permutations is therefore obtained by fitting the unchanged design to the within-cell outcome means. This expectation applies only to coefficients. Do not use it to estimate uncertainty from the residuals of a table of cell means.

For each of the 54 table-by-contrast combinations report the original and expected coefficients, their difference, matched real–real mean absolute discrepancy from the prior study and original measured point estimate. The principal descriptive scale is the absolute change divided by the archived real–real mean absolute discrepancy for that contrast. This denominator supplies a reference scale, without a success threshold. Show all nine contrasts, including opposite directions and variation across both authors. Use the same original-study data and previously exposed reference throughout. Compare the original coefficients to the archived results and verify the expectation with a centered within-cell covariance identity and all permutations of a tiny fixed fixture. Code and aggregates are public. The frozen virtual records stay local.
