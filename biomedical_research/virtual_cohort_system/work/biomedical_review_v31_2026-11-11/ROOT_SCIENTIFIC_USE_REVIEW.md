# Root review: connect the scientific use to the modeling basis

11 November 2026. Read-only examination of frozen candidate specifications,
recorded source/code audits, the completed lifestyle aggregate results and v0.31
claim/evidence decisions. No new patient-level analysis or independent biomedical
review was performed. These are interpretation judgments, not new experiments.

## Findings that matter for the central application

1. **The useful BMI result has an explicit evidential origin.** Both authors use
   the original pastoralist mean and descriptive percentage differences to set
   approximate group means. This is a legitimate reconstruction capability: an
   Agent makes records that can be passed through an adjusted scientific analysis.
   It should not be narrated as discovery of an association absent from its inputs.

2. **The weakest lipid pieces have materially different support.** Pastoralist HDL
   spread is a source anchor, but using it in other groups is a completion assumption.
   A1 holds the group marginal SD at 14.72; A2 holds its derived residual SD equal
   across groups. TC group magnitudes, most conditional effects and latent dependence
   are assumed. This makes targeted domain review possible. It does not causally
   prove that a particular assumption explains all observed mismatches.

3. **A source category is not a single manipulable biological exposure.** The
   recorded source Methods combine location, subsistence and dietary information
   in lifestyle labels. Age/sex-adjusted contrasts remain observational group
   comparisons. The use form asks what these comparisons can inform without
   relabeling them as intervention or causal estimates.

4. **The analysis uses different measured sample sizes.** The same eligible-analysis
   function uses 1,189 BMI, 400 TC and 405 HDL observations in the real reference,
   versus 1,226 complete virtual outcomes. Effects in raw units, not significance
   matches, are the starting point. Missingness and assay-error modeling remain
   unvalidated for the intended uses; they are not patched after viewing results.

5. **Implementation coherence and biomedical adequacy remain distinct.** The fixed
   source/code audits found finite moments and positive HDL/non-HDL construction.
   Those properties support a well-defined sampler but do not validate its
   conditional relationships or measurement error. The same distinction applies
   to the largely unchanged prior quality-assurance results.

The main questions concern the user's contribution: actual analytical usefulness,
defensible source/assumption bridges and one necessary next piece of evidence.
No reviewers were contacted and no reviewer responses were supplied by an Agent.

## Decision

Keep the existing manuscript and all frozen scientific artifacts unchanged. Use the
new review package to obtain qualitative biomedical interpretation. Do not expand
models, datasets or generation by default. After feedback, map each request to one
of: interpretation clarification; evidence-needed assumption; implementation defect;
or stronger unproven use. A concrete scientific-use gap, not publication cosmetics,
should decide whether another experiment is needed.
