# Admission of the spirometry comparison

The proposed endpoint compares the average of people's FEV1/FVC ratios with the ratio of the population's average volumes. Recruitment and measurement compatibility were assessed before looking at paired target measurements or target endpoint estimates. Both targets have pre-bronchodilator examinations, separately recorded best FEV1 and FVC, and distinct quality flags for the two volumes.

- US NHANES 2011–12. The first, baseline exam was broadly administered. Each released summary can be the highest acceptable measurement from a different maneuver for that same person. The two quality flags have levels A (exceeds) and B (meets standards). Volumes are in mL. The survey's paired spirometry estimates had not been evaluated in this project. This wave was used for handgrip. Public codebook ranges, marginal counts and grade frequencies were visible. The US demographic file already exists locally. [CDC spirometry codebook](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/Nchs/Data/Nhanes/Public/2011/DataFiles/SPX_G.htm) · [Demographic documentation](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/Nchs/Data/Nhanes/Public/2011/DataFiles/DEMO_G.htm)
- Rural Malawi, Adult Lung Health Study. The landing page describes pre- and post-bronchodilator testing at each visit. The deposited baseline spreadsheet contains only the best **post**-bronchodilator values. The public follow-up spreadsheet provides separate best **pre**-bronchodilator volumes in litres and two quality indicators. We choose the **first follow-up**, the earliest publicly released pre-bronchodilator visit, and take each participant once. Its worksheet has 628 distinct IDs. At the schema stage 586 people met the age and two recorded QC criteria, before checking the actual volumes. The published baseline count of 886 does not describe this sample. The released file lacks survey weights and village identifiers. Recruitment, attrition, between-person dependence and distinct repeatability standards limit generalization. The prior MEIRU lipid-source reading concerned other material. This case concerns Chikhwawa and a new outcome. [Original CC BY 4.0 data deposit](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/whbk485wbw/1) · [Depositor file metadata](https://data.mendeley.com/public-api/datasets/whbk485wbw)
- External source. The Polish Hope for Lungs study is independent of these US and Malawian samples. Its first table gives sex-specific arithmetic means and SDs of both components, and its second reports their within-sex Pearson relationships. We checked the published PDF against the archived original article XML. Polish participants were healthy, older on average and never-smokers. The source provides a model of starting values with a documented population mismatch. Source uncertainty and population transfer remain open. [Original article and Tables 1–2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3461438/)

The blinded constructors receive only the supplied task and the corresponding Polish table excerpts. Unrelated candidate sources screened in discovery, published target associations, the identities of the evaluation sites and their data files are omitted from all requests. The implementing investigator inspected the Malawi dictionary, headers, identifiers for uniqueness, sex, age eligibility, visit and QC flags, and CDC documentation. No target ratio, dependent pair, paired statistic or expected direction from either target was inspected. Model pretraining exposure is unknown.
