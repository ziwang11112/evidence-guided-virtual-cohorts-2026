# One request, autonomous choice among accessible studies, and a withheld measured reference

## Question and evidence boundary

Can an author select a study and produce paired virtual data that support a group-average error and an individual-level disagreement analysis? The target is the complete paired adult analytic sample of one selected original study. An independent feasibility screen uses public metadata, study design and a separately accessible row file to delimit an eligible set before selection. Require at least two distinct studies. Record all eligible candidates, the search terms and all transferred text. The search agent queries this set, chooses its own paper and reads aggregate article material. Every independent model author receives the selected paper and request. The evaluator receives neither a vote on source selection nor an opportunity to choose a favorable model. This tests selection within a feasibility-screened set. This run uses published summaries from the same study.

The primary outcome is 100 times the fraction of complete pairs with reported BMI minus measured BMI strictly below −2 kg/m². The companion endpoint is the mean of that same signed difference. A 2-unit discrepancy corresponds to about 5.8 kg at 1.70 m. It is a research-design threshold. Fix operational agreement for the percentage at 3 percentage points (30 people per 1,000 screened) and for the mean at 0.30 kg/m² (about 0.87 kg at 1.70 m). These are study-planning tolerances, not validated clinical margins. Show signed errors in original units, empirical sampling intervals and a result for each author. A research coordinator who sees a published study summary during admission records that exposure; the numerical target tail is never supplied by the coordinator.

## Construction

One research request; one search session; six fresh instances of the same configured GPT with equal materials and access. Use the existing evidence-to-program, isolated trial and one-review runtime. Builders choose their own model, units, moments, probability laws and sensitivity scenarios. All receive the exact same verified article projection and request. This extends the source-selection and modeling experiment. Optional image follow-up already demonstrated on the Hong study is outside this equal-input comparison. Use a bounded aggregate-only projection, exclude original data files and plotted individuals, and independently inspect the delivered article and search transcript for direct disclosure of the specific threshold proportion or an exact algebraic equivalent. If disclosed, label it source reproduction and keep the case. No replacement study or new paid author in response to errors or bad results.

Use 10,000 virtual people per delivered table and the three registered seeds in the existing runtime. Six construction slots, each with a cap of USD 1.75; search budget USD 1.25; total maximum USD 11.75. Existing configured account only. The first complete attempt per slot counts. Record rejected requests, runtime failures, missing primary tables, alternatives, costs and all receipt hashes. Seeds are repeats of a construction and do not count as separate authors or populations. Independence here means fresh contexts from one fixed GPT model.

## Freeze and scoring

Seal the request, protocol, candidate identities and exact retrieval code before paid selection. After selection, store the hash of the selected aggregate paper, the eligible measured workbook as opaque bytes, and the exact authoring runtime before launching the constructors. No source-specific parameter, expected sign, target row, spreadsheet-derived sample moment or target feedback enters a model call. Preserve outputs from all six assigned constructors and declare column roles and all executable scenarios before first computing measured endpoints. Unit tests and supplementary-file header inspection are permitted before reveal. Individual source values and any distribution or summary derived from them remain unavailable to everyone until the programs and mapping rules are sealed. A source author who documents an unavailable variable may abstain.

Prefer unrounded BMI calculated from the paired weight and height in that sample where both source measures are available; otherwise use the documented BMI field. Use both virtual height and weight if supplied for that side, otherwise its declared BMI. The measured reference includes unweighted complete adult pairs; follow a narrower explicitly defined article population if required. Describe any mismatch between the article and the available workbook. Drop documented missing codes before scoring; duplicate participant identifiers and unexplained nonpositive values require reconciliation and disclosure, with no silent deletion. Preserve genuine ties; treat differences within 1e−10 of the cutoff as equal. Bootstrap real participants as pairs for percentile intervals (2,000 draws, fixed seed); report rounding sensitivity if the measured inputs are recorded to limited precision. Report publication summaries separately from recalculated values.

Score every delivered scenario but use only the predetermined named primary for the main comparison. A constructor meets a use tolerance if all three primary tables do. The denominator is all six assigned slots, including failures. Report the number meeting each tolerance and both, uncertainty in the measured sample and between-constructor variation. A successful single-study example requires at least four of six to meet both tolerances, with evaluable source and blinded threshold endpoint. This is a descriptive design criterion, not proof of population equivalence or external transport. An unreconstructible measured endpoint, missing comparison, or exposed target is a reported outcome. Keep the frozen candidates unchanged after disclosure.
