# Can a published blood-pressure relationship support a new screening analysis?

Prospective transfer study on 18 November 2026. The goal is to generate a complete two-column scientific cohort with no target individual inputs. This is a matched-evidence diagnostic of an Agent's analysis judgments. The separate original-paper experiments in v0.34 evaluate autonomous reading.

## Eligibility and exposure

The first eligible publicly accessible Freetown community health screening family was chosen for independent recruitment, continuous paired blood pressure, comparable adult age groups and an available codebook. Searches of the pre-existing project found no Freetown outcome file, publication DOI or target-specific analysis. This is an operational project-history screen. Model pretraining exposure cannot be audited. Only catalogue, headers, codebook, original methods, ID uniqueness, age categories, zone availability and paired numeric completeness were inspected before freeze. Neither target results nor the target publication's abstract, tables, discussion or results section were provided to the constructor. Target files remain local.

Target release v8 (Dryad version 233167) contains 2,394 uniquely serialized records, including 1,244 with both numeric fields in the prespecified eligible age bands. Include Mastersheet rows with V8_A_Cats 2, 3 or 4 (40–49, 50–59, oldest band), nonmissing zone and unique serial, and two finite strictly positive recorded pressures. This is the observed complete-pair screening sample, with all recorded sexes, partnership and treatment categories retained. The separate source selected partnered adults aged at least 40. Target devices and collection differ. An inverted real pair is reported as a diagnostic and retained if positive. No outlier exclusion based on the answer.

The original Freetown methods describe multistage selection of communities and listed adults, screening at Victoria Park, and seated pressure measured with an OMRON M3 after 3–5 minutes of rest. Each measure is an average of two readings. The two columns are V6_PS and V6_PD in mmHg. No individual weights or second independently sampled subzone within each constituency are documented in the released fields. Estimate an unweighted sample quantity. Condition resampling on observed zones. This provides a within-zone participant sampling benchmark and omits uncertainty about selection of communities. ## Information and comparisons

Both constructors receive exactly the same five numeric summaries in SOURCE.json from Tables 2–3 of an independent Nouna, Burkina Faso study of partnered adults. It used the mean of the last two of three seated readings after at least 15 minutes' rest. Only the five selected published statistics are represented. Their rounding and sampling uncertainty carry forward. The source also prints other summaries outside this deliberately fixed packet. No individual target covariates, pressure summaries, target study title or authors, or scientific parameter selected from the target go to the Agent. Each model uses the source moment values as a working transfer scenario. Both arms receive the target sampling context in REQUEST.txt. [Original source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12185928/)

- Q1 is the unadjusted within-person Pearson correlation between the two measured pressures. The publication reports it in its own sample. Using it for the new sample requires transfer.
- Q2 is the fraction of people with SBP ≥140 **and** DBP ≥90 mmHg. This describes concurrent measured elevations relevant to a screening workload. Medication has no role in this endpoint. The five moments do not determine the full joint law.
- The direct source calculation transfers the raw Q1 value and records Q2 as undetermined. The matched statistical constructor uses positive diastolic pressure and positive pulse pressure with a bivariate lognormal law calibrated analytically to the five moments. The sum gives SBP. The law is an explicit assumption. See reference_arms.py.
- One fresh GPT-6.1 Sol constructor receives one named pair of scientific questions. It supplies a primary and an alternative stochastic law, its source constraints and a judgment for each question before disclosure. It chooses both laws and parameters. Case-specific manual corrections, choice of a better model after reveal and target feedback are excluded from this comparison. A single author with two scenarios provides a sensitivity test, not independent replication across authors.
