A research team has a published-evidence virtual cohort consisting of paired total cholesterol (TC) and HDL values. It wants to study adults aged 20 and older in later US surveillance samples, standardized to the US 2000 age distribution. Its primary research use is estimating the fraction with non-HDL (TC minus HDL) at least 160 mg/dL. Other uses are mean non-HDL, mean of individual TC/HDL, single-marker cutoff rates and rank correlation. You have the source evidence below and two older US population means. Your decision is how the two *individual marginal distributions* should change when given this evidence. Keep each person's rank within each biomarker fixed so a separate analysis can identify the role of pairing. You may use, discount, or decline to transfer either historical mean. Choose the scientific form, coefficients and assumptions yourself. Distinguish a population mean interval from person-level variability and a ratio of means from an individual ratio. No later survey measurements, statistics or feedback are available.

Write an executable deterministic transformation, separately for each ordered marginal column. Inputs spec["tc_mg_dl"] and spec["hdl_mg_dl"] are positive strictly increasing source value lists of length n, at the same grid of quantiles. The two grids as printed side by side are NOT a set of within-person pairs. They were represented using the smaller admissible single-marker lognormal scales from the Mwanza means and cutoff frequencies. spec["model"] is exactly the object you return, spec["task"]["n_rows"] is n. Your program should work with these inputs; constants you choose should be recorded with units, provenance, and an applicability rationale. Return a pandas DataFrame in this exact order: row_id (integers 0 to n-1), tc_mg_dl, hdl_mg_dl. Each generated column must contain finite, positive, strictly increasing values. The historical rank pairings are applied afterwards. The original evidence is from a different country; explicitly consider which parts of it describe the target well enough to retain. No individual-level target data and no model-generated patient diagnoses are needed.

Return a single JSON object with exactly the keys model_spec (object), generator_py (Python source string), decision_record (object). decision_record must have structure (short string) and four arrays named parameter_provenance, requested_estimands, limitations, checks_actually_performed. An empty checks_actually_performed is valid. Do not claim a future check was performed. Define generate(observed,spec,seed), using NumPy, pandas or SciPy. observed is None. No file, network, subprocess or install. There is one call, no package-specific SDK, and no target tuning. The executor supplies the original grids as described. Provide modeling decisions and provenance concisely.
