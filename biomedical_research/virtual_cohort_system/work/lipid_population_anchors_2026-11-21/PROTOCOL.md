# Do two published population means improve an unreported screening estimate?

Registered after the Virginia study and before scoring either new survey cycle. Virginia showed that changing pairings alone could not attain its observed screening percentage using the fixed source marginal values. Here the intervention is access to two national historical marker means for the target country. We test how this intervention alters the *same* scientific analysis while holding each virtual person's pair of ranks fixed. Each agent authors an adaptation of a generator, before scoring later survey periods. We reuse the nominal and alternative published-evidence lipid pairings. The previous Ethiopian and Virginia target records and their estimates are excluded from the authors' inputs and this evaluation.
