# Existing-data history and exposure audit

9 November 2026. Independent reviewer: `/root/v26_novelty_review`. This is a metadata/history audit of the full `biomedical_research` workspace, not a clinical analysis or a declaration that a dataset is globally unseen.

**Decision:** PNS systolic/diastolic blood pressure is a defensible **qualified new task in an already familiar study family and release**, conditional on a frozen measurement/selection/evaluation contract and independent external-source admission. No prior computation or disclosure of the proposed PNS laboratory-subset BMI–SBP slope, BMI–DBP slope, or age/sex/BMI-adjusted SBP–DBP residual correlation was located. No prior PNS BP numerical marginal summary was located in the historical local material searched. This is a bounded negative finding, not proof of universal non-exposure. It does not certify implementation readiness, source sufficiency, or statistical precision.

The workspace contains more existing clinical and biomarker resources than the current virtual-cohort cases alone. Prior exposure to a family is not a blanket reason to reject every other endpoint. Conversely, a new field, split, filename, wave, or Agent does not by itself establish a new independent cohort or an unexposed estimand.

## PNS: exact historical exposure versus the proposed task

The existing files are `virtual_cohort_system/work/analysis-utility-preparation-2026-10-29/pns_hba1c/custodian/raw/PNS2013_Exames.zip` and `dictionary_lab_2013_05052023.xlsx`. The retained source manifest names the 509-column member `EXAMES-PNS-2013-FINAL_05052023.xlsx`. Its stored ZIP SHA256 is `d31544efdad138f3292ab86c9606cd722bf091781cc86ef371354ba63c9994bb`; the dictionary's stored SHA256 is `5aa4a3685fa2b1a9b8aa3739e3da2b83cf72fdb0affe988f2b6cc4442a6833c0`.

The completed PNS task was M10 **HbA1c enrichment**, with an old analysis set of 7,979 from 8,952 laboratory records. Its builder saw age, sex, BMI and an opaque row identifier; HbA1c and weights were held by the custodian. The original `prepare_pns.py:32` sets `DATA_FIELDS = ['Z001', 'C008', 'z004', 'z005', 'P005', 'peso_lab', 'Z034']`, and lines 80–81 read only those seven columns. The separate audit script read workbook headers with `nrows=0`. Thus prior access to the entire list of 509 field names is established; prior extraction of BP values by those scripts is not.

`visible/field_map.json:797–798` already lists `W00407/W00408`, and that metadata was copied into both construction bundles and the public report. These **field names were not unseen**. No historical use of those fields outside the header-list copies was found. The old protocol `protocols/pns_hba1c.json` explicitly names `hba1c_pct` as its outcome, with age/BMI/sex/curvature analyses of HbA1c. Old generation and evaluator records therefore do not establish an old BP target analysis. The PNS source manifest/admission records identify external HbA1c sources (including SHIP, Hoorn and DASA); incidental blood-pressure covariate mentions in an insulin source packet concern other cohorts, not PNS BP measurements.

PNS creatinine `Z025` was already inspected at dictionary/proposal level: `docs/SCIENTIFIC_ANALYSIS_UTILITY_PLAN.md:81,90` names it as a reserve requiring further kidney-function/source work. `work/m13/exposure_audit.md:17` records incomplete auditing, not a completed creatinine evaluation. The current statistics reviewer reports dictionary definitions for BP `W00407/W00408` and renal/Hb fields `Z025/Z007`; this audit did not open the dictionary workbook. A renal/Hb task remains a possible qualified new task, but has more unresolved timing, assay and definition issues than BP.

The proposed BP target contract is: BMI coefficient in SBP adjusted for age/sex; BMI coefficient in DBP adjusted for age/sex; and SBP–DBP residual correlation after the same age/sex/BMI adjustment. Exact population, age range, weights, missingness, measurement routing, transformations and estimators must be frozen before the first candidate clinical read. The old HbA1c complete-case restriction should not silently define the BP cohort. `C008` is baseline IBGE age; the older laboratory task had a 2013 interview versus 2014–15 blood-draw timing limitation. BP visit/timing should be settled from measurement metadata separately. The old released laboratory workbook lacked explicit PSU/stratum and a stable public person identifier; `peso_lab` does not by itself justify national-population inference or design-based confidence intervals.

**New exposure event reported by the controller on November 9:** an official-method search unexpectedly displayed a national PNS age/sex low/high-BP prevalence table snippet from IBGE `liv94522.pdf`. The document was not opened, and the proposed laboratory-subset slopes/residual correlation were not displayed. Those national prevalence numbers are deliberately not copied here or into author inputs. This further rules out global study blindness and must remain in the task disclosure. It does not establish that the three proposed quantitative estimands were observed. Method-only leads reported by the controller were the official 2013 anthropometry/BP manual and `https://www.ibge.gov.br/biblioteca/visualizacao/instrumentos_de_coleta/doc3426.pdf`; this reviewer did not browse or download them.
