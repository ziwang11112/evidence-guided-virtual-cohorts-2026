# Which lung-study evidence reached the generator's self-check?

Independent development audit, run d02, 22 November 2026. The evaluator read the original article and the retained design, program, diagnostic payload and final decision. All three final table hashes matched their execution receipts. A separate calculation from those rows reproduced the results below. The author received no findings from this audit.

| Source or request | How the author used it | What the diagnostic actually tested and showed | Independent result on the final tables |
|---|---|---|---|
| Methods and Table 1. Selected Polish never-smokers aged 18–85 who passed the health and spirometry criteria. The original sample contained 1,625 females and 1,120 males. | The design described eligibility and source counts. The user requested equal sex weights. The generator modelled only sex, volumes and weights. Age, height and selection history remained descriptors. | Code checked allowed sex codes, positive weights and equal total weight by sex. The reviewer received the sex-code and weight summaries and a successful trial status. | Each final table contained 5,000 rows per sex and 50% weight per sex. Participant eligibility and age-specific relationships cannot be evaluated from these virtual columns. |
| Table 1. Four sex-specific volume means and four SDs in litres. | The program used each printed centre as a calibration target. SDs were implemented as weighted population SDs. | During generation, code calculated every within-sex mean and SD and checked them against the declared parameters. The diagnostic payload sent only pooled means, SDs and extrema, plus successful execution. It supplied no individual within-sex residual values. | All eight values agree at printed precision for both population and sample SD conventions at this run size. Equal-sex mean FEV1 was 2.9 L and mean FVC was 3.8 L. |
| Results and Table 2. Unadjusted within-sex Pearson correlation of 0.96 for both sexes. | The program set the correlation to 0.96 using two independent symmetric two-point latent variables. | The same within-sex check calculated and compared the weighted correlation. A numerical sex-specific correlation field was absent from the review payload. | Both correlations were 0.96. |
| Table 2. Unadjusted within-sex slopes and intercepts for FEV1 on FVC. | The design extracted both equations and promised a cross-check. At build, the author explicitly prioritized the displayed margins and correlation, observing that the rounded central values cannot all be exact. | Neither generator nor estimator computed either sex's slope or intercept. The reviewer saw no regression discrepancies and kept the program. | Female slope/intercept were 0.840 and −0.204 L versus published 0.84 and −0.23 L. Male values were 0.800 and −0.200 L versus 0.84 and −0.36 L. Only the female slope agrees at printed precision. |
| Table 2. Unadjusted explained variance of 92.0% in females and 93.1% in males. | The declared evidence map selected the printed correlation and equations. It omitted the more precise printed R² values as checks. | The reviewer received no source comparison for R². | Both generated R² values were 92.16%. They round to 92.2% and miss both printed values. |
| Research request. Fraction of individuals below 0.70 and ratio of equally sex-weighted mean volumes on the same rows. | The agent coded the strict threshold on each individual ratio, the ratio of weighted means and guards for finite positive volumes and FEV1 ≤ FVC. | The model's analysis returned 25.0% and 76.315789% on one trial seed. The code checked ordering on every row. The author kept this model. | Both estimates replicated on all three evaluation seeds. Each had eight distinct sex/volume profiles with 1,250 copies each and zero violations of the volume ordering. The article reports no observed prevalence with this weighting. |

**Coverage.** Of 16 numerical source entries in the two margins and the unadjusted joint relationship, 10 were explicitly tested by the author's generator (eight marginal summaries and two Pearson coefficients). Eleven agree at printed precision in independent recomputation, counting the incidentally matching female slope. The denominator describes specific table entries. These related summaries do not constitute 16 independent scientific outcomes. The previously documented [rounding-compatible numerical control](../lung_pairing_numerical_control_2026-11-22/RESULTS.md) shows why disagreement among printed centres and incompatibility of rounding intervals are different claims.

**One correction occurred before execution.** The design's illustrative eight-row list gave the wrong two male FEV1 values at FVC of 3.3 L. Taking that list literally yields 37.5% below the cutoff and a ratio of means of 72.63%. The build record flags the mistake and its program uses the design's consistent formula, giving male values 2.16 and 2.72 L at FVC of 3.3 L. All executed tables use this correction. The subsequent review retained the built program. The agent considered an unbounded Gaussian calculation at design time, discussed its physical limitations and delivered one bounded scenario. Seed changes shuffled its records. An alternative screening proportion was never demonstrated on a second executed table in this run.

**Other source information.** Table 2 contains analyses adjusted for age. In the discussion, equations 7–8 describe age-specific lower limits for the spirometric residual. Figure 1 describes observed volume pairings and moving medians; the text packet did not supply digitized pairs from the plot. These sources matter if someone uses the virtual table for age-dependent or physiological tail modeling. They do not supply a tabulated sex-standardized screening prevalence. The submitted generator omitted age and made no validated tail or clinical diagnosis claim. [Original article](https://doi.org/10.1186/1465-9921-13-57)
