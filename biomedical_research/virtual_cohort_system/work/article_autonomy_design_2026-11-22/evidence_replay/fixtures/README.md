# Historical acceptance fixtures

An evaluator transcribed these inventories after both historical model decisions. They exercise the new software on the original trial and final CSVs. The author of either historical candidate never saw these fixtures or their feedback. The cases and column mappings in this directory belong to offline acceptance testing. The live author builds its own inventory.

## Iron, d01

- The agent's design selected the two overall geometric means from Table I, the three ferritin geometric means from Table III, and the binary counts from Tables I, III and IV. The design used 120 g/L and 15 µg/L as strict thresholds. For ferritin it resolved discordant unit labels using the abstract and table notes. The retained program's measurement names and units are recorded in its [evaluation mapping](../../evaluation/iron_d01_mapping.json). The source's rounded interval for the middle Hb group's depletion percentage is based on the printed value **60**, which the article displays without a decimal digit. [Original article](https://doi.org/10.1024/0300-9831/a000014)
- The source has 1,671 women. Each archived virtual table has 10,000 rows with integer allocations. The 22 entries with no decimal precision compare derived proportions or the crude odds ratio, using signed differences without a rounding pass/fail. The Table IV comparisons use successive binary conditionals (folate, B12 given folate, depletion given both, anemia given all three). Together these describe the 16-cell joint table. Clinical endpoints are recalculated from the delivered measurements wherever the operation permits. The archived generator also checked the agreement of its Hb stratum indicator with the numeric cutoffs.
- Table V gives an adjusted odds ratio of 6.448. The design designated it as context. The fixture records why the absence of age, BMI and education prevents an equivalent fit. No source reference exists for continuous Hb–ferritin Spearman correlation. Hypothetical correlation values from the three generated scenarios are never used as published targets.
- **Expected on every archived table:** of 31 entries, seven agree at printed precision, the overall ferritin geometric mean falls outside, 22 give descriptive differences, and the adjusted OR is unresolved. Overall ferritin is 17.3086977 µg/L against 17.4. Within the three Hb strata the values are 1.3, 5.9 and 22.3 µg/L. The table-derived crude OR is 6.6516623 against the source's exact cross-product ratio of 6.6531367. [Independent row audit](../../IRON_INDEPENDENT_RESULTS.json)

## Lung function, d02

- The design explicitly selected eight means/SDs, two Pearson correlations and four coefficients of the sex-specific unadjusted regression of FEV1 on FVC. The fixture uses paper precision, litres and the sex codes from the retained candidate's [evaluation mapping](../../evaluation/lung_d02_mapping.json). The unweighted sample SD is evaluated within sex. Each row in these archived tables has the same weight. The sample and population SD conventions give identical printed-precision decisions here. [Original article, Tables 1–2](https://doi.org/10.1186/1465-9921-13-57)
- **Expected for all 14 selected entries:** 11 agree at printed precision. The male slope is approximately 0.80 against 0.84. The male intercept is −0.20 L against −0.36 L. The female intercept is −0.204 L against −0.23 L. These are related properties of the same volumes. [Independent row audit](../../LUNG_INDEPENDENT_RESULTS.json)
- **Separate independent challenge:** the source gives two R-squared estimates that the historical design and build did not inventory. Only [d02_independent_r_squared.json](d02_independent_r_squared.json) contains them. Both groups give approximately 92.16%, outside the printed intervals around 92.0% in women and 93.1% in men. Running a replay of the 14 selected entries alone cannot discover these two omissions.
- The paper does not tabulate the requested equal-sex prevalence below 0.70. The model's 25% is a generated scenario value. Age and height were intentionally omitted, so age-adjusted regressions and age-specific analyses have a different variable scope. No historical source check can establish the observed threshold prevalence from those virtual rows. [Historical coverage audit](../../LUNG_SOURCE_COVERAGE.md)
