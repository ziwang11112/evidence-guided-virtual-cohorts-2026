# What item-by-item replay changed

On 22 November 2026, the scientific agent received discrepancies computed independently from its virtual lung measurements while it could still revise the generator. It chose to keep the generator. All six final tables matched those from the preceding run byte for byte. The change established a working feedback path and exposed the tradeoff the agent made. ## What reached the reviewer

The author received the original Polish lung article and a short research request. It chose its measurements, evidence, two scenarios and sampling models. Among 19 registered entries were 14 numerical quantities and five explicit limitations of comparability or computation. In the first session the author declared units in the design but omitted a duplicate unit map in each operation. All 14 calculations were unavailable in each trial scenario. The recorded reviewer retained the model. [Original session audit](UNBOUND_INTEGRATION.json)

For the second review we retained the exact original author responses for design and build. The adapter obtained the missing units from the author's pre-generation variable declarations, recorded their locations, and verified the identical trial tables and diagnostics. No evaluator-transcribed reference, target data or new generator parameter entered this step. In both scenarios, all 14 numerical entries could then be recomputed, with 11 inside the article's printed rounding intervals and three outside. The aggregate discrepancies appeared in the captured GPT request. Each of the eight distinct deviations or unresolved entries requiring a decision received a response. The reviewer gave priority to the ten within-sex means, SDs and correlations and retained the sampling model. Its explanation described a mismatch among exact printed central values. The rounding intervals can in fact be satisfied together, as shown by the separate numerical control. Across three final seeds and both scenarios, five tables matched 11 of the 14 selected numerical values to printed precision and one matched ten. Two further published R² entries were absent from the author's inventory. The independent audit assessed all 16 numerical entries outside the authoring loop. Printed precision measures agreement with a reported number and carries no inference about population sampling error.
