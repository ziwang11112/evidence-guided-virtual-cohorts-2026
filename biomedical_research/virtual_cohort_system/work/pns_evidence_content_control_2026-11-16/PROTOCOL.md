## Three conditions

- **BASE:** unchanged historical evidence packet.
- **SUMMARY:** the same base packet plus the Burkina Faso citation, source population and measurement context, total-sample SBP/DBP/BMI means and SDs, mean age, and participant sex counts.
- **JOINT:** exactly the SUMMARY payload plus six reported pairwise Pearson correlations among SBP, DBP, age and BMI from the same source table.

SUMMARY and JOINT contain the same additional publication and all the same marginal/context fields. Only the `relationships` array changes. Neither arm receives the host-derived source partial correlation/covariance, a requested target parameter, a target result, or an old generator. The Agent must decide whether/how to derive and transport a relation. The common prompt is identical and does not name the condition or imply a preferred answer.

The original packet already contains relationship evidence; SUMMARY is not a no-relationship condition. Its *increment* is univariate. Omit all sex-specific biomarker moments, hypertension prevalence defined by an SBP-or-DBP event, pulse-pressure/non-HDL-style difference variances, significance statements about omitted correlations, and already derived partial relationships from SUMMARY. These can convey joint information despite looking like descriptive statistics. Preserve source selection/outlier/treatment limitations in both augmented conditions.

This is a content intervention with a nested information set. JOINT necessarily has extra informative numbers and tokens. The pilot controls additional paper identity, shared context, marginal facts, model and call ceiling; it does not isolate information content from every possible token-length or attention effect. It does not demonstrate an autonomous retrieval-policy advantage. Source retrieval was completed by the earlier search Agent; extraction/masking and admission here are root-led.
