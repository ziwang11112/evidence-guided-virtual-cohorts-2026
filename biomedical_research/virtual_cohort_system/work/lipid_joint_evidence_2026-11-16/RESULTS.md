## What this does and does not mean

The unit uncovered a concrete evidence-control issue: a derived lipid's variance can reveal pairwise covariance even when an explicit correlation is omitted. A fair marginal-versus-joint experiment must audit these algebraic routes. However, a possible raw covariance recovered from rounded moments neither determines the requested adjusted relation nor resolves source-to-target transfer.

There are two separate limitations. **Engineering:** unbounded accumulation of tool results prevented source-report completion; the attempted report was also too long for its output allocation. **Evidence:** the retrieved joint information remains partial and heterogeneous. Neither failure is evidence that Agent-generated lipid data became worse, that additional evidence cannot help, or that no suitable published source exists.

The completed PNS result remains a positive, bounded example of improved paired dependence after adding qualified external evidence. This lipid unit cannot be described as its successful replication or a failed quality replication: no new data comparison occurred.
