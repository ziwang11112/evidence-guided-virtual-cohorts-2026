# Source increment decision and bounded development construction

This new unit follows the completed single-supplement probe. The question is whether its new numerical evidence supports a substantive Agent-authored scientific change, and, if so, what scientific analyses the resulting data support. This is development on the already exposed E01 case, not independent confirmation or a workflow-superiority experiment.

## Inputs and roles

The constructing Agent starts in a fresh context. Allowed scientific inputs are the E01 request/task, the registered E01 source artifacts, explicitly supplied static initial candidate (evidence, model specification, generator and proposal only), and the admitted PMC6458679 supplement extraction/original converted text. Old generated tables, feedback/evaluation results, real rows, other cases, diagnostic reports and project-wide result indexes are forbidden to that Agent. The controller records exact allowed-file hashes. This is a procedural information boundary; generator execution is separately isolated.

The author chooses structure, parameters and whether to retain. The root does not prescribe a slope, residual scale, sign or distribution. The same original scientific request remains in force. The new supplement retains unlabelled outcome units, missing coefficient uncertainty and unresolved source-to-target assumptions. Plausible working interpretations are allowed if explicit; they are not reported facts.

## Stages and budget

1. Author submits a concrete scientific change proposal or retention decision, with a source operator mapping and explicit unresolved assumptions. No generation in this stage.
2. Independent scientific review checks the proposal without target outcomes. One bounded correction round may fix scientific or implementation errors; do not seek a desired outcome.
3. If a substantive admissible change exists, create a new bundle and candidate under a new task ID; bind the same source increment to it. Validate, freeze, execute via the existing WSL/bubblewrap adapter, and explicitly use semantic_feedback_v1 for any supplied source-statistic checks. Do not fall back to local generator execution or present target-context statistics as source replay.
4. Maximum one new scientific candidate, three predetermined seeds **5301, 5302, 5303**, 3,000 rows per seed, at most three successful final tables. At most two additional mechanical execution/format repairs, preserved and reported; repairs may not alter scientific law after target outcomes are read. Final scientific candidate freezes before all three scheduled runs and evaluation. No target-informed revision.
5. Reuse the existing E01 evaluation definitions and previously cached reference summaries. Evaluate Q1_age10, Q2_bmi5, Q3_bmi5 and the existing full diagnostics together. Do not introduce a new favorable endpoint. Independently recompute the three primary estimates and key conditional variability/rank diagnostics. Do not re-download/re-read patient rows merely to compare with cached exposed summaries.

If the author retains, or no defensible source mapping passes review, stop this unit with the decision and avoid duplicate generation. If isolated execution is unavailable, preserve the concrete failure and do not execute unsandboxed. No new literature search/download, training, HPC, paid API or new cohort is in this unit.

## Interpretation and outputs

Output: proposal, independent review, source-to-model changes, actual candidate/checks if applicable, all scheduled analysis results including failures, costs/interventions, and continue/retain/stop decision. A new evidence packet may affect data quality; this does not isolate acquisition-policy superiority or establish target-population truth. Confirmed signs alone do not require parameter changes. Original E01 files and the completed supplement probe remain unchanged.
