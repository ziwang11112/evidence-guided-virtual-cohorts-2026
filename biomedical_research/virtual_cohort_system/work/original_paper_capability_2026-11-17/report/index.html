Can the Agent read a paper and author a useful scientific generator?

Two existing cases, three fresh constructors per case. Each receives the complete original-paper entry point and scientific request, with optional screened literature search. No curator-written numerical packet or target answer is supplied.

What to read first

Each dot is one Agent-authored model averaged over three final seeds. The dashed line is the existing measured-sample reference. Small differences are encouraging for that particular analysis; they do not certify clinical equivalence or transport.

This is a known-paper reading and modeling test. Source selection remains researcher-provided, and the reference families were already exposed. The source/code audit and full result interpretation accompany the figures.

18/18 final tables executed; 142 model calls; estimated tariff cost $1.511. This is token-tariff accounting, not a billing invoice.

What the experiment found

Blood pressure: all three authors reproduce approximately the adjusted BMI associations: SBP effects 3.40–3.80 versus reference 3.52; DBP effects 2.29–2.40 versus 2.26 mmHg per 5 kg/m². Residual correlation and pressure dispersion remain higher than the sample reference.

Iron status: the positive association is retained, but its strength is weaker: odds ratios 6.35–7.14 versus 17.79. Joint prevalence and rank correlation also remain lower. A useful source constraint does not identify the whole target distribution.

No clinical equivalence margin was fitted to these outcomes. The results describe which particular analyses look useful and where discrepancies remain.

What the Agent actually authored

All three BP authors extracted the original correlation matrix and derived an age/BMI partial covariance. All three iron authors collapsed eight source-table rows into a two-by-two table and distinguished crude from adjusted odds. Each chose supplementary searches, a probability law and numeric assumptions, wrote the generator, inspected one trial and kept its first candidate.

The runner samples the Agent’s program. The coordinator did not supply a numerical evidence card or fill in the scientific relationships. This supports original-paper interpretation and model authorship; it does not test a revision advantage.

One author received a same-target-family search preview with study identity and demographics, then explicitly excluded it. The preview did not include the evaluated effect values. This exposure remains in the audit; screening is not a guarantee of source independence.
