## Question and independent reference

The proposed scientific question concerns the fraction of people whose online self-reported BMI is more than 2 kg/m² below BMI calculated from objective anthropometry of the same person. Define Δ = BMI_online − BMI_objective, and count Δ < −2 among paired eligible observations. This definition retains the sign and excludes equality. Source ages are 18–75 under the recruitment protocol. Adults volunteered at a local fitness centre and amateur sports club in 2016. Mask clinical rows, unpublished output summaries, the historical Malaysian results and other agents' submissions from authors. Direct text exposure is documented below. Prior model exposure to this public source is possible.

There are 94 recruits. Table 2 lists 91 comparable Stage 3 BMI pairs. The evaluator should predeclare an independent complete-pair algorithm before reveal, including data-field identity, recorded markers of missingness, BMI formula and units, and the use of recorded versus calculated BMI values. Stage 3 uses objective measures, distinct from the second self-report the interviewer obtained. Never infer that a blank equals zero. Audit duplicated identifiers and row integrity using the frozen rules. Report the resulting complete-pair N and any discrepancy with 91. Document rounding of the deposited BMI or underlying height/mass where it affects a strict cutoff. A failure to reconcile file, measurement or study population requires explicit handling under the protocol before the endpoint is used.

## What the paper discloses

- Table 1 lists online versus objective BMI marginal means and SDs (25.3 ± 5.9 and 25.4 ± 6.2 kg/m²). The descriptives refer to a study of 94; the table does not establish that every variable uses exactly the 91 Stage 3 pairs. A difference between these rounded means is a rough marginal contrast.
- Table 2, Stage 3 gives n = 91, BMI threshold ≥30, online 8 positive/83 negative, objectively measured 12 positive/79 negative, κ = 0.776 (reported interval 0.565–0.984), and continuous ICC 0.962 with RMS 1.3. ICC variant and the precise RMS calculation are not defined sufficiently to treat ICC as a Pearson r or RMS as the SD of the signed difference without an assumption.
- Table 2, Stage 2 gives different online vs interviewer **self-reports** (obesity n = 83, six positives on each side, κ = 1; continuous BMI n = 91, ICC 0.974, RMS 0.98). These quantities do not describe online vs objective BMI. Stage 1, physical-activity test statistics and blood pressure or cholesterol results also address different measurements.
- Let a be positive in both modalities, with online-positive rows and objective-positive columns. The four integer counts are (a,8−a,12−a,71+a), 0 ≤ a ≤ 8. Under ordinary unweighted Cohen κ, expected agreement from these margins is (8×12+83×79)/91². For a = 8 κ = 0.7764128 and for a = 7 κ = 0.6646192. κ increases monotonically in a, so 8 is the unique solution rounding to 0.776. Both positive 8, online-only 0, objective-only 4, both negative 79 are derived counts, not four printed cells. The source agreement is 87/91. Exchanging the two discordant cells reverses the direction relevant to underreporting.
- The individual signed BMI-error tail at −2 is neither reported in the supplied article nor determined by this table. The four people crossing 30 in one direction may have BMI errors above or below 2. People on the same side of 30 can also differ by more than 2. Marginals and second-moment summaries leave the shape of the signed differences open. The p-values for paired comparisons described for physical activity are outcome-specific. Even a paired test of mean BMI difference would not directly report a BMI-error tail. No matching tail count, percent or quantile was identified in the main article or aggregate tables.

## Release decision

The article is admissible for a pre-frozen source-choice comparison and conditional same-study utility check. The text representation supplied in earlier work covers 32/36 blocks and excludes figures and supplemental data (SHA-256 `404a9e3d943404222ba92ec4e708d93c57fe851ca1eace63d5e35be9674f8af6`). If a fuller article-only representation is used, hash and audit that exact representation too; remove supplemental bytes, clinical rows and direct access paths equally from both arms. Freeze prompts, evidence boundary, source-only adjudication, use estimator/seed plan and the measured-data scoring rule together. Seal all samplers and complete independent source-use adjudication before opening the withheld participant outcomes. The adjudicator materials must be inaccessible to the generating agents. The evaluators should report both source use and measured scientific utility even if they disagree. With one article and four paired blocks, the comparison is descriptive.
