# From a full research article to two virtual cohorts for a screening analysis

22 November 2026. This is a source-only extension of the KDD methods study. The scientific question is whether explicitly asking for a different defensible model improves the ability to exhibit a material change in an intended analysis under the same evidence. The biological use is planning a lipid screening study when a published survey reports marginals and a mean ratio but the investigator lacks paired individual measurements.

## Source and exposure

Both conditions start with the title, abstract, complete scientific body, all five tables and table notes of the published Kavishe et al. article. The article is mechanically rendered from the archived publisher XML. Bibliography and publisher metadata are omitted. Every author must locate the Mwanza municipal stratum and extract the quantities required for a TC and HDL generator. The runner supplies the task, unit conversion and marginal family. The initial session receives neither a numerical evidence packet nor a fitted coupling. The existing project source abstraction is private to the evaluator during construction. The paper, its thresholds and this case have been investigated previously. This experiment tests a new instruction comparison on a known source. It does not retest transport to a new clinical population. The original article is [Kavishe et al., PLOS ONE](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0223189).

## Matched counterfactual requests

Twelve separate first-author sessions each submit an extraction and a single generator. Each initial submission is then copied to two fresh, stateless author contexts. Both receive the identical article, task, interface, first-author artifact and token allowance. One requests another defensible candidate. The other specifically requests a defensible alternative that tests sensitivity of the screening estimate, without a specified target rate or direction. Both contexts may choose their own coupling. The two instructions, schedule and scoring rules are frozen before any generation. Calls for the two branches are interleaved in randomized order within blocks. Neither sees its sibling, the evaluator's gold, earlier project outputs, execution results or patient records. These are matched follow-up requests, not independent first encounters in the two branches.

Use GPT-6.1 Sol, medium reasoning, default service tier, one submission per session and at most 12,000 output tokens. The design permits 12 initial and 24 follow-up calls. A missing or structurally unreadable initial artifact leaves both pairs unsuccessful and the dependent calls unissued. A validly parsed first artifact is passed identically to both branches even if its source extraction or its data later fails. No automatic API retries or replacement authors. A transport interruption with ambiguous billing retains its reservation. Closure precedes scientific inspection. The USD 18 batch tariff ceiling uses the existing provider client's conservative token reservations. The user's stated monthly allowance is USD 400. No account balance or invoice is inferred. Source text, raw API interactions, generated assignments and any credentials remain local.

## Generation and endpoints

The program executes in the existing WSL/bubblewrap worker. Each submission's declared values determine its grids. The evaluator compares all eight extracted values in their reported units with the archived article and checks their table numbers and a nonempty row citation. Exact row-label agreement is an additional diagnostic. Index uniqueness, bounds and complete pairing, both fixed column multisets, HDL at or below TC and the source ratio are checked at three new common seeds (520221, 520223, 520229). The rounded non-HDL mean printed in the source is separately extracted. Its virtual value follows from the two source means within rounding. Program output is an index assignment. The resulting virtual cohort consists of the two matched measurement columns; the evaluator computes both requested analyses on it. The fixed marginal family is supplied by the investigator. The scientific author chooses the joint model and parameters.

Primary outcome per block and arm: both first and follow-up artifacts pass the numerical and table provenance checks, all three draws pass scientific constraints, and the absolute difference between the two pooled screening percentages exceeds 4 points, with a difference exceeding 2 points in the pooled direction at each matched seed. Use the original threshold protocol: each ratio within 0.02 and pooled within the half-open rounding bin [reported mean ratio minus 0.005, reported mean ratio plus 0.005). The two cohort means must be invariant to pairing within numerical precision. Incomplete artifacts and scientific failures remain in the denominator of 12. Report every block, both success rates, paired discordances and an exact two-sided matched sign/binomial test on discordant blocks, conditional on this article. Also report extraction, usable single models and complete valid pairs separately, unrestricted observed spreads for valid pairs, seeds completed, reasons and tariff. Read no clinical targets. A constructed difference is attainable under those checks; it is neither a patient-prevalence interval nor proof of clinical plausibility or superior point accuracy.

## Interpretation and next spending decision

A reusable positive signal on this article requires at least eight blocks with valid pairs in both arms, more material pairs in the targeted arm than in the ordinary arm, and an independent numerical check. This is an operational gate for another source, not a significance threshold. If validity is the bottleneck, address it before multiplying calls. New publications and scientific uses, rather than additional seeds of this single paper, supply evidence of generality. Any later multi-paper batch must register eligibility, per-paper constraints, important differences and equal model budgets before construction. Preserve all planned publications and failures.
