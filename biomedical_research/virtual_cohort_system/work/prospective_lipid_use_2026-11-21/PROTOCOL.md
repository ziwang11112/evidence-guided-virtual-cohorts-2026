# Before disclosure, which analyses would you use?

The experimental object is a single previously sealed, agent-authored virtual lipid cohort (three fixed draws pooled). Every adviser and the statistical rule assesses the same table. This isolates advice about the scientific use of a generated dataset from differences between generators. The new external target is a community screening sample in the United States, for which no endpoint has been inspected. One planned clinical recruitment frame failed the eligibility screen. We will publish every recommendation and all six analyses at all coverage levels.

## Scientific quantities and planning tolerances

| ID | Estimate on paired adult measurements | Absolute planning tolerance |
|---|---|---:|
| mean_nonhdl | Mean TC minus HDL | 10 mg/dL |
| mean_ratio | Mean of the individual ratios TC divided by HDL | 0.30 ratio units |
| tc_high | Fraction at TC ≥6.2 mmol/L | 5 percentage points |
| hdl_low | Fraction at HDL <1.0 mmol/L | 5 percentage points |
| nonhdl160 | Fraction at non-HDL ≥160 mg/dL | 2 percentage points |
| rank_tc_hdl | Participant-level Spearman rank correlation of TC and HDL, averaged ties | 0.10 |

Convert cholesterol from mmol/L to mg/dL with the frozen factor 38.67. The margins are research-planning assumptions for resolution, not clinical recommendations. The 2-point screening tolerance is inherited from the previous protocol. All other tolerances are selected before seeing this reference. A difference of 5 points corresponds to 50 participants per 1,000. The two correlation and ratio tolerances are measured on their respective scales. Sensitivity to tolerances is prespecified at 0.5 and 2 times the listed values; keep the frozen decisions in those recalculations.

## Competing decisions at the same coverage

Both procedures see the same source summaries and full study context, virtual point estimates, and matched analysis on the two already exposed Ethiopian frames. The full-information advisers and reference rule additionally see the range over the eight previously sealed author scenarios and two numerical source-compatible examples. The pooled nominal agent law is the same across all comparisons. A scenario is one model, not one seed. Use its pooled estimates, including the rank correlation computed on the pooled pairs. The control examples use one deterministic table each. Source mean non-HDL is also directly reported along with its sampling interval.

- Full arm: four independent fresh economical GPT sessions rank all six uses, report an unforced yes/no decision and concise scientific reason per use, and flag limitations. Each receives the same source-only alternative-model summary. No new target information or tools, one call per session.
- Reduced-information arm: four comparable sessions receive the identical request, source summaries, nominal and development results but no numerical alternative-model summaries. The assigned information condition is the only designed difference. Shuffled slot assignments are determined using seed 711223. Do not pair unrelated calls. Four sessions per arm characterize model variability conditional on this case.
- Statistical reference: before any target read, for each use compute three displacements in original units: the larger absolute excursion from the source point to either end of its 95% interval when the use is reported, the largest absolute discrepancy from the nominal estimate at either historical development site, and the largest absolute difference from the nominal estimate in the alternative-law set. Divide the largest available displacement by the use tolerance. Rank smaller scores first, resolving ties by the printed use order. Endorse if the score is at most 1. Source intervals for rank and threshold prevalence are unavailable and are labelled accordingly. The rule is descriptive and accounts for multiple dependence structures and historical transfer. It has no fitted transfer coefficient for a new country.
- Primary nominal coverage is 2 of 6. Report counts contradicted, within and unresolved among the top two for each procedure, plus counts at every other coverage from 1 to 6. Ranking is a constrained prioritization exercise. An actual assurance is counted only when the procedure separately says yes. Report unforced coverage and false assurances (and their denominators) including zero coverage. Summarize the four sessions with individual counts and median, retaining tied results, failures and abstentions. Estimates are not independent replicates across sessions, counties or biomarkers. No method winner or hypothesis test is defined from one frame.
- All rankings and individual judgments are final before target access. The same rankings are applied to both counties as a secondary robustness check. Use no target-specific threshold, post-reveal retirement or choice of a better law. Missing/incomplete outputs are transparent failures. No replacement call. Estimated tariff cap for the batch is USD 0.95, eight fresh GPT-6.1 Sol calls at medium effort, at most 4,800 output tokens each.

## Opening and scoring

Pool the six prespecified scientific estimates on the 400 unique eligible records. Within the US study, bootstrap participants independently with replacement within county keeping the observed county sizes. Use 4,000 draws with seed 711209. For the two individual counties use the same procedure within that county with seeds 711211 (Buckingham) and 711217 (Louisa). Recompute ranks with average ties in every draw. Form 95% pointwise percentile intervals for the measured estimates. Conditional on the frozen virtual table, reverse and subtract the interval endpoints to form intervals for virtual minus measured. Declare within only if the entire interval falls inside the adopted tolerance, contradicted only when the interval is disjoint, and unresolved otherwise. Also report point discrepancies divided by tolerance. Historical source sampling uncertainty, unknown within-community cluster correlation and unobserved target selection are not included in the reference intervals. Outcomes and intervals concern the sampled participants.

The nominal virtual table has a small number of previously disclosed HDL > TC observations. Retain it intact. The source exercise had a stricter order constraint; it serves as a set of alternative explanations, not a retroactive repair of the nominal estimates. Recompute numerical results independently from the native S serialization and an independently coded ranking and bootstrap estimator. Release aggregate evidence, decisions, prompts, scientific use results, coverage and recorded costs. Keep person-level real and virtual data, full externally hosted papers and raw provider archives local. A robust positive result requires strong measured support on a specified use. If advisers largely abstain, fail to identify the right uses or merely tie the statistical rule, report that outcome and the usefulness of the virtual data itself separately.
