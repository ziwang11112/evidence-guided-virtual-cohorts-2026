# v0.31: scientific use first

11 November 2026. Editorial integration of completed studies. No model calls, generation, patient-level fits or public synchronization in this unit.

## Main claim and sequence

A scientific Agent can turn a biomedical research request and permitted published evidence into an explicit joint generator and complete virtual records. Those records can be passed to familiar scientific analysis procedures. Their usefulness must be stated for particular analyses and information conditions.

1. **Show the product in use.** The first result now holds a lifestyle analysis fixed and replaces its data. Two fresh Agent-authored laws produce six complete BMI/TC/HDL cohorts with lifestyle groups. Figure 3 shows all nine adjusted contrasts in raw units. This makes the favorable urban BMI result directly interpretable, without hiding weaker lipid results behind standardization.
2. **Show what the Agent actually contributes.** The Agent reads studies, searches for missing evidence, chooses demographic and conditional structures, assigns parameters and authors the sampler. The host supplies task, tools, schema and evaluation. Existing request-only studies establish that the constructor can start without a curated bibliography. They do not establish consistent workflow superiority over an ordinary capable Agent.
3. **Show the scientific range and boundaries.** Glycemia adjustment patterns, paired-biomarker regressions, threshold analyses and repeated-follow-up reconstruction answer different use questions. Their different information conditions remain separate. Source-to-code provenance explains supported quantities and assumptions; it is not a calibrated confidence score.

The headline is useful, inspectable Agent-generated biomedical data. Feedback/revision is a tool, not an obligatory performance claim. No new failure-mechanism paper or unrelated benchmark is launched.

## What the lifestyle result does and does not add

- Urban-minus-pastoralist BMI: reference 1.882 kg/m², six virtual draws 1.700-2.251. This is an accessible example of scientific-use agreement.
- TC elevation is retained, but raw contrast magnitudes are lower. Standardized agreement is partly influenced by lower virtual SD.
- Rural lipid contrasts, HDL estimates, dispersion and one HDL interaction exception remain visible. All reference HDL intervals include zero; disagreement in point directions is not proof of opposite population effects.
- The same code is applied to real and virtual records. The code is a Methods-guided reimplementation, not recovered original author software or the full original 11-outcome analysis.
- The real lipid samples have missing measurements and smaller available n. Virtual completeness does not supply extra clinical observations or justify comparing p-values as a quality score.
- The original paper is authorized input. This is legitimate evidence-constrained reconstruction, not proof of transfer from independent publications or a new unexposed validation family.

## Remaining work, tied to claims

**Required editorial work:** confirm authorship/affiliations and declarations; decide submission format; ensure released code and aggregate artifacts match the submitted version. Confirmed numerical and layout review is recorded separately. Do not call this expanded working draft a submission-ready conference paper.

**Most useful interpretation check:** ask the existing biomedical collaborator to review the exact analysis use, clinical measurement assumptions and meaning of the observed BMI/TC patterns. The earlier review package remains available, but it predates this lifestyle addition. No human review has been obtained or fabricated. Such feedback is qualitative, not a powered clinical or user study.

**No additional experiment is required merely to integrate these completed results.** The current evidence supports a bounded paper about construction and analysis-specific utility. It does not establish full joint-distribution fidelity, unfamiliar-population inference or universal workflow superiority. Those claims can remain limitations.

**If a stronger reconstruction-reliability claim becomes essential:** freeze the exact lifestyle task and procedure and run additional independent authors once, using every output. Two current authors and three draws per author are limited evidence; more draws do not create more author-level replications. This would still be development in an exposed study family, not new-population validation. No such batch is scheduled here.

**If a stronger transfer claim becomes essential:** preselect one genuinely independent target under a separately frozen protocol and lawful data access. Reused cohorts, new endpoints in exposed data, and source-paper reconstruction do not establish this claim. Do not expand acquisition or tune completed generators merely to obtain a more favorable result.
