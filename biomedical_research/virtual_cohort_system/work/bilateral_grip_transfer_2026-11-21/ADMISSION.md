# Study admission and source semantics

The new question is how much the two hands of one person differ. It was selected from the availability of measured sides and independently published marginal summaries, before computing any of the three references. The four sex-by-decade cells share a common definition across sources and all three targets. The project already held HAALSI baseline v3. The NHANES muscle-strength module is a public CDC download. The Brazilian EDOC supplementary archive is CC BY as part of the paper. Its S1 package (54,350 bytes, SHA-256 b434cc6882da311ee2adb6ab975c7c0046520215e34c36519b37615948a52ca0) was inspected for file names, dictionary and design labels only. The source materials are Australian and German, with different recruitment from all three targets. Clinical exclusion criteria, measurement devices, handing of dominance and sampling frames can differ across studies.

| Study | What is released | Use in this protocol |
|---|---|---|
| North West Adelaide Health Study | Table 1, side-specific means and SD by sex and ten-year age group. Clinic protocol uses the mean of three trials and excludes specified upper-limb symptoms. | Source for all eight sessions. The reported distributions establish marginal constraints. The abstract and body disagree on overall counts, so neither count is used for weighting. |
| German motor function cohort | Table 1 gives the mean and SD of the **individual** ratio on a pressure device in 444 non-left-handed healthy adults, sexes/ages pooled. | Additional source for four J sessions and the ratio-based statistical comparator. The ratio can inform dependence without establishing a specific right-left kg correlation. |
| NHANES 2011–2012, US | Both hands measured repeatedly, anatomical starting side assigned at random, trial effort flags, design and examination weights available. | Primary. Mean of first two valid prespecified trials for each side. No ancillary US publications were supplied to authors. |
| HAALSI Agincourt, South Africa | Participant-level release 3.0, four cleaned left/right trial fields in kg, age, sex and village. | Primary. Mean of the two positive cleaned trials per side. This is an equal-cell sample analysis with village uncertainty. |
| EDOC Rio Branco, Brazil | Supporting DataSet.sav contains healthy-side maxima only, selection stratum, PSU and final weight. Caption identifies sex 1 as female, 2 as male. | External measurement stress test. Maximum per side, without silently treating it as a trial mean. Separate from previously used Brazilian national PNS. |

The custodian inspected target field labels and the Brazilian study's methodology, including ancillary single-hand public descriptions during search. Individual target measurements and target outcomes were not supplied to the authors. Model pretraining exposure is unobservable. Full articles, the Brazilian supplement, HAALSI original release and all actual or generated individual files stay local. No personal registration or participant identifiers are published. Outputs are for methodological research.
