# Does evidence about each hand support analyses of the same person's two hands?

This extension tests a new physiological domain with two principal recruitment families and a third measurement stress test. The Australian source reports both marginal distributions within four age and sex strata. Each author decides which two values belong to one person. We compare the downstream analyses with paired measurements, operating on the same fixed marker values across methods.

## Sources, information arms and scientific units

They used the average of three trials in seated, symptom-screened adults in Adelaide. Normal midquantile grids in each cell have exactly those reported centers and population SDs. This indirect evidence is optional in J. Slots are allocated in a fixed shuffled balanced dispatch order, using the same model version and budget. No cross-session input, internet, target rows or target statistics reach the authors. Every executable choice, failure, cost and target-blind source prediction is recorded. ## Analysis, decision and uncertainty

Each table supplies four analyses. The primary is the equal-cell mean absolute anatomical hand difference in kg. The three secondary uses are the percentage with an absolute gap of at least 5 kg, average bilateral strength in kg (the mean of each person's two sides) and the equally weighted mean of within-cell Spearman correlations, using weighted midranks for ties. Use margins are 1.0 kg, 5 percentage points, 2.0 kg and 0.10 respectively. The 5 kg cutoff and these margins serve planning comparisons and have no claim to a diagnostic or minimum clinically important difference.

The primary test compares uniform pairing with the four M authors' median **absolute error** on the mean absolute gap, separately in both primary cohorts. Require a gain of at least 1 kg at both measured points. Report whether this holds throughout each survey-design 95% interval. Mark the formal four-author comparison incomplete if an M author fails. An improvement over independence can coexist with missing the absolute use margin. Secondary comparisons include J versus M, J versus the Gaussian transfer rule and all outcomes in all three populations. No choice of best author, cohort, seed or grouping after disclosure. No claim of author-population inference from four authors. Mechanical errors stay in the denominator.

For measured intervals, delete one sampling unit at a time within its stratum, reweight the survivors, restandardize all four cells, sum the stratum jackknife variances and use a t quantile with number of units minus number of strata degrees of freedom. HAALSI villages form one stratum. Unevaluable cells or absent design degrees of freedom are reported. A fixed-law use is supported if the full interval lies inside its stated margin, contradicted if disjoint, and otherwise unresolved. For the prespecified method contrasts report the extrema over the measured interval, including all median breakpoints. These describe reference variation while holding the programs fixed; author uncertainty is reported by listing all authors.

A diagnostic replaces the Australian marginal grids with each target's *separate*, weighted within-cell empirical quantile grids. It applies the exact same archived permutations without any target pairing supplied to the generator. This is a descriptive reference-informed analysis on the same sample. Only point discrepancies are reported in this setting. The bilateral mean must be exactly invariant across pairings for the same grids. The rank correlation is invariant to strictly increasing transformations, but introducing the real distribution's ties can change it. No target-based tuning or further draws. Source outputs, joint choices and program hashes must be publicly sealed before accessing paired measurements. Failed or mismatched sites are retained. Private individual files, including generated records and ranks, remain local. Maximum 8 calls, USD 1.20 at the frozen tariff, no retries or additional model families.
