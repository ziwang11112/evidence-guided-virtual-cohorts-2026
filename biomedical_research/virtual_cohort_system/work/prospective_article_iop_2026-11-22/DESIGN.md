# One prospective article on paired pressure measurements

This is a single unassisted feasibility run on a study held out from project development. The case is Pye 2020, DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0224824. The question was selected using eligibility (new study and measurement domain, two same-person methods, original article open and public individual-level supplement). The search looked for PLOS papers on paired tonometers with supplemental data. Another biometry paper and glaucoma and posture candidate titles appeared; none were tested. The DOI, article title and Orssengo–Pye term returned no earlier project study match in a bounded search of Markdown, TeX and JSON. An unrelated geographic term matched older retrieval logs. Model pretraining exposure cannot be ruled out. Study selection was purposive, not a systematic review. The earlier four-study, two-arm proposal is separate from this one-case pilot.

## What is assessed

1. Use one matched pair of left-eye Pascal DCT and Goldmann measurements per participant. Compute mean(P−G), and 100 times the fraction with P−G strictly greater than 3 mmHg on the same complete pairs; ties contribute only to the denominator. Retain the workbook's stored values; use 1e−10 mmHg solely to handle numeric equality. Do not add a rounding scheme absent from the paper. Distinguish calculated IOPTcalc from measured Pascal. Apply identical estimators to each delivered CSV and the same-study complete cases. If the supplement cannot be matched unambiguously, flag the reference as unavailable. Report numerators, pair count, ties, and a 95% paired participant bootstrap interval (2,000 draws) for the measured estimates.
2. In each final table check the two means (16.89/15.06 mmHg), sample SDs (2.49/2.71), and the joint correlations reported in text and caption (0.5969/0.597/0.598). Design tolerances are 0.10 mmHg separately for each mean/SD and 0.025 from the nearest printed coefficient. The discussion reports 19/100 subjects with an absolute pressure difference at least 4 and in most of these Pascal was higher. Require the absolute tail proportion within 2 percentage points and more than half of these discrepancies positive. Require all paired pressures positive and all delivered rows complete. Report every item and all seeds. These operational tolerances allow simulation error and supply no clinical accuracy standard. Also show mean difference error against 1.83 from the source; predeclared use-level agreement within 0.10 mmHg per seed.
3. Before uncovering measured results, classify the author's prospective answer: does it recognize that the mean difference is supported and distinguish the signed strict threshold from the unsigned source threshold? Record scenarios and language claiming a point result or sensitivity. Separately, accept a numerical demonstration only if two authored scenarios each meet every registered source check on all three seeds, keep the mean difference within the above tolerance and differ in signed threshold proportion by at least 5 percentage points on average with the same direction on each matched seed. Report all pairs, including ineligible ones. Without two admissible scenarios a verbal conclusion of uncertainty is still graded separately. No claim of tight bounds.
4. After closure report signed absolute-unit discrepancies of both estimands for every table. Compare primary and all scenarios with the observed reference; report whether the measured proportion lies inside the observed span as descriptive only. Report whether the archived paper's own source summaries agree with the workbook; source conflicts are diagnostics, and do not authorize tuning. No claims of significance, population-wide accuracy or agent superiority from this single case. Any source key omissions found after the freeze are identified as post hoc.
