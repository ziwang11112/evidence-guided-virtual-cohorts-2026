# Prospective test of within-band dependence in an external iron-status cohort

## Scientific question

Given identical published evidence and identical univariate samples, does the pairing law written by a scientific agent improve a group-level analysis relative to a conventional generator that calibrates a nonzero dependence parameter from that evidence? The analysis is the Spearman correlation of measured hemoglobin (Hb) and serum ferritin. Standalone clinical or individual-level prediction is outside this experiment.

## Cohort and information boundary

The next candidate in the November 19 admission order was the 2011 Akwa Ibom survey, Nigeria, version 6.2, DOI 10.7910/DVN/29604. The project had previously seen its title. Admission used recruitment context, the codebook, permissions, duplicate keys, available measurements and cluster counts. The analytic variables in two official file formats were compared programmatically without reporting their values or calculating endpoints. Project-level outcome exposure and possible model-pretraining exposure are distinct. Only the evaluator may see target values.

Select records with Group M, recorded female sex (2), and mother age 18 through 49 inclusive. Use the unique LGA, EA, ID, Group key. Require finite positive HGB in g/dL and FER_ug_L in micrograms per liter. Use uncorrected ferritin and strict Hb <12 and ferritin <15 thresholds. Pregnancy is unavailable in these biomarker fields. Analyses describe eligible sampled mothers with equal person weights. Resample whole enumeration areas within local government areas for uncertainty. A missing sampling weight precludes a population-prevalence claim.

Both arms receive the same target description and the same common source packet. It contains Zhu et al. (2010, DOI 10.1024/0300-9831/a000014), two aggregate tables of 1,123 Chinese women from the CHNS A communities, the middle-band geometric-mean ratio 1.215 and its interval, and the definitions of the shared marginal generator. The latter three summaries come from the separately labeled research supplement. The Chinese source populations are external to Nigeria. Neither arm sees the Nigerian data, its paper results, target correlation, B-half CHNS outcomes, historical agent estimates, rival code, or outcome feedback. The survey receipt and codebook are private evaluator inputs. Both methods receive the same written request. The statistical rule specifies in advance which inputs it uses numerically.

## Two complete generators sharing the same marginal samples

Reuse the previously frozen fit of the three Hb-band counts and Hb and log-ferritin summaries from CHNS A. Its strictly positive marginal models use beta/shifted gamma for Hb and the fitted threshold-reweighted normal for log ferritin. At each of three shared seeds (201101, 201103, 201107), generate 12,000 independent marginal records. Sort Hb and ferritin separately within each band. Each method completes a cohort by choosing a one-to-one pairing of these order statistics inside the band. Thus every submitted law retains exactly the same marginal values, band counts and four threshold cells at each seed. The diagnostic isolates the choice of dependence while holding finite-draw margins fixed. Its scope is this component of the full system.

**Agent arm.** Six independent fresh sessions, one request each, existing GPT-6.1 Sol configuration, maximum 16,384 output tokens and USD 3.00 total estimated API tariff. Each author chooses its structure and parameters and supplies an executable permutation sampler, provenance and limitations. A law can use band-specific parameters, nonlinear or mixture dependence, and source-only calibration. Both positive and negative choices are admissible. The interface checks the returned permutation. The host never repairs or ranks a law using target outcomes. No retries or replacements after dispatch; all six slots stay in the denominator. Three seeds measure Monte Carlo variability within each law.

**Statistical arm.** Within each band, couple two standard-normal ranks with a Gaussian copula. Calibrate one common latent correlation to the supplied log geometric-mean contrast between Hb 13–<14 and 12–<13 within the middle band. The conditional probability of falling below Hb 13 given the latent ferritin score gives a one-dimensional integral for the two conditional log means. Solve their difference against log(1.215) on [−0.995, 0.995]; a missing feasible solution stops the comparison before reveal. Use this coefficient in all three bands. The extrapolation to both outer bands and from China to Nigeria is an explicit statistical assumption. Apply the correlated ranks to the same finite order statistics used by every agent. Keep the resulting finite source contrast as a diagnostic; it need not match its population target exactly. No target-fitted coefficient and no choice among copulas after disclosure.

## Freeze, scoring and decision

Freeze the scientific inputs, implementations, model configuration, prompt digest, seed list, data digest and admission record before dispatch. Seal every executable decision and source-only output before the evaluator computes any target endpoint. If the evaluator detects a changed digest it stops. Publish only code, protocols, model decisions, aggregate outputs and verification receipts.

For each law pool its three draws. Use average ranks for ties on measured and virtual records. Let D equal the statistical arm's absolute rank error minus the median absolute rank error across executable Agent laws. Positive D favors the Agent. Report all six slot outcomes, every per-law estimate and error, D, seed diagnostics and the crude threshold odds ratio. In 2,000 replicates (seed 201121), sample the same number of EAs within each LGA with replacement and recompute the empirical rank and both arms' errors against that same resample. The paired percentile interval describes target sampling variation conditional on the sealed constructions. It does not count constructors, synthetic persons or seeds as independent cohorts.

Evidence of a practically useful rank advantage on this cohort requires ≥4/6 successful authors, D ≥0.02, a lower 95% paired bootstrap bound above zero, and a common virtual OR within a factor of 1.50 of the measured OR with at least ten observations in every measured cell. A 0.02 change around rank 0.30 corresponds to about 13% in an illustrative Fisher-z sample-size calculation, a planning convention. The OR is an applicability check shared by both methods. It cannot distinguish their pairing laws. Insufficient threshold counts, low precision, inconclusive intervals or a reversed ordering remain reportable outcomes. Do not change the protocol, seek a better model, or replace the cohort in response. One independent cohort is one external test.

With zero executable Agent laws the measured reference stays sealed. With one to three, report the descriptive comparison and record the failed minimum-author criterion.
