# Prospective external iron-status comparison in Akwa Ibom, Nigeria

Six fresh GPT-6.1 Sol sessions independently wrote a program to pair hemoglobin with serum ferritin. All six chose a Gaussian rank copula calibrated to the same middle-band contrast. A statistical comparator specified before dispatch chose the same family. The observed rank in 603 mothers was 0.010. The six agents gave 0.308 and the statistical comparator gave 0.303. The prespecified advantage and threshold transport criteria both failed. [Frozen comparison](PROTOCOL.md) · [All estimates](RESULTS.json)

## What the comparison tests

Both arms received the same written request, Zhu et al.'s paper and separate aggregate tables for 1,123 women in Chinese CHNS A communities, including a ferritin geometric-mean ratio of 1.215 (95% interval 1.061–1.398) in two halves of the middle Hb band. A source-only model generated and sorted the same marginal values in each band for every method. The author chose how to couple them, which source statistics to use and how to transfer the dependence to Nigeria. The comparator calibrated one within-band Gaussian copula coefficient (0.13653) and applied it across three bands. None of the authors received this implementation. The evaluator kept Nigerian endpoints closed until the programs and all outputs were sealed. The analysis was unweighted in mothers aged 18–49 with both biomarkers. The intervals use 2,000 resamples of enumeration areas within the ten local government areas. The six separately returned programs had distinct source-code hashes. At all three shared seeds, five produced identical arrays. The other program's middle-band ratio differed by 0.000014. Each program succeeded. The three seeds describe Monte Carlo variation for each law. The six model sessions and 21 virtual draws represent one external cohort assessment. The six calls had a combined tariff-estimated cost of US$0.483814. [Dispatch receipts](AUTHORS.json) · [All per-seed numbers and output hashes](DRAW_CLOSURE.json)

Statistical absolute error minus median agent absolute error was −0.00509. Both percentile limits rounded to −0.00509. For a reference rank below both fixed estimates, its value cancels in the difference between their absolute errors. The interval conditions on the sealed draws. It says little about uncertainty across other cohorts or model families. Even before disclosure, the maximum distance between any agent rank and the statistical rank was 0.00509. By the reverse triangle inequality this put the required 0.02 gain out of reach for any reference. [Before-disclosure observation](CLOSURE_NOTE.md)

The measured threshold cells were 31, 297, 22 and 253 in the order low Hb/low ferritin, low Hb/higher ferritin, higher Hb/low ferritin and higher Hb/higher ferritin. The common virtual odds ratio was 4.49 times the measured value. The prespecified guard allowed 1.50. The shared marginal values within each band make this statistic exactly identical across methods here. The continuous ranks and threshold associations estimated from the Chinese evidence were too large for this Nigerian sample. Pregnancy, inflammation, lab procedures and other population differences could matter. The experiment does not isolate their effects. A sampled reference and its interval describe the available evidence in this cohort.

## Integrity and reproduction

The independent scorer reproduced all seven laws, 21 arrays, within-band marginal multisets, the Stata reference and the 2,000-resample interval endpoints. The original verifier's 1e−12 tolerance across file formats failed. The two official representations differ by up to 2.84e−14 in ferritin. Text serialization merged two ferritin values, changing each of their midranks by 0.5. Its reference rank was 0.01029415 versus 0.01028983 in Stata. The measured cells, shared conclusions and signed gap agreed. The original failure and the additional check are both available.
