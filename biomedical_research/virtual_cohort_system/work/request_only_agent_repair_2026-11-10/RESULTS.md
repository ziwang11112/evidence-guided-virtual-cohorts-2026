This separately frozen development round kept the same research requests, scientific arm policies, GPT configuration, 24-call allowance and 250,000-byte request ceiling. Both arms searched independently from empty source collections. Only shared search-result presentation and exact model-specification handoff changed. No scientific parameter was supplied or repaired by the host.

## Source boundary and interpretation

The source audit found an incidental target-family exposure in PNS ordinary: a returned page from PMC12622929 cited a Brazilian national-survey hypertension prevalence. The cached bibliography identifies National Health Survey provenance. This was not one of the held evaluation slopes/correlations. The author stated that it ignored the quantity and the audit found no visible numerical use, but absence of use is not absence of exposure. The episode cannot be described as strictly free of excluded-family information. Exact displayed offsets, hashes and the author note are recorded in the [scientific provenance audit](review/SCIENTIFIC_PROVENANCE_AUDIT.md).

All references were already exposed at project level. The generated sample ranges describe variation under fixed laws; the clinical reference estimates have their own sampling/design/transport limitations. These are developmental analytical comparisons, not new external clinical confirmation.

## Bounded continuation decision

After the terminal delivery outcomes, but **before this round's reference evaluation**, root precommitted one final shared execution-budget diagnostic.
