# Scientific checking and Agent-led revision: bounded paired pilot

Version 1, 11 November 2026. Authorized by the user's request to integrate mathematical properties, physiological consistency and joint-relationship checks and let the Agent diagnose and revise. This is a targeted revision study, not a fresh request-only construction comparison or a new clinical holdout.

## Scientific question

Can Agent-led review repair problems in a published-evidence-based virtual-cohort generator and improve its requested scientific analyses, without receiving clinical target results or host-written scientific parameters? Separate three outcomes: discovery and repair of defects, coherence of returned data, and analytical agreement with existing sample references. An improvement in one does not imply an improvement in all.

The preceding twelve-session study supplies **all twelve** frozen starting generators. Each is copied byte-for-byte to a neutral imported candidate v000, with its specification, scientific notes, provenance and captured full-source artifacts. Each starting law receives two fresh GPT review sessions: ordinary discretionary review and structured review of all three dimensions. Thus there are twelve paired starting laws and 24 review episodes, covering two existing scientific contexts. Original author policy is a lineage characteristic, not the new treatment. Old files and all initial outputs remain unchanged.

## Information and treatment

Both paired sessions receive the same original research request, task, immutable starting law and source catalog. Sources may be read through the same captured-artifact tool or supplemented through the same screened literature tools. No old evaluation, clinical reference, postclosure audit, reviewer finding, requested numerical fix or prior Agent conversation is supplied. Full articles and scientific artifacts are imported; old search previews are not imported. The project's earlier target-family preview exposure remains in lineage. Source aliases are not a perfect firewall and any new incidental exposure is retained and reported.

The scientific task fields and original request remain unchanged. Task budget metadata alone is updated to the live review limits (eight queries, six downloads, three new revisions). A shared `analysis_requirements.json` names the three requested operators, variable sets, adjustments and output units for each context, without numeric answers. It is available equally in both review arms and prevents invented analysis IDs from satisfying coverage.

Both review arms have identical tools, model, generation interface and resource ceilings. Ordinary review is free to search, analyze mathematical properties, propose physiological rules, inspect joint assumptions and revise; it is not a one-shot or deliberately weak baseline. Structured review adds explicit coverage of the three dimensions and requires an inspection record for the selected candidate before finalization. The treatment is therefore a prompt-plus-completion-gate bundle, not exclusive tool access. Honest unresolved, inapplicable and justified no-change decisions are allowed. No quantity of revisions, positive finding or desired scientific sign is required.

Neutral envelope identifiers reduce origin cues, but unchanged notes/code may reveal original style or policy. Complete blinding is not claimed. No current session can access its paired session's work. Human hosts may implement generic mechanics but cannot edit candidate distributions, parameters, source selections or repair instructions after looking at target agreement.

## Three checks

1. **Mathematical law.** The Agent records support, transformations and moment claims, with code locators and justification. A bounded AST scanner returns structural warnings and unsupported cases. It is heuristic: no warning is not proof of finite moments, and a warning is not a theorem of divergence. Observed finite rows do not prove population integrability. Numerical caps, rejection, conditional/global random parameters and actual versus counterfactual laws must remain distinct.
2. **Physiological consistency.** The Agent proposes source-backed or explicitly assumed declarative linear identities, inequalities or ranges. The checker evaluates those declarations on isolated generated output and records violation counts and denominators. Rules are not automatically certified biological facts. Empty rule sets require an explanation. No generic code evaluates arbitrary expressions or secretly inserts case-specific relationships.
3. **Joint analytical relationships.** For each requested analysis the Agent records relevant variables, adjustment, dependence/scale parameters, their reported/derived/assumed/unresolved origin, source operators and uncertainty. Coverage and registered-source checks concern declared consistency and availability, not correctness of extraction or transfer. Unreported covariance must not be treated as known independence. The checker never provides a desired target slope, correlation or SD.

These checks supplement the shared isolated trial execution and generated-only statistics. The Agent chooses whether to search, change structure, change source interpretation, run a check, retain or stop. Revised candidates are immutable new submissions. Store the exact check contract, actual results, candidate hashes and revision/retention reason. A new note or completed checklist is not a change in the generated law.

## Frozen resources and orchestration

Use `gpt-6.1-sol`, high reasoning, default service tier and the existing Responses API loop. Rates and availability are inherited from the same-day verified receipts; preserve exact requested/returned model IDs, usage, prompts and tool histories. No model fallback, automatic HTTP retry, hidden paid reviewer or provider switch.

Each of 24 review episodes: at most 24 model calls, 16,384 output tokens per call, 750,000 serialized request bytes including history, 24 dispatched tool actions, eight provider queries, six new full-source downloads, three new candidate submissions and three isolated trial executions. Imported v000 and captured sources do not consume new submission/download quotas. Any inspection-triggered execution consumes a trial slot; inspecting a bound existing trial does not rerun it. Fixed trial seed 53001. USD2 accounted model ceiling per episode and USD48 for the whole new unit include unknown/pending reservations. Large request reservations can exhaust the monetary ceiling before the byte ceiling. Cost is a token-tariff estimate, not an invoice or total research cost.

Maximum two concurrent episodes. Deterministically interleave sorted PNS and lipid starting-author IDs, assigning R01–R12, with generic then structured branches. Blocks contain two pairs/four sessions. Inspect only interface, isolation, accounting and exposure handling after block 1; do not inspect target comparisons or change policy based on scientific returns. Subsequent blocks require prior block closure. Integrity/uncertain-state failures pause fresh dispatch; ordinary recorded scientific, tool or budget failures remain outcomes. No replacement sessions or quota expansion to obtain a favorable result.

The entire code, policies, imported package manifest, dependency hashes and analysis plan must pass independent preflight and be bound in a launch freeze and root approval before paid calls. Tests use mocked HTTP and artificial frames; old generators execute only through the existing WSL/bubblewrap worker. No local-execution fallback, new patient access, raw-data download, training or HPC.

## Final evaluation

All 24 episodes must be terminal and candidate histories sealed before final sampling or clinical-reference comparison. Three final seeds (54001, 54003, 54007), 3,000 rows each, give at most 72 final tables. Initial references use the already generated, hash-pinned 36 tables from the preceding unit and are not regenerated or refitted. Exact retention of v000 is a real decision, not a fabricated revised success; failures remain missing final outputs, not silently substituted initial tables.

The six primary analyses and common scientific estimators remain unchanged: age/sex-adjusted BMI associations with SBP, DBP, TC and HDL, and within-pair residual Pearson correlations adjusted for age, sex and BMI. Preserve unit conversion, saved uncertainty conventions and all dispersion/coherence diagnostics. Clinical reference and initial-output hashes are evaluator-only. Compare initial, generic-review and structured-review results for every starting law, retaining unfavorable changes, no-ops and unavailable analyses. Same seed labels create a useful numerical pairing but may no longer represent matched random events after a structural change. Seeds are nested within starting law/review branch; they are not new patients or independent clinical populations.

Previously exposed sample references remain developmental and uncertain. No post hoc equivalence tolerance or pooled quality score will be defined. After closure, an independent reviewer inspects all final scientific laws with uniform criteria and recomputes every delivered table. Agent-declared physiology rules can differ; compare the pre-existing common physiological diagnostics across branches separately from each Agent's self-selected checks. Improvement of a self-selected rule alone is not global quality improvement.

## Decision and reporting

Report whether defects were noticed and correctly addressed, whether new defects appeared, whether assumptions became explicit, and whether requested analysis estimates improved, worsened or stayed similar. A generic review that succeeds equally is useful evidence for simplifying the method. If structured review only improves documentation or catches a tail issue without improving clinical agreement, report that narrower benefit. Missing joint evidence and transport remain separate from implementation defects.

Stop after this bounded comparison and independent audit. Do not add cases, repeat failed sessions, use revealed references for further repair, or launch additional models automatically. Preserve all negative and unavailable outcomes. Public export, if later performed, must exclude individual real/generated tables, full third-party articles, credentials and raw API captures. The primary product remains virtual data for scientific analyses, with checkable modeling choices.

New checks are client-executed function tools; the model decides their use and receives their recorded outputs. This documentation is not evidence of scientific validity.
