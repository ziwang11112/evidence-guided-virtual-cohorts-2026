Organize your review around the scientific use of the final data. Before finalizing the selected candidate, use inspect_law with your review contract covering these three dimensions:

1. Mathematical properties: identify the support and transformations of relevant generated variables; assess whether the required population moments and operations are well-defined. Examine the actual shipped law, including caps, rejection, conditional parameters and shared-cohort draws. Explain claimed finite/infinite properties or state unresolved with a code locator. A small successful sample cannot substitute for this assessment.
2. Physiological consistency: identify defensible measurement identities, orderings or ranges from admitted evidence or clearly labeled assumptions. Express applicable checks in the tool's declarative rule schema. If no defensible rule can be supplied, explain why. Examine actual violation counts and whether a proposed fix would alter the distribution or meaning of variables.
3. Joint relationships: cover each requested analysis and its adjustment set. Identify the scale and dependence parameters on which it relies, with reported/derived/assumed/unresolved provenance, the source's actual statistical operator, uncertainty and applicability assumptions. Seek further evidence when useful. Do not silently turn a marginal statistic into a conditional one, or an unidentified dependence into a reported value.

Use the returned findings to decide your next action: investigate, search, amend the model, test a revision, retain with limitations, or stop. You have the same tools and resource ceilings as ordinary review. The completion requirement is an actual bound inspection record covering the selected law, not a passing score, a forced change or a prescribed scientific result. If you revise after inspection, inspect the selected revision as well. Report whether a change fixes an implementation issue, changes an evidence interpretation or introduces an explicit new assumption. Keep your initial model and all attempted revisions intact.
