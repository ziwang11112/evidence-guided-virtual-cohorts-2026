## Disposition

**The corrected design is scientifically coherent for bounded admission work. Execution remains unreleased.** It asks one contribution-defining question: whether adding applicable joint/conditional evidence improves multiple analyses of the same Agent-generated whole cohort. A fixed-procedure E0/E1 comparison addresses information value; the E1 conventional generator provides same-information context. Ordinary-Agent generation remains an acceptable core method, and Agent superiority is not required.

The artificial pilot already answers the elementary responsiveness question. Another toy or marginal-only target batch would not resolve biomedical applicability. Complete this highlight, or record its bounded admission failure, before final manuscript/method consolidation as requested by the user. Neither outcome is promised.

The source matrix appropriately distinguishes a recovered finding, a qualified mathematical comparison, exact source replay and target transport. Missing original weights or coefficient precision do not automatically forbid an explicitly qualified point constraint. Full joint identification is unnecessary. The separate Jansen projections must remain separate; Bao and MIND correlations retain their Pearson/rank and measurement distinctions; quarantined information remains excluded. No stronger source-replay requirement is recommended.

Verify one officially accessible target, exposure/overlap status, required measurements and eligibility fields without effect-based selection. No ready target is established by these documents. A complete source joint law or exact original-study replay is not required.

2. **Exact analyses and primary decision.** Freeze the three estimands and their common target population, the seed-to-constructor summary, constructor-to-arm summary, signed E0/E1 discrepancy contrast, and named reference-uncertainty sensitivity calculation. Absolute error of a seed-averaged estimate differs from average absolute seed error; this choice cannot follow results. The current phrase “reasonable reference-uncertainty sensitivity” is an intention, not yet an executable rule.

3. **Failure treatment in the primary denominator.** Specify what happens when a constructor, one of its three final draws, or a particular analysis fails. A positive contrast cannot be computed on successful survivors while failures appear only in a separate appendix. Predeclare whether such failures block the positive claim or enter an explicit comparison rule, while retaining computational and scientific failure categories separately. Do not invent an equivalence margin to solve this.

4. **Arm-specific packet, review and feedback binding.** Commit E0 and E1, the conventional generator and the finite attempt schedule. Check on invented inputs that A cannot receive E1 source values or E1-informed feedback/reviewer decisions, including through source discrepancies or pass/fail messages. Reusing shared software does not itself prove this boundary.

5. **Case implementation and one-time evaluation.** Review any needed approximate-OLS collector/interpretation binding without weakening strict unavailable states; check unit conversion, context linkage, exact case arithmetic and target-output sealing using invented inputs. Freeze a finite technical-repair policy. Independently review the concrete release, then evaluate the measured reference once after global construction closure and independently recompute all results.

These are concrete remaining release items, not grounds to add a generic platform, a new loss, new training, or more Agent roles.

## Claim and feasibility limits

Without an independently justified scientific-use tolerance, a favorable result supports **reduced discrepancy on two named analyses**, with observed construction spread and reference-uncertainty limits. It does not establish clinical equivalence, quantitative replacement, calibrated uncertainty or general reliability. Null/independence anchors help detect a nondiscriminating success rule; they do not replace the primary contrast. The third use remains reportable even when it is assumption-dependent or unsuccessful.

The two Jansen projections are distinct candidate scientific uses rather than redundant representations of one coefficient. They nevertheless share a source population and would share one target, so they are not independent clinical validations. A third tail/subgroup use needs measurement support and a frozen scientific question. Merely withholding an analysis label does not make an algebraically implied answer a novel discovery.

Existing operators and the common source/target bridge make a narrow arm-aware implementation plausible. The present feasibility gap is a fresh accessible target and a defensible source-to-target interpretation, plus the explicitly identified case feedback integration if used. Neither source coefficients nor access announcements close that gap.

The documents preserve the negative Wave A gate and close HAALSI, Turkana and earlier revealed targets to tuning or fresh confirmation. Reusing externally published source material does not reopen old targets. Any context-exposed candidate must retain that label; a new endpoint or generator cannot make it wholly unseen.

No further design-level change is required for the next bounded admission unit. Final method/claim consolidation follows complete prospective evaluation or the recorded admission stopping decision, not a presumed successful outcome.
