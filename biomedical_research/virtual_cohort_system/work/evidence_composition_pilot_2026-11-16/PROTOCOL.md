## Unified outcomes

Adjusted family: OLS-residual Pearson correlation of X and Y after intercept + Z adjustment (primary); marginal X–Y Pearson correlation; slope in X ~ intercept + Z. Combined evidence implies .21875, .5 and .6 respectively. The common moments and any supplied pairwise correlations are source checks.

Longitudinal family: Pearson correlation of within-person changes (primary); average of the two same-visit X–Y Pearson correlations; Pearson X_visit1–Y_visit2 correlation. Stipulated stable components plus independent visit deviations identify -.6, .52 and .64 with both records. These structural assumptions are provided equally in every arm. Person components cannot be directly observed in output; between variance/correlation are estimated through cross-visit covariance, and within variance via half the change variance. These checks depend on the stipulated visit independence.

Primary analyses are not identified with only common, A-only, or B-only evidence. Partial conditions are evaluated against their available constraints and the correctness of identification labels. Their distance from the full-information world is reported ONLY as scenario separation, never as failure to recover an identifiable truth. Witness covariance constructions in the earlier preflight establish nonidentification but not the full feasible interval or a probabilistic prior.

For identified analyses report value, absolute error and a descriptive Monte Carlo tolerance of .08 in correlation/slope units. Source diagnostic tolerances are .08 for correlations, 12% for variances, .12 marginal standard deviations for means. These thresholds are intentionally simple preregistered numerical diagnostics, not scientific equivalence margins, formal hypothesis tests or 95% coverage. Report continuous errors as well. The output distribution is author-chosen; the diagnostics do not assume every law is Gaussian.

Each author declares an identified/scenario/undetermined status for the three named functionals and a value or null. Compare identifiedness with the algebraic classification.
