## Scientific interpretation

The current request-only execution delivered four selected laws and twelve final tables. Both Agents found numerical external dependence evidence. BP residual correlations of 0.696 and 0.727 are close numerically to the existing reference of 0.717, while both BMI–BP effects are too large. Lipid slope, dependence and dispersion behavior remains analysis-specific. Workflow is not consistently better than ordinary Agent, and neither revised its initial successful candidate in the final round.

The earlier twenty delivered fixed-packet laws all understated residual dependence within two tasks. The new BP results narrow that observation: it is not a universal property of Agent-built cohorts. Sequential experiments differ in evidence, construction, model configuration and allowances, so they do not isolate the causal effect of retrieval.

Traceability identifies a published quantity, its statistical operator, the adopted parameter, an algebraic or scientific assumption and the implementation that produces an analysis. It helps inspect what is documented; it does not certify transport, recover hidden reasoning or assign validated confidence. The scope is now explicitly linked to adjacent work, including G-Sim, LLMSynthor, SynthIPD, KMGen and PROV-AGENT. See [novelty positioning](NOVELTY_POSITIONING.md) and the [claim/evidence map](CLAIM_EVIDENCE_MAP.md).

## Focused extensions designed, not run

The [extension plan](EXPERIMENT_EXTENSION_PLAN.md) separates four questions:

1. Repeat the frozen current workflow with twelve fresh sessions: two requests × two policies × three constructors, at most 36 final tables. This addresses authoring variability; it creates no new clinical reference.
2. Reuse the four final laws in a provenance-usability audit. Compare the same source/code materials with and without the navigation map, using independent adjudication and reviewer accuracy/time. A single biomedical friend's review is qualitative feedback, not a comparative user study.
3. If scientific breadth is the priority after repetition, admit one existing glycemia request under the unchanged runtime only after a schema/operator/reference review. Six sessions and at most 18 tables would be a separate developmental unit, with no new patient acquisition implied.
4. If model dependence is the priority, add one compatible economical GPT configuration in a balanced design. Broader provider portability is a separate optional claim. Existing fixed-packet multi-provider results are unbalanced and cannot be used as a ranking of the live workflow.

These are ordered choices, not an automatic combined campaign. The user's economical GPT preference is retained. No additional spending, training, HPC, data-access application or reviewer contact was initiated.
