## Story and contribution

The main question is whether an Agent can turn a biomedical research request and incomplete external evidence into actual joint records that support specified scientific analyses. The contribution is presented as implemented scientific construction, demonstrated analysis-specific utility, and auditable links between evidence, assumptions, code and analytical consequences. Ordinary capable Agents remain valid implementations. A specialized workflow is not declared superior, and more Agent roles or mandatory feedback are not presented as necessary scientific ingredients.

Explainability means recoverable scientific dependencies: source statistic with its operator and population; adopted numerical or structural choice; full-law implication where derivable; sampled analysis; measured reference. It does not mean private chain-of-thought, calibrated confidence, an automatic source-applicability certificate, or a demonstrated user-time saving. The method explicitly separates what the author is asked to record from the connections independently found in completed artifacts.
