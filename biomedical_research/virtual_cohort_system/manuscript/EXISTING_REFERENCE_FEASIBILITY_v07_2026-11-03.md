# Existing-reference feasibility for v0.7

3 November 2026. Bounded local metadata review for one optional prospective external-evidence validation. **Decision: DEFER the extension. No case is admitted or released.** The reviewed local inventory does not establish an acquired, usable, project-unexposed reference with a compatible external evidence package. Continue the current developmental/reconstruction claim in v0.7.

This is an admission finding, not a negative scientific result or proof that every unused endpoint is exposed. Three alternate-outcome leads were examined. HAALSI lipids is the strongest **qualified metadata lead**, but all three belong to already exposed families. A different outcome or estimand cannot support a fully new-cohort claim. Exact-estimand non-exposure also remains to be established; absence from a bounded record search is insufficient.

### 1. HAALSI lipids: qualified, not admitted

**Existing access and files.** The [access record](../reports/scaleup_2026-11-01/admission_next/HAALSI_ACCESS.json) records official DOI `10.7910/DVN/F5YHML`, version 3.0, accepted custom DUA and completed guestbook. It records the acquired `admission_next/private_data/HAALSI_baseline_dataverse_14Apr2017.dta`, SHA-256 `27c968d5c9e53948bc8ee21e0a5bdc2262ed3c17f1dbafb5bb303050b6715b9f`, with publisher MD5 match. Its earlier pending/unexposed statements are historical and superseded by Wave A closure. No new terms or access application are needed merely to review retained metadata.

**Definitions actually available.** In the retained `admission_next/private_documents/HAALSI Baseline Survey and Variables.docx` (SHA-256 `451d7dad209bae21b324604c95696f60143f825acedd28b12360ddb3de416c67`), zero-based table 41, row 36 defines `c_bs_chol` as cleaned point-of-care cholesterol in mmol/L; row 42 defines `c_bs_hdl` as cleaned point-of-care HDL in mmol/L. Table 38, rows 78/82 and replacement/re-entry rows identify CardioCheck measurements. The cleaned rows have no explicit color override; raw rows direct the reader to the cleaned replacements. This proves documented definitions, not populated fields in the released file. Row 58 calls `c_bs_ldl` calculated LDL; it is not a substitute independent measured outcome. Flag rows 76/80 document range rules, not observed values. Their handling and any boundary/censoring convention remain unresolved for the lipid use.

Age `rage` is age at sample selection on 31 July 2014; `rsex` is census-preloaded sex; `c_bs_bmi` is calculated from cleaned height/weight. The [existing definition audit](../reports/scaleup_2026-11-01/admission_next/HAALSI_CODEBOOK_SCHEMA.md) documents baseline recruitment of adults at least 40, point-of-care context and derived fasting timing. Its glucose-specific audit cannot certify lipid availability, eligibility, fasting status, sampling weights or assay equivalence.

**Possible use, not a frozen analysis.** A complete virtual cohort could be tested on a prespecified HDL–BMI coefficient adjusted for age/sex and a TC–HDL dependence measure adjusted for the same declared covariates. These are scientifically connected to the available source information. No expected sign, numerical tolerance, weak-effect claim or target population size is selected here. A new protocol would have to fix all of those relevant definitions before any target disclosure.

**External evidence.** The local [Greiner operator audit](../reports/scaleup_2026-11-01/GREINER_SOURCE_OPERATOR_REVIEW.md), [independent packet review](../reports/scaleup_2026-11-01/SOURCE_PACKET_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW_2026-11-02.md) and `case_k02/request/sources/greiner_source_packet.json` retain source-specific lipid/BMI geometric means, low-HDL proportions and contextual sex-specific relationships from Masaka GPC/MEIRU. These are distinct named recruitment frames from HAALSI; participant non-overlap is not independently certified. The source exclusions, age composition and laboratory assays differ from HAALSI. Regression raw/log mapping remains unresolved and cannot silently become a numerical partial-slope constraint. Geometric-mean CIs are not individual SDs. AWI-Gen links in HAALSI's history require explicit site/recruitment checks if other existing African sources are proposed.

**Exposure and claim boundary.** HAALSI glucose and its evaluated associations are exposed. The reviewed records do not record disclosure of the proposed HAALSI lipid coefficients, but do not certify that those coefficients or equivalent analyses are project-unexposed. A later favorable result could at most support a prospectively fixed alternate-outcome use in an exposed cohort family, with disclosed history and transfer assumptions. It would not establish a wholly unseen cohort, broad African transport, clinical risk validity or causal discovery.

**Existing access and measure.** The [acquisition ledger](../reports/chns_admission_2026-11-01/ACQUISITION_LEDGER.json) records `private_data/biomarker_09.sas7bdat`, SHA-256 `cfef4075d994e3ee30a393695d3b05a8df21fe931037c7d9cd28c8709f2f2948`, plus acquired `pexam_00.sas7bdat`, `mast_pub_12.sas7bdat` and `surveys_pub_12.sas7bdat`, with publisher MD5 matches. The [biomarker audit](../reports/chns_admission_2026-11-01/BIOMARKER_SCHEMA_AUDIT.md) warns that the legacy codebook is not a verified universal mapping for this current file. **Evidence and exposure.** The retained urate source audit (local-only audit: `work/m13/urate_evidence.md`) provides non-CHNS Bogalusa and Asian source evidence, but explicitly parks broad adult obesity/urate use. Standardized forward effects, reverse regressions, categorical contrasts and selected-population SDs are different operators; the package does not yet identify a compatible conditional model or uncertainty scheme. The [CHNS exposure map](../reports/chns_admission_2026-11-01/EXPOSURE_MAP.md) records an already read result-bearing CHNS urate–HOMA/insulin-resistance abstract, including BMI/age/glucose/insulin context. That target-family paper is not external construction evidence.

An age/sex-adjusted urate–BMI analysis is a conceivable alternate use, but the existing record does not settle equivalence to previously exposed urate relations. It is therefore not admitted as project-unexposed. Changing to a subgroup or reversing a regression would not resolve this. At most, a later separately justified case could support qualified developmental or alternate-estimand reuse, never a fresh CHNS cohort claim.
