This inventory assigns completed experiments a role in the paper. It is not a count of every development run, a pooled benchmark, or a new evaluation. The principal manuscript uses three separately designed studies; the remaining records provide context and limitations. Historical no-go results are retained.

The [historical-results supplement](SUPPLEMENT_HISTORICAL_RESULTS_v05.md) retains fuller developmental narratives. The [source-and-assumption supplement](SUPPLEMENT_SOURCE_AND_ASSUMPTIONS_v05.md) documents the actual A01 construction, its residual-correlation assumption, unfitted source check and target mismatch.

The earlier enrichment protocols had their own reserved values and disclosure rules; they are now revealed and cannot be reused as fresh confirmation. They add mode-specific feasibility evidence, not extra independent validation of the current whole-cohort procedure. Missing-cell completion remains a formulation/interface capability unless a separately audited missing-cell experiment is cited.

No grand sum is used for participant counts, clinical cohorts, accuracy or experiments. Source-context tables, trial outputs, duplicate retained stages, final seed draws and independent scientific constructions answer different questions. Paper displays preserve each denominator rather than treating additional synthetic records as additional observations.
