# What is the scientific agent contributing?

The paper studies a practical problem. A researcher has published group summaries and a new question, such as the fraction eligible for lipid screening. A synthetic table makes that question computable. Its result may depend on how the generator assigns measurements to the same individual. Our method delivers the table, the executable model that generated it, and an alternative model that exposes whether this assignment matters for the specified analysis. A real cohort can test the external accuracy of a frozen choice. The experiment's ability to produce alternatives and its ability to recover measured associations have separate outcomes.

## Questions a reviewer can reasonably ask

**1. What is new relative to classical dependence bounds, LLMSynthor and KMGen?** Bounds for distributions of sums given their margins are classical. Reordering fixed measurements is also classical. LLMSynthor already uses an LLM to infer dependence and generate micro-records that fit aggregate statistics. KMGen already uses an agent to extract published survival evidence, constructs patient data and validates against held-out trials. Our empirically tested contribution is to take a named scientific use, ask an author to construct a second executable dataset satisfying the same source checks, and quantify how much the same estimator changes. We compare the yield of that request to ordinary second authoring and test selected frozen models in measured populations. Describing a new theorem, a general-purpose solver, or the first agent to generate biomedical data would go beyond these results. [Makarov](https://doi.org/10.1137/1126086) · [LLMSynthor](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.14752v4) · [KMGen](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v340/jiang26a.html)

**2. Would a statistician or optimization program find the same alternative?** Yes for several tested uses. In an earlier, separate lipid experiment a blocked assignment solver returned admissible prevalences of 9.375% and 41.406%. In iron, assigning ranks in the same or reversed order inside each Hb band gives exact finite-grid endpoints of −0.427 and 0.928. A new post-outcome deterministic lung control also passed every original check and exhibited 1.18% and 52.46% below 0.70 on the same grids. It was given the already extracted quantities. These finite-grid witnesses establish feasible sensitivity and do not certify a globally optimal span. [Numerical method and independent check](../../work/lung_pairing_numerical_control_2026-11-22/RESULTS.md) They supply no matched estimate of the time saved by automating a new article. The cost of extraction, human setup and expert correction would need prospective measurement before claiming lower effort. [Constructive lipid control](../../work/kdd_v40_pairing_review_2026-11-20/REVIEW.md) · [Iron endpoints](../../work/article_portfolio_2026-11-22/iron/RANK_AUDIT.json)

**3. Did the prompt simply demand a different number?** The desired use and materiality threshold were visible. No direction, target prevalence or measured reference was provided. The submitted program had to use each observed grid value exactly once inside the registered groups and independently pass the population, statistical and physiological checks at three seeds. Requesting a change cannot make an impossible assignment feasible. A material difference establishes the existence of a counterexample under these checks. The matched design tests an instruction effect on that task. It cannot establish that the alternative is more accurate or that the ordinary prompt exhausts possible joint laws. [Exact instructions](../../work/article_pairing_2026-11-22/experiment.py) · [Lung use and checks](../../work/article_lung_pairing_2026-11-22/REQUEST.txt)

**4. Are some counterexamples artificial?** Yes. A post-outcome audit found a median 15.28% of targeted lung records in the band 0.695–0.705 around the 0.70 cutoff, versus 2.63% with the ordinary request. The iron extrema amount to perfectly ordered or reversed ranks inside bands. These are transparent sensitivity scenarios. A target prevalence, a distribution of plausible effect sizes, or clinically realistic frequency near the cutoff requires additional paired evidence. [Lung audit](../../work/article_lung_pairing_2026-11-22/CUTOFF_AUDIT.json) · [Iron constructions](../../work/article_portfolio_2026-11-22/iron/RESULTS.md)

**5. How autonomous is the method, and how much replication is there?** The controlled design supplies a case-specific contract, interpretable operators and marginal families. It measures the effect of giving the agent freedom over pairing after those design decisions. The broader six-session original-paper study allowed the agent to choose distributions as well. In the matched design, 315 recalculated tables came from 105 qualified programs, 36 assigned starts, three reused articles and one GPT configuration. The sequential stopping rule closed the expansion at iron. Seeds quantify numerical repeatability and papers are the units across which scientific generalization matters. The registered per-article results are 0 versus 11, 6 versus 11, and 12 versus 12 successes out of 12. Only lipids gave a small two-sided paired p-value (0.00098). [Scope, failures and denominators](../../work/article_portfolio_2026-11-22/RESULTS.md)

**6. Can this tell us which analysis to trust in a new population?** A feasible alternative warns the user that source evidence leaves a modeling choice. Even a perfectly fixed source mean can miss the target mean after transport. In a prospective Virginia sample, 4/4 advisers who saw alternatives ranked the screening estimate last, compared with 0/4 without alternatives. That use had the largest discrepancy, but all eight and the statistical rule agreed on the top two uses: the first failed its margin and the second was unresolved. Thus sensitivity can reveal a vulnerability, while measured confirmation remains necessary for external use. [All six prospective recommendations and outcomes](../../work/prospective_lipid_use_2026-11-21/RESULTS.md)

## The validation that would most change the claim

A stronger automation claim needs a test of the step that is currently partly hand-designed. Give each constructor a previously unseen article, target population and analysis question. Keep expert-coded units, denominators, adjustment sets, definitions and rounding conventions hidden. Score the agent's extraction and translation against that independent reference, then pass its output and the expert's output through the **same** numerical generator/checker. Record operator errors, eligible artifacts, matched scientific estimates, explicit abstentions, human minutes and API usage. An outcome-independent sample of articles and commitment before authoring would reduce selection bias. This isolates whether the system saves scientific setup effort while preserving the intended analysis. The current manuscript makes no quantified setup-time or arbitrary-article claim. The studies in this release use frozen scientific artifacts.

## Did the agent choose the evidence or receive it?

In the corneal-thickness development case, the agent selected the article, requested the device-agreement table and revised the paired dispersion. It left Table 1 unread. Its model preserved the signed difference and overestimated both absolute instrument means by about 16 µm. The eye-pressure case supplied the original article and froze the models before revealing the 100 measurement pairs. An eligible primary prediction was close to the measured threshold, while both alternatives failed source or plausibility criteria. These observations identify the scope and consequence of two distinct evidence decisions. [Retrieved evidence](../../work/model_source_return_2026-11-22/RESULTS.md) · [Measured comparison](../../work/prospective_article_iop_2026-11-22/RESULTS.md)

A separate registered test combines source selection and held-out evaluation in Chia et al.'s Malaysian data. Five primary generators met the mean BMI-error tolerance. None of the six assigned sessions met the prespecified tail tolerance, and one session produced no program. This supplies a direct counterexample to a general claim that source selection and model authorship suffice to recover an unreported event rate. The observed result concerns the original study and its adult complete-case population.
