# Revision v0.6: scientific utility as the main story

3 November 2026. The user's priority is evidence-guided Agent generation of useful biomedical data. This revision implements that priority in the manuscript rather than initiating another bespoke statistical comparison.

## Changes

1. The title, abstract and introduction lead with the biomedical information gap, Agent model authorship and the data's intended analytical use.
2. The two-case main study retains all six effects, all constructors and their original discrepancy definition. Existing statistical references remain visible and fully reported; they no longer define the paper's success criterion.
3. The complete six-author CHNS L/R experiment is presented as a separate main-text information setting. Both L and R authors appear in the figure. Its 30 supplied summaries are legitimate construction inputs, and summary-assisted reconstruction is a positive result with that scope.
4. Main-study E0/E1 and historical L/R are not pooled. No causal information-by-method interaction or new confirmation is claimed from combining their plots.
5. The later purpose-built statistical calibration is retained as a supplement, including its pre-launch failure/recovery record. Its success does not require another manual-cost experiment or a new custom baseline.
6. Uncertainty, study scope and untested extensions are concentrated in the Methods/captions and limitations. Unfavorable scientific results remain in the main Results where they affect interpretation.

## What changed experimentally

Nothing. This unit reads existing code, source documents and aggregate results and produces manuscript/figure artifacts. It does not reread patient or synthetic individual tables, refit a generator, change an endpoint or add a random seed. The manuscript's newly prominent summary-assisted results were already executed and independently audited.

The public-example feasibility audit is a separate access/compatibility decision. Any actual native-method reconstruction and Agent generation will receive its own compact task and result record before being added to Results.
