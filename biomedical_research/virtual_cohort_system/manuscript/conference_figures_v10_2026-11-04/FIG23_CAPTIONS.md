# Figures 2 and 3: compact conference-width analytical displays

## Figure 2

**Figure 2. Scientific relationships generated under basic and additional relational evidence.** All six prespecified regressions are displayed in native units. Panels (a–c) use CHNS and panels (d–f) use ENSANUT; the response is HbA1c in percentage points (pp). Each panel contains five Agent constructions under E0, five under E1, and one prescribed statistical construction per condition. E0 provides external marginal/basic evidence; E1 additionally provides qualified relationship evidence. Small dots show all three fixed seed draws per construction; open diamonds show their means, and thin vertical segments span the three draws. Within each Agent group, constructions are ordered A01–A05 from left to right; they are not paired between conditions. Across the two cases, the display retains all 24 constructions, 72 final tables and 216 coefficient values. Dashed lines show the previously exposed sample estimates. Gray shading is the CHNS 95% community-cluster CR1-t interval or the ENSANUT empirical cluster-resampling stability band, respectively; these different reference summaries are neither equivalence margins nor common national-survey confidence intervals. Seed variation conditions on each chosen law and does not capture structural or transport uncertainty. All outcomes, including the CHNS BMI discrepancy and conditional-age sign mismatch, remain visible.

## Figure 3

**Figure 3. Accessible target summaries support reconstruction of joint-data analyses.** The separate CHNS information experiment compares external literature alone (L) with literature plus 30 target summaries and accompanying reference metadata (R). The three adjusted regressions and the glucose–HbA1c Pearson and Spearman correlations are shown for all three independently authored constructions per condition and all three fixed seeds: 18 distinct delivered tables and 90 displayed statistics. Dots are seed estimates, open diamonds are constructor means, and thin vertical segments are the ranges across those seeds; within L and R, constructors are ordered 01–03 from left to right. Dashed lines show target summaries, which were construction inputs in R. Gray bands in (a–c) are the available 95% community-cluster CR1-t intervals; no reference interval was available for the two correlations. Initial and final values were identical, so each distinct delivered table is plotted once; the experiment still records 42 physical executions and 24 unique table contents including trials. The separate later statistical-calibration supplement is not pooled into this six-author comparison. These results evaluate supplied-summary reconstruction, not prediction of withheld target quantities.
