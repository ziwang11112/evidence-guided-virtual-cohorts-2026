3 November 2026. This is a synthesis of completed experiments, not a new execution release. The [full draft](DRAFT_v0_7.md) and [figure package](integrated_figures_v07_2026-11-03/FIGURE_PLAN.md) are the current writing artifacts. Earlier study versions, outputs and failures remain unchanged.

## One paper, one scientific purpose

**Use a scientific Agent to turn available biomedical evidence into virtual data that researchers can analyze, when individual measurements are inaccessible, incomplete or absent.**

The empirical center is complete-cohort generation with no individual construction input. Existing enrichment examples show a related use with observed covariates. Directly authored rows are also an implemented historical route; the current principal studies use Agent-authored executable generators. Missing-cell completion remains a supported formulation rather than a newly validated result in this manuscript.

The table is a representation of an explicit joint model. The Agent's meaningful role is to select that model, interpret the numerical meaning of reported evidence, and make the missing structural assumptions visible. Numerical software samples the law. A new research request may lead to a different law; the reusable contribution is the procedure, not one unchanged generator for every analysis.

## The Results narrative

1. **The data product is executable.** Introduce the generated variables and scientific analyses. Keep constructions, seeds, tables and real reference samples distinct.
2. **External study fragments provide useful but incomplete constraints.** Show every main-study endpoint. Discuss the glucose improvement together with CHNS BMI worsening and remaining conditional-effect mismatch.
3. **Accessible target summaries can support coherent reconstruction.** Show the separate L/R study. The R packet legitimately supplies the comparison summaries plus associated metadata; that answers a practical reconstruction question.
4. **Detailed public models permit a more complex data product.** Show the longitudinal HRS example and its original-method reconstruction, with all 16 effects available. The low-HbA1c interaction is reproduced more consistently than the weak upper interaction; the high-value spread remains too narrow.
5. **Explain the scientific decisions and remaining limits.** Numerical use is not automatic identification. Revision can be a no-op. The historical failures show the limits of a particular use rather than being removed from the paper.

These are three separate information settings, not an ordered causal ladder or a pooled accuracy score. HRS supplies its analysis coefficients; CHNS/ENSANUT references were exposed development data. Adding their table counts does not add independent clinical validation.

## What is still missing

The current evidence supports this bounded construction/reconstruction paper and permits substantial drafting now. It does not by itself assure acceptance or a high-impact contribution. [The focused gap review](KEY_EXPERIMENT_GAPS_v07_2026-11-03.md) identifies two optional claim upgrades:

- **Priority 1: prospective external-reference validation.** One previously unexposed, already accessible case with a small, frozen set of analytical uses would strengthen the most important scientific claim: usefulness beyond development references. No revealed case can be relabelled fresh.
- **Priority 2: original-document extraction audit.** Needed only for an autonomous-paper-processing claim. A fixed corpus and source-located adjudication can assess extraction/semantic fidelity without generating another clinical cohort.

Do not add more seeds, a bespoke opponent, a multi-Agent tournament or another revision loop without changing a specific supported claim. Broader biomarkers, calibrated structural/transport uncertainty, arbitrary-query quality, lower human effort and clinical substitution remain limits or future studies unless separately evidenced.
