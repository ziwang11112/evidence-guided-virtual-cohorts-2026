- **Predecessors:** G-Sim's multiple-data/aggregate scope and LLMSynthor's mixed-source/downstream capabilities are acknowledged. The prior turn's fresh v4 reading supports the updated versioned reference; this review did not repeat browser access.
- **Actual source handling:** CHNS generated no source-context tables. Both strict correlation checks remained unavailable; approved equal-weight target proxies supplied the numerical diagnostics. The prose does not claim original-paper extraction certification or general population/assay replay.
- **Scientific uses:** age/sex-adjusted BMI–HbA1c, age/sex-adjusted glucose–HbA1c and age–HbA1c under the specified BMI/glucose/sex adjustment are retained. They are observational projections. Directional agreement does not become quantitative effect estimation, prevalence validation, sample-size accuracy, clinical decision benefit or causal inference.

Attenuated effect magnitudes, compressed variability, retained models and no revision gain remain visible. The artificial illustration remains a separate stipulated translation example with unidentified tails and author/evidence confounding.
- **Stage boundaries:** the new central experiment remains whole-cohort generation. Other observation regimes and direct-row generation retain separate development status. Six unique tables are not relabelled as twelve independent replications.
