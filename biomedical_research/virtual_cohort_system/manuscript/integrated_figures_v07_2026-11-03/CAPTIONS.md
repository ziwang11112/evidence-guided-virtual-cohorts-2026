## Figure 2. Complete analysis-specific results from externally informed virtual cohorts

All six prespecified HbA1c coefficients in the main developmental experiment are shown: CHNS 2009 BMI adjusted for age/sex, glucose adjusted for age/sex, and age adjusted for sex/BMI/glucose (A–C); ENSANUT 2018 age adjusted for sex/BMI/glucose, BMI adjusted for age/sex, and BMI additionally adjusted for glucose (D–F). E0 supplies marginal/basic external evidence; E1 adds qualified relational evidence. All five Agent constructions per case/condition and the prescribed statistical construction S01 are shown. A01–A05 labels across arms are unpaired. S01 is the original main-study construction, not the later summary-fitted statistical supplement.

Small dots are final-seed estimates, vertical marks are Agent means, diamonds are statistical means, and horizontal segments span each construction's three-seed minimum and maximum. These ranges are simulation variation conditional on a fixed law, not confidence intervals. All 24 constructions and 72 final 3,000-row tables contribute across their three analyses (216 seed coefficients). Solid lines mark previously exposed sample-reference estimates. CHNS shading is the selected-sample community-cluster CR1-t 95% interval; ENSANUT shading is its equal eight age/sex-cell cluster-resampling stability band, not a national survey interval. Neither is an equivalence margin or acceptance region. Axes retain each predictor's native scale; populations, estimators, and effect units are not pooled. Negative and attenuated results remain visible.

## Figure 3. Supplied target summaries support reconstruction of several analyses in one table

The separate CHNS information experiment includes all six Agent constructions. L01–L03 received external literature; R01–R03 additionally received the retained target-summary packet, including 30 target statistics and accompanying uncertainty, design, and quality metadata. Panels A–C show the three CHNS regression definitions in Figure 2; D–E show glucose–HbA1c Pearson and Spearman correlations. All five displayed statistics were provided to R, so agreement measures supplied-summary calibration. This historical L/R experiment uses different authors and a different protocol from E0/E1; differences between figures are not a controlled causal contrast.

Dots, vertical means, and horizontal ranges display all three final seeds per author, with 3,000 rows per table: 18 final tables and 90 displayed seed statistics. Author identifiers across L/R are unpaired. Seed ranges are not confidence intervals. Solid lines mark the exposed CHNS reference; A–C retain its community-cluster CR1-t 95% intervals. Correlation reference intervals were unavailable and are not added. The 18 final deliveries duplicated their corresponding initial deliveries and are shown once; the complete study retained six trials plus 36 initial/final deliveries (42 executions, 24 distinct tables overall). Reusing tables across analyses does not create independent clinical replicates. All 30 statistics and the separate statistical calibration supplement remain available in the retained reports.

## Figure 4. A published longitudinal model becomes a complete virtual cohort supporting its scientific analysis

Two prespecified HRS interactions relate memory-score change per decade to the HbA1c spline below 6.5% (left) and at/above 6.5% (right). The Agent-authored recursive generator and corrected published-law reconstruction each supplied three fixed-seed tables, each containing 11,509 synthetic subjects at four visits (0, 2, 4, and 6 years; 46,036 rows). All six tables were analyzed by the same native R/lme4 model. Circles show the 12 fitted seed coefficients; squares show the two exported source coefficients. Horizontal intervals are model-conditional 95% Wald intervals; source intervals use the exported coefficient and standard error. Dashed vertical lines mark the source coefficients; gray solid lines mark zero. These are not the three-seed ranges used in Figures 2–3.

Published aggregate model exports and method code were legitimate construction inputs; this is source-rich implementation and analysis preservation, not prediction of withheld relationships. The lower-branch negative interaction is retained in all Agent seeds. The weaker upper-branch point estimates differ in direction between implementations, and one Agent interval excludes zero while the source interval includes zero; exact significance decisions are not uniformly preserved. Three draws per construction and differing random-effect sampling do not establish a method ranking. The displayed comparator uses the documented technical correction: nine physical generations included three invalid initial comparator tables caused by our metadata parser. No Agent table was regenerated. All 16 effects appear in Figure S1; the complete distribution comparison retains the upper-HbA1c spread mismatch with the original sample. Synthetic rows add no independent clinical evidence.

Asset: `figures/fig4_longitudinal.svg` (PNG counterpart available). All plotted values, standard errors, and interval bounds: `data/hrs_focal_plotted_values.csv`; complete source table: `data/hrs_all_fixed_effects.csv`.

## Figure S1. Every fixed effect in the HRS longitudinal reconstruction

All 16 fixed effects from the same six valid tables in Figure 4 are shown (96 seed estimates). Dots are the three fixed seeds per construction, diamonds are their means (32 means), and horizontal segments are their observed minima/maxima. These ranges are Monte Carlo variation conditional on a fixed law, not confidence intervals. Dashed lines mark the exported source coefficients, which were supplied to both constructions. Each table contains 11,509 synthetic subjects with four visits. This display retains all effects, including unfavorable deviations; it introduces no aggregate winner score and supplies no additional independent clinical evidence beyond Figure 4. “Published law” denotes the corrected comparator, with all three invalid original attempts retained in the incident record. The accompanying 27-field baseline comparison distinguishes agreement with the supplied simulation law from agreement with the published original cohort.

## Provenance

All images are byte-identical copies of existing project figures, assigned v0.7 filenames. `NUMERIC_MANIFEST.json` binds the original assets, source aggregates, original audit records, copied data, build script, and captions. The package builder reads retained aggregate records only. It performs no generation, model fitting, or individual-record analysis.
