# Story and figure audit of the frozen main study

Prepared 3 November 2026. This is an independent editorial and scientific-claim audit for the next manuscript revision. It uses the completed frozen main study; it does not reopen construction, source acquisition, reference analysis, model selection or evaluation. Only this recommendation file was created.

## Recommended paper story

**When individual-level inputs are unavailable, a scientific Agent can translate study-qualified published evidence and a declared analysis request into an executable joint-data generator. The resulting cohorts support ordinary scientific analyses, and additional relational evidence was associated with better quantitative agreement for several requested analyses. The same results show where joint-model completion and transport remain unresolved.**

The positive contribution is a biomedical evidence-to-data method and its demonstrated analytical uses. Its product is a complete virtual table with an inspectable scientific recipe. The Agent selects the joint law, numerical uses of admitted evidence and completion assumptions; numerical software draws the rows. This is more concrete than a proposal to use literature for synthesis, and more scientifically relevant than a record of successful checks. The results also provide an empirical account of what this route can and cannot recover under the tested information conditions.

The central empirical finding is the analysis-specific value of relational information: five of the six prespecified Agent contrasts have smaller constructor-first absolute discrepancy under E1, while CHNS BMI worsens. That result should be followed immediately by effect magnitudes and the conditional-regression counterexamples. It supports useful analytical information in these constructed cohorts, without establishing an acceptable error tolerance, reliable quantitative replacement, or decision benefit.

Suggested title: **Agent-Assisted Construction of Virtual Biomedical Cohorts from Published Evidence for Requested Analyses**. A shorter alternative is **From Published Evidence to Virtual Biomedical Cohorts: Analysis-Specific Benefits and Limits**. Neither title requires an algorithmic-superiority claim.

The v0.4 draft is substantively candid, but it leads its abstract and Results with audit throughput and devotes considerable main-text space to hashes, operation counts and experimental history. Rebalance that emphasis. Start with the missing-data scientific use, explain the evidence-to-law step, and present what the resulting tables permit investigators to estimate. Preserve the audit and unfavorable findings in their appropriate roles.

## Claim hierarchy and innovation assessment

| Level | Claim to carry | Supporting evidence | Boundary that changes its meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary method contribution | An implemented, task-aware procedure constructs complete virtual biomedical cohorts from curated published evidence without individual-level construction inputs. | All frozen constructions delivered analyzable tables; reviewed specifications and programs expose the chosen laws, parameter sources and assumptions. | Patient data existed for evaluator-side reference analyses. Say **without individual-level construction inputs**, not that no individual data were used anywhere in the research. Literature acquisition and packet preparation were researcher assisted. |
| Primary empirical contribution | The value of additional relational evidence depends on the requested analysis. | All six prespecified information contrasts, all constructors and final seeds; E1 is closer on five contrasts and farther on CHNS BMI. | This is descriptive agreement with uncertain selected-sample estimates. It is not a pooled success rate, an equivalence result or an isolated causal effect of a reasoning mechanism. |
| Method explanation | Study-specific statistical meanings can be carried into actual model calculations rather than left as citations. | All ten E1 Agents numerically constrained at least one relation; uptake records distinguish constraints, checks and context and identify completion assumptions. | A numerical-use record does not establish that every source value was matched, that the source population was reproduced, or that the law transports. The scientific operation matters more than the number of records. |
| Scope of analytical use | Delivered tables support several declared regressions, including conditional projections not directly supplied as target coefficients. | CHNS conditional age and ENSANUT conditional BMI are evaluated alongside source-analogous requests. | Their generated values depend on completion assumptions; they are not independent biological discoveries. The three analyses share each case's table and are not independent validations. |
| Task-adaptive design property | The author can choose a probability law for the declared population and analytical request. | Authors received the requested analyses and could choose different laws within the fixed information profile. | The main study does not isolate the benefit of adaptation by holding the packet fixed and experimentally changing only the request. Describe a task-adaptive procedure; do not claim that adaptation itself was shown to improve performance. |
| Supporting reproducibility | Retained outputs, summaries and mappings are numerically reproducible. | The independent audit reports agreement for all retained evaluated statistics; the current aggregate bindings match. | This verifies arithmetic and reporting, not biomedical authenticity, source extraction, transport or external clinical validity. |

**Do the results support innovation?** They support an implemented biomedical methodological contribution with bounded empirical value. They do not establish firstness, a novel synthesis algorithm or general superiority. A new loss function or a mandatory comparison with another Agent is not necessary to state this contribution. Conversely, repeating that the workflow is transparent or that an Agent authored code cannot by itself establish originality against close predecessors. The manuscript should make the concrete scientific decisions visible and complete the outstanding related-work positioning separately; this audit adds no literature search and makes no new priority claim.

The most informative operation to explain is the translation from separately adjusted published projections to a coherent joint law under explicit additional assumptions. Separate regression coefficients are not automatically coefficients of one joint equation. The retained records show choices about covariate distributions, residual covariance, measurement representation and source-to-target transfer. Those choices create the virtual data's unprovided conditional associations. This gives the paper a scientific center without relabelling semantic checks or provenance infrastructure as the main contribution.

## Main Results order

1. **Whole-cohort construction from evidence.** Give one short opening paragraph: two development reference families, all constructions complete, complete tables with no individual-level construction inputs, and all intended analyses available. One sentence can state independent numerical agreement. Do not open with a separate arithmetic-audit subsection or generator timing.
2. **Relational information and the six requested coefficients.** State the five-versus-one direction of the descriptive information contrasts, provide the complete six-row table, and direct readers to the figure showing every constructor. Define constructor-first mean absolute discrepancy once. CHNS BMI must appear in the same paragraph as the favorable count.
3. **Direction, effect size and conditional analyses.** Explain the substantive pattern visible in that figure: CHNS glucose is closer under E1; CHNS BMI remains attenuated and worsens by the declared discrepancy metric; CHNS conditional age retains a negative constructor mean; ENSANUT E1 age is high and both BMI projections are low. Distinguish the source-analogous requests from the two unprovided conditional coefficients. This is the evidence that usefulness has to be judged for each analysis.
4. **Prescribed statistical references.** Report mixed ordering with all comparator results visible. Name CHNS glucose and ENSANUT conditional BMI as cases where the prescribed E1 statistical construction is closer than every corresponding Agent, and ENSANUT age as a converse example. Explain the asymmetric allocation and differing uptake decisions without claiming a causal explanation for their rankings.
5. **What the scientific authors actually did.** Use one compact paragraph on numerical relational use and completion assumptions, linked to a method figure or evidence-use table. Include the absence of feedback-driven scientific revision here: seven Agents retained after feedback and thirteen finalized without exploratory feedback. The interface operated, but this experiment supplies no revision-benefit result.
6. **Developmental evidence in context.** Retain a concise main-text paragraph that the earlier studies include failed quantitative criteria, incomplete relationship use and a targeted revision trade-off. Place their full tables and narrative in a clearly labelled supplement. Preserve their original protocols and unfavorable outcomes; do not pool them with the completed main study or present them as additional confirmations.

An appropriate opening Results paragraph is:

> The frozen procedure constructed complete age, sex, BMI, glucose and HbA1c cohorts from curated external evidence and a target-population request, without individual-level construction inputs. All 20 Agent constructions and four prescribed statistical constructions delivered their three fixed 3,000-row tables, and every scheduled analysis was available. The following results evaluate those delivered tables for the prespecified regressions in two previously exposed development reference families.

An appropriate core result paragraph is:

> Additional relational evidence was associated with smaller constructor-averaged absolute discrepancy for five of the six prespecified coefficients. CHNS glucose discrepancy decreased from 0.134019 to 0.024079 HbA1c percentage points per mmol/L, whereas CHNS BMI discrepancy increased from 0.020380 to 0.031557 percentage points per kg/m². The complete coefficient distributions show why the contrast is analysis specific: directional agreement and closer arm-average estimates can coexist with persistent magnitude differences and an incorrect conditional-association direction in an individual construction.

The abstract can omit the total check count and machine-precision maximum. A brief statement that independent recomputation agreed is sufficient. Preserve the complete numerical audit in reproducibility material. Avoid turning a claim boundary into a repeated disclaimer after every sentence; state the relevant design limits once and retain the actual counterexamples in Results.

## Main figures

### Figure 1: the scientific evidence-to-data method

Use a compact method figure to explain the object being built and the scientific decisions that produce it. Its sequence should be: declared population and requested analyses plus study-qualified evidence; Agent choice of a joint law and explicit completion/transport assumptions; executable generator; complete virtual cohort; ordinary regression analyses and comparison with evaluator-only sample references.

Keep the target reference values outside the construction pathway. Show the researcher role in evidence curation and the numerical runtime role in sampling. Distinguish reported, derived and assumed quantities visually. A concise, traceable example can show that separate source-adjusted projections constrain a model only after additional joint-structure assumptions; do not insert numerical source-to-model equalities unless the exact retained analytic artifact supports them. An illustrative constructor must be labelled as an example of the implementation, never as the best-performing or representative author.

Optional review/retention can be a small side branch labelled as a permitted operation. Do not draw a prominent improvement loop that implies the completed main study demonstrated revision gains. The figure should explain the method rather than enumerate file hashes, checker layers or orchestration steps.

### Figure 2: all six scientific uses, all constructions

The existing `main_scientific_uses` figure contains the right scientific evidence and should remain the principal results figure. Its two case rows and three endpoint columns preserve the complete prespecified analysis set. All five E0 Agents, all five E1 Agents and both statistical constructions must remain visible within every panel. Every final seed must be represented. Do not substitute a best author, narrow the axis to hide unfavorable coefficients, remove the CHNS BMI panel or replace the display with a pooled score.

Recommended presentation changes to a new publication derivative:

- Add panel letters and case labels with readable spacing: “CHNS 2009” and “ENSANUT 2018.” Shorten panel titles to the focal coefficient and state adjustment variables immediately below.
- Use clear group headings or spacing for “E0 Agents,” “E1 Agents” and “Prescribed statistical models.” Keep stable A01–A05 order within each arm. Matching author labels across arms are unpaired; do not connect them with lines.
- Replace raw machine units such as `%/(5kg/m^2)` with “HbA1c percentage points per 5 kg/m².” Preserve every original predictor scale. Different axes across endpoints are appropriate and must not be read as a common accuracy scale.
- Add legend keys for the individual seed, constructor mean, seed minimum–maximum, statistical mean diamond, sample-reference line and uncertainty shading. The current color legend alone does not explain all marks.
- Keep a visible zero reference and the full observed coefficient span. Avoid traffic-light pass/fail coloring. The reference uncertainty region is not a target zone.
- Reduce the oversized overall title and check final-size label readability. The existing vector format is appropriate; inspect the final rendered publication figure as well as the source image.

Caption requirements: include the nonpaired author labels; actual seed ranges rather than confidence intervals; CHNS selected-sample community-cluster CR1-t intervals versus ENSANUT equal-cell cluster-resampling stability bands; different case selections and units; and uncertainty conditional on the fixed law. The shaded bands apply to their own focal reference coefficients, not to the generator's uncertainty and not to unrelated diagnostics.

### Is a third main figure necessary?

No. A second performance figure displaying the same E0/E1 mean absolute discrepancies largely duplicates the six-row main table. If retained for accessibility, it must display every endpoint with native units and preserve all individual constructor summaries and statistical references; no normalized composite, overall rank, post-hoc “acceptable” region or new significance marker should be introduced. A concise method figure plus the complete coefficient figure is a stronger default than several redundant performance figures.

## Overclaims and inferential gaps to remove

| Avoid | Use instead |
|---|---|
| “Reconstructed the real cohort” or “recovered population truth.” | Constructed virtual cohorts and compared requested coefficients with uncertain selected-sample estimates. |
| “No real data were needed for the study.” | No individual-level data were supplied for cohort construction; real-reference data were used by the evaluation process. |
| “Five of six successes” or “83% accuracy.” | Five of six prespecified descriptive information contrasts had smaller discrepancy; list all six values. |
| “More evidence caused better generation.” | The E1 constructions had smaller average discrepancy on the named endpoints; distinct authors, laws and uptake choices prevent isolation of a causal mechanism. |
| “Task adaptivity was validated” as an advantage. | The tested procedure allowed task-specific model construction; its separate incremental benefit was not isolated. |
| “The unprovided association was discovered.” | The completed joint law implied an unprovided conditional projection, whose agreement was evaluated. |
| “All source relationships were faithfully reproduced.” | Numerical use was documented under declared source-context approximations; source replay and transport remained unestablished. |
| “Statistical synthesis was beaten.” | Prescribed statistical and Agent constructions had mixed endpoint-specific ordering under an asymmetric comparison. |
| “Iterative refinement improved quality.” | Review/retention was available, but no feedback-driven scientific revision occurred in the main study. |
| “Independent validation” without a qualifier. | Independent numerical recomputation of retained outputs; the clinical reference families were previously exposed development samples. |
| “Within the reference interval” as evidence of equivalence. | Position relative to the case-specific reference interval or stability band is descriptive uncertainty context. |
| “Fast, low-cost research” based on execution receipts. | Report generator-only elapsed time in reproducibility material; total authoring, curation and review costs were not measured. |

No new experiment is required to implement this manuscript organization. The present result can be reported as a positive, bounded methodological contribution with its failures intact. The available record does not warrant strengthening it into reliable clinical substitution, a general performance advantage or an untouched confirmation study.
