# Focused experimental gaps and draft checklist, v0.13

4 November 2026. This is a scope judgment, not a guarantee of acceptance. The core contribution is an Agent-based procedure for creating complete virtual biomedical cohorts from qualified published evidence, with demonstrated usefulness for selected scientific analyses. It does not require universal superiority over a bespoke generator, additional training, multiple collaborating Agents or direct token-by-token row generation.

**Current decision: proceed with the manuscript.** The completed four-author open-retrieval batch addresses the earlier single-construction workflow limitation. No unresolved shared implementation defect in the reviewed completed units requires wholesale regeneration. Existing mathematical, numerical, source and output checks support the stated bounded implementation claim.

## Key missing evidence, ranked by the claim it would enable

| Priority | Missing experiment | What it could support | Required now? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1, if stronger transport is central | Prospectively freeze a request and assess against a previously unexposed real reference, retaining all analyses and access history | Evidence beyond the two exposed developmental reference families | Required for a new-population generalization claim; not supplied by fresh authors on CHNS/ENSANUT |
| 2, if deeper mechanism evidence is desired | Hold retrieved evidence fixed and vary one declared unsupported dependence or residual-allocation assumption under a small, prespecified scenario set | Which analytical results are robust or sensitive to modeling completion | Optional; earlier conditional-BMI sensitivity already provides one concrete example |
| 3, only if revision is advertised as a benefit | Matched information/budget intervention comparing allowed feedback strategies | An attributable gain from retrieval or scientific revision | Not currently established; all-retain outcomes are reported and no such gain is claimed |

The most valuable extension for a stronger empirical paper is the first row. Before acquiring anything, audit existing data/exposure records for a genuinely eligible unexposed reference. Do not recycle a revealed target as confirmation or choose a case after looking at its effects. This checklist does not launch a new dataset search or experiment.

The smallest optional methodological extension is the second row: select a construction by a fixed identifier rather than reference agreement, define at most two evidence-motivated alternative scenarios before viewing their outputs, retain the original law and every analysis, and report sensitivity rather than choosing a better-fitting winner. Analytical implications can answer the question without generating more rows when available. Unjustified scenario bounds must not be presented as calibrated probabilistic uncertainty.

## Appropriate current limitations

- Source-to-target transport and unreported joint dependencies remain assumptions. Two cohorts and HbA1c-centered tasks do not establish broad biochemical coverage.
- Generated seed ranges and fitted-model intervals omit source, extraction, structure and transport uncertainty.
- ENSANUT residual variation is compressed in the latest constructions; power, interval coverage and clinical decision adequacy have not been validated.
- Open retrieval is bounded and host-dispatched with independent review. Arbitrary-request standalone autonomy and total preparation-time/cost savings are unmeasured.
- Feedback inspection works operationally, but this experiment records retention rather than scientific improvement through revision.

Do not add more same-law seeds, a large model zoo, another Agent layer or an unrelated benchmark merely to increase experiment count. Those additions do not resolve the identified scientific gaps. The current draft should remain centered on useful virtual data and the traceable scientific construction that produces them.
