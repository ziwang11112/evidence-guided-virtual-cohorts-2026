# Main-study Methods text

Working manuscript component, 3 November 2026. Written during construction, before common reference evaluation. This file adds no results and does not alter the frozen release.

## Evidence-guided whole-cohort construction

We evaluated a task-specific procedure for constructing virtual biomedical data when individual-level inputs were unavailable. Each request specified a target population, five variables (age, sex, BMI, glucose and HbA1c), measurement units, sample-selection requirements and three intended regression analyses. The research questions were visible to the scientific author. An Agent received a curated external-evidence packet and chose a joint probability law, its parameters and explicit assumptions for quantities not identified by the packet. The output was executable generator code and a structured model specification, rather than individual-level measurements recovered from a source study. Numerical software sampled the Agent-authored law. Different requests and independent construction contexts could yield different laws.

An evidence record retained the reported population, measurement, units, statistical operator, adjustment set, estimate and available uncertainty. For each admitted relational operation, the author recorded whether it constrained the model, was checked analytically, supplied context or was declined. For a numerical use, the corresponding calculation had to follow from the actual probability law under a declared source-context approximation. In particular, coefficients from separate adjusted regressions were treated as separate projections, not automatically inserted as coefficients of a single joint equation. Completion and transport assumptions remained distinguishable from reported quantities. Fixed scientific parameters did not acquire an uncertainty interpretation merely because a generator used multiple random seeds.

Source-population simulation was not supplied in this allocation. Source-statistic comparisons on generated target tables were therefore unavailable. A versioned semantic checker verified the declared statistical, measurement and role information, while deterministic analytic artifacts and independent review assessed the qualified numerical uses. These checks did not authenticate source extraction, establish transportability or convert an assumed source context into the original study population. Optional exploration supplied generated-table diagnostics rather than target reference answers.

## Cases and information conditions

The two development cases used existing preparations for CHNS 2009 and ENSANUT 2018. Both reference samples had been exposed to the research team in earlier development. They were not treated as untouched confirmations, and a fresh Agent context did not restore clinical independence. No new source or target acquisition was permitted for this comparison. The source packets also overlapped across the two cases.

CHNS construction covered ages 35–74, used glucose in mmol/L and retained the original unweighted selected-sample analytical definitions. Its requested analyses estimated the BMI coefficient in HbA1c regressed on age, sex and BMI; the glucose coefficient in HbA1c regressed on age, sex and glucose; and the age coefficient in HbA1c regressed on sex, BMI, glucose and age. ENSANUT construction covered ages 25–79, used glucose in mg/dL and followed its original fasting, prior-diagnosis and anthropometric selection definitions. Each generated ENSANUT table contained exactly 375 rows in each of eight sex-by-age cells. Its requested uses were the age coefficient in the full age/sex/BMI/glucose model, the BMI coefficient in the age/sex/BMI model and the BMI coefficient in the full model. ENSANUT estimates used the prespecified equal-cell standard; they did not estimate national survey-weighted effects. Units, scaling and reference uncertainty remained case specific.

For each case, E0 supplied external marginal and basic measurement evidence. E1 supplied the same information plus the admitted relational evidence. An author could decline a numerically incompatible operation if the reason was recorded. The comparison therefore assessed the use of additional available information within the procedure; it did not isolate an algorithmic mechanism or require every relationship to be accepted. Target reference values were withheld from construction and revision. Intended analyses were disclosed, including analyses not directly used as numerical calibration objectives. An algebraic implication of supplied information was not presented as a newly discovered relationship.

## Allocation, review and execution

The release allocated five fresh Agent construction contexts per case and information condition, giving 20 Agent constructions. Four additional constructions used one fixed, independently reviewed statistical synthesis specification per case and information condition. The statistical procedure used the same admitted information through source-qualified moment constraints and explicit completion assumptions. The comparison with five independently authored Agent models was descriptive and asymmetric; one prescribed statistical specification was not a representative sample of all statistical synthesis methods.

Each construction delivered three prespecified seeds (5101, 5102 and 5103), each containing 3,000 rows, for 72 scheduled final tables. At most two exploratory row-producing attempts (5201 and 5202) were allowed per construction. Failed attempts consumed the same allowance, and the total ceiling was 120 physical table attempts. Deterministic analytic calculations were retained separately. Each author could use at most one actual feedback-to-scientific-revision cycle, retain the initial model, or finalize without exploratory feedback. Selection did not depend on a favorable clinical comparison. Unexecuted analytic drafting was distinguished from feedback-driven revision.

The coordinator froze candidates before execution, recorded actual author identities and enforced fixed final slots. Independent review checked the implemented probability law, numerical uptake, support and required finite moments before final delivery. The author selected scientific structure and parameters; the coordinator did not supply target-derived coefficients or desired directions. Author tool access was procedurally restricted to approved inputs and their own artifacts. Generator execution used the existing isolated runtime; this did not provide host-level isolation for the author's general tools. Hash-bound artifacts recorded procedural commitments, not cryptographic authentication of scientific truth.

All cases, conditions and final attempts had to close before the common reference evaluation. No favorable author, seed or earlier candidate could replace a final output. Failures and unavailable endpoints remained represented among all scheduled slots. Earlier completed experiments and their negative findings were retained under their original protocols.

## Analysis and reporting

Evaluation reported each prescribed regression coefficient, its direction and discrepancy from the corresponding real-sample estimate, together with 27 CHNS or 28 ENSANUT distribution and joint diagnostics. Real-sample estimates were uncertain references, not known population constants. Their original uncertainty intervals were not treated as equivalence margins. Repeated synthetic rows and seeds characterized model-conditional simulation, not additional clinical observations or independent evidence.

For each analysis separately, the prespecified information contrast was the E1 minus E0 difference in arm means of constructor-level mean absolute discrepancies over the three final seeds. Each constructor's values and seed range were also reported. If a required seed or endpoint was unavailable, complete-arm comparison was unavailable and any available-case description was labelled incomplete. Unlike estimands and units were not pooled into a single accuracy score. The separate statistical constructions were reported alongside the Agent constructions without a general superiority claim.

A separately written implementation recomputed the generated-table statistics using independent regression and weighted-summary arithmetic, checked frozen reference-aggregate mappings and rechecked the constructor-first contrasts. It did not regenerate rows or reopen model selection. The original patient-reference calculations retained their earlier independent audit; final recomputation was not described as a new patient-level reanalysis. Execution receipt timings were recorded separately from unmetered Agent authoring, source preparation and review costs.
