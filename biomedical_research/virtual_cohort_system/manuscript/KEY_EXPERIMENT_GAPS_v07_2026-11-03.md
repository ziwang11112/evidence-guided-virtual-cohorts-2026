# Key experiment gaps for manuscript v0.7

3 November 2026. Independent claim-to-evidence review of v0.6, the completed main study, the HRS result report and manuscript addendum, and the current contribution-led priorities, followed by a read-only check of integrated v0.7. This is a writing decision, not an execution protocol or authorization for another campaign. No generation, fitting, download, paid call, or participant/generated-record inspection was performed for this review.

**Recommendation: write v0.7 now; zero new experiments are necessary for the precise current claim.** The completed HRS example resolves the planned public-method example and adds longitudinal scope. At most two optional experiments would enable distinct stronger claims. Neither is a prerequisite for presenting the completed contribution, and neither requires beating an indefinitely customized statistical generator.

**Claim support does not assure competitive publication.** The existing evidence permits an accurate applied-methods manuscript; the distinctiveness of the contribution, depth of demonstrated utility, reproducibility and fit to a journal remain separate editorial judgments. No experiment requirement for the narrow claim is not a promise that this evidence will satisfy a particular venue or reviewer.

The supported claim is: **An Agent can turn curated, study-qualified biomedical evidence and a specified analysis request into an executable joint model and complete virtual data; the demonstrated analytical agreement depends on the supplied information, with useful reconstruction and identifiable failures.** External-evidence developmental analysis, target-summary reconstruction, and published-model reconstruction are separate results. None demonstrates clinical discovery or a new independent clinical sample.

## What existing aggregate evidence can still answer

The following work needs no new fit, seed, case or raw-table access. It strengthens the current paper without changing the exposure status of any result.

| Question | Existing material and allowed interpretation | What it cannot establish |
|---|---|---|
| Does added relational information improve the requested analyses? | Retain the six main-study endpoint discrepancies, all constructor means and seed ranges, and reference uncertainty. The observed answer is five improvements and one deterioration in this developmental allocation. Explain direction and effect-scale agreement separately. | A general improvement rate, paired-author causal effect, or transferable accuracy guarantee. |
| Which apparent successes use their comparison quantities as inputs? | Classify each reported analysis as an admitted target summary, a consequence of the admitted source model, or a target quantity not directly supplied. CHNS R and HRS primarily support the first two categories; the main study contains unprovided conditional targets. | An unprovided coefficient is not automatically an independent discovery: joint assumptions can determine it, and the target family may already be known. |
| Are weak or uncertain conclusions preserved in HRS? | Existing all-seed estimates, SEs, Wald intervals and source SEs permit a complete descriptive concordance table for all 16 effects. The upper-threshold age interaction already shows a material exception: one Agent interval excludes zero while the source interval includes zero. Preserve that exception alongside the strong below-threshold relationship. | A false-positive rate or known-null test. An interval including zero does not establish that the underlying effect equals zero. Three draws cannot calibrate inferential error. |
| Does source-law reconstruction restore the original distribution? | The retained 27 baseline diagnostics directly answer this. HRS upper-HbA1c mean/SD are about 7.15/0.56 in both implementations, compared with 7.7/1.4 in the reported original sample and 7.2/0.6 in the original paper's synthetic summary. | Full empirical distribution recovery, participant recovery, or resolution of the inherited upper-tail defect. |
| How variable are the completed constructions? | Separate within-law seed variation from between-author variation using existing estimates. Preserve CHNS reference intervals, ENSANUT stability bands and HRS model-conditional SEs with their original meanings. | Coverage for source estimation, model choice or population transport. Between-author spread is descriptive and may miss shared bias; seed ranges and synthetic model SEs are not total epistemic uncertainty. |
| Did review or revision improve the method? | Main-study retention decisions and identical initial/final outputs in the six-author experiment establish zero observed additional-stage improvement. The separate historical revision pilot documents a dependence-versus-regression trade-off. | A general benefit of iterative revision, or a need to run another revision campaign merely to obtain a positive result. |

No post-hoc equivalence margin should convert the existing discrepancies into a pass rate. Reporting interval compatibility is descriptive; neither overlap with a reference interval nor a source nonsignificant result is proof of equivalence. The HAALSI historical zero-anchor result also warns that a permissive resolution can admit an uninformative construction; it is an evaluation-limit example, not a calibrated known-null experiment.

## Uncertainty and known nulls: keep the claim narrow

The current data support conditional analytical results and transparent assumptions. They do **not** support calibrated inference about new biomedical relationships. Recomputing existing aggregates cannot supply the missing source-parameter covariance, plausible alternative model distribution, or transport uncertainty.

If the future priority-1 use is purely exploratory, it can remain an estimate-plus-sensitivity exercise with explicit abstention when the evidence leaves a conclusion unstable. Its protocol should require that declaration before target release. A truly known-null check needs a controlled data-generating law with an effect fixed at zero; the HRS weak interaction is not such a null. If an inferential reliability claim later becomes essential, a prespecified truth-known null/alternative fixture and enough independent source-estimation repetitions to assess the chosen error or coverage rate would be needed within that focused extension. Three sampler seeds, a single real-reference comparison, or agreement among authors would not suffice. That larger inference claim is **excluded from v0.7**, so this is not a third requested experiment or a drafting gate.

## Stop rule and immediate manuscript action

Complete v0.7 using the existing evidence. The HRS comparison should be presented as an Agent reconstruction of a published family, with the nine-attempt ledger, three invalid initial comparator tables, six retained valid comparisons, conditional-GLS audit and inherited distribution mismatch disclosed. These are two constructions in one example, not six independent clinical validations.

Keep the main-study source-to-target results developmental, label the CHNS supplied-summary results as reconstruction, and remove stale statements that the predecessor comparison is still pending. Do not reopen CHNS/ENSANUT or HRS to improve their revealed numbers. No additional bespoke comparator family, factorial suite, new disease domain, larger virtual cohort, multi-Agent tournament or retrieval benchmark is needed to support the stated contribution. A result that is merely known to a fresh author remains known to the project.

If a stronger claim is chosen later, choose **one** of priorities 1 and 2 according to the claim being added. Priority 1 contributes more directly to scientific analytical usefulness; priority 2 addresses workflow autonomy. Do not make both a prerequisite for this paper, and do not substitute either for unmeasured clinical validity.
