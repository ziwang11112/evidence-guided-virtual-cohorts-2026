# Historical developmental results accompanying manuscript v0.5

Working supplement, 3 November 2026. This text preserves the historical developmental findings reported in manuscript v0.4 while moving their detailed account outside the main paper. The original experiments, protocols, outputs and unfavorable findings remain unchanged. These studies used different information regimes and method versions; they are not pooled with the frozen two-case main study or counted as independent confirmations of its results.

## Earlier CHNS construction

The earlier CHNS construction retained all three requested association directions, but BMI coefficients were approximately 30–39% of the observed reference estimate and glycemic variability was reduced. These quantitative limitations remain part of the development record. See the [original CHNS case results](https://github.com/ziwang11112/evidence-guided-virtual-cohorts-2026/blob/26ccdfb5a77c665ddce1d1c0c73ce991b7107fb3/biomedical_research/virtual_cohort_system/reports/chns_admission_2026-11-01/f2/CASE_RESULTS.md).

## Wave A: HAALSI glucose and Turkana lipids

Wave A evaluated HAALSI glucose and Turkana lipids with ten fresh Agent constructions and two prescribed statistical constructions. All 84 scheduled source/target tables executed, and 6,704 independent comparisons agreed. The frozen positive criterion failed: three of five Agent constructions passed the HAALSI case and none passed the Turkana case at the specified resolution. The HAALSI zero anchors also passed that resolution, limiting its discrimination. Those results did not support general quantitative usefulness or comparative superiority. No Wave B was started. See the [frozen Wave A results](https://github.com/ziwang11112/evidence-guided-virtual-cohorts-2026/blob/26ccdfb5a77c665ddce1d1c0c73ce991b7107fb3/biomedical_research/virtual_cohort_system/reports/scaleup_2026-11-01/production_release/RESULTS.md).

## Historical marginal-versus-relational comparison

A completed CHNS marginal-versus-relational evidence comparison failed its overall joint-improvement criterion. A construction audit found predominantly qualitative relationship use and infinite population moments in seven proposed laws. Finite-table results were retained. Later mathematical repairs and audits did not retrospectively turn these historical failures into positive results. See the [original benchmark results](https://github.com/ziwang11112/evidence-guided-virtual-cohorts-2026/blob/26ccdfb5a77c665ddce1d1c0c73ce991b7107fb3/biomedical_research/virtual_cohort_system/reports/existing_data_benchmark_2026-11-02/RESULTS.md).

## Single-author reference-assisted revision pilot

A separate single-author reference-assisted pilot generated nine distinct tables. A dependence revision improved rank agreement in all three paired seeds, while regression absolute discrepancies increased slightly. This targeted trade-off is retained as a separate observation; it does not establish a general revision benefit. See the [original adaptive-reference pilot results](https://github.com/ziwang11112/evidence-guided-virtual-cohorts-2026/blob/26ccdfb5a77c665ddce1d1c0c73ce991b7107fb3/biomedical_research/virtual_cohort_system/reports/adaptive_reference_pilot_2026-11-03/RESULTS.md).

## Six-context information-and-revision experiment

The subsequent information-and-revision experiment used six fresh contexts to generate 3,000-row CHNS cohorts. Three received the request, target description and external literature; three additionally received all thirty existing CHNS reference statistics. This was an explicit information-provision experiment on an already exposed development case. Every author used one initial trial and delivered three fixed seeds, then reviewed permitted diagnostics and selected a final revised or retained version. Initial delivery was allowed feedback-bearing trials, so its stage comparison concerned an additional review opportunity, rather than no feedback versus feedback.

All 42 executions succeeded: six trials and thirty-six initial/final deliveries. All six authors retained their scientific models, so eighteen final tables exactly duplicated their paired initial tables. There were 24 distinct tables overall and eighteen distinct delivery tables. Independent recomputation matched all 1,260 statistics, with maximum numerical difference 7.11 × 10⁻¹⁵. All three focal coefficients were positive in every distinct delivery table. Table H1 preserves the quantitative information-regime comparison. No renewed infinite-moment defect was identified in its six laws; that supported mathematical consistency, not biological authenticity.

Reference-assisted constructions were closer to the supplied quantities, establishing calibration consistency under admitted information. Literature-only constructions retained directions but attenuated the BMI projection and overstated the rank association. Six retention decisions, unchanged code/specifications and eighteen identical paired output hashes established exactly zero additional-stage numerical change. ## Relationship to the frozen main study

The main study subsequently required qualified numerical-use records and finite-moment review, and all ten E1 Agent constructions used at least one admitted relation as a model constraint. That later implementation does not erase earlier qualitative-use or moment failures. Its own delivered results remain mixed: CHNS BMI discrepancy increased, one CHNS conditional-age mean was negative, ENSANUT magnitude mismatches persisted, two E1 statistical comparisons favored the prescribed statistical construction over every corresponding Agent, and no feedback-driven scientific revision occurred. The main study's descriptive information contrasts include only its 24 constructions and 72 final tables. Historical table counts, source checks and revisions are not added to those denominators.
