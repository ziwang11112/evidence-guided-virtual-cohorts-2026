# Which Scientific Analyses Can Agent-Generated Biomedical Cohorts Support?

KDD Research working manuscript v0.39, 20 November 2026. [PDF for external reviewers](external_review.pdf) · [Anonymous manuscript](paper.pdf) · [Anonymous Overleaf project](overleaf_v39.zip) · [External review Overleaf project](overleaf_external_review_v39.zip)

Figure 1 follows the threshold cells from an evidence sample, the agent's choice of continuous pairing and the result of changing it. A fourth panel shows what happened when six fresh agents and a statistical law with nonzero dependence received the same Chinese evidence and the same virtual marginal values. In 603 mothers in Akwa Ibom, Nigeria, measured Hb–ferritin rank correlation was 0.010 (95% interval −0.064 to 0.086), compared with 0.308 from the agents and 0.303 from the statistical generator. The shared virtual threshold odds ratio was 5.39, compared with 1.20 measured. The six agents independently chose the same copula family as the comparator. Table 2 shows the separate, retrospective CHNS re-pairing comparison. Appendix Table 7 reports every new constructor, the statistical generator and the measured Nigerian sample. The contributions are the traces from source evidence to executable pairs, an analysis-specific attribution check and a prospective test of transport. The new experiment found no practical advantage over the statistical generator.
