# Which Scientific Analyses Can Agent-Generated Biomedical Cohorts Support?

KDD Research working manuscript v0.40, 20 November 2026. [External review PDF](external_review.pdf) · [Anonymous PDF](paper.pdf) · [Overleaf source](overleaf_v40.zip) · [Overleaf source with review links](overleaf_external_review_v40.zip)

**Figure 1, page 2.** An iron-status article supplies four counts. They determine a threshold odds ratio of 6.65. The agent still chooses which continuous ferritin measurement belongs with each hemoglobin measurement. Seven generators that passed the source checks produced odds ratios of 6.55 to 6.86 and continuous rank correlations from −0.272 to 0.446. The two line matchings in the middle panel illustrate the operation. The dots on the right show the seven program outputs. **Figure 2, page 3.** The Kenyan lifestyle study supplies a practical test. The same age- and sex-adjusted regressions run on six virtual tables and the measured records. For the urban versus pastoralist BMI contrast, the real–real mean absolute discrepancy was 0.316 kg/m². Across the six virtual tables the virtual–real values ranged from 0.314 to 0.428 kg/m². The same tables underestimated the rural versus pastoralist cholesterol contrast by 7.80 to 9.54 mg/dL. All nine contrasts appear in the appendix. [Matched-size analysis](../../work/matched_size_utility_2026-11-17/RESULTS.md) · [Numerical inputs](../../work/matched_size_utility_2026-11-17/CONTRAST_SUMMARIES.csv)

**Figure 3, page 7.** A fresh agent and a statistical generator received identical published Tanzanian summaries and separate lipid values. The agent chose the same family of pairing model independently. Both methods estimated mean non-HDL of 128.77 mg/dL from the supplied means. They estimated that 25.53% and 25.52% would reach 160 mg/dL. The measured proportions were 68/321 in Mekelle and 244/1,164 at Haramaya University. Under the prespecified ±2 percentage point planning tolerance, Mekelle remained unresolved and Haramaya was outside tolerance. The two study samples are independent recruitment frames within Ethiopia.
