# Figure 1. The scientific agent's construction loop

Start at the research request and published evidence. The agent authors a generator and records the assumptions that connect the study findings to the requested population. The purple path takes that candidate to an isolated worker. The blue return arrow carries permitted trial summaries or supported source checks back to the author. The author may revise within the study budget or settle on a program. The upper branch freezes the selected generator and samples the virtual cohort. The measured reference enters the downstream comparison after the tables are sealed in the illustrated protocol. The controller manages requests, permissions, budgets, versions and seeds. Section 2.2 describes its role.

The program route accepts both curated sources and, under applicable protocols, bounded author-directed literature search. Q5 returned generated diagnostics and marked unsupported source checks unavailable. The iron pilot also calculated published statistics in the source context. These two uses of feedback have different evidential interpretations.
