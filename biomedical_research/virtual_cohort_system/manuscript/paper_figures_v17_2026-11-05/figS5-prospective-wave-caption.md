**Figure S5. Scientific analyses of complete virtual cohorts generated from external evidence in Wave A.** Rows separate the prospectively admitted HAALSI glucose target from Turkana, where investigators had encountered related study findings before the six constructions were frozen. Each panel shows three fixed draws for each of five agent laws (A01–A05) and a prescribed statistical law (S01). Points follow seed order 2101–2103 from top to bottom within each law. Short segments span the three draws. Dashed lines mark measured-sample estimates. Shading shows approximate 95% HC3 working sensitivity intervals for five regression effects. The residual correlation has no reference interval. Table S7 gives estimates in their native units and the frozen case decisions.
