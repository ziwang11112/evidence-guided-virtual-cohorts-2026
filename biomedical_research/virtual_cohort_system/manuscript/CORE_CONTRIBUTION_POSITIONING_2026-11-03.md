# Core contribution and primary-literature positioning

Prepared 3 November 2026. Literature access date: **2026-11-03**. This bounded manuscript audit uses the completed main study, not a proposed extension. It does not establish field-wide priority. No generators, experiments, reference analyses or frozen artifacts were changed.

## Recommended scientific claim

**An Agent-assisted procedure can turn curated, study-qualified biomedical evidence into executable whole-cohort generators without individual-level construction inputs; its analytical value is specific to the requested analysis and remains incomplete even when quantitative evidence use is documented.**

The defensible contribution combines an applied methods procedure with an empirical characterization of its benefits and limits. The present study does not establish a new general synthesis algorithm. That distinction does not reduce the work to engineering: interpreting what an adjusted biomedical estimate can legitimately constrain is a scientific modelling task. Its value must come from an identifiable operation and demonstrated consequences, rather than the novelty of using an Agent, code, synthetic rows or feedback.

Suggested title: **From Published Biomedical Evidence to Virtual Cohorts: An Agent-Assisted Procedure and Its Analysis-Specific Limits**. The existing v0.4 title is also defensible. Avoid a title promising accurate reconstruction, autonomous discovery or iterative improvement.

The motivating problem remains **whole-cohort construction with zero individual-level input**. A researcher specifies a population and intended analyses, while accessible articles contain only fragments of the relevant distribution. The output is an explicit joint law, executable sampler, virtual table and evidence-use record. Indicator augmentation and missing-cell completion are separate information regimes, not replacements for this central problem. Here, “zero individual input” describes construction access; real individual data were used to establish the earlier reference evaluations, and both case families were already exposed to the research team.

The practical reason to produce a table is that investigators can execute familiar analyses and inspect implications of a completed joint model. A generated table does not create new clinical observations. Its inferential content remains limited by admitted evidence and completion/transport assumptions. Intended analyses may guide construction; universal reuse of one table across arbitrary questions is not established.

## Three contribution candidates, graded against the completed work

These candidates should form one coherent paper, not three inflated novelty claims. Candidate 1 describes what was built; candidate 2 supplies the scientific result; candidate 3 establishes what readers can verify. The method's relevance follows from an actual biomedical information bottleneck. Its present evidence supports a methods-and-evaluation paper more naturally than a claim of a general learning algorithm breakthrough. No venue or acceptance outcome follows from this assessment.

## Evidence that must stay in the main story

The main study completed **24 constructions and 72 final 3,000-row tables**, including 20 Agent and four statistical constructions. The independent audit verifies retained-output arithmetic, not biology. Fresh authors do not turn two exposed reference families into 20 independent clinical validations.

The central finding should be reported as six separate estimates in their own units. E1 reduced constructor-first mean absolute discrepancy for five of six prespecified coefficients, while CHNS BMI increased from **0.020380 to 0.031557 HbA1c percentage points per kg/m²**. One CHNS E1 conditional-age construction retained a negative mean. ENSANUT E1 age coefficients overshot and BMI coefficients undershot their sample references. These results distinguish directional agreement from effect-magnitude agreement.

The statistical E1 constructions were closer than every corresponding Agent for CHNS glucose and ENSANUT conditional BMI. Other orderings favored Agents. One prescribed specification against five independently authored models is descriptive, and equal available packets do not imply identical numerical evidence use. Neither method family has established general superiority.

Seven Agents retained their initial models after exploratory feedback; thirteen finalized without exploratory feedback. All selected models were initial models. A feedback interface is therefore an implemented option, **not an observed mechanism of improvement**. Source-population replay was not available, so numerical uptake must never be relabelled as successful reproduction of original studies.

The appropriate lead figure shows every construction and final seed against each uncertain sample reference, with statistical constructions visible. A method figure should explicitly connect source conditions, numerical roles, completion assumptions, the executable joint law and requested analyses. It should not depict successful source replay, calibrated transport uncertainty or a revision loop as demonstrated results. The uptake inventory is most informative alongside the discrepancy panels: it shows why using a source numerically and producing an analytically adequate table are different claims.

## Known unknowns and prohibited shortcuts

- **Priority remains bounded.** This targeted comparison does not exhaust aggregate calibration, indirect inference, statistical matching, population synthesis or biomedical evidence synthesis. Absence of an identical pipeline in five papers is not evidence of firstness.
- **No comparable-input predecessor experiment exists here.** The review identifies overlap; it does not measure performance against G-Sim, LLMSynthor or SynthIPD. No such experiment is authorized by this note.
- **Source fit and transport remain unresolved.** Neither declared operator compatibility nor citation validity proves that an estimate was correctly extracted, fitted simultaneously with others, or transported across populations and measurement systems.
- **Uncertainty remains incomplete.** Three seeds describe sampling from a fixed scientific law. They do not supply source-estimation, structural or transport uncertainty. Sample-reference intervals/stability bands are not equivalence margins.
- **Input provenance matters.** Article-only construction, summaries specially estimated from source records, and supplied target macros are distinct information resources. They must not be pooled under the ambiguous phrase “no real data.”
- **Current software availability was not re-audited.** Earlier local repository observations remain dated observations; this note does not newly assert that an official predecessor implementation is absent or reproduce its results.

Avoid: “first biomedical literature-to-data system,” “reconstructs patient measurements,” “recovers the true joint population,” “discovers new biological relationships,” “automatically validates literature,” “general evidence compiler,” “clinically interchangeable,” “causally valid,” “iteratively improves,” “superior to statistical methods,” and “saves recruitment costs.” Each exceeds the present evidence. Do not replace them with an implication that applied methods need a novel optimizer to be scientifically useful.
