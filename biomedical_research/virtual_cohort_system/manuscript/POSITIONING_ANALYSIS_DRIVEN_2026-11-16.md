# Positioning proposal: analysis-driven biomedical cohort generation

16 November 2026 local. Editorial proposal based on manuscript v0.32 and the closed PNS/iron evidence-content experiments. This document does not change the manuscript, frozen methods, data or results.

## Recommended identity

**From Fragmented Evidence to Analyzable Biomedical Cohorts with Scientific Agents**

One-sentence pitch:

> We study how scientific Agents turn a research request and fragmented published evidence into an executable joint data model, and which scientific analyses the resulting virtual records can support.

Keep the virtual-data product central. The task covers whole-cohort generation without target individual measurements, as well as related enrichment/completion settings. Distinguish the validation of those information regimes. The current strongest programmatic route lets the Agent author the scientific law and lets numerical tools sample it.

The method is an analysis-driven scientific modeling workflow. Its intended output is usable records plus an inspectable model and evidence/assumption record. Do not turn the main paper into a generic failure benchmark or claim that a workflow component must beat an ordinary Agent to justify the task.

## The problem that connects the method and results

Biomedical analysis often requires a joint distribution that no available paper reports in full. A requested adjusted regression, paired association, joint-threshold analysis or repeated-visit contrast needs different relationships. Available studies may report marginal distributions, differently adjusted coefficients, selected subgroups or partial joint tables. Their populations and measurement definitions also differ.

The Agent must translate these statements into model constraints, compose compatible information, and choose explicit assumptions for what remains unspecified. All resulting variables must coexist in one executable law for a given delivery. A different research request can legitimately produce a different law. Freeze is an evaluation boundary within an experiment, not a permanent restriction on user requests.

The scientific question is: which analyses become usable when an Agent constructs this joint law from the available evidence, and what changes when the evidence includes the relevant joint relationship?

## Method presentation: three substantive responsibilities

| Responsibility | Concrete implemented behavior | Artifact to show |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Interpret evidence for the requested analysis | Identify population, measurements, units, statistical definition and adjustment set; inspect missing relationship information; retrieve when the task permits it | One request and two or three source records with explicit study conditions |
| Construct an executable joint model | Agent chooses distribution families, dependence, numerical parameters and transport assumptions; derives quantities where the available information permits | Agent-selected model components and one short source-to-parameter walkthrough |
| Deliver and assess analyzable records | Isolated tools sample the Agent program; mathematical, physiological and supported statistical checks inspect it; the same analysis implementation is applied to virtual and reference records | Data table, requested-analysis panels and source/assumption links |

Retrieval, inspection and revision are available actions. The new content-control experiments deliberately hold evidence and calls fixed to test information content. They are not full autonomous retrieval episodes. Original-source admission included root-led review. Show these execution variants rather than depicting every experiment as the same fully automatic loop.

Do not prescribe a copula, correlation, expected sign or number of Agents in the method figure. Those are choices made in individual constructions. A single scientific Agent can exercise the relevant capabilities.

## Three paper contributions

1. **A concrete scientific data-generation task and working implementation.** Given a population, variables, intended analyses and permitted evidence, an Agent authors a joint generative program and delivers records that existing analysis software can consume. Whole-cohort generation can start with no target individual input. This is a task/system contribution; it is not a claim to have invented summary-statistic synthesis.
2. **Evidence composition for requested analyses.** Controlled mathematical tasks demonstrate composition under compatible constraints, and biomedical evidence-content interventions test whether adding relevant joint statistics improves a prespecified analysis beyond marginal-summary additions. This is the strongest current experimental contribution.
3. **Analysis-level utility with inspectable scientific assumptions.** Report scientific estimates and their complete profiles alongside the source-to-model record. Traceability helps explain adopted assumptions; it is not a calibrated confidence score or proven predictor of error.

These contributions support an AI-for-science system and empirical study. A standalone new optimization algorithm, universal quality advantage or fully validated transport method is not established.

## Evidence map and reading order

| Reader question | Main evidence | What to retain beside the useful result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Can the delivered data support a recognizable biomedical analysis? | Lifestyle case: same Methods-guided analysis on real and virtual tables; urban BMI contrast 1.700–2.251 versus 1.882 kg/m² | All nine contrasts, weaker lipid effects and dispersion; source-rich reconstruction label |
| Can the Agent combine information rather than only reproduce individual summaries? | Two artificial composition tasks; 24 constructions across four information conditions | Only identified functionals are scored against identified values; artificial populations are not clinical validation |

| What determines the remaining discrepancies? | Source-to-model walkthroughs, declared-law implications, frozen programs and independent recomputation | Parameter provenance is not proof of source applicability; common seeds/convergent laws are not independent evidence |

The PNS and iron comparisons are separate within-case interventions. Their BASE packets and discrepancy scales differ. Do not average their relative improvements into one quality score. Both references were already exposed; new constructor contexts and prospective freezes do not create new clinical holdouts.

## Storyline and figure plan

Open with a scientist who has a research question and published evidence but lacks a complete measurement table. Explain the requirement to construct the relationships needed by that analysis. Introduce the Agent's scientific decisions and the data product. Show a concrete analysis carried out on the output, then test how evidence content changes its usefulness. End with the source/assumption map explaining the observed boundaries.

Recommended figure roles:

1. **Overall method.** Research request at the left; an Agent workspace in the center containing source records, joint-model choices and the executable sampler; virtual records and familiar analysis outputs on the right. Provenance connects source facts to model components. Checks/revision appear as a supporting path. No case-specific formula is presented as the universal algorithm.
2. **Scientific use.** One familiar real-versus-virtual analysis with all prespecified contrasts, showing useful and discrepant patterns in the same graphic.
3. **Composition capability.** The two artificial tasks, with identifiable full-evidence quantities distinguished from underidentified scenarios.
4. **Evidence-content experiments.** PNS and iron shown as separate rows of small multiples, with all prespecified core analyses. Highlight the requested relation's benefit while retaining adjacent trade-offs.
5. **A worked modeling decision, if space warrants.** Reported statistic, valid derivation, adopted transport assumption and resulting analysis. Do not invent a calibrated confidence score or a trust card that has not been prospectively validated.

Use the completed joint-block controls for the primary numerical claims. The earlier two-arm PNS search follow-up can show the retrieval episode and motivate the control; do not use its larger 92.89% change as a substitute for the more controlled 78.58% result.

## Suggested abstract

Biomedical researchers often have published statistical evidence but lack the individual measurements needed for a planned analysis. We study scientific Agents that translate a population description, requested variables and intended analyses into an executable joint data-generating model. The Agent interprets study-conditioned evidence, selects distributions and dependence structures, and records which choices are reported, derived or assumed; numerical tools sample the model and perform available checks. We evaluate the resulting virtual cohorts using the same scientific estimators as measured references. A lifestyle application demonstrates selected analysis reconstruction, while two controlled tasks test the composition of complementary statistics. In separate three-condition biomedical experiments, adding joint evidence beyond marginal summaries reduced residual-correlation discrepancy by 78.6% in blood pressure and log-odds-ratio discrepancy by 46.8% in iron status. Improvements were analysis-specific: blood-pressure slopes did not improve, and iron joint prevalence and continuous rank association became less close to the measured sample. Source-to-model inspection connects these trade-offs to transported margins and assumed dependence structures. These studies demonstrate targeted scientific uses of Agent-authored virtual data and the value of relevant joint information, while distinguishing source consistency from validity in a target population.

This is proposed wording, not a new submitted manuscript or a comprehensive superiority claim.

## Near-neighbor positioning, checked 16 November 2026

- [G-Sim, ICML 2025](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/holt25b.html): LLM structural simulator proposals and iterative empirical parameter calibration are already present. Program authorship or a feedback arrow alone cannot carry the distinction.
- [LLMSynthor](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.14752v3): the paper targets aggregate-aligned micro-record synthesis and includes dependency inference and discrepancy-guided proposals. Do not claim that it lacks joint relationships or that evidence-only generation is unique.
- [KMGen](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22618): the abstract describes agentic KM extraction and a mechanistic IPD sampler combining trial-derived archetypes with survival/adverse-event processes. Agentic biomedical generation is an existing category. This turn checked the abstract, not its full implementation.

Our defensible emphasis is the combination of requested biomedical analyses, Agent-authored model structure, study-conditioned evidence use, and empirical tests of which joint information supports those analyses. This is a positioning argument, not a verified claim that no prior work has that combination. Novelty should be stated in terms of the concrete task, implementation and demonstrated findings.

## Submission-facing decisions

For the intended KDD/AI-for-science direction, lead with the scientific modeling problem, the executable capability and the evidence-content experiment. Put implementation controls and historical no-change results in a concise reproducibility section and complete supporting record. Do not conceal adverse prespecified endpoints.

The remaining publication-risk question is whether this task/system plus the controlled findings is sufficiently distinctive and broad for the chosen track. Rewording cannot resolve that by itself. A selected near-neighbor comparison, a structured biomedical assessment of intended use, or an independent-target test would support different stronger claims; choose one only after fixing the desired claim. This proposal does not launch another grid, source search or paid run.
