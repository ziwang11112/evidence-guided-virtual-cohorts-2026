## Core story supported by completed work

The method connects a biomedical research request and study-qualified published evidence to an Agent-authored joint generating law and reusable virtual tables. Whole-cohort generation without participant-level construction inputs is directly exercised. Source interpretation, numerical use and assumptions can be inspected. Existing statistical families do the sampling; Agent decisions connect the evidence to those families.

## Additional experiments depend on the intended claim

The completed results are sufficient to start and review a scoped methods/capability draft. They are not a guarantee of acceptance, and novelty remains a judgment against the actual contribution and adjacent literature.

1. **For reliable quantitative use in a new population:** predeclare a previously unexposed real reference, the intended estimands and uncertainty interpretation before construction. Existing exposed targets cannot become that test through more seeds or a fresh Agent context.
2. **For a component-level mechanism claim:** use a fixed-evidence controlled contrast that changes only the proposed component. The current six different laws differ in several choices, so their differences cannot identify one causal mechanism.
3. **For calibrated scientific uncertainty or power calculations:** separately evaluate source, structural and transport uncertainty. Monte Carlo seed ranges and current conditional intervals are insufficient.
4. **For broad automatic request coverage or time savings:** test a declared request set and measure complete preparation/review costs. The 35.83 seconds of adapter time excludes scientific authorship and review and is not an end-to-end speed result.

These are claim-specific options, not a mandatory new benchmark. The next decision should follow scientific review of the integrated paper. Current outputs may serve transparent, assumption-dependent relationship exploration and workflow inspection; they do not replace participant measurements or establish a universally accurate synthetic population.
