**Figure 2. Age-by-HbA1c interactions in the HRS longitudinal reconstruction.** Diamonds show source coefficients supplied to both constructions. Circles and squares show mixed-model estimates from three draws of each law (seeds 6101–6103, top to bottom), each with 11,509 synthetic subjects at four visits. Bars show approximate 95% Wald intervals from the exported source standard errors or the generated-table fits. Below 6.5% HbA1c, all six generated estimates are negative. At or above 6.5%, the source estimate is +0.0129 and its interval includes zero. All three agent estimates are positive and all three reconstructed-law estimates are negative.
