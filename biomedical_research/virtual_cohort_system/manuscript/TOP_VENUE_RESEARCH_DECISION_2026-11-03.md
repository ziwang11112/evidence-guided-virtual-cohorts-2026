# Research decision: strengthen the data-generation capability before selecting a venue

3 November 2026. **Status: literature- and result-informed design decision. No new experiment executed or released.** The empirical v0.8 manuscript, source records, public reproduction inputs and previously revealed references remain unchanged.

## Decision and scope

Prioritize one use-inspired AI-method capability test before deciding whether a stronger conference paper is realistic: **use the intended scientific analysis to select additional, source-qualified joint evidence, then turn that evidence into more useful virtual data.** Retain the current manuscript as the record of completed construction and reconstruction experiments. PLOS Digital Health is a journal and an existing fallback preparation route, not the chosen top-conference destination.

The product remains generated biomedical data: a complete cohort without individual input, enrichment of observed variables, or missing-measurement completion. A user can disclose the intended analysis. The Agent may adapt its generator to that request. Multiple Agents, training, a new loss, or universal superiority over a bespoke statistical generator are not prerequisites.

This proposal is not a claim that evidence acquisition is new. It asks whether a specific evidence-to-data operation solves an observed limitation better than competent finite alternatives. Favorable controlled results would justify further investigation, not establish biomedical transport or top-venue readiness.

## What the closest methods already do

The [four-paper neighbor audit](TOP_VENUE_NEIGHBOR_AUDIT_2026-11-03.md) inspected methods, evidence requirements and evaluations, rather than treating an Agent label as novelty.

- G-Sim already combines LLM-authored simulators with calibration and diagnostic feedback, including fragmented observations and discussion of observational equivalence. [Published paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/holt25b.html).
- LLMSynthor already includes missing joint-statistic identification, constraint selection/expansion and request-conditioned generation. Adding retrieval or dynamic requests alone is too weak a distinction. [Methods](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.14752v4), [official KDD listing](https://kdd2026.kdd.org/papers/).
- SPADA uses LLM-induced dependencies with numerical conditional generators. SynthIPD constructs covariate-bearing data from published material. Neither should be reduced to a straw-person hard-coded baseline. [SPADA](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.525/), [SynthIPD](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.16466v2).

**Additional root audit — ModelSMC.** The published ICML 2026 paper represents executable models as particles, combines LLM proposals/refinement with likelihood-based weighting, and studies alternatives under limited identifiability. The inspected algorithm takes observed data and context as inputs; external literature-item acquisition is not an explicit step there. That does not imply the framework could not be extended to it. Multiple candidate models, weighting or uncertainty language therefore cannot be our residual contribution. Sections 3.2–3.3, Algorithm 1, 4.6 and 5 were inspected; no code reproduction or full theorem audit was performed. [Official publication](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/wahl26a.html), [full methods, v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.18266v2).

The possible distinction is narrower: select a genuinely observed statistic for its relevance to ambiguity in the requested analysis; execute its actual population, measurement and adjustment conditions; and demonstrate the consequence in generated tables. This builds on active design and model criticism. Its novelty and usefulness remain hypotheses.

## Concrete example and proposed operation

Illustratively, one paper reports a BMI–outcome projection, and another a glucose–outcome projection, each adjusted for age and sex. The user wants a virtual cohort for a regression containing both BMI and glucose. Different joint BMI–glucose structures can fit the separate projections but imply different adjusted BMI coefficients.

A further mean may add little. An applicable joint covariate statistic or conditional relationship may distinguish those completions. A similarly named statistic in a different age-selected population or measurement scale may not do so without additional assumptions. This is a hypothetical example, not an assertion that a particular compatible published table has been found.

The proposed sequence is:

1. Receive the analysis request, permitted base evidence and candidate-source metadata.
2. Let an Agent construct a small set of scientifically distinct compatible joint laws.
3. Compute each law's implied requested analysis and candidate-source operators in their own study conditions.
4. Choose one additional evidence item that can distinguish relevant completions.
5. Reveal its observed value, incorporate it through the common generation/update procedure, then freeze and sample the final dataset.
6. Assess the requested analysis on that dataset, including damage to already supported relationships.

An analysis-directed score could emphasize candidate distinctions that matter to the requested estimator, rather than maximum disagreement over any variable. Its exact definition and competent numerical comparator remain to be frozen. Candidate spread is a heuristic unless its uncertainty interpretation is justified; it is not automatically a posterior.

## First discriminating pilot

Use two new **invented known-law tasks**, not another patient download: (1) ambiguity caused by incomplete joint evidence; (2) ambiguity involving source population, measurement or adjustment context. A fixed study-like corpus exposes metadata but discloses the selected numerical evidence only after the acquisition decision. Neither task supplies the exact requested target coefficient as an obvious selectable answer.

Compare three policies with the same initial scientific model pools, source metadata, analysis request, numerical tools, one-item evidence budget and common integration procedure:

- a capable ordinary Agent allowed to reason freely;
- a specified numerical active-evidence rule with the same source context;
- the proposed analysis-directed acquisition policy.

The ordinary Agent must not be deprived of metadata or forbidden from discovering the proposed strategy. The numerical comparator must be chosen from the relevant goal-oriented design alternatives, not assumed adequate because a raw-variance rule is easy to implement.

The illustrative ceiling is **2 tasks × 3 policies × 2 independent initial pools × 2 final seeds = 24 final tables**. The [complete budget](CORE_EXTENSION_CANDIDATES_2026-11-03.md#proposed-total-budget-not-just-final-tables) also caps all intermediate work: at most 40 model turns, 12 one-item disclosures and 576 analytic operator calculations. No additional patient-like intermediate tables are hidden in this count. Each final table provisionally contains 5,000 invented rows; rows and sampling seeds are not independent scientific validations.

A shared, nominated initial law provides an analytic no-acquisition reference. An independent all-eligible-information calculation checks compatibility and remaining identifiability; it is not a perfect Agent or a same-budget competitor. If simulation is necessary for source operators, its executions must be explicitly budgeted before release.

Primary outcome: the requested scientific estimator applied to generated data, compared with the known-law quantity in prespecified units. Also retain source compatibility, failures, retentions, chosen evidence and actual costs. Evidence-selection accuracy alone is not success.

**Before execution:** commit the two hidden laws and fixture definitions; define the operator set, acquisition criterion, comparator, natural-unit resolution/tolerances and seeds; independently review information boundaries and budgets; then freeze. None of these unspecified details may be filled in after observing favorable outcomes.

## Continue, simplify or stop

| Pilot result | Decision |
|---|---|
| No genuinely distinct compatible initial completions | Stop the selection test; do not manually insert diversity or recruit replacement authors. |
| No available evidence can constrain the requested analysis | Report the information limit; do not promise arbitrary-variable generation. |
| Ordinary Agent or numerical policy obtains equivalent useful data | Simplify the system; keep data-generation feasibility without claiming this policy adds value. |
| Chosen evidence improves fit but not the requested analysis, or damages required relations | Reject this component as the current headline. |
| Repeated gain in the requested analysis under matched budgets and nontrivial source contexts | Consider broader controlled and real-evidence evaluation, with a separately specified budget. |

Positive results from two artificial tasks are only mechanism evidence. Real papers introduce extraction, finite-sample uncertainty and transport problems that these fixtures do not settle. Existing exposed clinical references may support explicitly developmental demonstrations; new confirmation requires separately admitted data. No new clinical case is admitted by this decision.

## Venue implications and writing

Official NeurIPS guidance includes use-inspired work, while ICLR asks for meaningful new knowledge and rigorous evidence rather than requiring state-of-the-art performance. Nature Communications seeks advances significant to specialists. These are criteria, not acceptance predictions or checks that a submission window is open. [NeurIPS 2026 guidance](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/ReviewerGuidelines), [ICLR 2027 guidance](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/ReviewerGuidelines), [Nature Communications scope](https://www.nature.com/ncomms/aims).

My current judgment is to test the AI-method capability first. A conference-oriented story could be: fragmented evidence leaves consequential joint ambiguity; the system chooses informative study-qualified observations; generated data become more useful for the requested analyses. A Nature Communications story would need a substantial, repeatable scientific-methods advance and convincing biomedical applicability. Changing the template or increasing synthetic row counts would not supply that evidence. See the [independent venue strategy review](TOP_VENUE_STRATEGY_REVIEW_2026-11-03.md).

The existing v0.8 draft remains the empirical foundation. Do not insert the proposed capability into its Abstract or Results as accomplished work. If later supported, new figures should show the information-to-generator mechanism, paired analysis outcomes, source-context failure modes and a real-evidence application. For now, current navigation points to this research decision; the earlier PLOS preparation documents remain historical fallback materials.
