Whether a distinguishable operation advances prior methods remains a related-work and evidence question. The current record does not establish algorithmic superiority.

## Results that must remain visible

These findings change the interpretation of the main contribution and belong in Results, not only in a limitations paragraph:

- **CHNS effect-scale and variability discrepancies.** Relative BMI/glucose/age effect magnitudes are C1: 30–33% / 73–75% / 65–78%, and C2: 37–39% / 89–99% / 33–47% of the reference estimates. HbA1c SD is 0.954 in the reference, versus 0.719–0.736 in C1 and 0.534–0.578 in C2. Glucose SD is 1.545 mmol/L, versus 1.292–1.339 and 0.984–0.992. Similar means or positive coefficients cannot substitute for these results.
- **No observed revision gain.** Both CHNS constructors retained their initial model; the repeated retained outputs are hash-identical. The artificial pilot had no numerical-feedback round. Neither study demonstrates that feedback improves generated data.
- **Bounded evidence responsiveness.** Conditional completions C/D satisfy the planned translation criterion at every seed label, but the marginal-evidence construction B is farther from the minus-world target than the description-only construction A. This is a finding about these constructions, not a general causal effect of adding evidence.
- **An unresolved reuse endpoint.** Normal and standardized Student-t(3) residual laws satisfy the supplied conditional constraints but imply different tail contrasts. These examples prove nonidentification; they do not define a complete identification interval or establish clinical importance.

Restricted populations/variables, uncertain transport, optional untested regimes and direct-row generation are scope limitations. Missing calibrated source, structural and transport uncertainty precludes calibrated-inference claims; seed spread cannot replace it. Unknown pretraining exposure and incomplete runtime metadata must be disclosed. No leakage, unit error or invalid computation may be reclassified as an ordinary limitation if discovered. CHNS is revealed and closed to tuning or fresh-confirmation labels.

## Necessary remaining manuscript and submission work

1. **Reconcile the dated draft.** Replace obsolete statements that a real case has not been evaluated. Describe core-v0 as implementation history and CHNS F2 as the later completed implementation. Do not pool historical method versions, repeated seeds or retained copies into independent biomedical validations.
2. **Finish the bounded methods/results narrative and citations.** Carry every primary result, the no-revision finding, source-proxy qualification and artificial-tail boundary into the new draft. Resolve the prior-work discussion using the existing implementation audit and reference records; keep novelty wording provisional wherever that comparison remains insufficient. Preserve exact source locators, units and adjustment conditions in the supplement.
3. **Prepare submission packaging.** Complete bibliographic formatting, author/funding/conflict statements, applicable data-access/ethics language, and a public availability statement consistent with local-only participant tables and third-party materials. Disclose unavailable model/temperature/token/cost metadata and distinguish execution time from total effort. A code-and-aggregate release must not be called a self-contained replay if private execution bundles are omitted. Venue choice and submission strength remain judgments after the bounded paper is assembled.

**No new experiment is required merely to prepare the current draft.** A stronger revision-benefit claim would require an actual frozen before/after scientific change with source and target outcomes. A method-advantage claim would require a concrete distinguishable operation and an appropriate same-information generator comparison on a genuinely fresh reference, with declared analytical uses. A changed evidence packet alone does not isolate algorithmic advantage. Neither claim creates a mandatory baseline against another Agent. Any such future work requires separate admission and a fixed stopping decision; it is not a remaining task in this consolidation.
