# Figure 1. Scientific agent cohort construction

The research request, study evidence and any allowed observations enter on the left. The agent writes a generator and records its assumptions. During a trial the isolated worker executes the selected candidate and returns the feedback permitted by the study protocol. The agent may revise that program or choose it for final sampling. The sealed virtual tables and the measured reference meet at evaluation. The reference can consist of target participant measurements or a previously computed sample estimate, according to the study design.

The *Write or revise* caption belongs to the horizontal connector from the scientific agent to the generator. In the editable graph that connector has source `agent`, target `program` and ID `write`. Its caption is the edge-relative child `write-label`. The *Run trial*, *Inspect trial* and *Freeze and sample* captions are attached to their respective connectors in the same way. The figure uses a plain white page, dark ink, blue construction arrows and a muted brown measured-reference branch. The paired-column table identifies the delivered data.

The figure describes the program route documented by the [bounded retrieval study](https://github.com/ziwang11112/evidence-guided-virtual-cohorts-2026/blob/aa2401eafd9833ae062a9db023ed22f268042bd9/biomedical_research/virtual_cohort_system/reports/core_workflow_replication_2026-11-04/RESULTS.md), [trial and decision controller](https://github.com/ziwang11112/evidence-guided-virtual-cohorts-2026/blob/aa2401eafd9833ae062a9db023ed22f268042bd9/biomedical_research/virtual_cohort_system/reports/evidence_use_cross_task_2026-11-05/runner.py), [isolated worker](https://github.com/ziwang11112/evidence-guided-virtual-cohorts-2026/blob/aa2401eafd9833ae062a9db023ed22f268042bd9/biomedical_research/virtual_cohort_system/cohort_harness/execution.py) and [iron-study protocol](https://github.com/ziwang11112/evidence-guided-virtual-cohorts-2026/blob/aa2401eafd9833ae062a9db023ed22f268042bd9/biomedical_research/virtual_cohort_system/reports/iron_breadth_2026-11-05/PROTOCOL.md). Trial diagnostics and availability of source replay depend on the protocol.
