## Scientific narrative

The paper now leads with the biomedical data gap: measurements may be absent or inaccessible, while published knowledge remains available. Its data product represents a scientific model whose assumptions and implications can be inspected using ordinary analysis tools.

Three contributions are stated through actual operations and results:

1. **Scientific model authorship from qualified evidence.** The Agent interprets supplied study definitions, assigns numerical roles, selects a joint law and records completion/transfer assumptions. The retained worked example shows how separately adjusted projections become joint coefficients under an assumed covariance.
2. **Evaluation through intended analyses.** The paper retains all adjusted effects, joint associations, distribution diagnostics and unfavorable results, with interpretation determined by the information supplied.
3. **Cross-sectional and longitudinal construction.** Complete-cohort generation without individual construction records is central. The HRS example demonstrates repeated measurements and individual effects under a supplied published model.

The revision does not claim new regression algebra, first literature-to-data generation, fully automatic evidence extraction, proven labor savings, general scalability or superiority over every tailored statistical generator.
