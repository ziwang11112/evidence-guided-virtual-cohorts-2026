# Manuscript supplement: generating iron-status markers for three analyses

**Proposed Results paragraph.** We extended evaluation beyond glycemia and lipids by asking two independent agents to generate continuous hemoglobin and ferritin measurements for nonpregnant US women aged 20–44. The agents received external study summaries, including threshold co-occurrence and ferritin geometric means within hemoglobin bands, but no target outcomes. Both generators reproduced the source crude odds ratio (6.58 and 6.73 versus 6.65). Across three deliveries each, both preserved the direction of the association between low ferritin and low hemoglobin, but both underestimated its magnitude in the measured NHANES sample (generated odds ratios 4.84–6.56; survey estimate 17.79, 95% CI 9.28–34.11). Performance differed by intended use. Construction A produced a joint low prevalence of 6.66–6.88%, against 7.63% in the measured sample; construction B produced 10.02–10.44%. Conversely, the continuous rank correlation from B (0.391–0.430) was closer to the survey estimate (0.398) than that from A (0.156–0.195). The rank coefficient was not supplied, although the band-specific summaries constrain it indirectly. These measurements qualify the analyses the generated data supported in this population; they do not establish reconstruction of individual values.
