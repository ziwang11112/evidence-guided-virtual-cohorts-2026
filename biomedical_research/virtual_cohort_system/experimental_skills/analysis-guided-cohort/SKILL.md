# Analysis-guided cohort construction

Produce data that support the user's intended scientific analyses. Use the task's information and execution contract, including its allowed literature retrieval and feedback. Preserve the requested generation scope and choose among supported output routes; no particular model family, relationship sign, agent count or clinical endpoint is prescribed.

This is a project-local experimental skill. It does not replace an installed skill, implement an agent launcher, authorize new tools, or upgrade the frozen harness. Use its records outside a frozen candidate unless the supplied interface explicitly admits them.

## Define success in terms of the requested data use

Build an initial task rubric using [references/rubric.md](references/rubric.md), or apply a supplied rubric when the experiment fixes one. Bind criteria to the requested population, variables, analysis and allowed evidence. Refine diagnostic questions as evidence and computations reveal new needs; retain the requested analyses, earlier findings and the reason for each change. The rubric describes useful output, not a required sequence of operations. A user may disclose the research question and may permit reference statistics for calibration; classify that comparison accordingly.

Separate executable requirements, evidence-supported numerical checks and assumptions that affect interpretation. Do not invent a target sign, desired p-value, clinical tolerance or hidden answer. A reference sample estimate is not a known population constant. When a defensible success margin is unavailable, report the discrepancy and uncertainty without creating a pass threshold.

## Choose the next useful operation

Choose the next action yourself within the supplied tools and resource allowance. You may search for a missing relationship, examine an alternative source interpretation, construct a first candidate, compare plausible structures, check a current output, change the model family, or stop with a useful restricted result. Retrieval need not occur only at the beginning, and revision may change structure as well as parameters. Earlier fixed-source, single-construction experiments are local study designs, not general system prohibitions.

Load [references/procedures.md](references/procedures.md) only when a procedure is useful. Adapt or combine it, skip it with a reason, or use another appropriate analysis. The library is not an exhaustive action list or a sequence to complete. Additional latent variables, auxiliary measurements, mixtures and alternative dependence models are legitimate scientific choices when their role and assumptions are explicit; retain the requested deliverable. Keep unsupported intermediate variables distinct from real measurements.

Select a construction yourself. Compute the implications relevant to the requested analyses, using approved tools; mathematical checks of a declared law, empirical checks of a finite table and source-population replay are different assessments. A program's execution receipt does not replace any of them. For direct-row generation, evaluate the resulting table without inventing a population law or claiming certified tail moments.

## Act on findings

For each material finding retain: affected analysis, source or computed artifact, concrete observation, explanation status, next action and the check that could resolve it. Choose among a justified correction, focused retrieval, alternate construction, explicit sensitivity, retain or stop. Distinguish missing tools from missing source information and from a transfer assumption. Do not solve all three by adding arbitrary parameters or extra noise.

If a revision targets one criterion, recheck supported criteria that its change can affect. Judge the final data and scientific analyses; more complete annotations are a separate process outcome. Keep the earlier artifact and its negative findings. New evidence can justify new diagnostic checks; it does not erase an unfavorable requested analysis or retroactively relax its acceptance rule. Version an actual change in the user's scientific question separately.

## Return usable data with an honest use profile

Return the requested data/program and the compact criterion-by-criterion result, not a pooled quality score. Explain which analyses are supported at which evidence level and which are assumption-dependent or unassessed. An unknown relationship may be explored through stated scenarios rather than silently set to independence. Do not make an absent measurement look recovered for a real person.

An independent reviewer may apply the same rubric to raw permitted evidence and computed artifacts. The reviewer need not share the author's explanation, and agreement between agents is not a measurement. Respect the supplied budget; a valid retain or unresolved decision does not require another iteration.
