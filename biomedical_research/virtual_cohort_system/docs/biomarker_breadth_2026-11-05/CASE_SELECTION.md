# Which additional biomedical analyses would change the paper?

Decision memo, 2026-11-05. Status: literature and metadata feasibility; no new target estimates, virtual patient records, or comparative performance. The existing [manuscript](../../manuscript/DRAFT_v0_17.md) and [prospective expansion decision](https://github.com/ziwang11112/evidence-guided-virtual-cohorts-2026/blob/5982266787fa1e1dd44e3dc1e00785af2b26d4c1/reports/scaleup_2026-11-01/production_release/CLAIM_LOCK.md) remain unchanged.

## The gap

The manuscript draws on five named study families (CHNS, ENSANUT, Turkana/Laikipia, HAALSI and HRS) in different roles. It already includes adjusted slopes, interaction, residual correlation and longitudinal modeling. The HRS case tests reconstruction from fitted model exports. Much of the external-evidence work concerns glycemia and lipids. Multiple draws of one constructed law are not independent studies. The [existing target audit](https://github.com/ziwang11112/evidence-guided-virtual-cohorts-2026/blob/5982266787fa1e1dd44e3dc1e00785af2b26d4c1/biomedical_research/virtual_cohort_system/reports/manuscript_v17_2026-11-05/FRESH_TARGET_REVIEW.md) does not establish a new untouched validation just because a new endpoint is available.

The most useful addition is an analysis a scientist could run on the **same delivered data**, with an estimand and evidence form that differ from existing cases. Prioritize one clinical study of threshold-defined co-occurrence; qualify one expression signature if it offers a credible measurement bridge. Keep other tasks in reserve.

## First choice: iron status and low hemoglobin

**Question.** Can a generated population of nonpregnant women aged 20–44 support (1) a threshold association, (2) an absolute public-health quantity, and (3) a continuous dependence analysis? The first two have direct counterparts in external evidence; the strength of the third requires additional choices. The scientific product is a table of continuous Hb and ferritin values that can answer all three questions. Do not generate three different tables to match the three answers.

**Source operators.** In a selected cohort of 1,671 parous, nonlactating IUD users in northern China, [Zhu et al., Tables III–V](https://storage.imrpress.com/imr/journal/IJVNR/article/501902/1752835456699.pdf#page=7) reported raw plasma ferritin and Hb. Summing the published counts over folate and B12 strata yields:

| Source raw ferritin | Hb <120 g/L | Hb ≥120 g/L |
|---|---:|---:|
| <15 µg/L | 173 | 331 |
| ≥15 µg/L | 85 | 1,082 |

The crude odds ratio is 6.653 by arithmetic. It is distinct from the authors' age/BMI/education-adjusted 6.5, and from the model adding folate and B12 deficiency (6.448). Table III also gives ferritin geometric means by three Hb bands: useful additional information beyond the four cells. These group summaries do not directly report the continuous rank coefficient. In [Pita-Rodríguez et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10049065/), the corresponding Cuban association (OR 3.023, 95% CI 1.816–5.033) uses *inflammation-corrected* ferritin. Do not pool these ORs as estimates of the same quantity. If using the Cuban evidence, the source model must replay the reported correction or label it contextual only. Avoid treating all low hemoglobin as iron-deficiency anemia.

**Why this is informative.** [threshold_witness.py](threshold_witness.py) constructs two exact mathematical rank laws with the same four cell probabilities and the same continuous uniform rank margins. Within each threshold cell, couple the two ranks in the same or opposite order. Their Spearman coefficients are +0.614 and −0.272, respectively, despite identical threshold odds ratios. These are two examples, neither sharp bounds nor plausible fitted biomedical models. This argument concerns **Table IV alone**, not all constraints in the paper. It explains why the third analysis supplies information that simply fitting the four cells cannot.

**Target metadata.** CDC NHANES August 2021–August 2023 has [CBC](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/Nchs/Data/Nhanes/Public/2021/DataFiles/CBC_L.htm) (Hb in g/dL), [serum ferritin](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/Nchs/Data/Nhanes/Public/2021/DataFiles/FERTIN_L.htm) (µg/L), [hsCRP](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/Nchs/Data/Nhanes/Public/2021/DataFiles/HSCRP_L.htm) (mg/L), [AGP](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/Nchs/Data/Nhanes/Public/2021/DataFiles/AGP_L.htm) (g/L), [demographics](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/Nchs/Data/Nhanes/Public/2021/DataFiles/DEMO_L.htm) and [BMI](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/Nchs/Data/Nhanes/Public/2021/DataFiles/BMX_L.htm). Link by SEQN and specify nonpregnant status (RIDEXPRG=2), phlebotomy weights WTPH2YR and design variables SDMVSTRA/SDMVPSU; estimate uncertainty as a survey subpopulation. Raw Hb <12 g/dL and raw ferritin <15 µg/L match the source *numeric thresholds*, not the population or instruments. Predefine detection-limit and missingness rules; CRP/AGP availability need not narrow a raw-ferritin primary analysis. The local dictionary for [CHNS biomarkers](https://chns.cpc.unc.edu/data/datasets/biomarker-data/) offers a lower-acquisition alternative (HGB in g/L, FET in ng/mL, HS_CRP in mg/L); matching eligibility, assays and completeness still require checking. Do not pick between targets after seeing either effect. NHANES and CHNS are project-exposed families.

**Smallest defensible pilot.** Keep the existing whole-cohort generation interface. Allow population descriptors and external papers; keep target biomarkers out of generation, retrieval and revision for this test. A fresh constructor chooses the joint law, parameter values and measurement mappings. No hand-entered target sign, odds ratio or correlation. For this developmental case, use a priori the NHANES target described above; do not use NHANES-derived outcomes as external evidence. Commit the evidence packet, the permitted target descriptors, endpoint definitions, seeds, uncertainty method and decision criteria before viewing any target effects. Multiple draws describe simulation noise, not uncertainty about the law. Retain differing constructions, source replay failures and nonmatches.

| Same delivered dataset | Scientific analysis | Boundary of interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Primary: crude low-ferritin/low-Hb association | Cross-product odds ratio, evaluated on the log-odds-ratio scale, with target survey uncertainty | Tests transfer of an externally constrained binary association. |
| Secondary: joint prevalence | Fraction with both low; absolute percentage-point discrepancy and target uncertainty | Tests absolute burden, which can move even when an odds ratio agrees. |
| Secondary: continuous association | Spearman rank correlation of Hb and raw ferritin, using survey-weighted midranks for the target | Not directly supplied by the four cells; record indirect constraints and any directly reported coefficient in the actual admitted packet before calling it uncalibrated. |

Freeze how to handle zero cells, small event counts and unstable design-based uncertainty before opening participant measurements. Check whether complete-case coverage or selective measurement materially changes interpretation. If precision is insufficient, report non-estimability, without replacing the endpoint after viewing its sign. Report the measured sample estimate and its interval next to each generated estimate, with changes in scientific interpretation; neither interval overlap nor a significance vote defines success. Choose intended-use tolerances before unblinding or keep the pilot descriptive. One workable figure is three rows (odds ratio on log axis, joint prevalence in %, rank correlation), each in native units, with the sample interval and each generator's draws.

## Second choice: a focused transcriptomic case, conditional on assay comparability

**Question.** Can virtual participant-level expression of a small, externally chosen gene signature support a case/control analysis? Systemic lupus erythematosus offers a defined PBMC interferon signature (MX1, IFIT1 and IFI44). [Palli et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6428719/) give a three-gene qPCR score with median (quartiles) 7.1 (2.7, 19.7) in 48 SLE patients without APS and 4.2 (2.5, 5.4) in 28 healthy controls. These numbers constrain a score, not three separate variances or their covariances. [GSE50772](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE50772) has 81 sample records on array platform GPL570 with a single-timepoint PBMC design. Sample records do not establish 81 independent usable participants.

Before admission, check sample identity/overlap, clinical inclusion and therapy, site and batch balance, study-level exposure, GPL570 probe mappings for the three genes, preprocessing and the cross-platform definition of the score. Do not apply a qPCR-unit cutoff to microarray intensities. Prespecify a comparable score construction and a within-platform rank effect (probability a case scores above a control, ties split). Ranks do not by themselves validate the construction of a multigene score across platforms. If an interpretable mapping cannot be justified without target outcomes, stop or restrict to a compatible assay. If gene-level variability is claimed, require independent gene-level constraints; score-only evidence cannot support a genome-wide expression claim. A well-controlled small signature tests a new data type. Listing many differential genes or enrichment plots would not by itself do so.

## Reserve: kidney function and hemoglobin on existing data

The [Brazil PNS files](https://www.pns.icict.fiocruz.br/bases-de-dados/) and a local acquisition record document a laboratory component with Hb (Z007, g/dL), creatinine (Z025, mg/dL) and a laboratory weight. [Han et al., Table 4](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0150029) provide independently observed sex-specific, adjusted Hb means by eGFR band. This could test sex-stratified shape or a preregistered stratum contrast in an existing survey. First resolve creatinine assay and eGFR equation, adjustment-set compatibility, small strata and limited public design variables. One examination does not establish chronic kidney disease. PNS is an exposed project family; the result would add analytical breadth. Do not launch this just to increase the number of endpoints.

## What was and was not inspected

This review used source publications, target documentation and local inventories. No participant files were imported, downloaded or analyzed for this memo. The NHANES documentation contains univariate frequency tables; search snippets about adjacent NHANES analyses were incidentally visible, not used as independent evidence. No target-cycle ferritin–Hb effect, GEO expression values or GEO target-study results were used to select a favorable relationship. Registry/history checks must precede any stronger independence claim. No patient-level generation, confidential data or new performance result is part of this artifact.
