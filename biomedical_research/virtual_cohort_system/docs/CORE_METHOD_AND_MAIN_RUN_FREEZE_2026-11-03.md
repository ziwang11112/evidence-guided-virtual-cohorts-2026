## The scientific procedure being fixed

The product is task-appropriate virtual biomedical data. Inputs are a visible research request, population/measurement specification, published evidence, and any explicitly authorized observations or reference summaries. Zero-individual-input whole-cohort generation is the primary experimental setting. Enrichment and missing-cell completion remain broader capabilities, with their historical evidence kept separate.

One scientific Agent chooses the probability structure, scientific parameters, applicable evidence and explicit completion/transport assumptions. Deterministic tools execute the authored generator and calculate diagnostics; the coordinator does not insert target-derived coefficients, directions or numerical fixes. A different research request may legitimately lead to a different generator. The reusable object is this procedure, not a universal fixed equation. Analyses are visible to the author; same-table reuse is an optional additional claim, not a prerequisite for every application.

Every evidence record retains population/selection, measurement, units/transforms, operator/adjustment set, estimate and available uncertainty, overlap and provenance. A numerical-uptake record must show how each quantitative relationship enters the model, a deterministic calculation or an executable statistic, or explain why it remains contextual/unavailable. Citation alone does not demonstrate numerical use. Existing context-qualified numerical operators and source/target bridges are reused; this is not a claim to have invented them or to supply unrestricted source-population reconstruction.

For source comparisons, distinguish strict semantic agreement, explicitly assumption-qualified approximate calculations and unavailable replay. Operator-semantic agreement is not numerical agreement. Separate-source coefficients cannot silently become coefficients of one joint regression. Unidentified quantities remain assumptions; an evidence gap does not force independence or a fabricated estimate.

Construction may use bounded diagnostic feedback and may revise or retain. A separate mandatory post-final revision factorial is removed from the main run: the latest six-construction experiment produced no change. A valid no-op ends the process. Finite-moment/support checks and independently checked arithmetic remain required; these checks do not establish biological truth.

Each final task-specific configuration freezes before its final evaluation. Information used for construction is calibration information. Reference estimates are samples with uncertainty, not known population constants; uncertainty intervals are not automatic equivalence margins. All planned results and failures remain visible.

## Finite main experiment to prepare

They are two different target families, not two untouched confirmations; their source evidence also overlaps. Preserve each cohort's actual eligibility, fasting/diagnosis rules, demographic standardization, assay/measurement definitions and estimators. Do not quietly standardize away these differences.

Turkana and HAALSI remain closed historical cases and optional evidence in the paper. A third cohort is not needed to begin this main run. No new acquisition or original-paper intake is opened by this plan.

Proposed information conditions are E0 (external marginal/basic evidence) and E1 (the same information plus audited applicable joint/conditional evidence). The exact cards must specify which quantities are reported, algebraically implied, assumption-dependent or unavailable. At least one intended analysis must not be a direct numerical calibration target; any algebraic implication is labelled and is not presented as a newly inferred relationship. The questions themselves remain visible. Target numerical answers are not added as feedback in this comparison. The completed full-reference experiment remains supporting calibration evidence.

This is a new, explicitly labelled developmental reuse protocol. The completed CHNS E0/E1 experiment already used five authors per arm and found a mixed/negative overall result, with predominantly qualitative relation use and several invalid population-moment laws. Earlier source-aware numerical calculations were also executed. The next study therefore tests the consolidated procedure's robustness and cross-setting use; it is not the first evidence-value contrast or first numerical source calculation. Historical-versus-new differences cannot isolate a mechanism effect.

Thus the complete ceiling is **48 exploratory attempts + 72 final attempts = 120 physical table attempts**. Deterministic analytic calculations may be used and must be retained. A case card lists exactly which source contexts are computed analytically, replayed within those two slots, approximated or unavailable; two slots do not promise replay of three or more contexts.

All final deliveries use a prospectively fixed seed list and are primary. Initial versions are archived, but there is no extra obligatory initial/final table multiplication. Failure consumes its slot. No best-author, best-seed or favorable-stage replacement is allowed. There are only two target families; generated rows and seeds do not enlarge that replication count.

The statistical reference must genuinely use the same admitted numerical information and supported operators, with explicit assumptions and a reviewed implementation. An existing independent-marginal sampler is insufficient for a claim about exploiting joint information. Five stochastic Agent constructions versus one prescribed statistical specification is an asymmetric descriptive comparison; it does not by itself establish general superiority. If the comparator cannot be implemented under the admitted information, resolve or remove that optional comparative claim and revise the allocation **before any construction**, retaining the pre-release decision. Do not silently proceed with a weak substitute or drop a comparator after seeing results.

For each E1 case, the release must bind at least one applicable non-marginal evidence operation that has an executable or independently checked analytic numerical path. Its source/context assumptions remain explicit. This gate concerns operation availability, not favorable fit. Report actual uptake for every author, including contextual-only or declined operations; do not silently repeat a qualitative-only evidence comparison under a numerical-integration label.

Each case specifies approximately three scientific uses: two quantitative relationships and one further joint/conditional or subgroup/shape use supported by its measurements. Case cards, not this generic plan, fix exact formulas, analysis populations, adjustment sets and units before execution. All necessary distribution/quality diagnostics are retained. Direction, effect magnitude/contrasts and reference uncertainty are reported jointly. Use a scientifically defensible practical tolerance only when one is justified in advance; otherwise report discrepancies without an equivalence verdict. Synthetic-sample p-values are not evidence of newly measured biological relationships.

For each case and analysis separately, the prespecified E1–E0 descriptive contrast is the difference of arm means of constructor-level mean absolute discrepancies across the three fixed seeds. Also show every constructor's effect values, directions, reference uncertainty and seed ranges. Do not combine unlike estimands into one accuracy score. All scheduled constructions and failures remain in the denominator of completion reporting. If any required endpoint/seed is unavailable, report the available-value summary as incomplete and do not claim a complete arm advantage from a survivor-only mean. This contrast measures added information under the procedure, not a pure algorithmic effect or clinical equivalence.

All scientific decisions and all final delivery attempts for **both cases and every comparison condition** must close before the common target evaluation is computed or supplied. The coordinator already knows historical reference results, so author-facing corrections must remain mechanical or grounded in the admitted evidence, without target-directed scientific hints. No early case result may steer a later author.
