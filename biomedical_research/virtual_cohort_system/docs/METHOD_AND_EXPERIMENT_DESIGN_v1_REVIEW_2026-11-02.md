The experimental reviewer independently checked the planned arithmetic: 36 target slots per cohort; 144 at the four-case ceiling; at most 48 stage records, 720,000 virtual rows and 432 primary slot-level analysis evaluations. Retention aliases initial outputs, leaving at most 72 sampled tables if every candidate retains. These are ceilings and accounting units, not completed scientific results or independent biological replications.

The final-stage primary clarification is prospective and outcome-uninformed. It does not edit frozen historical protocols or results. Both initial and final are reported; a changed final that fails cannot be replaced after disclosure by a better initial output. Explicit pre-disclosure retention remains valid and has zero revision gain.

The method reviewer confirmed that the draft does not describe general source-population reconstruction or K02 cross-context model linkage as completed. The experimental reviewer confirmed that K02 independence and its 0.10 working-resolution proposal remain subject to final admission/release, and that no new campaign outcome is claimed.

**Conclusion:** the consolidated design is consistent within the documented read-only review scope. This is not an approval of source transport, calibrated uncertainty, case admission, a live end-to-end campaign or biomedical effectiveness. Those require the concrete prospective evidence and execution described in the design.
