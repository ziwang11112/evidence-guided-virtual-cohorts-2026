## Completed mechanism check

Both ordinary and extra-consistency instructions recovered the same Gaussian joint law. All twelve attempts succeeded, yielding six distinct tables under shared seeds. It does not test clinical transport or uncertain evidence extraction.

The existing-code audit found numerical relationship use in all ten Q1/E1 constructions. Consequently, the remaining diagnostic question is whether adopted statistics are jointly appropriate and whether source-to-target assumptions are justified, not a blanket claim that agents ignore coefficients. No old clinical target was regenerated or tuned in this unit.

Figure 1 illustrates the [scientific agent's action and feedback loop](../manuscript/paper_figures_v24_2026-11-06/fig1-agent-loop.drawio.svg).
