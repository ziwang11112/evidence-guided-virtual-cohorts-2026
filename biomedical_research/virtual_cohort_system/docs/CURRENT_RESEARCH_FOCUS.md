## Completed evidence

The current writing unit is v0.12, with a [figure/guide review](../reports/paper_figure_revision_v12_2026-11-04/FIGURES_AND_GUIDE_REVIEW.md) and [final PDF review](../reports/paper_figure_revision_v12_2026-11-04/FINAL_PDF_REVIEW.md). Its [six-endpoint interpretation](../reports/paper_figure_revision_v12_2026-11-04/FIG23_READING_GUIDE_ZH.md) distinguishes mean discrepancy improvements from complete recovery of a relationship. It adds no case or generation. The [v0.10 story design](../reports/conference_draft_v10_2026-11-04/STORY_AND_VISUAL_DESIGN.md), [independent numerical review](../reports/conference_draft_v10_2026-11-04/INDEPENDENT_DRAFT_REVIEW.md) and v0.9 consolidation remain the prior narrative and empirical checkpoints.

- [External-evidence main study](../reports/main_study_freeze_2026-11-03/RESULTS.md): two exposed development references, 20 Agent plus four statistical constructions, 72 final tables; five of six discrepancy contrasts improved and CHNS BMI worsened.
- [CHNS information-provision study](../reports/information_revision_ablation_2026-11-03/RESULTS.md): six Agents; accessible target summaries support closer reconstruction; no additional-stage revision gain. Different protocol and source packet from the main study.
- [Statistical calibration supplement](../reports/summary_comparator_2026-11-03/RESULTS.md): three final tables from one purpose-built family, retained as a descriptive reference.
- [HRS public-model reconstruction](../reports/hrs_public_example_2026-11-03/RESULTS.md): six valid longitudinal tables from two constructions; nine physical generations include three invalid initial comparator tables and a separately reviewed parser repair. Source coefficients were provided; no HRS participant records were accessed. This resolves the earlier pending public-method-example task.
- [Live Agent system integration](../reports/agent_system_live_2026-11-03/RESULTS.md): two fresh scientific authors actually retrieved original papers, authored different complete-cohort laws, executed eight 3,000-row tables and retained six deliveries. A age and B glucose relationships had useful agreement, while B's glucose-adjusted BMI point direction was opposite to the existing CHNS estimate. That disagreement is descriptive and is not a calibrated equivalence or global-quality test. All source replays were unavailable; both authors retained their initial candidates. This is closed developmental integration, not a new clinical confirmation or revision-gain result.

- [Autonomous gap-directed source search](../reports/gap_search_2026-11-04/RESULTS.md): a fresh Agent selected its own queries and studies without DOI seeds or desired signs. Ten queries led to five seriously reviewed originals. Independent review verified a continuous conditional BMI coefficient, complementary age evidence, and contrasting differently conditioned BMI results. This improved the available evidence packet; it generated no data and establishes no quality gain. Exact target transfer remains an assumption.

- [Reviewed evidence to actual generation](../reports/evidence_to_generation_2026-11-04/RESULTS.md): one fresh constructor used the five-paper packet, authored a complete six-column joint law and produced three delivered 3,000-person tables plus one trial. All executions and independent recalculations passed. Secondary BMI/age and primary age had useful descriptive agreement; primary BMI/glucose were weaker and female/quantile/rank differences remained. The Agent retained its model after all source/generated feedback. A pre-generation dependence sensitivity showed that an unsupported BMI–glucose relationship can reverse the primary BMI projection while preserving other bivariate laws. This is development on an exposed aggregate reference, not fresh confirmation or controlled search/revision benefit.

- [Real-versus-real interpretation context](../reports/real_real_calibration_2026-11-04/RESULTS.md): 100 prescribed community-disjoint splits of the existing 6,631-person CHNS sample, with 3,000 people per side, were compared with the three frozen evidence-build tables. All 36 metrics were retained. A regression terms, age-related B behavior and BMI–glucose rank correlation had discrepancies on similar numerical scales to real-sample variation; B glucose/female terms, some joint relations and distributional tails remained farther away. This is descriptive within-sample context, not an equivalence threshold, new cohort, 100 clinical replications or generator improvement.

All these units are closed. The integrated ledger also retains earlier enrichment, direct-output, failed transport, infinite-moment and revision-trade-off results. Synthetic rows, seed draws, authors and clinical references are different units and are not pooled as a single validation count.
