## Completed mechanism check

The [8 November diagnostic](../reports/review_revision_2026-11-08/controlled_joint_moments/RESULTS.md) supplied four fresh authors with the same exact, compatible population summaries. Both ordinary and extra-consistency instructions recovered the same Gaussian joint law. All twelve attempts succeeded, yielding six distinct tables under shared seeds. Independent numerical and distinct-person checks verified the result. This artificial test shows no benefit for the extra instruction; its single-batch stopping rule is reached. It does not test clinical transport or uncertain evidence extraction.

The existing-code audit found numerical relationship use in all ten Q1/E1 constructions. Consequently, the remaining diagnostic question is whether adopted statistics are jointly appropriate and whether source-to-target assumptions are justified, not a blanket claim that agents ignore coefficients. No old clinical target was regenerated or tuned in this unit.

Figure 1 illustrates the [scientific agent's action and feedback loop](../manuscript/paper_figures_v24_2026-11-06/fig1-agent-loop.drawio.svg). The host dispatches the scientific task and verifies the artifacts. The scientific author supplies the numerical uses, assumptions and program. The worker samples the tables. Source checks can inform an author's retain or revise decision when the protocol supports the specified statistic. A separate evaluator computes final scientific comparisons after the seal.

## Prospective evidence

The earlier HAALSI evaluation froze the target analyses before disclosure. Across five agent constructions and three draws per construction, both glucose main effects agreed with the sample in point direction, while eight of 15 draws agreed on the interaction. Three of five constructions passed the primary criterion. Four were required, and the margin also admitted three zero effects. In Turkana, none of five passed. The existing [frozen record](../../../reports/scaleup_2026-11-01/production_release/RESULTS.md) supports this limited conclusion for the earlier workflow. The November 5 interface reused the same Turkana measurements.

## Decision and next step

Keep the simpler workflow; do not promote the tested extra instruction to a method contribution. Complete biomedical review of source applicability, the clinical analyses and the scope of usable outputs. The manuscript can report the completed analysis-specific utility and transparent model choices now. A stronger transport claim requires a genuinely new, admissible reference under the frozen method; a broader reliability claim requires additional independent authoring conditions. Neither is established by more random draws from an existing law. New experiments should answer one of these claim-specific gaps rather than repeat revealed targets or enlarge the system without evidence.

The [KDD comparison decision](KDD_LLMSYNTHOR_COMPARISON_PRIORITY.md) separates a scientific biomedical comparator from methodological neighbors G-Sim and LLMSynthor. The [optional adaptation protocol](../reports/kdd_neighbor_design_2026-11-05/PROTOCOL.md) is not scheduled. A published model refit or another published paper's headline score is not evidence of transport to a new cohort.

Review the completed prospective and developmental results separately. A claim that the later evidence-use interface transports to a new population requires an independently admitted target selected before outcome disclosure. The [local admission review](../reports/manuscript_v17_2026-11-05/FRESH_TARGET_REVIEW.md) found zero such cases ready for evaluation. The earlier stop decision stands.
