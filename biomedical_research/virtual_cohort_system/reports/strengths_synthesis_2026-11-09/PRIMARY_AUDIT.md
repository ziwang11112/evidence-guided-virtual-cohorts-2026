# Primary comparative-evidence audit

9 November 2026. This read-only synthesis covers nineteen assigned canonical units. It uses frozen reports and aggregate JSON, with arithmetic summaries of stored discrepancies only. No generators, row tables, patient-reference recomputation or web research were used. Later November 4-9 scientific-use units are outside this subaudit and are reviewed separately.

## Strongest findings and proposed emphasis

1. **Relational evidence yields analysis-specific gains.** The main study improves five of six prespecified discrepancies, most clearly CHNS glucose and ENSANUT age/BMI. There are also favorable regions against the delivered statistical comparator, shown in full below. This is the strongest biomedical comparative result in this assigned set.
2. **Sufficient supplied summaries support strong reconstruction.** Complete CHNS summaries lower twenty-seven of thirty arm-average discrepancies, tie three binary quantiles, and worsen none. Conventional calibration also succeeds. HRS extends reconstruction to longitudinal mixed-model data. This is evidence-to-data implementation and analysis preservation, not unseen biological discovery or exclusive Agent capability.
3. **Specific repairs and evidence operations can work.** One adaptive pilot reduces rank-correlation discrepancy in every paired seed but slightly worsens all three primary regression discrepancies. The artificial conditional-evidence test produces the intended opposite target directions. These are bounded positive results, not generally effective iterative architecture.

The prospective result remains a hard scope limit: HAALSI meets the fixed criterion in only three of five authors when four are required, its threshold also admits zero relationships, and Turkana meets it in zero of five. Most feedback stages retain initial models.

## Why the E0 and ENSANUT-age advantages need qualification

**E0 input boundary:** CHNS: sanitized source populations/selection/measurement, fifteen grouped marginal records and unit guidance, zero relationship records/compiled relational items, no full paper/broad summary, no disclosed correlation coefficient/direction. ENSANUT: seventeen records of Lifelines margins, duplicate converted representations and unit guidance. Broad historical summaries were excluded because they contained relationships. Intended analyses visible in both arms.

**Author completion freedom:** Authors choose and explicitly declare unreported dependence, residual distributions and target transport. Unsupported cross-covariances are not zero facts. Existing model knowledge is not erased, and pretraining nonexposure is not established. No new retrieval, repository search, source IPD or numerical target access allowed.

**Fixed statistical E0:** Fixed positive Gaussian copula source-moment family minimizes -log(det R). With no relational constraints, source components are independent. CHNS is an equal Jansen/Bao/MIND donor mixture, which may induce between-component dependence, with requested uniform ages and donor-specific female fraction. ENSANUT is a Jansen donor under exact target age/sex cells. Near-zero focal effects follow a particular missing-dependence completion; they are not a limitation of statistical synthesis as a class.

**Fixed statistical E1:** CHNS constrains both separate Jansen projections, Bao Pearson and MIND Spearman in separate qualified source contexts. ENSANUT constrains two Jansen projections; Pani age slope and eight age bands remain context-only for missing-source-law reasons. All ENSANUT E1 Agents instead constrain Pani age slope. That uptake difference is concrete but not experimentally isolated as the cause of the age advantage.

**Budget fairness:** Same available empirical packets and final three-seed schedule. Agent authors allowed at most two exploratory attempts and optional decision; the fixed statistical recipe used one deterministic source fit from an all-zero dependence start, 300-iteration cap, no alternate-family/start search and no exploratory row draw. Five independent Agent models versus one specification. Equal data access is not equal scientific search/design/feedback effort.

**Interpretation:** E0 six-of-six is a comparison of selected Agent joint-law completions with an entropy/independence completion. The evidence supports flexibility under incomplete specification as a candidate explanation; neither pretrained biomedical knowledge nor evidence integration is causally identified. The paper's strongest claim should not rest on this restrictive baseline alone.

The fixed reference is a meaningful transparent source-moment baseline, especially under E1 where it outperforms every Agent on two endpoints. It is not a representative sample of capable statistical synthesis. The six E0 wins cannot carry a general-method-superiority claim.

## Coverage and controls

All nineteen assigned canonical units were located. Core-highlight and CHNS admission are preparation records; evidence-revision is software-only; the developmental pilot never generated data; core-v0 is an implementation fixture. They do not increase the clinical experiment count.

Distinct authoring experiments may reuse the same clinical sample. Independent arithmetic audits and new manuscript presentations verify or display outputs, not new scientific replications. The summary comparator reuses historical Agent tables, the continuation reuses pilot laws, retained stages reproduce identical outputs, and HRS recovery corrects one existing comparison.

A focused problem supported by these results is how to turn available study-level information into reusable data for specified analyses, and how the supplied joint information and declared completion assumptions determine the useful analyses and remaining failures. This follows actual gains and counterexamples; it adds no novelty, venue, clinical-replacement, causal-validity or general-system-superiority assertion.
