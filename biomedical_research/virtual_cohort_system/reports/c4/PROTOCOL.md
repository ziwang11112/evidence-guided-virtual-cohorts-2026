# Frozen C4: accepting a virtual-data revision for a specified analytical use

31 October 2026. Authorized by the user's continuation after the completed C3 results. C3 and every previously exposed clinical target remain closed. This unit is an artificial mechanism pilot, not a new clinical case, published-evidence experiment, or novelty demonstration.

## Question

On an identical pool of Agent-authored whole-cohort generators, does rejecting source-incompatible revisions change the accuracy of a source-unreported analysis on untouched simulation draws? Does source qualification fail to determine an analysis when the admitted evidence is nonidentifying?

The task remains generating reusable virtual data. A selector is a supporting component; passing its own gate is not a scientific utility result. No ordinary-Agent superiority or multi-Agent contribution is claimed.

## Tasks and boundaries

An independent custodian creates two explicitly invented three-variable Gaussian source contracts, before any candidate executes. Both generate all x,y,z measurements and an identifier, with no individual input. Their source population moments are constructed values, not estimates from patients or publications. Task A admits the means, SDs and all pair correlations. Task B omits one pair correlation; two distinct compatible hidden laws witness the resulting identification limit. The hidden evaluator and analytical endpoints are committed before authorship. Their operators and values are withheld from the generator author and source-feedback interface.

Source moments do not by themselves recover biology. Task A's withheld projection can be derived mathematically from its admitted Gaussian moments; it is unprovided as a calibration target, not new independent information. The same Task B outputs are scored under both compatible laws, which are ambiguity witnesses rather than independent cases.

The author reads only approved visible contracts and task-bound technical harness interfaces. The root and reviewer do not open custody laws or evaluator results until all candidate selections are sealed. The generator runs only through the existing isolated executor. Source-feedback computation is trusted controller code bound to actual generated output. No on-host import/execution of Agent generator code is allowed.

## Authorship and fixed pool

One fresh scientific Agent authors an initial candidate for each task. After reviewing the three prescribed initial source-feedback draws, it proposes up to three distinct revisions per task. A justified retention or duplicate is valid; the Agent is not forced to create a bad baseline or a successful revision. Exactly four frozen labels per task—initial,r1,r2,r3 (stored as c0,c1,c2,c3)—make the selection schedule unambiguous. Duplicate or unchanged proposals are identified explicitly.

All scientific structures, parameters and assumptions belong to that author. The controller defines the information boundary, execution budget and evaluation rule, not desired hidden relationships. No candidate can be altered or proposed after election begins. Each policy sees precisely the same four candidates and election diagnostics. All initial and revised artifacts are retained.

## Fixed source score and selection rules

For each source statistic, compute its declared operator exactly: arithmetic mean, sample SD with ddof=1, or Pearson correlation. The fixed denominator is every admitted statistic (nine in A, eight in B). A table's loss is mean_j[((estimate_j - reference_j) / scale_j)^2]. Scales are 0.10 for means,0.10 for SDs,0.05 for correlations, in the standardized artificial task. They are engineering conventions, not confidence intervals, source uncertainty or clinical equivalence margins. Candidate election loss is the mean of its three election-table losses. Any failed/nonfinite election draw makes that entire candidate ineligible with election loss +infinity; never average only its successful draws. If no candidate has finite complete diagnostics, seal a failure/retain-unqualified result and do not imply analytical suitability.

Protected source uses are all and only the admitted pairwise correlations. A candidate passes these if the absolute discrepancy of every protected correlation is <=0.05 on each election draw. No hidden endpoint enters the score, gate, feedback or candidate construction.

Three policies are compared:

1. **Retain:** choose initial.
2. **Minimum loss:** choose the lowest mean election loss among the four; ties favor the fixed label order initial,r1,r2,r3. A revision is therefore selected only with strict improvement over initial.
3. **Protected-use acceptance:** among revisions that pass every protected-use check and strictly improve mean loss over initial, choose the minimum mean loss with the same tie order. If none qualifies, retain initial and report whether that initial model itself passes the source-use gate. Retaining an unqualified baseline is not approval of its uses.

All policies get a qualification flag for their chosen candidate. That flag refers only to admitted source uses, never automatically to a withheld or clinical analysis. Test results cannot alter the choices.

## Scientific evaluation and decision

The independently committed evaluation contract determines the source-unreported primary projection and secondary analytical endpoints before authorship. Compute them on all 24 final tables, compare every chosen policy against the fixed analytical truth, and retain the full candidate pool to expose opportunity costs. Report raw values, absolute/squared error, direction, all three draws and both Task B laws. Do not turn simulation draws or the two compatible laws into independent clinical evidence. No significance or universal policy-superiority claim is intended.

A meaningful favorable result requires a nontrivial selector difference and improvement in the primary withheld analysis, without hiding adverse seeds or either compatible world. If the choices coincide, the protected selector always retains, there are no distinct revisions, or no qualifying revision exists, the mechanism is nondiscriminating in this pilot. If source-qualified outputs fail under a compatible hidden law, explicitly report the identification limit. No thresholds or tasks are replaced to rescue a preferred result.

Independent review verifies source/task commitments, election/test separation, provenance, diagnostics, selector arithmetic and withheld analytical calculations. Root preserves all failures and issues a continue/simplify/stop recommendation. The unit closes after this review; clinical data or additional experiments require a separately recorded next unit.
