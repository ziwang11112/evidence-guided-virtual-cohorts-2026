## Central scientific story

The contribution is an Agent-controlled workflow that connects a biomedical request and study-qualified partial evidence to an executable joint law and analyzable scientific data. Whole-cohort generation without individual inputs is directly demonstrated. The Agent takes on the task-specific model equations, distributions and parameter derivations, while the researcher defines the scientific request and access conditions. This division of work supports low manual scientific-model specification as a design property; human time savings have not been measured. Established statistical families supply the sampling machinery.

The manuscript retains the broader objective of useful biomedical data generation. The current cross-task outputs have a narrower demonstrated use: analysis-specific, assumption-dependent scenario data for qualitative association exploration and analytical workflow inspection. This empirical limit does not redefine the general method as permanently limited to scenarios. Enrichment remains supported by its earlier record, and missing-cell completion remains unevaluated.

## Claim controls and retained limitations

All 54 primary points agree in direction with the corresponding cached sample points. This is not an overall accuracy percentage or 54 clinical replications. The TC reference interval spans zero. Magnitudes, residual lipid dependence and dispersion remain mixed, so the manuscript does not claim general joint-distribution fidelity, quantitative cohort substitution or calibrated power/design use. Six same-model contexts and 18 seeded tables do not create clinical independence.

The batch demonstrates numerical use of source values through reviewed parameter derivations, while all runtime source comparisons remain unavailable. No measured numerical source-fit feedback or revision benefit is claimed. Reviews and the independent arithmetic check establish inspected implementation properties, not transport validity. Optional retrieval used seven query credits and one original download, without a controlled retrieval-benefit comparison.

These boundaries appear at the relevant result and in a compact discussion synthesis. The new abstract and introduction foreground the evidence-to-law-to-data contribution. Earlier numerical outcomes and supporting links remain available; the manuscript does not remove unfavorable outcomes or pool distinct studies into a single score.
