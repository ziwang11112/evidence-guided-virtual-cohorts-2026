# Public scope of the v0.9 consolidation

This unit exports original manuscript text, method/rerun decisions, independent aggregate audits, figure code, scientific display values and SVG figures. It performs no new patient-level analysis, construction or cohort download. Local PNG/PDF reading assets accompany the figures; their SVG versions and rendering instructions are provided publicly.

Real and generated individual records, full third-party papers/codebooks, credentials and internal author contexts are excluded. Reported coefficient rows, uncertainty values, distribution summaries, hashes and aggregate plotting bindings are not individual records. Source hashes identify their exact local artifacts; whitespace-compacted public JSON has a separately recorded public-byte hash.

The unchanged old manuscript and experiment records remain available under their original versions. The v0.9 paper does not relabel heterogeneous historical studies as one current-version benchmark. Some detailed historical source/control artifacts are local only; any unresolved Markdown links to those artifacts are explicitly rendered as plain text and listed in the export manifest instead of adding unreviewed material.

The `ROOT_README.md` local template is published as the repository-root `README.md`; it is not an experiment report. Navigation updates in the project and manuscript READMEs and current-focus note point to the new scientific draft. The figure renderer's exact local/public input requirements are documented with its captions. Clinical reruns still require lawful access and case-specific environments.

No paper has been submitted. Author declarations, journal selection, final length and typeset production remain separate work.
