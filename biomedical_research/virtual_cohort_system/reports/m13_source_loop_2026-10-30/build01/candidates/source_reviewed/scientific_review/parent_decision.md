# Scientific construction decision

One shared conditional DXA body-fat law uses Gallagher's original inverse-BMI Equation 1 as its nonlinear baseline. For the supplied Asian indicator only, a convex blend toward the Thai development age/BMI/sex equation starts at 0.35. Shared level and male shifts start at zero. This acknowledges the Thai evidence without treating the two populations as interchangeable or adding an unavailable nationality/device covariate. The Asian blend necessarily applies to both Thai and Japanese rows; that unresolved transport conflict must remain visible in source replay. Non-Asian rows retain the Gallagher baseline before the shared shifts. The identifier, context label and row count never determine biological behavior.

Individual variation follows a bounded beta law with the conditional mean equal to the bounded regression location. The male SD starts at Gallagher's reported SEE of 4.31 percentage points and female SD at 1.10 times that value. The beta family, constant sex-specific variance and 1.10 ratio are assumptions. Thai marginal SDs are not conditional residual SDs. Guards constrain the mean to 0.5–99.5% and cap variance at 95% of mean times 100-minus-mean; they preserve a valid distribution over the declared computational support and can change extreme-tail regression behavior. This is individual variation, not a posterior distribution over model, evidence or transport uncertainty.

Both source contexts are covariate-only numerical integration approximations. Thai development counts are multiplied by40 (8,920 rows); all six Gallagher sex-by-ethnicity counts by6 (9,756 rows). Bounded beta marginal quantiles reproduce age and Gallagher BMI means/sample SDs. Thai BMI instead uses15% uniform25–29.999 and85% beta14–24.999, with the lower component's moments derived from each sex's overall mean/sample SD. Applying the full-cohort rounded15% BMI>=25 to each development sex is an explicit unidentified scenario, not a reported subgroup fact. An initial single-beta arithmetic check implied32.35% development BMI>=25, exceeding the entire cohort's approximate count budget; that assumption was rejected before generator execution. The final female lower-component shape is strongly U-shaped, exposing how uncertain reconstruction is despite exact moments. Thai age support is20–84 and BMI14–29.999; Gallagher age18–97 and BMI14–35. Unknown lower endpoints and shapes are assumptions, not extracted facts. Thai zero BMI>=30 is respected. Within-group latent age–BMI correlations are fixed at−0.15 for Thai and+0.10 for Gallagher, with realized Pearson correlations recorded. These unknown dependence choices are frozen scenarios, not optimizer parameters. Different correlations or marginal shapes require scientific sensitivity analysis.

The independent v2 model domain covers both source and target supports while leaving the qualification task unchanged. Five design columns are recomputed exactly from base covariates; they introduce no hidden variables. Original White/African-American group memberships set the Gallagher covariate mixture but are not supplied to the generator. Asian coding reflects the sources' category, including Japanese/site/device confounding; it is not interpreted as a causal ancestry effect. Identity transport across the named DXA devices is unverified. No four-compartment equation or invented assay bridge is used.

The replay has exactly10 Thai diagnostics (sex-specific means, SDs, Pearson correlations, and intercept/BMI/age/male coefficients) and8 Gallagher DXA coefficients using the original seven-predictor design with no standalone Asian intercept. Every diagnostic appears once in the locked objective. Reported coefficient precision/covariance are unknown; supplied scales are engineering units, not standard errors or evidence-independence weights. The many summaries overlap within their source cohorts. Thai validation is visible contextual evidence, excluded from fitting; conflicting signed biases are preserved without repair. Additional Gallagher relationships remain contextual and unreplayed.

Only four top-level scalars are proposed for calibration: Asian blend[0,0.8], step0.15; shared level shift[−3,3] pp, step1; male shift[−3,3] pp, step1; residual multiplier[0.75,1.4], step0.15. These ranges represent modest transport/residual scenarios and do not have a confidence-interval interpretation. Nine trials permit an initial value and one positive/negative comparison for each scalar. Seeds, all18 term-specific scales, shrink0.5, minimum step fraction0.01, target loss0 and execution limits follow the controller exactly. No context, equation structure or covariance parameter is adjustable during that search.

The preparation stage only constructs covariates, checks contracts and parses generator syntax. It does not import/execute the generator, freeze, calibrate, or evaluate outcomes. Source fit remains unknown until controller replay. Source agreement, if obtained, would not establish validity in the invented qualification grid or a later patient population. Under this information boundary, retaining visible source conflicts is preferable to inventing subgroup corrections or unreported conditional quantiles.
