# Whole-cohort request and information boundary

Generate all five requested scientific variables. Task and analysis definitions
are visible; measured target values, moments and outcomes are not inputs. Use
only the assigned evidence packet and allowlisted files. No new retrieval,
DOI lookup, source IPD, repository search or reference access is authorized.

Choose and document your scientific structure and parameters. Record reported,
algebraically derived and assumed quantities separately, including age/sex
composition, dependence, residual variation, selection and transport. Retain
finite first/second population moments and task support; finite sample output
does not prove finite law moments. A source selection rule is not a license to
clip target draws or silently remove incompatible rows.

At most two total exploratory row-producing attempts and three final 3,000-row
deliveries are available per construction. A failed attempt consumes its slot.
All source/task/revision trials share the two-attempt cap. One optional
diagnostic revision or retention decision is allowed; no-op is valid. Global
release defines exact seeds/runtime before any attempt. Deterministic analytic
calculations are retained and do not authorize hidden row-producing trials.

Common marginal evidence does not identify a joint law. Use only the
quantitative relationship information present in your assigned packet. Every
applicable quantitative relationship needs a numerical-uptake record, or an
explicit contextual/declined/unavailable explanation. Citation alone is not
numerical uptake. Source moment/rank checks describe a separately declared
source scenario; a target-population calculation is not source replay.

Published uncertainty is not individual dispersion, an equivalence tolerance
or automatic precision weight. Unit conversions are representations, not assay
or population harmonization. No target-directed numerical correction or
expected direction is supplied. All final scientific decisions and tables for
both cases/conditions/comparator must close before the common evaluation.
