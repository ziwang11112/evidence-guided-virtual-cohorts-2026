# Scientific decision

Use a bounded affine factor construction for the task's 3000 fictional individuals and exact eight-cell demographic quotas. BMI, glucose and HbA1c receive the admitted Jansen marginal locations and scales through an explicit target-transport assumption. The directly reported HbA1c percent mean 5.6 and SD .3 are used once; the alternative IFCC conversion is retained as unit context. Source age/sex summaries do not override the task's equal-stratum design.

E0 contains no non-marginal operations. Accordingly numerical_uptake.json has an empty records list. Positive modest round factor loadings were chosen as a transparent joint completion scenario, with a small female shift of either sign and independent symmetric beta residuals. None of the loadings, requested projection coefficients, factor independence or technical support limits is identified by the source packet. The construction supports inspection of analyses under this scenario; it does not supply evidence-based target effect estimates.

The bounded law makes all outputs and requested first/second moments mathematically well defined. The deterministic retained calculation derives the actual generator law's supports, moments and regression projections from its specification. Source comparisons on target-table feedback are unavailable because source replay is not supplied and selection/population differences remain unresolved. The controller alone may execute the generator; local generation or Monte Carlo has not been used.

Selection is handled by specifying a conditional fictional population with the task's stated eligibility, not by pretending to verify latent clinical exclusions in five measured fields. Mexican transport, age-band uniformity, shape and dependence, lack of anemia/medication restriction and suppressed tails are material limitations. Sampling repeats only vary synthetic individual residuals.

Stopping choice: select initial_final without exploratory table attempts, conditional on the recorded static and deterministic checks completing successfully. Those checks address implementability and internal mathematics; no scientific feedback suggests a defensible refinement of unsupported E0 dependence. This is the initially selected model and is not claimed as an improved model. Final configuration is fixed after decision.json is written. Freeze, independent review and three final seeds belong to the coordinator.
