# CHNS2009 E0 A05 construction decision

The submitted law is an 80-component age/sex mixture of correlated lognormal BMI, serum glucose and HbA1c. All 3000 rows are generated jointly and all analyses use the same complete table. MIND-China supplies a coherent set of marginal scales from one Chinese cohort retaining known diabetes and measuring serum glucose. Its population is nevertheless selected, older, rural and later than the target. Using its BMI mean/SD 25.0/3.6, glucose 5.2/1.4, HbA1c 6.1/1.0 and female fraction 2049/3547 is an explicit unvalidated transport scenario, not estimated target composition or a target fit.

Age is discrete uniform from 35 through 74. Log-scale age trends, sex differences and one shared Gaussian residual factor provide an assumed joint structure. These numerical choices are fully recorded in model_spec.json. E0 supplies no quantitative relationships: nothing identifies these signs or strengths. The separate requested OLS coefficients are consequences of this chosen joint law, not source regression coefficients, independent findings or causal effects. analytic_properties.json calculates those consequences from exact finite sums and Gaussian moment formulas.

Jansen's diabetes/anemia exclusions, European selection and plasma glucose, and Bao's prior-diabetes exclusions and plasma assay limit their transfer here; they remain source context and are not pooled with MIND. The packet's NGSP scale relation is retained as context and not used for assay or serum/plasma harmonization. Summaries from the same cohort are dependent evidence, and individual SDs are not estimator uncertainty. Source sampling uncertainty, original weights, tail shape and field-specific counts remain unresolved. No synthetic row or seed creates additional clinical evidence.

The positivity-preserving mixture has finite first and second moments and uses no unsupported biological caps or row removal. Mathematical support is broader than verified physiological support. Exact law moments and regressions provide a deterministic implementation specification; they do not demonstrate execution success or clinical validity. Every target-as-source comparison is marked unavailable. The analytic script is not a source-population reconstruction or Monte Carlo simulation.

Select initial_final after static validation without exploratory row-producing attempts. Marginal evidence cannot identify the unknown joint law; generated diagnostics alone would not supply that evidence. No favorable realization, coefficient sign or P-value is a stopping requirement. The candidate remains unfrozen for the controller and independent reviewer. Three fixed final seeds are controller-owned; no local generator import or execution has occurred.
