# Initial scientific decision — CHNS2009_E0_A01

This is an unfrozen all-generated candidate for the 3000-row five-variable request. It uses MIND-China BMI, serum-glucose and HbA1c mean/individual-SD pairs as fixed marginal anchors, and the MIND female count ratio as an assumed composition. The reason for choosing this one source is its serum analyte and inclusion of known diabetes. Its older, selected 2019 population, imputation and measurement timing differ from the requested 2009 analytical population; transporting these margins is an unverified scenario, not target calibration. Exact age composition is unknown, so age is discrete uniform on the task's 35-74 support.

E0 contains no non-marginal relationship records. The candidate completes the joint law by mutual independence, with positive lognormal continuous margins. The choice is deliberately explicit: it fixes E1, E2 and E3 at zero in the population law. The resulting table allows the requested computations but cannot substantiate their biomedical effect sizes, signs, causal meanings or target validity. Nonzero finite-sample slopes would be sampling fluctuation under this specified law. The model is not presented as a validated association model.

Jansen and Bao margins remain contextual; neither is combined with MIND as an empirical pooled cohort. NGSP supplies only a unit relation and is not used as a clinical constraint. Quartile endpoints are not converted into SDs. Individual SDs are not converted into SEs. All source means, SDs and count ratios retain their original roles, locators, selection, measurement and missing uncertainty. MIND margins share one source population and are not independent confirmations.

The candidate has no clipping, clinical cutoff, row removal, source replay, target-reference feedback, or sample-based moment correction. The mathematical law has finite first and second moments. Deterministic calculations and static checks are retained. No row-generating code has been executed by the author. Whole-cohort and semantic source comparisons are all unavailable because only target-table execution is supplied; knowing a source definition does not make the target table a source reconstruction.

Initial construction stops after static validation. A first coordinator-run exploratory execution is requested to check runtime feasibility, table support and fit availability, not to select a direction or tune towards hidden target values. The initial candidate must be frozen by the coordinator before that run. There are two total exploratory slots and at most one feedback-to-scientific-revision cycle. Final selection has not yet been made; no approval, independent-review or execution record is authored here.
