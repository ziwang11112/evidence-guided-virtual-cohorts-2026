# CHNS2009_E0_A02 initial scientific candidate

This is a fixed, assumption-dependent whole-cohort scenario for 3000 rows of age, female, BMI, serum glucose and HbA1c. The generator accepts exactly three positional arguments and receives no participant table. No generator execution, local sampling or Monte Carlo calibration has been performed by the author. The candidate is submitted unfrozen for the controller's first exploratory seed 5201.

The chosen continuous margins use the MIND-China arithmetic means and individual SDs, because that supplied source reports serum glucose and retains known diabetes. This choice does not establish transport from selected rural adults aged 61-80 in 2019 to the requested released-data population aged 35-74 in 2009. Prior-year BMI, imputation, sampling, assay, fasting and complete-case differences remain unresolved. MIND's female count ratio is borrowed as a composition assumption. Age is discrete uniform over task eligibility. No source age distribution is treated as target composition.

The positive continuous variables follow a multivariate lognormal law. A single shared standard-normal factor has author-assumed log-scale loadings 0.30, 0.60 and 0.70 for BMI, glucose and HbA1c. These magnitudes and their signs are not supplied relationships. E0 contains no non-marginal operation, so numerical_uptake.json has an empty records list. No source association is silently set to zero. Independence of age and female from the continuous variables is separately assumed and algebraically makes the population E3 age coefficient zero. E1 and E2 are determined by the chosen covariance law; analytic/moments.json reports those implications without target or source calibration.

Jansen and Bao are retained as contextual marginal evidence, with their diabetes exclusions, plasma measurements, IFCC scale or unknown quartile algorithm intact. No IQR-to-SD conversion, plasma-to-serum conversion or NGSP conversion is applied. Repeated summaries within each study are not independent confirmations. Source SDs describe individuals; estimator uncertainty remains unreported and is not estimated from synthetic n. All parameters remain fixed across seeds. Residual variability, external-estimate uncertainty, structural uncertainty and population transport are distinct; the latter three have not been integrated into a probability distribution.

The scenario permits inspection of the visible OLS procedures. Its association estimates are assumptions made concrete, not recovered individual measurements, clinical evidence, causal effects, validated target estimates or evidence of measurement savings. No clinical p-value interpretation or national survey-design claim is supported. Unbounded positive lognormal tails are an explicit support limitation and are not validated extreme physiology; there is no clipping, row rejection or source-derived diabetes threshold.

Source-population reconstruction is unavailable. Every source comparison in the target-table feedback plan is therefore not computable, including the contextual NGSP item. The semantic binding preserves missing source uncertainty and does not substitute target rows for source rows. The analytic report establishes properties of the declared law only. The checks record lists actual static and deterministic checks; successful static validation is not execution success. Final selection and any allowed revision will follow the controller's recorded feedback, with the initial candidate preserved after freeze.
