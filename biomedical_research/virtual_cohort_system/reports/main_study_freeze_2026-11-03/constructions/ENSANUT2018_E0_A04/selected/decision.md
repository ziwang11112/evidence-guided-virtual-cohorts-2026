# Scientific construction and stopping decision

The candidate is an independent marginal completion using only E0 marginal evidence and explicit assumptions. BMI and HbA1c are gamma distributed; glucose is scaled beta on (0,126) mg/dL. Marginal mean/SD inputs are 26.5/4.1 kg/m^2, 91.8/9 mg/dL, and 5.6/0.3 percent. These are source-anchored working parameters under unverified transport, not discovered target values. Gamma and beta family choices provide positive support and tractable finite moments; neither family was reported by the source.

Exactly 375 fictional adults populate every female-by-age-band cell. Age is uniform over integer years within its band. The source age mean/SD and source female proportion are retained as context, not substituted for the prescribed design. Source plasma glucose is treated as interchangeable with target serum glucose only by a stated measurement assumption. The entire detailed task eligibility condition is presumed, with no individual eligibility records reconstructed.

E0 supplies no joint statistics. The product copula leaves every dependence assumption visible and avoids inventing a numerical relationship as if it came from a paper. Independence is a strong substantive completion assumption and could be badly wrong. Its population coefficients for all three requested HbA1c slopes, and their difference, are zero as a mathematical consequence; this is not evidence that the clinical associations vanish. Finite samples can have either sign. The requested association analyses are usable computationally but fully assumption-dependent scientifically. This model does not estimate a causal effect or identify the unreported joint population.

The directly printed HbA1c percent representation is selected once. The IFCC row and its NGSP conversion remain alternatives affected by reporting precision. Glucose mmol/L and converted mg/dL values likewise constitute one constraint, not replication. All clinical numeric items come from the same Lifelines cohort. NGSP contributes measurement guidance, not an independent clinical sample. Source standard deviations denote individual dispersion; source estimator uncertainty is not supplied and is not simulated. Structural and transport uncertainty are not measured by synthetic seed variability.

All target-as-source checks are unavailable. No source-context replay, target reference, patient row, generator execution, random draw, Monte Carlo calculation or numerical quadrature was used by the author. Retained deterministic algebra derives support, population moments and projection implications from the actual generator law. Structural validation does not establish successful execution or biological validity.

Stop with initial_final without exploratory execution. A trial table cannot resolve the missing joint evidence, and matching fixed input marginals by construction is not independent validation. The controller owns freeze, review and the fixed final executions. No favorable sign, fit or final-seed outcome is required.
