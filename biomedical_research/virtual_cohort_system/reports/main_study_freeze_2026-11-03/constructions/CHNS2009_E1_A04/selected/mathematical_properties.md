# Actual probability law and finite moments

The specification fixes every scientific constant. No random table has been produced by the author. `analytic/derive.py` independently evaluates the finite-factor formulas below, reads the specification, and calls only the admitted source population moment operator. It never imports `generator.py`. `analytic/results.json` retains actual results and hashes. This is closed-form algebra and small linear-system arithmetic, with no Monte Carlo or quadrature.

Let U,V,E be mutually independent standardized uniforms on [-sqrt(3),sqrt(3)]. Let A be the stated context age law, F a Bernoulli with probability f and D a Bernoulli with probability p. These are independent of U,V,E and each other. Only the target age is discrete uniform on the 40 integers 35,...,74. Source contexts use the continuous uniforms stated in the specification; source age bounds are assumed and were not restricted to the target range. The target age has mean 54.5 and variance (40^2-1)/12=133.25. For source continuous uniform [l,h], mean=(l+h)/2 and variance=(h-l)^2/12. F has variance f(1-f), and D has variance p(1-p).

With rho=0.25 and centered age Ac, female Fc and class Dc, the law is

    B = mB + sB U
    G = mG + sg [rho U + sqrt(1-rho^2) V] + kG Dc
    H = mH + betaB (B-mB) + betaG (G-mG) + a Ac + s Fc
        + (kH-betaG*kG) Dc + sh E.

Thus total HbA1c class shift is kH, not betaG*kG+kH. Generator and analytic script use this same decomposition. The target accepts no observations, generates exactly the design's 3000 rows, and returns the six named columns. Source scenarios are separately specified laws used only analytically, not source rows generated by this allocation.

## Separate source regressions and shared structural terms

For Jansen the class probability is zero, BMI SD=sB=4.1 and glucose SD=sg=0.5. Cov(B,G)=c=rho*sB*sg. Age and sex independence is an explicit assumption. The reported separate adjusted slopes in NGSP units are uB=0.09148*0.13 and uG=0.09148*2.29. Intercept-including covariance projection gives

    uB = betaB + betaG*c/sB^2
    uG = betaG + betaB*c/sg^2.

Consequently betaB=(uB-(c/sB^2)*uG)/(1-rho^2), betaG=uG-(c/sg^2)*betaB. The positive denominator shows unique joint coefficients under this chosen covariance. The two reported slopes are never treated as coefficients of the same combined regression. Changing the unreported rho changes the derived joint terms. The source nuisance coefficients are not reported and are not inferred; a=0.005 and s=-0.03 are assumptions.

The source Jansen HbA1c mean and SD use NGSP=0.09148*IFCC+2.152. Means use offset, SD/slopes only multiplier. The analytic operator converts source percent covariance back to native IFCC exactly once. Converted values are not new evidence. The selected Jansen FPG upper support is below 7, as checked algebraically; its self-report/anemia eligibility is declared for the selected law, not reconstructed with unmeasured variables. Its distribution is not the actual GWAS sample or guaranteed regression sample. The intercept/equal-weight comparison is qualified, not strict replay.

## Variance decomposition and existence

When glucose SD is a total SD, sg^2=SDG^2-kG^2*p*(1-p); otherwise the specification's glucose SD is within-class sg. Let L_U=betaB*sB+betaG*sg*rho and L_V=betaG*sg*sqrt(1-rho^2). The HbA1c variance excluding residual is

    Vexplained = L_U^2 + L_V^2 + a^2 Var(A) + s^2 f(1-f) + kH^2 p(1-p).

For a total HbA1c SD, sh^2=SDH^2-Vexplained; otherwise sh is the specified residual SD. All retained contexts have strictly positive sg^2 and sh^2. Neither unknown covariance nor printed uncertainty is silently used as a residual variance. Jansen's reported individual SD is a marginal distribution anchor under the source law, not a coefficient SE. Target MIND-based SDs are assumed transports across populations.

For centered independent factors Z=(Ac,Fc,U,V,Dc,E), the output vector equals mean+LZ. The exact L matrix is retained for each context in `analytic/results.json`. Therefore E[X]=mean and Cov(X)=L diag(Var(A),f(1-f),1,1,p(1-p),1)L^T. This derives the supplied source moments from the actual affine law; they are not an independently chosen desired covariance matrix. The source Jansen/Bao projection and correlation operators consume these moments after the declared source selection. Bao parameters were fixed without using 0.619 as a fitting criterion; its raw difference is retained, with no pass margin or source precision claim.

Every factor has bounded support. Every output is a finite linear combination of those factors. The exact interval arithmetic retained in the result proves all measurement variables are strictly positive, and hence both E|X| and E[X^2] are finite (indeed all moments exist). There is no exponentiation, quadratic normal term, truncation of realized outputs, rejection sampling or clipping. These support endpoints are consequences of deliberately bounded modeling assumptions; they are not known biological population limits and exclude heavier tails that may exist in the true target. The glycemic mixture is finite and both branches have bounded support, so it cannot invalidate moment existence.

The covariance is positive definite: age, female, U, V and E all have positive variance, the age/female loadings are independent coordinate directions, and BMI, glucose and HbA1c have successive nonzero U, V and E loadings. The optional D factor adds a positive-semidefinite term. Thus the population OLS predictor matrices are nonsingular. A finite random draw can still be rank deficient, for example if one sex category is absent; no local execution has asserted sample rank. The controller must check realized output. The target age effect conditional on all other generated covariates is algebraically a=0.005, not a result learned from evidence. E1 and E2 vary with target covariance and class assumptions and are not set equal to the two source projections.

## Uncertainty and use limits

U,V,E,D,A,F produce individual variation only. External source-estimate uncertainty is not sampled; source SEs are unavailable, Jansen printed interval level is unconfirmed, and no precision pooling is used. Model structure and population/assay transport are separate unresolved uncertainties. Same-family records are correlated evidence; NGSP duplicate representations add no information. Bao/MIND pair-specific n is unreported. MIND Spearman is not recoverable from the supplied ordinary moments, and no MIND post-imputation source rank law is claimed. Every source comparison on the target table remains unavailable. Analytical source agreement is a construction property, not individual recovery, external validity, causal evidence or a claim about synthetic p-values.
