# Initial scientific decision

This is a transported marginal scenario with an explicitly assumed joint law. The E0 packet contains no non-marginal operations; numerical_uptake.json therefore has an empty records list. No relationship coefficient, external knowledge value, target summary, or desired effect was used to choose the loadings.

MIND-China provides arithmetic means and individual SDs for all three positive continuous quantities, and its serum-glucose measurement is the closest specimen analogue in this packet. Its inclusion of known diabetes avoids importing a source diabetes exclusion into the target. These are reasons to select one coherent marginal source, not evidence that its age 61-80 rural sample represents the target. Directly transferring BMI 25.0/3.6 kg/m^2, serum glucose 5.2/1.4 mmol/L and HbA1c 6.1/1.0 percent to ages 35-74 is a major, unverified assumption. Prior-year BMI, source mean filling, assay differences and complete-case selection are unresolved.

Jansen and Bao marginal summaries remain contextual. Jansen excludes diabetes and anemia; Bao excludes prior diabetes and supplies median/quartiles instead of individual SDs. Their summaries are not pooled, translated into SDs, or used to estimate dependencies. Source age and sex composition do not identify the target composition; uniform integer ages and female probability one-half are declared completion choices. NGSP unit guidance remains contextual because the chosen HbA1c input is already in percent. It is not glucose or assay harmonization. The source families are distinct as described in the packet, but exact participant overlap across publications is not established; no independence-based evidence weighting is performed. Repeated summaries from each cohort and the single common artifact are not independent confirmations.

The lognormal law supplies positive support and finite moments without ad hoc upper bounds. A shared standardized log factor with assumed loadings (0.35, 0.75, 0.65) creates a hypothetical co-varying metabolic component. These magnitudes and their signs are neither measured nor calibrated. Residual log variance remains positive in every variable. Age and female are independent of this component, so the requested age-adjusted and sex-adjusted projections are well-defined, but their interpretation is limited to this structural scenario. E1 and E2 are algebraic consequences of assumed loading products; E3 is exactly zero in the population because age is independent. Sample differences around those values would be Monte Carlo variation, not discoveries. An alternative copula, age pattern, source transport, or tail family could change the analyses while preserving the admitted marginal evidence.

The retained analytic script computes closed-form law moments directly from model_spec.json without importing generator.py, drawing rows or doing numerical integration. Its formulas follow the generator's exact factor and exponentiation equations. All target-table/source checks are unavailable; these calculations are not source replay or target validation. The evidence supports selected source marginal descriptions. Computational testing of regression code and sensitivity to this one documented scenario is supportable; clinical association recovery, causal claims, individual prediction and publication-ready target inference are unassessed.

No exploratory execution is requested. A target sample could check runtime and numerical representation but cannot identify the missing E0 dependence or population transport. After static and analytic verification, the initial configuration is selected as final without scientific revision. The coordinator may freeze, independently review and run the fixed final seeds. No successful execution, source reconstruction, target agreement or scientific improvement is claimed by this decision.
