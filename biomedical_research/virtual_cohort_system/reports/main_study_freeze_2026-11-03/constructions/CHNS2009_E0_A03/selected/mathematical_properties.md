# Probability law and moments

All rows are independent and identically distributed. Let A be discrete uniform on the 40 integers 35 through 74, F be Bernoulli(p) with p=2049/3547, and C be Gamma(shape k=4, scale t=1). A, F and C are mutually independent. The specification's units are year, unitless female membership, kg/m^2, mmol/L and percent.

For j in BMI, glucose and HbA1c, the specification supplies mean m_j, SD s_j and common variance fraction q_j. Set v_C=k t^2, e_C=k t, loading a_j=s_j sqrt(q_j/v_C), residual mean u_j=m_j-a_j e_C, and residual variance v_j=s_j^2(1-q_j). Let R_j independently follow Gamma(shape u_j^2/v_j, scale v_j/u_j), independently of all other latent quantities. The generated variable is X_j=a_j C+R_j. These exact formulas are implemented in generator.py. The retained analytic/derive_moments.py separately evaluates finite algebra only and never imports or calls the generator.

The submitted means/SDs are (25,3.6), (5.2,1.4) and (6.1,1). Variance fractions are (0.10,0.65,0.65). Thus every loading is strictly positive, every fraction lies strictly between zero and one, and every residual mean and variance is strictly positive. Explicit derived values and numerical round-off residuals are in analytic/moments.json. These facts establish valid positive Gamma shapes and scales before sampling.

Gamma variables with positive finite shape and scale have E[Z]=shape*scale and Var(Z)=shape*scale^2. Their moment generating function is (1-scale*tau)^(-shape), finite on an open neighborhood of tau=0. For X_j, the moment generating function is the product (1-a_j*t*tau)^(-k) times (1-(v_j/u_j)*tau)^(-u_j^2/v_j), finite near zero. Hence each X_j has finite absolute moments of every positive integer order; in particular its first and second moments exist. Nonnegative mixtures, clipping, log transforms, exponentials and unbounded quadratic latent terms are absent.

By construction E[X_j]=m_j, Var(X_j)=s_j^2 and E[X_j^2]=m_j^2+s_j^2. For j different from l, Cov(X_j,X_l)=a_j*a_l*v_C=s_j*s_l*sqrt(q_j*q_l), so Corr(X_j,X_l)=sqrt(q_j*q_l). These are consequences of the assumed shared-factor allocation, not reported correlations. Mixed second moments are finite by Cauchy–Schwarz, and higher cross moments also exist. X_j is strictly positive almost surely and has support (0,infinity). There is no additional scientific lower or upper cutoff and no post-generation selection. Real-valued mathematical support does not promise that a floating-point RNG can never encounter an implementation failure; the isolated runtime must still check its actual output.

Age has mean 54.5, variance (40^2-1)/12=133.25 and finite second moment 3103.5. Female has E[F]=E[F^2]=p and Var(F)=p(1-p)>0. Demographic cross-covariances with the three continuous variables and with each other are zero by assumption. Both variables obey the exact required integer support.

The covariance matrix of BMI, glucose and HbA1c equals diag(v_B,v_G,v_H)+v_C*a*a^T. For every nonzero vector z, z^T Cov(X) z=sum(v_j*z_j^2)+v_C*(a^T z)^2>0, because each v_j>0. Together with positive age and female variances, independence makes the full predictor covariance matrix positive definite. Population OLS with an intercept therefore exists uniquely for the three requested predictor sets, with finite residual second moments.

Independence of A and F implies E1's BMI coefficient is Cov(B,H)/Var(B) and E2's glucose coefficient is Cov(G,H)/Var(G). E3's age coefficient is zero even after including BMI and glucose. These values are derived in analytic/moments.json and are fixed by the chosen law. They are not source estimates and must not be described as newly inferred scientific relationships. Finite-sample rank is not guaranteed for every possible draw: the discrete variables could, with very small positive probability, lack variation. Actual execution diagnostics must retain any such failure; no rows are replaced or discarded to repair it.

The source anchors are treated as fixed point inputs. The common factor and residuals represent individual variation, not uncertainty in published means or SDs. External-estimate uncertainty is unavailable, structural uncertainty in the Gamma family and dependence fractions is unquantified, and transport uncertainty from MIND-China to the target is unresolved. Three different seeds sample one fixed assumed law; they do not quantify these uncertainties or recover any person's measurements.
