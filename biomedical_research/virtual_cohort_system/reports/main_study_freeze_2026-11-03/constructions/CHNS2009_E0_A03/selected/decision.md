# CHNS2009 E0 A03: initial scientific decision

This is a whole-cohort completion scenario for the requested 3000 adults, not a recovery of individual measurements. The E0 packet contains no numerical relationship evidence. Marginal evidence therefore cannot identify any of the requested joint analyses. The candidate chooses a positive shared-Gamma law and makes every resulting dependence explicit as an assumption.

MIND-China provides the chosen BMI mean/individual SD of 25.0/3.6 kg/m^2, serum glucose mean/individual SD of 5.2/1.4 mmol/L and HbA1c mean/individual SD of 6.1/1.0 percent. Serum rather than plasma measurement and inclusion of known diabetes motivate using its marginal summaries. Its age range, rural and functional selection, measurement timing and assays differ from the requested analytical population. Equality of these model margins to those source point summaries is a transport assumption, not evidence about CHNS. MIND-China's 2049/3547 female fraction is likewise an assumed composition. Uniform integer age 35–74 uses only the allowed support. No diabetes, pregnancy or treatment exclusions are imposed.

Jansen's diabetes/anemia exclusion and western-European sample, Bao's exclusion of previously diagnosed diabetes, and their plasma glucose measurements make direct substitution uncertain. Their full marginal records remain contextual. The law does not pool their samples or reinterpret their quartiles as SDs. NGSP guidance is retained as unit context; no IFCC conversion is needed for the chosen HbA1c percent anchor. No source age summary is transported because none describes the task's complete-case age selection. All source estimate uncertainties remain unreported; individual SDs are used solely as variation within the chosen law.

A common Gamma factor explains assumed fractions 0.10, 0.65 and 0.65 of BMI, glucose and HbA1c variance. Those fractions and its shape 4 are choices made without source correlations, target observations, preferred coefficients or calibration draws. Independent Gamma residuals match the chosen marginal point anchors by algebra. This is a possible positive dependence scenario, not a physiological or causal model. Age and sex are independent of the biochemical law and of each other; absent reported associations do not establish this independence.

E1 and E2 are consequently fixed population projections of this assumed covariance structure. E3's population age coefficient is exactly zero by demographic independence. The retained analytic artifact derives those implications before any generated table is inspected. Agreement with a model implication does not validate a target coefficient. No empirical source replay is claimed; all target-table source comparisons are marked unavailable, and the E0 non-marginal uptake record is empty.

The law has positive biological-variable support and finite moments of every polynomial order, with no clipping, exponentiation or quadratic latent term. Residual shapes/scales are derived from scientific constants in the specification. The mathematical proof and deterministic moment script accompany this candidate. At this initial submission no generator has been imported or executed locally, and no rows or Monte Carlo draws have been produced by the author. Static contract checks and their actual retained results are recorded separately.

The initial candidate is submitted unfrozen for one optional controller trial (seed 5201) to inspect implementation and table diagnostics. Any later retention or scientific revision will cite actual feedback and preserve this candidate after freeze. Final candidate selection has not yet been made at this initial stage.

The table can support software exercises and examination of consequences of the declared assumptions. Relationships, subgroups, disease prevalence, causal analyses and national or clinical inference remain unvalidated. Repeated seeds describe the same assumed law and do not supply new clinical evidence or quantify source-estimate, model or transport uncertainty.
