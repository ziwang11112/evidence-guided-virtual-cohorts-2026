# Probability law and mathematical properties

Let the dataset contain fixed 375-row cells for each F in {0,1} and age interval [25,39], [40,54], [55,69], [70,79]. Within each cell each row has independent discrete-uniform integer age A. Each row independently draws B, G and H from the laws below, and these three draws are independent of A and F. Across rows the biological draws are independent; the fixed demographic allocation means rows are stratified, not identically distributed. Population moments here refer to a uniformly selected row from this equal-cell design.

For a positive marginal X with arithmetic mean m and SD s, define t=log(1+s^2/m^2), u=log(m)-t/2, and X=exp(u+sqrt(t) Z), with Z standard normal. BMI uses m=26.5,s=4.1; HbA1c uses m=5.6,s=0.3. For every finite real k, E[X^k]=exp(k*u+k^2*t/2), hence E[X]=m and E[X^2]=m^2+s^2. In particular both first and second moments, and all positive integer moments needed for polynomial cross-products, are finite. Exponentiation is of a linear Gaussian, not an unbounded quadratic; the lognormal moment formula therefore applies. Both variables are strictly positive. Their unbounded upper supports are distribution assumptions, not claimed observed limits.

For glucose let L=0,U=126,W=U-L, p=(m-L)/W, v=(s/W)^2, kappa=p(1-p)/v-1, alpha=p*kappa, beta=(1-p)*kappa, and Y~Beta(alpha,beta). Set G=L+W*Y. With m=91.8,s=9, alpha and beta are strictly positive. E[Y]=p and Var(Y)=p(1-p)/(alpha+beta+1)=v, so E[G]=m, E[G^2]=m^2+s^2. The continuous law has 0<G<126 almost surely. Its boundedness establishes finite moments of every order. The endpoint floating safeguard in generator.py is not a scientific winsorization: only an exact floating endpoint is replaced by its nearest interior representable value. Exact algebra refers to the continuous probability law before finite-precision representation; endpoint guarding can introduce minute rounding differences, which are not source calibration.

Age and female are bounded. For a uniform integer on l,...,u, E[A]=(l+u)/2 and Var(A)=((u-l+1)^2-1)/12. Average first/second moments over the four equally weighted bands. Female has mean 1/2 and variance 1/4 and is independent of age under the design mixture. The fixed quotas are structural; sample means and variances of biological variables are not forced to equal the population parameters.

By the product assumption, every distinct pair among A,F,B,G,H has zero covariance. This follows from model factorization, not missing source information. Cauchy-Schwarz (or factorization here) establishes finite mixed second moments. The biological variances are strictly positive, as are age and female variance. Consequently the covariance matrices of all predictor sets in Q1,Q2,Q3 are positive definite; adding an intercept gives a nonsingular population normal-equation matrix. The transformed predictor variances scale by 1/100 for age10 and glucose10 and 1/25 for bmi5.

For every requested model, Cov(X,H)=0 and the population slopes are zero. The intercept is E[H]=5.6 and the residual variance is Var(H)=0.09, so residual RMS is 0.3. Q2 minus Q3 is zero. For the secondary glucose10 projection on age10 and female, slopes are zero and the intercept is (91.8-100)/10. These are exact implications of this assumed law, not estimated empirical effects. Sample regressions vary with seed and are not guaranteed to have any particular sign, p-value or coefficient. They remain finite whenever the realized sample design has full rank; continuous biological draws imply full rank with probability one given the nondegenerate age/sex design. A seed-specific degeneracy would be an execution/analysis failure to retain.

The separately stated source context is the selected Lifelines Table 1 population. The same B,G,H marginal sublaw matches its selected source moment pair algebraically by construction; this is traceable numerical use of univariate evidence, not external validation. Source demographic/joint reconstruction is not claimed, and source age/sex moments are not substituted for task quotas. The retained analytic script reads model_spec.json and evidence.json and evaluates these formulas, including transformed units and alternate HbA1c representations. It performs no quadrature, random simulation or generator import.

Residual variation is the row-level distribution conditional on fixed parameters. External-estimate uncertainty is unreported and is not replaced by the source individual SD. Structural uncertainty includes independence and marginal family choices; transport uncertainty includes all source/target population and measurement differences. These uncertainty components are not quantified or pooled. Repeated rows and seeds generate no additional clinical evidence.
