# Scientific decision

This is an evidence-minimal product-law construction of 3000 fictional selected adults, with exactly 375 rows in each required sex/age cell. BMI and HbA1c use positive lognormal marginals, while glucose uses a scaled beta on the task-defined open interval (0,126) mg/dL. Their arithmetic means and SDs come from the admitted Jansen Table 1 packet; age composition and sex composition follow the task instead of the source. All scientific choices and values are in model_spec.json.

The packet gives no non-marginal numerical operations. I choose independent marginal completion as a sparse baseline, rather than claim an identified dependence law. Independence is a substantive assumption. In the mathematical model, all three requested association coefficients and the derived Q2 minus Q3 contrast are zero. These are model implications, not reported source results, not evidence of absence of biological association, and not target answers. The model is useful for marginal inspection and testing the mechanics of the prespecified analyses; inference about real adjusted or unadjusted relationships is unsupported. No causal or individual-measurement recovery claim is made.

Use the directly reported HbA1c percent mean 5.6 and SD 0.3 as one constraint pair. The IFCC row and its NGSP conversion are retained as alternative, rounded representations; they are neither pooled nor treated as independent measurements. Glucose uses the fixed factor 18.0. All biological marginal constraints arise from one Lifelines cohort. NGSP is a measurement standardization source, not an independent clinical cohort.

Transport is unvalidated: the source excludes significant anemia, differs in ancestry and age/sex composition, measures fasting plasma rather than the task's fasting serum, and lacks the task's exact 8-12-hour fasting and measurement-route selection. The selected population is modeled directly, without creating unsupported diagnosis, anemia or medication fields. The product structure, lognormal/beta shapes and integer-uniform within-band ages remain explicit assumptions.

The retained deterministic analytic script derives the generator's transformed parameters, marginal first/second moments, population covariance and requested model projections. It never imports or runs generator.py and contains no random or Monte Carlo calls. Source-context moment checks use the very same biological marginal sublaw under a stated transport assumption, without reconstructing a source joint population. All target-as-source feedback entries are unavailable.

No exploratory execution is requested. The construction contains no fitting, sample rescaling, target-dependent tuning, or favorable-seed selection. Static validation and analytic feasibility are sufficient to commit this initial scientific configuration; execution and all fixed final seeds remain controller work. Successful static checks do not demonstrate executable output validity. Final choice is initial_final, subject to truthful recording of controller execution failures. The author does not freeze or independently review this candidate.
