# Scientific decision record

This candidate reconstructs the published HRS source model. It uses the full reported conditional law because that law directly supplies the requested joint baseline covariates and longitudinal memory analysis. No participant records are provided or inferred, and reproducing this law is not an independent validation of the HRS population.

Generate each subject's age/sex/race cell categorically from its reported count; draw age uniformly inside its original bin. Draw childhood SES from the reported Gaussian linear model, education from the two reported multinomial logits versus College, and HbA1c from the reported Gamma log-link model with shape=1/dispersion and scale=mean*dispersion. The exact within-bin age distribution is unknown, especially in the broad 80–100-year bin; the source method's uniform rule is retained.

For each of four visits at years 0, 2, 4 and 6, retain baseline sex, race, education, SES and HbA1c, update age and age60, and use all 16 reported memory fixed effects. The HbA1c basis is U65=min(HbA1c−6.5,0) and O65=max(HbA1c−6.5,0). Use jointly normal subject intercept/slope effects, and independent normal visit errors. Female, white and College are the reference categories. The random slope multiplies age60, including its baseline offset; it does not multiply time since entry alone.

Use the printed variance values 0.373813, 0.007483 and 0.576884 and derive standard deviations by square root. The intercept-slope covariance is 0.28*sqrt(0.373813*0.007483). This preserves the variance column's printed precision, whereas squaring the separately rounded SD column would produce slightly different values. Exact unrounded source values are unavailable. The source's extra age70 variable is irrelevant to the HRS memory formula and is omitted. The source mem.s output is already on its fitted standardized scale, so no sample restandardization is applied.

Every scientific numerical parameter is in model_spec.json and bound to reported evidence or an explicit assumption. The same HRS sample underlies the source files and paper tables; these do not constitute independent confirmations. Reported coefficient SEs are retained in evidence, but the generator treats point estimates as a fixed law. Full coefficient covariance matrices are unavailable, so joint parameter uncertainty is not sampled. Subject residual variation and variation across final random seeds are not parameter or transport uncertainty.

The subsequently admitted paper Table 2/3 transcription was read only to document limitations. Published original/synthetic discrepancies in age, HbA1c subgroup distributions, education and baseline memory warn against claiming distributional equivalence from regression-effect reproduction. No law or parameter was changed to match those rounded synthetic averages. Complete balanced follow-up intentionally yields 46,036 visits instead of the observed 43,500; attrition, mortality, informative missingness and time-varying glycemia are unmodeled. Therefore original SEs and tail distributions need not be reproduced even under the intended conditional law.

The admitted files do not fully describe eligibility, clinical selection, childhood SES construction, exact memory instrument/normalization, biomarker measurement error, higher moments, omitted predictors, or the true within-bin demographic distribution. These missing facts prevent strong transport, causal, clinical prediction, missing-data, subgroup-tail or measurement-saving claims.

Preparation is restricted to source extraction, static code/contract checks and analytic identities. No HRS-configured data table was generated; the generator was not imported or called; no clinical model was fit. No simulated-source table or declarative source-statistic replay is submitted because that would require prohibited preparation-stage HRS generation, and the reported mixed-model coefficients are not relabelled as OLS checks. The coordinator will perform isolated final generation after freeze. There is no source-feedback improvement claim and no output/effect-driven revision. Stopping reason: the admitted source law is fully specified to its published precision and the requested candidate is ready for fixed-seed execution, subject to the actual static/analytic check record.
