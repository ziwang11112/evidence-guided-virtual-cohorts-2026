## Core argument

Scientific requests and study-qualified partial evidence become executable joint laws and analyzable virtual cohorts without individual-level construction records.

The revised narrative begins with the data-access and missing-variable problem, then explains the scientific difficulty: heterogeneous study findings do not directly specify one joint distribution. Three central agent decisions address that difficulty: qualifying evidence against the request, translating its statistical meaning into numerical constraints, and completing an executable joint law. Execution and inspection make the consequences testable using ordinary scientific analyses.

The agent is responsible for task-specific equations, distributions and parameter derivations; the researcher supplies the scientific request and access conditions. Whole-cohort construction without individual inputs remains the principal demonstrated mode. Established probability families provide the sampling machinery. The manuscript does not claim the first agent-based synthesis method, a new distribution, a new estimator, or priority for generation without individual records.

## Claim discipline without repeated caveat paragraphs

The main results lead with what the data establish. The E0/E1 section reports improvement for five analyses and the worsening CHNS BMI result. The cross-task section reports point-direction agreement, then retains the effect-size, residual-dependence and dispersion discrepancies that constrain its use. The summary section states clearly that relevant values were supplied and agreement measures reconstruction. The open-retrieval and longitudinal examples are supporting capabilities under their own input conditions.

The shared design paragraph carries exposure and replication boundaries. The discussion states that the current cross-task outputs are analysis-specific, assumption-dependent scenario data; it does not permanently narrow the general method objective. It also records that all six initial cross-task laws were retained and all 132 runtime source checks were unavailable, so no source-fit feedback or revision benefit is established. No claim is made of universal statistical superiority, fully autonomous end-to-end operation, independent clinical replication, calibrated power/design use or replacement of measured cohorts.
