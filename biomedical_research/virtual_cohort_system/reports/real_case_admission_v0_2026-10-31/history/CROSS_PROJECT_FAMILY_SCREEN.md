`Outcome exposure` means documented previous viewing/use of study relationships or outcomes, including published summaries; it does not mean that this screen inspected participant outcomes. `No documented` is bounded evidence, not a proof of global/pretraining non-exposure. Metadata counts, schema and file sizes alone are not outcome exposure.
