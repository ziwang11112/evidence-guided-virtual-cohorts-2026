## Exposure must be assessed at the right level

**PNS:** The prior index documents a revealed confirmation/development target. Under the current admission rules, a changed endpoint, subgroup, filename or reacquisition does not reset this history. PNS is closed to fresh confirmation in this unit. This is a restriction on its evidential role, not a claim that PNS is scientifically unusable for all future authorized work.

**ANSUR II:** The old note explicitly records incidental sex-specific marginal means/percentiles and does not establish prior joint-data analysis. That prevents an unqualified claim of blindness. Evaluations of those exposed marginals, corresponding sex contrasts, or quantities algebraically recoverable from them cannot be labelled unexposed. It does **not** prove that conditional dependence, multivariate accommodation or paired self-report-error estimands were already disclosed. Whether any such use qualifies requires a prospective estimand and exposure/equivalence map, plus source, measurement and access review. A strict clean-blind rule can hold/reject the current admission on the documented exposure; an exposure-aware descriptive project must be explicitly justified and labelled, not silently substituted for fresh confirmation. This review does not grant that exception.

**CHNS:** Prior approved source-search packets contained result-bearing abstracts on urate/beta-cell/insulin-resistance relationships and BMI/lipid/cognition relationships. No individual data were established as previously inspected in the reviewed record. Those known relationships and their equivalent consequences cannot be presented as unseen confirmation. Exposure to these abstracts does not establish that every possible CHNS variable or estimand is contaminated. Conversely, absence of IPD access does not make the abstract exposure disappear. The current access note additionally logs incidental result-bearing dysregulation text; the controller should record its scope without reopening results merely to recover numerical detail.

The cross-project screen's family-level flags are useful warning indicators. They should not replace the analysis-level decision. For each proposed analysis, record the prior exposure type, variables/population/wave where known, affected relation or equivalence class, and allowed evidential role. Unknown scope should remain unknown rather than being promoted to clean status. A different agent does not reset the project's exposure history.
