## Measurement implications already established

The official [biomarker methods table](https://chns.cpc.unc.edu/wp-content/uploads/Biomarker_Methods.pdf) distinguishes central and field measurements. For example, `GLUCOSE` and `Y48_1` are different measurement fields, and HbA1c is `Y50` with multiple listed instruments. This prevents treating every similarly named field as the same observation. Documented assay units and laboratory reference ranges are not empirical population means, standard deviations or probability distributions. None were inserted into a generator.

Detailed release version, identifiers, eligible person count, age/sex/anthropometry joins, missing-value codes, repeated waves, selection, and uncertainty handling are still unverified for an acquired file. Metadata alone cannot complete those checks.
