# Local readiness and minimal nearest-method protocol review

5 November 2026. Design review only. This unit read protocols, code, manifests, source-admission records and existing aggregate reports. It did not read participant or generated rows, import scientific evaluators, refit references, call a model for construction, generate data or run tests. The proposed experiment below is not an execution release.

## Recommendation

Use one **paper-based LLMSynthor reimplementation with an explicitly named biomedical evidence adapter** as the principal nearest-method comparison, conditional on a pre-performance fidelity and input-compatibility review. Compare it with the current evidence-qualified law-authoring workflow on the existing ENSANUT glycemia and Turkana lipid requests. Use **five fresh end-to-end episodes per method per request: 20 scheduled final tables, each with 3,000 rows**. The native LLMSynthor cumulative table is its final output. Give our workflow one final delivery per episode, without post-freeze Monte Carlo replicates in this main comparison.

This is a compact developmental comparison of two synthesis workflows given the same admitted external evidence. It can establish useful analytical output and document how the workflows turn evidence into that output. It cannot establish fresh population transport, isolate the causal contribution of an individual component, or establish that Agents outperform every purpose-built statistical model. A direct analysis Agent is outside this data-product question.

Do not repeat the conventional CHNS R30 comparison. It was completed on 3 November; the recommendation in `docs/FAIR_PREDECESSOR_COMPARISON_PLAN_2026-11-03.md` section 8 is a historical plan, superseded by `work/summary_comparator_2026-11-03/RESULTS.md`. The completed conventional law and historical Agent laws all achieved close supplied-summary reconstruction. Their unequal construction budgets and the conventional family's bespoke design prevent a general ranking. Retain this result as evidence that summary calibration is shared, not as a reason to build an indefinitely stronger statistical adversary.

### Exact biomedical output and estimator contracts

**ENSANUT2018.** Generate `row_id`, integer age 25–79, binary female, positive BMI, glucose strictly between 0 and 126 mg/dL, and positive HbA1c in percent. Produce exactly 375 synthetic people in each female-by-age-band cell, with bands 25–39, 40–54, 55–69 and 70–79. No individual construction inputs are supplied. The target is the existing selected analytical population, not a nationally representative Mexican population; preserve its documented fasting, diabetes-history, anthropometry and pregnancy/lactation restrictions.

The primary estimands are `Q1_age10` from HbA1c ~ 1 + age10 + female + bmi5 + glucose10; `Q2_bmi5` from HbA1c ~ 1 + age10 + female + bmi5; and `Q3_bmi5` from the full model. Transformations are `(age-50)/10`, `(BMI-25)/5` and `(glucose-100)/10`. Reference analysis uses equal age-sex-cell WLS with 1/8 mass per cell. Exact generated quotas make generated weights equal, but this does not authorize replacing the reference estimator. Retain all 28 secondary diagnostics, including Q2-minus-Q3, the glucose coefficient, residual RMS and population-SD marginal summaries.

**Turkana.** Generate unique `id`, age at least 18, literal sex category F/M, positive BMI and positive TC/HDL in mmol/L. Do not add fasting, disease, outlier or TC >= HDL restrictions. Keep F/M as admitted codes without an invented biological legend. Evaluation multiplies lipids by 38.67 and uses unweighted intercept-including OLS with age10, female indicator `I(sex == 'F')` and bmi5. The primary estimands are the HDL and TC bmi5 coefficients in mg/dL per 5 kg/m² and the Pearson correlation of the two full-model residual vectors. The cached reference applies common finite complete-case criteria and positivity; invalid synthetic delivery rows must not be silently discarded. Retain all 23 generated diagnostic values; compare only the two matching sample SDs, with `ddof=1`.

## Freeze the demonstrated method, not a broader promised system

The demonstrated current entry is a host-dispatched scientific author operating the `evidence_use_cross_task_v1` workflow, with `semantic_feedback_v1` and `evidence_use_v1`, whole-cohort program generation, independent scientific review and isolated numerical execution. Its candidate interface is `generate(observed, specification, seed)`, with `observed` absent. The actual procedure uses task/request/source packets; source proposals and review; model/evidence/specification/code and use-record authoring; freeze; bounded generation and allowed feedback; retain/revise/stop; final delivery; closed-author evaluation.

`cohort_harness.request_intake` validates structured requests and prepares a handoff. It is not a natural-language parser or a model launcher. `cohort_harness.scientific_loop` is not an autonomous retrieval-and-edit engine. The cross-task `runner.py` explicitly has no model launcher or reference reader and hard-codes six authors, three per case, five seed slots and 30 executions. Therefore it cannot be invoked unchanged for the proposed 20-episode comparison. A new derivative controller must preserve old files while binding the new policy and both output routes.

The frozen method label should be **“evidence-qualified Agent law construction, cross-task v1, fixed curated evidence”**. Changing retrieval from optional to zero is a declared experimental restriction. Removing old three-draw delivery in favor of one final draw is a declared evaluation change. Do not call a newly added source optimizer, automatic evidence acquisition or structural sensitivity engine part of this frozen method without a separate version. ## LLMSynthor fidelity and input gate before biomedical performance

The inspected local predecessor audit and the current companion method audit identify no executable official release. Call the implementation a **paper-based reimplementation**, cite the exact paper version, and bind its implementation choices. Do not call it an official-code replication. A failed local implementation is not evidence that the published method fails.

The fidelity gate should be completed on invented schema, histogram and contingency-table fixtures before biomedical episodes. It must establish the named mechanisms: dependency grouping; fixed supplied macro-statistic access; discrepancy calculation over the cumulative data; bounded category/range/count proposals; the declared within-proposal sampler; append-only accumulation; and one native final cumulative table. Resolve the published ambiguities about grouping schedule, integer boundaries, within-box dependence, proposal parsing/repair and exact row-count handling. Preserve those choices in a table against paper sections and appendix pseudocode. A reviewer must distinguish a bug from a deliberate adaptation. No clinical output or reference discrepancy should choose among those choices.

**CHNS R30 is not a substitute for this gate.** R30 supplies means, SDs, selected quantiles, adjusted regression coefficients and Pearson/Spearman correlations. It does not supply complete marginal histograms or joint frequency tables. Inventing a joint table from these statistics imports a distributional model; it is not parsing. A later optional R30 reconstruction demonstration would need its own declared moment-to-macro assumptions and would test that extension, not the unmodified native algorithm. There is no need to spend biomedical rows on that demonstration before the two-case experiment.

The principal input problem is similarly real: heterogeneous source-qualified slopes, correlations and dispersions are not a single target-population macro table. A credible adaptation needs an explicit **evidence-to-macro interface**. Give both workflows identical source facts and accessible originals/extractions. A common neutral preprocessing layer may normalize spelling/units, transcribe reported values, and preserve source population, eligibility, assay, transform, adjustment, weighting, uncertainty and provenance. It must not supply inferred target histograms, joint probabilities, missing SDs, source-mixture weights or derived target coefficients.

The LLMSynthor adapter then records, for every macro constraint: reported compatible quantity; algebraic unit conversion; assumed transfer; assumed distribution/quantization; or unavailable. Its own LLM can make the admissible approximation choices allowed by the paper, but those are method decisions rather than observed targets. Unavailable target sub-bin queries stay unavailable. The original workflow makes its evidence-to-law decisions from the same packet. Both methods receive the intended analyses; neither receives expected answers.

Do not place our law compiler or our authored joint law invisibly in front of LLMSynthor. A shared inferred-law-to-histogram compiler would make this a comparison of samplers conditional on that compiler, and remove much of the proposed scientific distinction. Conversely, silently treating every external statistic as a target statistic would handicap the baseline through an invalid interface. If the adapter cannot preserve native proposal sampling while stating these scientific choices, stop at “input incompatibility under this adaptation.” Do not turn the resulting failure into a headline quality win.

The main comparison is therefore an adapted **workflow** comparison, not an isolated sampler experiment. Report interface differences and evidence uptake beside final quality. G-Sim is optional only if this gate rejects LLMSynthor and a pre-result decision selects a single alternative; it must not be added after seeing an unfavorable LLMSynthor result. Its calibration would likewise be limited to admitted, computable information, without hidden target access.

The model ceiling is equal; actual compute need not be. A native proposal sampler spends calls inside accumulation, while a law author spends calls writing/reviewing code and can produce rows cheaply after freezing. Enforcing one revision for both would distort LLMSynthor; granting unlimited model calls to one would confound the comparison. The current-method pathway above permits at most 90,000 generated rows across its ten episodes, including development draws; native LLMSynthor produces at most 30,000 cumulative rows across its ten episodes. These are transparent method-specific native allocations within a common per-episode maximum of 9,000 sampled rows, not equal realized sampling work. If equal row expenditure becomes the scientific question, that is a different controlled experiment. All partial or invalid rows and failed calls remain in the cost ledger.

## Primary estimands and useful-data evidence

The primary panel is the three frozen ENSANUT estimands and the three frozen lipid estimands. For every episode and endpoint, show the final estimate, sample-reference estimate, signed difference and absolute difference in native units. Plot all five episode points per method and case; summarize mean and median absolute discrepancy and their episode ranges. Keep delivery/analysis availability as a separate outcome. Failed episodes remain in the scheduled denominator and are not assigned a fictitious numerical error or silently removed from a success-only ranking.

For a method comparison, the descriptive contrast is the difference between the two methods' mean absolute reference discrepancies **for each endpoint separately**. Report the two means and all ten underlying values. With five episodes and two exposed cases, formal global superiority, pooled patient-level tests and an overall heterogeneous-unit score are not justified. Do not manufacture pairing by matching episode ordinals. Correlations, slopes and marginal SDs are not interchangeable metrics; sign agreement is secondary.

The scientific data-product claim is that one final table supports every requested analysis using ordinary analysis code. Preserve reduced versus glucose-adjusted BMI behavior, HbA1c residual RMS, lipid residual dependence and dispersion, rather than selecting the single closest slope. This demonstrates the consequences of a completed joint law beyond isolated verbal estimates. No direct-analysis-Agent contest is required, and neither workflow must outperform every handcrafted statistical law to make that contribution.

For each primary endpoint attach a compact source-to-output trace: which source findings were admitted; which exact operator/scale they had; which derived values or macro constraints entered the construction; which unreported relation remained assumed; and which part of the output analysis was therefore a supplied constraint versus an unprovided implication. Count numerical uptake only where an executed parameter/proposal path exists. A lengthy trace alone is not a quality advantage, so place these facts beside the analytical results.

A favorable method difference supports the relative performance of these complete implementations under this packet and budget. It does not isolate source conditioning, reasoning, sampling representation or feedback as the cause. The existing E0/E1 contrast and worked source derivations can support the paper's broader evidence-use story. A new component ablation is optional for a stronger mechanism claim and should not be silently added to this small comparison. In particular, the six historical unchanged laws and 132 unavailable checks cannot be cited as evidence that revision improved quality.

## Reference variability without new patient analysis

References are selected-sample estimates, not known population truth. Display the existing ENSANUT empirical cluster-resampling stability bands and Turkana slope HC3 sensitivity intervals with their original labels. The TC slope interval crosses zero; matching its positive point estimate is not validation of a confidently positive population effect. There is no cached lipid residual-correlation interval. Do not manufacture one or reuse another endpoint's interval.

Separate variation among complete construction episodes from generated Monte Carlo variation, source-estimate uncertainty, unidentified-law/transport uncertainty and real-reference sampling uncertainty. The main design's five episodes mix LLM construction and within-episode sampling variability; it does not estimate those components separately. Larger synthetic row counts would not remove source or transport uncertainty.

The completed CHNS real-real study is useful existing evidence for the paper's evaluation rationale. Its 100 community-disjoint resplits at matched row counts must remain a separate CHNS context panel. Neither a CHNS median discrepancy nor an ENSANUT confidence-band width can be relabeled as a Turkana real-real error distribution. Interval overlap is descriptive, not equivalence. No exact-match demand, retrospective pass line, arbitrary relative error near zero or inferred clinical acceptability margin should be introduced.

## Exposure, stopping and reproducibility

Both primary reference cohorts are previously exposed developmental targets. New author contexts should receive only the fixed task/evidence and generated/source feedback, but this procedural separation does not reset project exposure or prove pretraining non-exposure. All old models, rankings, target aggregates and target-derived articles stay on the evaluator side. Existing closed laws remain byte-preserved; new runs are newly named benchmark episodes. No new-confirmation language is admissible.

Complete the fidelity gate, freeze one implementation per method plus all input/endpoint/budget manifests, and then run the scheduled episodes once. Run order may be randomized before dispatch to reduce service-order effects. Do not expand cases, constructors, families, bins, tokens, seeds or evidence because of the observed ordering. An incompatible interface stops before the biomedical performance phase; an exhausted budget or invalid final output is a reported episode failure. After aggregate verification, close regardless of which method appears closer. A negative or inconclusive comparison remains useful evidence of the limits of this implementation and information regime.

Release original implementation code, neutral extraction/schema definitions, source citations and admissible factual extracts, exact prompts, paper-to-code decision table, model settings, proposal/decision records sufficient to audit operation, environment lock, seeds/substreams, all call/repair/failure receipts and complete endpoint aggregates. Keep third-party full texts and patient/generated rows local under existing policy. Provide an aggregate-only result reproduction command and, where source permissions permit, a separate fresh-run construction entry. These are different levels of reproducibility and should be named separately.

## Concrete implementation blockers before execution

1. **No executable LLMSynthor implementation is bound.** The exact paper version, algorithm-to-code specification, proposal schema, sampler assumptions and fidelity review have to exist before performance claims. The current repository/archive inspection is not such an implementation.
2. **No honest source-evidence-to-macro contract is frozen.** The external packets are operator-qualified heterogeneous findings, not native target frequency tables. The adapter must preserve reported/derived/assumed/unavailable status without borrowing our joint-law compiler. Its inability to do this is a compatibility result, not a failed sampler score.
3. **The existing runner hard-codes the wrong experimental unit.** `runner.protocol()` requires six authors, three per case, three final seeds and exactly 30 executions; `initialize()` hard-codes optional retrieval and revision budgets. The new controller needs 20 end-to-end episode identities, one final slot each, zero retrieval and method-specific native execution accounting. Do not edit the closed runner in place.
4. **Direct cumulative rows are not covered by this batch's program-only author path.** The generic harness accepts direct rows, but the cross-task pilot explicitly excludes that route. The LLMSynthor path needs a first-class cumulative-proposal execution receipt and immutable final-table closure, without fake repeated seed slots. Existing `id` versus `row_id` differences must remain explicit.
5. **Model dispatch and resource accounting are external today.** Request intake and the runner do not launch a model. The selected model/version, stochastic settings, context isolation, finite token/call budget and complete usage/repair log must be bound through an actual dispatcher before running. Historical adapter seconds cannot fill this gap.
6. **The common packet must be rebuilt from the curated admission manifests.** Existing H/L bundles now also contain author additions, generated feedback and frozen decisions. Copying whole bundles would leak unequal information. The glycemia and lipid curated hashes above, source files and measurement metadata supply a concrete starting list.
7. **The evaluator is reusable scientifically but not mechanically.** It imports the six-author runner, loops over three `final_seeds`, emits `scheduled_slots=3` and labels fixed-law seed summaries. A derivative must preserve estimator functions/reference selectors and the closure-before-read rule, but calculate five independent episode summaries and missing-episode status. The 21 unavailable lipid diagnostic references must stay unavailable.
8. **No matched ENSANUT/Turkana real-real resplit distribution is verified.** This is a reporting limitation, not a requirement to reopen patient rows. Reuse current reference bands and the separate CHNS context; do not invent a new acceptance scale.

These blockers concern a concrete, bounded reimplementation and comparison. They do not require a new clinical dataset, a direct analysis Agent, a larger bespoke statistical benchmark, new source retrieval, new reference fits or a new uncertainty methodology.
