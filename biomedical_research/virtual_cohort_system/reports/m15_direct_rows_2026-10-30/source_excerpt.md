The adult cross-sectional cohort consists of 3,402 nondiabetic participants examined once, selected by combining the three adult surveys in Bogalusa, Louisiana. The reported adult survey ages span 20–56 years. The study's race and sex labels are retained as published descriptive strata; they are not biological causal mechanisms.
