# s1: initial scientific decision

Additive conditional arithmetic mean with lognormal individual noise. One raw-scale BMI slope and sex-specific linear age slopes; constant residual CV. Effects add in arithmetic mean, so BMI slope remains constant away from positive regularization.

Oh reduced slope and sex-specific moments directly anchor the local mean and residual variance. Female age increase and positive skew can accommodate the Bansal location/rank summaries without treating their marginal correlations as conditional coefficients. This is a rationale for proposing the structure, not a numerical compatibility finding. No generator has been executed by this builder.

The evidence and source replay recipe are identical across all three candidates. Oh Table 1 values are arithmetic marginal means and individual SDs; Table 3 is the age/sex-adjusted BMI slope. Table 4, mixed-model Table 5 (SE, not SD), and co-twin Table 6 are different estimands and remain unreplayed. Table 2 summary type is ambiguous and remains reported_other. Bansal fasting evidence includes original quantiles (convention unreported), actual cumulative category fractions, marginal Spearman correlations and covariate means/SDs conditional on generated TG groups. None is silently substituted for a conditional TG regression coefficient.

Source reconstruction uses only published covariate marginals and explicit independence/shape assumptions. It never supplies an outcome or a source TG tertile assignment to a row. All source-context and check assumption descriptions are fixed; shared source/measurement caveats are in sa.json. The legacy_proxy mapping is particularly uncertain for Oh because glycerol blanking is unspecified. The target assay equation is task-provided technical context and is applied after biological generation.

Limits: Linear age response, no raw-mean interaction, distribution transport and independent source covariates are not identified by the sources. Positive floor affects unsupported low-mean combinations. Raw-scale OLS may agree while TG quantile asymmetry, rare tails or female age-TG rank correlation and tertile ages disagree; increasing CV changes both tail mass and rank associations. Published uncertainty is retained in evidence but is not treated as calibrated parameter uncertainty. The seed varies individual draws only.
