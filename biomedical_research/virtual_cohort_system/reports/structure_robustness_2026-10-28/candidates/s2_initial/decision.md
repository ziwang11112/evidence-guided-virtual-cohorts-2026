# s2: initial scientific decision

Saturating multiplicative log-location model with smooth female age transition. BMI enters a bounded nonlinear function of log TG; female age enters a sigmoid. Arithmetic-scale BMI effects depend on age/sex and saturate at high BMI.

Positive skew, Table 2 qualitative female age transition and high-BMI flattening, plus Bansal fasting quantiles motivate this alternative. Source moment-equivalent medians and approximate local slope anchor it. This is a rationale for proposing the structure, not a numerical compatibility finding. No generator has been executed by this builder.

The evidence and source replay recipe are identical across all three candidates. Oh Table 1 values are arithmetic marginal means and individual SDs; Table 3 is the age/sex-adjusted BMI slope. Table 4, mixed-model Table 5 (SE, not SD), and co-twin Table 6 are different estimands and remain unreplayed. Table 2 summary type is ambiguous and remains reported_other. Bansal fasting evidence includes original quantiles (convention unreported), actual cumulative category fractions, marginal Spearman correlations and covariate means/SDs conditional on generated TG groups. None is silently substituted for a conditional TG regression coefficient.

Source reconstruction uses only published covariate marginals and explicit independence/shape assumptions. It never supplies an outcome or a source TG tertile assignment to a row. All source-context and check assumption descriptions are fixed; shared source/measurement caveats are in sa.json. The legacy_proxy mapping is particularly uncertain for Oh because glycerol blanking is unspecified. The target assay equation is task-provided technical context and is applied after biological generation.

Limits: The plateau width and age transition are unreported; they are not measured menopausal state. One lognormal family may miss observed extreme tail/category counts. Marginal-to-conditional centering is approximate. May better represent age curvature and TG quantiles while changing raw-scale BMI OLS, sex-specific SDs and high-BMI extrapolation. Same source diagnostics expose these tradeoffs without selecting endpoints. Published uncertainty is retained in evidence but is not treated as calibrated parameter uncertainty. The seed varies individual draws only.
