# Calibration history and scope

The current C3 source-only whole-cohort work adapts an existing numerical calibration mechanism to two source contexts. It must not be presented as the first implementation, first test, or first actual source fitting in this repository.

Evidence inspected: `cohort_harness/calibration.py`, `docs/BOUNDED_CALIBRATION.md`, and the M11/M12/M13 milestone summaries only. No patient tables, generated tables, raw target results, old generator programs, or archived source-fit trial artifacts were opened. The summary-document read inadvertently included M13-B target numerical results; this was disclosed to the controller. Those results are excluded from this review's scientific judgments, and this reviewer has no candidate-authoring role.

- **M11:** source marginal calibration and feedback-driven numerical revisions had already run. The summary documents source executions and source-calibrated versions. Its existence alone rules out a claim that numerical source fitting had never occurred.
- **M12-A:** independent source contexts and isolated replay were implemented, while the general numerical calibration session was still a stated next step. This historical statement applies to M12-A, not the present repository.
- **M12-B:** `calibration.py` implemented bounded coordinate search over whitelisted scalar parameters, immutable trial bundles, all-constraint scaled squared discrepancy, infeasibility for missing statistics, fixed budget reservation, and fit-only selection sealed before disjoint replay seeds. The milestone documents 26 actual parameter trials and 56 isolated executions across compatible, contradictory, and undefined invented fixtures; it reports 97 passing new calibration tests plus one environment-related skip. These are historical reported checks, not rerun here.
- **M12-C:** an actual externally launched scientific Agent constructed a source model, reviewed feedback, and retained/promoted its initial vector. Only one vector was evaluated and no numerical parameter changed; this is integration evidence, not a demonstrated optimization improvement in that fixture.
- **M13-A:** real-source numerical fitting ran for two constructed models: two sessions, eighteen trials, eighty generator executions, and subsequent scientific retention/promotion. The selected parameters changed, though neither session reached its declared fitting threshold. Two-seed replay was source simulation stability checking, not a new source cohort or validation of transfer.

The existing implementation preserves the scientific formula, evidence, source populations, operators, adjustment sets, and unselected parameters across trials. Its loss is an engineering discrepancy objective, not a likelihood, confidence interval, clinical-equivalence criterion, or proof of identified joint structure. Local bounded search does not establish a global optimum or scientific incompatibility when its trial budget ends.

The present increment is the source-only whole-cohort adapter, its integration boundary, and the comparison of two predeclared scientific structures under the same fixed source populations and unreported covariance assumptions. It is not a new general fitting invention, target admission, target-performance optimization, API/model training, or validation of individual measurements. The controller reports that Cycle 2 itself made no numerical-fit action; that narrow statement must remain distinct from the earlier repository history above.

It will check units, adjustment sets, source populations, projection-versus-conditional interpretation, fixed unknown covariance, bounded parameter and loss-scale choices, implementable acceptance criteria, structural distinctions, selection before independent-seed replay, final freeze, and source-operator preservation.
