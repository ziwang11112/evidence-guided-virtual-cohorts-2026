## Actual access gate

Clicking the public codebook's Adobe PDF download opened a **Dataset Terms** dialog with an **Accept** button. The page requires analysis-only use, protection from misuse, no identification/contact of participants, communities or service providers, no identifying publication/reporting, notification of unauthorized use/disclosure, and specified citation/acknowledgments. An as-is disclaimer also appears. The complete text remains accessible through the official release's Terms tab/custom-license link.

Accept was not clicked. No personal form, login or controlled-access application was submitted. The browser confirmation rule requires action-time confirmation for accepting a legally binding agreement; ordinary authorization to retrieve public data is not treated as automatic acceptance. This is an action-specific consent gate, not a finding that CHNS is scientifically unusable. Do not bypass it through a raw API or a redistribution.

The live wang Chrome codebook tab was marked for handoff, and a local screenshot `terms_confirmation.jpg` was saved for the user's review. It is excluded from public export. No individual record appears in the captured terms dialog; the document thumbnail in the background was not used as source evidence.
