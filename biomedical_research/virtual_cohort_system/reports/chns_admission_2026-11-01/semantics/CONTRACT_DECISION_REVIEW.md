## Released-data reference

The proposal's distinction between a release-defined reference and underlying population truth is sound (lines 74–82). An exhaustive catalogue of unknowable positive sentinels is not a feasible prerequisite. Conversely, failure to find a code is not evidence that it cannot occur. The residual release-quality assumption must be retained in the final interpretation, including if a result is favorable.

Four details need a concrete frozen decision before F2:

1. **Specify the actual analyzed population.** “All-comers” means no added medication, diabetes or pregnancy exclusion; it does not erase age/wave eligibility, linkage losses or required-field availability. The report should identify the released-data eligible, linked, observed subset. Freeze whether E1/E2/E3 share one complete-case denominator or each uses its own required fields. These choices change estimands and cannot be selected after seeing results.
2. **Freeze arithmetic and measurement-domain rules.** Native missing values, finite values, the documented GENDER 1/2 mapping and exact flag actions are appropriate. “Mathematically undefined BMI” alone is insufficient: a negative height still yields a number after squaring, and a negative weight yields a finite BMI. Require finite, strictly positive height and weight for BMI, explicitly as the declared analysis domain rather than an invented missing-code dictionary. Do not add target-derived plausibility thresholds or blanket 88/99 masks.
3. **Retain released corrections without certifying them.** Accepting corrected, averaged or extrapolated released values is a reference policy. Do not apply the original Jin conversion again, replace height again, or exclude every positive cleaning flag. A flag labelled “set missing” can support the declared missing action, with any inconsistent retained value recorded as a quality issue. Missing or unlisted flags are not a guarantee of clinical correctness.
4. **Predeclare post-reveal failure handling.** The proposal properly prohibits trying alternative recodings against target effects. Make the consequence explicit: preserve the failed projection and withhold the affected estimand, or stop when required quantities cannot be interpreted. A later corrected analysis must be labelled post-reveal. Reference missingness/flag counts must not return to constructors or motivate an unbudgeted replacement campaign.

The recovered category labels improve interpretability, but do not prove that the legacy codebook exhausts current release codes. Current field names and units remain anchored to the actual file headers and reviewed methods. Serum/plasma differences, individual fasting adherence and source exclusions remain transport/measurement limitations; identity or numerical-scale ambiguity remains a blocker for the affected quantity.

**Correction requested in the new measurement-policy decision:** its initial GLUCOSE/HbA1c rule requires positive values. OLS does not mathematically require that restriction, and reviewed metadata do not establish how zero or below-detection results were represented. A positive-only selection could be adopted as an additional explicit analysis-domain assumption, but it is not decoded missingness or an implication of the assay documentation. Prefer finite released values for numerical availability, with nonpositive results handled as a predeclared quality concern. If their coding remains essential and uninterpretable, withhold the affected estimand under the frozen failure rule rather than silently drop/recode observations. For BMI, finite positive height/weight remains an appropriate physical measurement-domain rule; positivity is not solely a division-by-zero requirement. The policy otherwise incorporates the subset, denominator, OTHER, correction-retention and post-reveal qualifications above; F2 still needs the concrete eligibility and denominator choices.

## Conditional equal-weight correlation proxy

The proposed exception is reasonable for an explicitly named Pearson or Spearman estimator whose analytic weighting is unreported (lines 50–60). It is an approved interpretation assumption, not newly discovered provenance. The strict implementation currently marks unknown/weighted estimators unavailable at [semantic_feedback_v1.py](../../../cohort_harness/semantic_feedback_v1.py), lines 252–260; its declared-operator equality, units and type gates remain binding at lines 235–249. The existing [source_checks.py](../../../cohort_harness/source_checks.py), lines 171–181, computes ordinary Pearson and average-tie Spearman on the selected complete pairs. Missingness behavior is separately enforced at lines 148–157.

The main fail-open risks to reject in the coming code review are:

- Substituting `weights: none` into a strict source sidecar, upgrading strict availability, falling back after strict structural rejection, or allowing known-weighted/quarantined/noncorrelation evidence through the exception.
- Treating equal-weight approval as approval of every unknown. Freeze separate source-selection and post-analysis/missingness assumptions. A complete generated distribution is not reconstruction of MIND's unknown imputation effects. Known role, unit, adjustment, selection-operator or transform mismatches must not be excused by a weighting assumption.
- Relabelling the target task or serum variable as the actual source population/plasma measurement. Preserve source and target population, assay and timing descriptions, plus the transport assumptions, in the separate report and revision feedback. A computed proxy discrepancy can guide construction under those assumptions; reducing it does not demonstrate transport validity or biomedical improvement.
- Reporting partial results before validating all declarations, or binding only prose approval while allowing evidence, recipe, output or engine bytes to change. Require complete preflight and verified output before arithmetic; bind the conditional manifest and report to their exact artifacts. Preserve the original strict report and unavailable reasons.

Keep strict and conditional counts separate. Unknown source sample size or precision remains unknown; generated n and three-seed spread cannot supply it. Report the raw discrepancy without turning it into a calibrated uncertainty or pass/fail claim. “Every executable check passes v1 preflight” (line 94) should mean the whole strict plan passes structural validation; it must not imply that the conditional item has become strict `matched_declared` evidence.

No source-context adapter, new tables, weighted engine, measurement conversion or additional study is needed for the authorized narrow proxy. Their optional discussion in the proposal is not execution permission. Subject to these limits and the later implementation review, there is no remaining design-level reason to require endless dictionary searching before making a transparent release-defined decision.
