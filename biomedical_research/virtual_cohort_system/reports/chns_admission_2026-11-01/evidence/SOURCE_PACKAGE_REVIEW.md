## Claims that should remain bounded

The two new studies add different joint evidence: Bao reports an unadjusted **Pearson** plasma-glucose/HbA1c association; MIND reports **Spearman** associations involving separately measured serum and plasma glucose and HbA1c. Neither supplies the missing common five-variable conditional model, predictor dependence, residual law or CHNS target-population moments. A source mean difference is not an individual serum/plasma conversion constant. MIND's baseline anthropometry versus later laboratory timing and imputation remain part of its observation operator.

The source packages correctly separate previously diagnosed diabetes from screen-detected disease, and composite history/treatment/threshold labels from biomarker-only labels. Bao's OGTT-based groups require a two-hour glucose measure not established in CHNS. MIND's composite agreement requires verified history/treatment semantics. Neither can be replaced by an FPG-only selection and called source-matched. Missing SD/SE/CI details are not repaired by significance P values or source-wide n.

[SOURCE_REVIEW_RESULTS.md](../SOURCE_REVIEW_RESULTS.md) appropriately says no new statistic operator is needed **for the three unweighted target questions**, keeps Pani's weighted source check unavailable, and makes no efficacy/novelty claim. Its reported independent-check extent is also appropriately limited: Bao Methods/main results were cross-read, without claiming every table was independently recounted; MIND Tables 2/3 and their aggregate arithmetic received the deeper independent check. This review does not expand either claim.

The conflicting MIND bundle must not re-enter calibration through a derived-value alias.

No further original is justified merely to increase the source count. Use one of the four remaining slots only for a precisely named gap that can alter admission or the frozen question set—for example, a compatible joint/conditional estimand or a necessary measurement/selection bridge. Current priority is A–C, not a broader search, new operator suite, target preview or benchmark expansion.
