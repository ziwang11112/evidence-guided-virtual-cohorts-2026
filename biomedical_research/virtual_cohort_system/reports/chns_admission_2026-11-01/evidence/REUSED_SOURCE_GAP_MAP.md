## Keep source evidence separate from target exposure

The [exposure registry](../../../docs/DATASET_EXPOSURE_REGISTRY.md) closes ENSANUT 2018–2019, NAGALA and prior revealed references to tuning/fresh-confirmation claims. Reusing **external source audits prepared for ENSANUT** is different from importing ENSANUT effects, selected generators, parameter changes or post-reveal lessons into a construction packet. No ENSANUT target result was consulted here. This would be reuse of an evidence portfolio, not a new evidence discovery.

The [CHNS exposure map](../EXPOSURE_MAP.md) identifies result-bearing target-family abstracts PMC8214016 (urate/insulin/HOMA) and PMC7282081 (lipid/cognition/BMI), plus a broader physiological-dysregulation snippet. They are **not independent external construction evidence**. Their age/BMI/glucose covariate mentions do not establish that every proposed pairwise or conditional relation was exposed, but neither do they establish cleanliness. Before F2, classify each proposed estimand as known overlap, unresolved, or no documented overlap, including algebraically equivalent formulations. No globally unseen-cohort or pretraining-blindness claim is justified.

Incidental CHNS codebook marginal-summary exposure is already disclosed. Those summaries must not become construction constraints, target-population moments or an excuse to choose favorable endpoints. Measurement/protocol metadata may define intended procedures; they are not empirical joint constraints.

## Three prospective questions without target-effect selection

These are provisional association questions already justified by external evidence. They do not assert a target sign, causal effect, clinical decision rule or expected success. Let H be HbA1c, A age, S the documented sex/gender coding, B BMI and G the prospectively chosen glucose measure. Freeze outcome orientation, intercept, units, population, weights, complete-case/missingness rules and denominator before any target evaluation.

| Question and exact projection | Source-information classification | CHNS applicability and execution boundary |
|---|---|---|
| **Adiposity and glycation:** coefficient of B in H ~ A + S + B. Does whole-cohort generation retain the specified adjusted association? | Directly reported in Jansen Table 2; any percent-scale conversion is algebraically derived, not additional evidence. | Population/assay transport remains assumption-dependent. Deliberately no G adjustment; adding it changes the question. Unknown row n/CI level must remain unknown. |
| **Fasting glycemia and glycation:** coefficient of G in H ~ A + S + G. | Directly reported in Jansen Table 2; separate from Liu's unadjusted projection. | CHNS G is serum with intended fasting collection, not automatically the same plasma exposure. Do not choose central versus field measurement by target fit. |
| **Age association beyond glycemia/adiposity:** coefficient of A in H ~ S + B + G + A. | Directly reported by Pani for its survey-weighted NHANES population. | Current S1 v1 does not support weighted feedback. A CHNS unweighted sample coefficient can be prospectively studied, but is a different target estimand and its correspondence to Pani is assumption-dependent. It cannot be labelled a faithful numerical replay of the source coefficient. |

Thus **three useful scientific questions can be planned now; three source-matched numerical feedback checks cannot yet be certified**. Two Jansen projections share a source population, and the Pani/Liu material overlaps a survey family. Three analyses are not three independent validations. Their study utility is whether the proposed evidence-informed construction preserves these specified associations under declared transport assumptions. A good source fit alone is not evidence of target utility.

An unprovided interaction, tail co-occurrence or BMI–FPG relationship could instead be a predeclared assumption stress test, but the existing packet does not identify its coefficient or justify selecting it to obtain a third success. The present three questions avoid that arbitrary substitution while exposing the weighted-operator limitation.

## Minimum decisions before F2

1. Resolve current CHNS field definitions, coding, eligibility and the one chosen glucose measurement without target-effect inspection. If source selection cannot be represented, retain an explicit applicability gap rather than silently weakening it.
2. Re-admit the reused source items for this case, binding the original outcome/term/adjustment/selection/weights/units and uncertainty roles. Preserve quarantines and unknown row n/CI level. The old case's approval is provenance, not truth certification.
3. Under [semantic feedback v1](../../../docs/SEMANTIC_FEEDBACK_V1.md), weighted Pani checks remain `not_computable`; unknown essential definitions also remain unavailable. Any necessary conversion or weighted/source-population operator needs a separately reviewed implementation scope. Relabelling converted values as original identity measurements or falling back to legacy feedback would defeat the repair. The current collector checks generated task-population statistics; it does not itself reconstruct each original source population.
4. Independently review estimand-level exposure and applicability, then freeze questions, assumptions, output schedule and complete reporting. No new papers are required merely to name these questions. Any subsequently allocated original-study audits should close a specific gap—compatible joint dependence, measurement/selection comparability, or a usable source operator—rather than accumulate supportive-looking coefficients.
