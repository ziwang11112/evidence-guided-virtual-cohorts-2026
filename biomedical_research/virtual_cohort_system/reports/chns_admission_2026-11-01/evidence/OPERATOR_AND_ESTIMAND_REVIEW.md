# CHNS operator and estimand review

1 November 2026. **Three association estimands are measurable in principle with existing raw-OLS arithmetic; three faithful source-feedback checks are not yet established.** Source selection, measurement conversion, weighting and clinical missing-code gaps remain distinct from operator availability. This is a read-only code/document review and a provisional question set, not F2 admission or a protocol freeze. No participant values/effects, new original study, generator or test were opened/run. No collector or state was modified.

Inputs: [measurement contract draft](../semantics/MEASUREMENT_CONTRACT_DRAFT.md), [reused-source gap map](REUSED_SOURCE_GAP_MAP.md), and the already authored [Bao source audit](BAO_2010_SOURCE_AUDIT.md). Reading the latter audit did not reopen its original paper. Whole-cohort generation remains the objective; these analyses assess selected scientific uses of a joint virtual cohort, not recovery of individuals or proof that the entire joint distribution is identified.

## Proposed set: three explicit raw projections

Let H be current `HbA1c` in %, G the prospectively selected `GLUCOSE` in mmol/L, A survey `age` in years, S the documented master `GENDER` coding, and B BMI in kg/m² derived for the reference from `WEIGHT` and `HEIGHT`. `Glu_field` is a separate candidate measurement, not an automatic substitute. Let P denote a prospectively fixed CHNS 2009 target population and missingness policy. P is not chosen from observed effects here.

| Proposed scientific estimand | Required reference fields / precise operator | Evidence and interpretation boundary |
|---|---|---|
| **E1. Adiposity-associated glycation:** BMI coefficient in H = intercept + A + S + B + residual. | `HbA1c`, `age`, `GENDER`, `HEIGHT`, `WEIGHT`; relevant measurement/cleaning metadata. `ols_coefficient`, outcome H, predictors A/S/B, term B, intercept included. Unit: percentage points of HbA1c per kg/m². | Jansen Table 2 directly reports this age/gender-adjusted BMI projection in its selected population. Do not add G and call it the same estimand. Any outcome-unit conversion is derived information; transfer to P is assumption-dependent. This is an association, not a causal BMI effect. |
| **E2. Glycemia-associated glycation:** glucose coefficient in H = intercept + A + S + G + residual. | `HbA1c`, selected glucose field, `age`, `GENDER`. Same raw-OLS operator, term G. Unit: percentage points per mmol/L. | A separate Jansen Table 2 projection. It is not Liu's unadjusted training-sample equation. Source fasting plasma versus CHNS serum/intended-fasting measurements needs an explicit applicability decision; shared units do not resolve it. |
| **E3. Age association conditional on glycemia/adiposity:** age coefficient in H = intercept + S + B + G + A + residual. | All five task variables; `HEIGHT`/`WEIGHT` to define reference BMI. Raw-OLS term A, intercept included. Unit: percentage points per year. | Pani directly reports a **survey-weighted**, selected NHANES projection. An unweighted CHNS sample projection is scientifically definable but a different target estimand. It is an assumption-dependent transport question, not faithful numerical replay of Pani. Weighted source feedback remains unavailable in v1. |

These projections probe different conditioning choices and are jointly useful for checking analytical utility. They are not three independent cohort replications; E1/E2 share Jansen, and survey evidence has known overlaps. No target sign, coefficient or success threshold is specified. Freeze whether analyses use a common complete-case population or explicitly different analysis-specific populations; a common five-variable complete-case rule itself selects on availability of all five variables, including G for E1. Do not introduce that selection silently.

The provisional set preserves questions already motivated by the retained evidence rather than inventing an unprovided third coefficient. Its limitation is explicit: E3 cannot currently be source-checked as reported. The parent may change the set based on additional approved external evidence, never on CHNS effects. Bao's unadjusted Pearson relation is a possible evidence-supported alternative, but its audit leaves weighting, pair-specific n and selection/measurement issues unresolved; it is not automatically an executable replacement.

|---|---|---|

It does not estimate a slope, identify a copula, or provide correlation uncertainty. |

Semantic lines 68-80, 123-132, 155-173. | E1-E3 arithmetic is already available for unweighted raw coefficients. Converting a beta requires the source's exact convention and matching analysis-sample dispersions; target SDs are not substitutes. Dimensionless units alone cannot certify standardization, so faithful source extraction remains essential. |
| Single-event proportions | One predicate for the event, plus an AND-list of selection predicates; the denominator is the selected complete event-variable rows. Source checks lines 85-101, 128-158, 194-199. | Supports simple prevalences and certain conditional proportions. Putting glucose status in `selection` and HbA1c status in `event` estimates P(H-status | G-status), not P(H-status AND G-status) in the full cohort. |
| Classification cross-tabs | No multi-variable event, common-denominator 2×2 table or confusion-matrix operator. Semantic `_bind_evidence` identifies a proportion's primary variable solely from its one event column (lines 155-173). | Four joint cell probabilities are not directly represented by that scalar operator. Algebraic reconstruction from conditional/marginal proportions would require one shared, explicitly verified denominator and missing policy; it must not be improvised across different selected samples. |
| Weighted fits/statistics | Sidecar accepts `none`, `weighted`, `not_reported`, but only `none` computes; weighted/unknown weighting yields `not_computable`. Semantic lines 221-222, 252-255. | No weighted OLS, weighted correlation or survey-design variance. Describing a weighted source as unweighted to obtain a number is not permitted. Unknown weight use is not evidence for `none`. |

The collector reads verified **generated task-population** output. Its plan requires the actual task population and identity measurement; it does not recreate a source sample ([whole_cohort_feedback.py](../../../cohort_harness/whole_cohort_feedback.py), lines 43-52; semantic lines 273-318). Even a supported operator can therefore have an inapplicable source comparison. Missing source SE alone need not prevent raw feedback, but it cannot be fabricated from participant SD or generated sample size.

One denominator limitation needs particular care: selection predicates are evaluated before the operator's missingness check. Missing selection cells evaluate false; `missing_policy: error` then checks only the operator operands. Coded clinical sentinels are not decoded by this function. Thus a predicate such as “not equal to the treated code” could include an unresolved numeric missing code. Valid field decoding and explicit selection-missing handling are prerequisites, not benefits supplied by the semantic gate.

## Necessary additions only

1. **No new statistical operator is required for the proposed unweighted target projections.** Source-unit harmonization may still need a separately reviewed deterministic bridge, notably original HbA1c scale versus %. A known affine conversion must distinguish outcome levels from slopes and SD/SE; it is not a new biological constraint. Current identity-only feedback cannot silently perform it.
2. **Weighted OLS is conditional, not an automatic next component.** It is necessary only if the final scope requires numerical replay of a genuinely weighted coefficient and appropriate weights/source context are available. A point-estimate weighting implementation alone does not reproduce Pani's sampling population, stratification, uncertainty or CHNS population weighting. Keep the source check unavailable if those requirements cannot be met; do not add arbitrary weights to satisfy the interface.
3. **A joint-event/common-denominator proportion operator is needed only if a classification cross-tab becomes a frozen scientific endpoint.** Bind every event variable, exact thresholds/boundaries, missing policy, denominator and unit. No cross-tab implementation can manufacture the missing two-hour OGTT reference required by Bao's diagnostic classification. HbA1c/glucose threshold discordance in CHNS could be described as agreement between two measures, not validated diagnostic sensitivity/specificity against absent truth.

A standardized-OLS operator is unnecessary for E1-E3. Add it only if the final admitted evidence actually requires standardized coefficients and defines the scaling population/convention. Pearson and Spearman require no new implementation. ROC/AUC, diagnostic optimization, interactions, causal estimators and broad survey machinery are not required by this proposed set.

## Can restrictions avoid unresolved medication and cleaning mappings?

An explicitly defined all-comers target population can avoid using medication/pregnancy as exclusion variables. That is a legitimate target choice, but it does **not** reproduce Jansen's diabetes/anemia exclusions, Pani's diabetes/FPG selection, or Bao's prior-diabetes and other exclusions. State the broader target and corresponding transport assumption. Do not replace an unavailable reported-diagnosis exclusion with an observed glycemic cutoff: Bao retains screen-detected diabetes, and such a cutoff changes its population. An `approximate` label cannot erase a known operator/selection mismatch.

Unknown drug/pregnancy mappings need not block a question that genuinely does not depend on those variables. Unknown measurement missing codes and clean-flag meanings still affect the validity of H/G/A/B/S themselves and cannot be solved by dropping an exclusion. Preserve released measurements/flags; do not infer code meanings, call all positive cleaning flags invalid, or mask every 88/99. Avoiding a flag-based filter is not proof that the remaining numerical measurements are usable. Resolve required field definitions before the custodian performs clinical eligibility work, or declare the dependent analysis unavailable.

Existing prior exposure remains disclosed. None of these recommendations supplies target-population margins to the constructor, requires superiority to another Agent, or converts source-fit gains into target-transfer evidence.
