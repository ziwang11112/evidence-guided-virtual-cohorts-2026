# CHNS evidence-only virtual cohort: F2 protocol

Status: prospective protocol for independent review, not an execution release. Prepared at the scheduled continuation of 1 November 2026. No clinical target values, eligible clinical sample size, associations or generated biomedical tables were inspected to select this design. The final F2 release must bind the reviewed files before a construction starts.

## Scientific question and scope

Can an Agent use external published study evidence and a population/measurement description, without participant input, to construct one joint virtual cohort that supports several prespecified scientific associations in an independent reference sample? This is a feasibility and transport study. It does not claim recovery of individuals, national representation, identification of an entire joint law, calibrated uncertainty, a new biological mechanism, or superiority to an ordinary Agent. The Agent chooses the stochastic scientific structure and its parameters; the controller governs information, execution and reporting, not the desired relationships.

The whole-cohort task generates age, female category, BMI, central-serum glucose and whole-blood HbA1c. The independent reference is the CHNS 2009 public release after the fixed restrictions below. Age 35-74 was chosen before inspecting target effects because it defines a midlife/older adult glycemic-analysis use case. It does not match or harmonize the Bao source age range, and it does not harmonize the other source populations: the older rural MIND sample and selected European/US studies retain substantial differences. No target moments are provided even for age and sex. Their composition is therefore an explicit, consequential construction assumption.

Each table contains 3,000 fictional records, a computational design size unrelated to an observed eligible CHNS count. Increasing virtual rows supplies no additional independent biological information. No individual observed columns are passed to the constructor. Complete-cohort synthesis remains the principal mode; this experiment does not evaluate missing-cell imputation or individual recovery.

## Fixed reference and measurement policy

Use only the four previously acquired, checksummed v1.0 SAS releases in `analysis_plan.json`. Inner-link biomarker and survey/examination records on exact integral person ID plus wave 2009, and master gender on person ID. Validate one-to-one person/wave and many-to-one master linkage; a new duplicate or incompatible key is a stop, not a deduplication choice. Do not require component-survey flags in addition to actual linked measurements. No new patient file is required.

Select released survey age 35-74 inclusive and documented master GENDER 1 or 2; female=0 for 1 and female=1 for 2. Use released age, labelled calculated to zero decimal points, without reconstructing dates or claiming known examination timing. Retain only common complete cases for age, gender, BMI, central GLUCOSE and HbA1c. The same sample supports all three primary projections, including the availability of glucose for the first projection. Thus the target is an age/wave/linkage/five-variable-availability-selected released sample, not all CHNS participants, all Chinese adults, untreated participants, or people without diabetes.

Preserve native missing values. Use HEIGHT in cm and WEIGHT in kg, retaining released corrections, extrapolations and average-height replacements. If CLN_HT or CLN_WT equals exactly 9, make its measurement unavailable. Do not apply unit corrections twice, drop all positive flags, treat OTHER as numeric zero, or invent a universal 88/99 missing mask. BMI=WEIGHT/(HEIGHT/100)^2 requires finite positive height/weight and a finite positive result. Retain and summarize flags at final evaluation, without conditioning the primary sample on other flag values.

Use current central `GLUCOSE` in mmol/L, not field glucose or legacy guessed renames; use `HbA1c` in percent. Finite released glycemic values remain measurements under the residual release-quality assumption. Nonpositive glycemic values or essential contradictory coding trigger a documentation interpretation check before affected effect calculations. Without a supported resolution, withhold the affected analyses or stop the case; no alternative recoding or selected exclusion may be tried to improve agreement. Unknown positive sentinels, fasting adherence, instrument comparability and blood/examination timing remain limitations. The already reviewed [measurement policy](../semantics/MEASUREMENT_POLICY_DECISION.md) controls details.

## Prespecified scientific analyses

Let H=HbA1c in %, A=released age in years, S=female 0/1, B=BMI in kg/m^2, G=central-serum glucose in mmol/L. All fits include an intercept and are unweighted raw OLS on the common complete-case sample. Neither source coefficients nor desired signs are inserted into the evaluator.

| ID | Scientific use | Operator and reported term | Evidence classification |
|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | Adiposity-associated glycation adjusted for age/sex | H ~ A + S + B; B coefficient, percentage points/(kg/m^2) | Source-analogous Jansen projection; source reporting and any unit conversion are separate from assumption-dependent transport. |
| E2 | Glycemia-associated glycation adjusted for age/sex | H ~ A + S + G; G coefficient, percentage points/(mmol/L) | Source-analogous Jansen projection; serum/plasma and source selection differences remain. |
| E3 | Age-associated glycation conditional on glycemia and adiposity | H ~ S + B + G + A; A coefficient, percentage points/year | Pani gives a related weighted selected-population projection. Unweighted CHNS interpretation and joint dependence are assumption-dependent. |

These are three uses of the same joint table, not three independent cohort replications. They are held out from numeric revision feedback. Their exact evaluation formulas are not included in the builder bundle; however the source packet contains analogous source analyses, so withholding formulas does not make them unknown or novel relationships. All three are reported regardless of results. There is no selection of a best seed, best construction, best stage, or best analysis after reference reveal.

Necessary final diagnostics: all five marginal means/SDs/10th/50th/90th percentiles, Pearson and Spearman G-H, native missing/eligibility and retained-cleaning-flag counts for the reference, and model rank/conditioning. These describe failure modes and plausibility. They are not a single aggregate accuracy score or a new source-feedback channel. Target diagnostics are revealed only after all candidate/stage decisions are fixed. The task schema permits a zero glycemic value as a syntactic boundary; this does not certify a biologically interpretable measurement. Any generated nonpositive glycemic value triggers the same prespecified interpretation/withholding gate before affected scientific estimates. Do not delete such rows or quietly count the table as scientifically valid.

## Reference uncertainty and interpretation

The real sample is an uncertain reference, not a known population distribution. Report raw OLS point estimates and, when valid, nominal 95% community-clustered sandwich intervals with small-sample correction G_clusters/(G_clusters-1) x (n-1)/(n-k), where k includes the intercept, and t degrees of freedom G_clusters-1. Grouping uses released survey `commid`, which is metadata-labelled community ID. Require every analysis-row group ID to be finite, integral and positive, at least 20 distinct communities, a full-rank design and residual degrees of freedom. If grouping/inference conditions fail, keep the unchanged full-sample point estimate and report interval unavailable; do not drop rows merely to obtain a CI or silently substitute IID inference. No official survey weights are used, so these are not national design-based intervals. Cluster validity and exchangeability remain assumptions.

For each table and E1-E3 report generated coefficient, reference coefficient/interval, signed difference and direction. Report relative effect size only if the reference interval excludes zero and label it descriptive. Report across-seed range and every estimate; stochastic seed spread is conditional simulation variability, not evidence/transport uncertainty. A direct-row output replayed under different seeds has no independent resampling and must not receive Monte Carlo credit. Synthetic-row sampling precision is not patient evidence and is not pooled with the reference interval.

Reference direction is supported only if its nominal interval excludes zero; otherwise direction assessment is inconclusive. For a construction-stage, a direction is stable only if all three planned seeds yield valid coefficients with the same nonzero sign. Fewer than three valid coefficients makes that stage incomplete/unresolved, even if the available signs agree. Matching a supported reference direction is limited directional utility, not equivalence. Stable opposite sign is a substantive failure; unstable signs are unresolved. Whether generated estimates lie inside the reference interval is descriptive compatibility, not a formal equivalence test. No arbitrary error tolerance, p-value agreement rule or global success score is created after seeing results. Point differences remain visible even when reference intervals are wide.

The case-level decision is continue a narrow directional-use pilot, simplify the feedback mechanism, or stop this particular source/target configuration, supported by the complete per-analysis evidence. Even three direction matches cannot establish general accuracy, transport to other tasks, publication readiness or superiority. Revision is considered useful only to the extent it improves prespecified held-out analyses without concealed degradation; a better source proxy alone does not establish this. Conflicting improvements are reported as tradeoffs, not summarized into a winning number.

## Evidence, interpretation and feedback boundary

Use only the frozen reviewed external evidence packet. CHNS-derived papers, source IPD, target codebook numeric summaries and prior target results are excluded from construction. Prior source exposures remain disclosed. The existing relation-level map establishes no documented exposure of these exact CHNS HbA1c projections; it does not prove global or pretraining blindness. The constructor receives no target file path, rows, eligible n, clinical moment or effect. Human/root access to metadata is separately disclosed in the exposure registry.

There are two predeclared possible raw feedback quantities: Bao's named Pearson glucose-HbA1c and MIND's named Spearman glucose-HbA1c. Each retains its original population, measurement, reported n/uncertainty scope and unknown weighting/missingness. Their evaluation on a complete generated task table is an explicitly assumed task-population proxy, not replay of source populations. Do not pool Pearson and Spearman, average these into a fitting loss, infer SE from individual SD, or treat unavailable precision as known. The inconsistent MIND kappa item stays quarantined and cannot constrain generation.

Every candidate invokes `semantic_feedback_v1` explicitly. Unknown weights remain unavailable/null in its strict report. Only the separately approved `conditional_feedback_v1` plan can supply the two interpretation-defined raw proxy diagnostics. No fallback to a legacy collector. Fixed recipe/interpretation facts are supplied by the reviewed packet; a candidate may decline a proxy under an explained applicability judgment, but may not rewrite its meaning to gain a computed value. Candidate-specific bindings and scientific assumptions receive an independent review before freezing.

All three initial-seed reports are returned together in one feedback round. The Agent may retain its structure, revise it with source-supported reasons or state that feedback is insufficient. No change is a valid outcome. There is no target-performance feedback, no automatic best-seed ranking, no extra source-context generation and no numerical calibration run outside the declared table budget. A candidate's offline program design must not execute trial biomedical generators. Schema/unit checks and invented engineering fixtures are permitted and distinguished from scientific experiments.

## Construction, execution and stopping

Two independently initialized fresh-context constructions C1/C2; initial plus at most one source-feedback revision each. Three fixed seeds per stage: 1701, 1702, 1703. Maximum 12 final target tables, 3,000 rows each. One failed reserved execution consumes its slot; retain its error. Do not retry the same scientific slot, add a seed, or edit a frozen candidate. A no-change revision can refer to the initial frozen generator explicitly and is not a newly improved method; if repeated outputs are produced they still consume their declared slots. Failed or absent stages are reported, not replaced by favorable ones.

Use the existing isolated execution runtime, at most 180 seconds per table. Two construction turns plus at most one revision turn per construction; packet clarification may repair a pre-execution schema misunderstanding but may not expose target answers or provide scientific parameter values. No source query/download or new paid API is budgeted for the constructors. No training, HPC, optional mechanism experiment or additional source-context table is allocated. The two already audited original studies and three already screened target families remain 2/6 and 3/3; unused literature allowance is not a reason to search.

The construction process is released only after the concrete source packet, this protocol, the machine analysis plan, budget, controller and their independent review are hash-bound. Each candidate/sidecar is independently reviewed and frozen before its executions. After initial feedback and revision-or-retention decisions for both constructions, freeze the complete final-stage inventory before the custodian first reads clinical values. The evaluator implementation must independently pass invented-data checks and bind to that final evaluation release; no real data are used to develop it. Then evaluate every available planned output and the reference once, followed by an independent numerical recomputation. Do not change the generator or select new analyses afterward.

Retain all failures, missing/unavailable diagnostics and source/target disagreements. Pause after this single reviewed real-case continue/simplify/stop decision, admission failure, or verified required-resource obstacle. The hard end date remains 14 November 2026 America/Chicago. This F2 design does not reopen any earlier revealed reference.
