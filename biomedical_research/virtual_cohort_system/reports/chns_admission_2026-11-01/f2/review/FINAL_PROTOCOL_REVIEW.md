## Scientific and information-boundary findings

The protocol preserves whole-cohort generation without participant input and gives the constructor scientific model/parameter choices. Its three adjusted HbA1c projections are clearly specified, share a fixed complete-case reference population, and have coherent units and conditioning sets. Common availability of glucose for E1 is disclosed. Source-analogous E1/E2 and the related weighted-source E3 are labelled transport questions rather than wholly unprovided biological discoveries. Withholding the exact evaluation formulas is not misrepresented as absence of analogous information in the evidence packet.

Age 35-74, wave 2009, released measurements, documented gender categories, linkage and five-variable availability define a selected reference. No national, untreated, nondiabetic or individual-recovery claim is made. The release policy preserves corrections, handles exact flag 9, keeps native missingness, and avoids generic sentinel masks, target-selected exclusions and silent glucose-field substitution. The source plasma/target serum difference remains an assumption rather than an automatic conversion.

Reference point estimates and community-clustered CR1/t intervals are distinguished. The written and machine plans specify the correction, intercept-inclusive k, at least 20 valid community identifiers, and interval withholding without changing point-estimate rows or substituting IID inference. They explicitly disclaim national survey-design intervals and retain clustering/exchangeability assumptions. Directional utility is limited, effect differences remain visible, and all three valid seed estimates are required before declaring a construction-stage direction stable. Three seeds are conditional simulation realizations, not biological replication or calibrated uncertainty. Direct-row repetition receives no Monte Carlo credit.

The no-target-feedback boundary, all-output reporting, source-proxy distinction, immutable candidates, maximum twelve scheduled target tables, one feedback round per construction, failed-slot accounting and separate final evaluation release are consistent. No better-source-fit or sign-only outcome is promoted to broad method accuracy. The final source packet and controller still have to realize these rules; prose alone does not demonstrate enforcement.

## Corrections found and verified

The review required the following changes before passing. Their history must remain available rather than describing the first draft as error-free.

1. The original claim that age 35-74 matched Bao 2010 was false. The reviewed Bao audit describes a different age scope. The final text now presents 35-74 as a prospectively fixed scientific-use range and explicitly denies source-age harmonization; the machine motivation agrees.
2. The original machine plan used two incorrect case-sensitive field names. Biomarker `IDind` and examination `WAVE` now match saved actual-header metadata. All requested names and all four input-file hashes were checked against `SCHEMA_AND_KEY_AUDIT.json`, without reading patient files.
3. The original prose allowed a stable direction from merely the valid subset of seeds. It now requires all three planned seed coefficients to be valid and agree; an incomplete stage remains incomplete/unresolved.
4. Syntactic glucose/HbA1c zero is explicitly separated from scientific measurement validity. Generated nonpositive values receive the same interpretation/withholding gate as the reference before affected estimates; no row deletion is permitted to obtain a favorable result.
5. CR1's k is explicitly defined as the number of design columns including the intercept in prose and JSON.
6. An edit introduced UTF-8 mojibake in the protocol. The final bytes were rechecked after explicit encoding repair and ASCII normalization; the quoted hashes refer to those repaired bytes.
