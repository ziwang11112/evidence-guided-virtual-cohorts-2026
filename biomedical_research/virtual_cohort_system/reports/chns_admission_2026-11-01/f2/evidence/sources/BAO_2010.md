# Bao 2010: external SHDS II evidence

Authored summary from an existing audited extraction, prepared 1 November 2026; no original was reopened for this packet. Original: Bao et al., *Glycated haemoglobin A1c for diagnosing diabetes in Chinese population: cross sectional epidemiological survey*, BMJ 2010;340:c2249, [DOI 10.1136/bmj.c2249](https://www.bmj.com/content/340/bmj.c2249). Review-ready, not yet admitted.

SHDS II sampled six Shanghai communities in 2007–2008 using a multistage age-stratified design. The analyzed sample has 4,886 participants: 1,828 men and 3,058 women. Previously diagnosed diabetes, missing questionnaires and participants younger than 20 were excluded; the prose's “over 20” wording leaves the exact age-20 boundary unresolved. The wider survey's observed upper age is not an explicit support rule. Cancer, severe psychiatric disturbance, chronic kidney disease, pregnancy and glucocorticoid treatment are listed exclusions. Screen-detected diabetes remains included. No separate anemia exclusion was established.

Measurements: a stated 10-hour overnight fast; fasting **plasma** glucose by glucose oxidase, mmol/L; HbA1c by HPLC with the printed instrument name HLC-73G7, Tosoh, %. Measurement within two hours is reported. Quality-assessment participation is not proof of NGSP/IFCC certification. BMI is kg/m^2.

Direct joint report: Methods names Pearson correlation. The Results paragraph after Table 1 gives **r(HbA1c, fasting plasma glucose) = 0.619**, unadjusted, P<0.001. Source analysis n is 4,886, but pair-specific n, SE and CI are not supplied. Analytic weights and the original correlation missingness rule are unreported. No variance-stabilizing interval is derived for this packet.

Quartiles are endpoints of the central 50% interval, not SD/SE or a confidence interval. Their interpolation convention is unreported. The structured records retain medians; the endpoints remain explicit source context. No normal-distribution conversion is performed. A source female fraction may be derived as **3058/4886** from the reported counts; it is an unweighted count ratio, not an independently reported survey-weighted population proportion.

This source differs from an age-35–74 CHNS all-diagnosis released-data subset in population, selection, calendar period and measurement. Plasma and central serum are not measurement-identical. A conventional equal-weight Pearson interpretation on complete generated values is only a proposed conditional target proxy, never reconstruction of the source's sample/missingness process. Exact original weights remain unknown. The source family is distinct from CHNS; participant-level non-overlap is unverified.
