# Pani 2008: contextual survey-weighted source association

Authored summary from retained, independently audited source records; no original was reopened for this packet. Pani et al., [DOI 10.2337/dc08-0577](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2551641/). Review-ready contextual use only.

The retained result concerns NHANES 2001–2004, source analysis n=3,270, age at least 25, excluding self-reported diabetes and fasting plasma glucose at least 126 mg/dL. These are source restrictions, not instructions to impose them on the intended target. HbA1c uses NGSP/DCCT-standardized HPLC; plasma glucose uses hexokinase; exact fasting hours are unspecified. The analyses account for complex survey design and weights.

The Results immediately before Table 2 report an **HbA1c age coefficient of 0.010 percentage points per year**, adjusted for sex, BMI and fasting plasma glucose; P<0.001. Coefficient SE/CI and coefficient-specific complete-case n are unreported in the retained audit. The source-wide n does not fill that gap. This is a direct reported source estimate with known survey weighting, not an unweighted target effect or an expected target sign.

Strict unweighted source feedback is unavailable, and the equal-weight conditional-correlation exception does not apply to this coefficient. Population, disease selection, assay and plasma/serum differences remain. It can inform an explicitly labelled modelling assumption but cannot be called a replayed or matched source constraint.

Source age-stratum means and survey SEs are omitted from this minimal packet. A survey SE is not an individual SD, and multiplying it by sqrt(n) is not a permitted dispersion reconstruction. The separate Framingham result uses another population and a different adjustment set; it is excluded here. Pani shares survey years with Liu's NHANES material, which is also excluded; it is not independent replication of Liu. No participant-level non-overlap with other studies was established.
