# Evidence-only whole-cohort construction brief

Construct a joint virtual biomedical cohort for the population and columns in task.json using only the supplied external evidence and source summaries. This is generation with no individual input. Its intended use is reusable descriptive and adjusted association analysis of adiposity, age and glycemia; it is not an estimate of any named participant's missing test result.

You choose the scientific model, distribution families, dependence structure, parameter values and any transformations. Every substantive choice must cite a supplied evidence item or an explicit assumption. A source reports a quantity in its own population/measurement/analysis context; do not treat all reported quantities as mutually exchangeable target constraints. Age/sex composition, joint dependencies, source-to-target transfer and uncertainty not fixed by the evidence must remain declared assumptions. No target clinical moments or reference rows are available.

Deliver a harness-compatible candidate with model_spec.json and a generator program, or a directly authored complete table using the supported direct-row route. Programmatic generation is usually practical for 3,000 rows; direct rows remain a valid implementation. The route is recorded, and identical direct rows under different seed labels do not represent independent resampling. No clinical plausibility or target-effect value is prescribed by the coordinator.

Do not run trial biomedical generators or calibrate on hidden evaluation. You may inspect the supplied contracts, perform metadata/schema/unit validation and use invented engineering fixtures. After the initial candidate is reviewed and frozen, the controller executes exactly the three fixed seeds and returns all source-feedback reports together. You then have at most one scientific revision, or may explicitly retain the initial model. Use source-supported reasons, disclose tradeoffs and preserve uncertainty. A better proxy statistic is not proof of better target data.

Strict semantic feedback and any separately approved conditional interpretation diagnostics have different meanings. An unknown original source weight or definition does not become known because a computation is possible. Do not rewrite approved source operators, units, source selection or reported uncertainty. You may state that an approved proxy is not useful for this target; it must remain labelled accordingly. Source-population replay, target feedback and additional trial tables are not permitted in this case.

You receive only task.json, the reviewed source packet and interpretation policy, the harness interface description/skill and initial or authorized revision materials. Do not inspect any real patient files, project history, target summaries, evaluation formulas, other construction, previous target result or the coordinator's internal review files. Do not browse or search outside the frozen packet in this bounded case. This is a fixed-information experiment, not a general ban on evidence retrieval in the method. If essential supplied semantics remain unclear, report the uncertainty without inventing a source fact.

Preserve the difference between literature constraints, algebraic transformations and untested assumptions in the resulting model. Include an explanation of how the generated data could fail for scientific analysis. No requirement to match a desired sign, achieve an unknown coefficient, outperform another Agent or revise when evidence does not justify a change.
