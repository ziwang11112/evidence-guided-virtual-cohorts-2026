# Wang 2024: external MIND-China evidence

Authored summary from an existing audited extraction, prepared 1 November 2026; no original was reopened for this packet. Original: Wang et al., *Comparing glycemic traits in defining diabetes among rural Chinese older adults*, PLOS ONE 2024, [DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0296694](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0296694). Review-ready, not yet admitted.

The analysis comprises 3,547 selected rural residents aged 61–80 in Yanlou, Shandong, free of dementia and functional disability. The 2019 follow-up excludes missing laboratory/history records. Known diabetes history is included. This is not a nationally representative adult sample. Demographics and BMI were obtained in 2018; laboratory testing occurred in April–May 2019. The source reports mean filling of continuous variables and mode filling of categorical variables, without affected-field counts. Exact analytic weighting is unreported.

Source **FSG** is serum from a coagulant tube, measured by glucose oxidase on a CS-600B in the local laboratory within two hours, in mmol/L. Source HbA1c is EDTA whole blood, HPLC Huizhong MQ6000, in %, at an NGSP-certified KingMed laboratory within two days. Overnight fasting is reported; exact duration was not retained. The separate source **FPG** is fluoride plasma measured by hexokinase elsewhere; it must not be renamed serum glucose or used to infer an individual conversion.

Direct joint report: Methods names **Spearman** correlation. The full-analysis Results give **rho(FSG, HbA1c) = 0.529**, unadjusted, P<0.001. The analysis n is 3,547, not an established pair-specific n. Pair-specific n, SE and CI are not supplied. The original tie convention and field-specific imputation effects are not established by these records. A conventional average-tie, equal-weight implementation would be an explicitly reviewed interpretation on complete generated analysis values, not a claim to reconstruct the original processing.
