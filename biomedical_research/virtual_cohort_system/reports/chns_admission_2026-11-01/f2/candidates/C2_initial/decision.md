# C2 initial construction decision

The program route supplies one assumed joint distribution for 3,000 complete fictional adults in the task's age 35-74 range. It is not fitted to any participant records or target moments. The constructor used only the admitted source packet, task, brief, policy and allowed harness interfaces. Scientific parameters were selected independently; no desired target association or previous candidate was supplied.

Age follows a discretized Beta(2,2) reference composition and female is Bernoulli(0.60), initially independent of age. Conditional BMI is lognormal with an assumed shallow midlife maximum. These choices fill gaps left by the source summaries; neither Shanghai's age distribution nor MIND's older composition is reconstructed. The female count ratios only suggest a reference composition, without survey weighting or precision pooling.

Glucose and HbA1c follow a conditional two-component bivariate lognormal mixture. The elevated component adds distributional heterogeneity, with an assumed probability depending on age and BMI. It is not a diabetes or treatment label, and its probability is not a disease prevalence estimate. No diagnostic threshold or source diabetes exclusion is imposed. Lognormal support ensures positive values without clinical clipping; this does not validate its tail probabilities.

Bao's central values and MIND's individual dispersion provide scale context for explicitly assumed component medians and log residual SDs. MIND mean HbA1c 6.1 and Bao median 5.6 are different statistics from different populations, not conflicting estimates of one target parameter to average. The model does not match either source's full set of marginal summaries. Source quartile endpoints are not converted to SD; individual SD is not converted to SE or silently treated as residual SD.

The conditional normal-residual correlation 0.40 is a modelling assumption. Shared components and covariates also induce outcome dependence. Bao's raw Pearson 0.619 and MIND's Spearman 0.529 motivate positive shared variation, but neither statistic is substituted for this residual correlation or promised as an output constraint. No trial cohort or feedback-based calibration was performed.

Pani's survey-weighted age association and Jansen's separate age/gender-adjusted BMI and plasma-glucose projections remain contextual. The assumed conditional log-scale shifts are not those source coefficients, and the separate Jansen projections are not concatenated into a regression. NGSP's scale relation does not establish assay equivalence or a serum/plasma conversion; no coefficient conversion is used here.

All substantive settings have evidence/assumption provenance. Source populations, selection rules, measurements, reported quantities, unknown weighting, and uncertainty are retained in the exact evidence copy. Source-wide sample sizes are not pair-specific or regression-row sample sizes. MIND's imputation effects and Bao's correlation missingness remain unknown. Repeated source representations and distinct study names do not establish independent evidence or participant non-overlap.

Both optional interpretations are selected: I_BAO_PEARSON_TARGET_PROXY and I_MIND_SPEARMAN_TARGET_PROXY. They may provide raw descriptive diagnostics on complete generated task values after controller review. Bao's plasma/serum gap and MIND's age, timing, imputation and assay gaps remain. Equal weights and average Spearman ties are explicit interpretation assumptions, not recovered source methods. The fixed recipe and semantic sidecar are copied unchanged; strict discrepancies remain unavailable with unknown original weights. No approval, original source reconstruction, or numeric strict-feedback result is claimed.

The output can support inspection and descriptive or adjusted-association calculations within this declared scenario. Associations partly reflect its assumed mixing, covariate effects and conditional independence choices. It cannot establish target effect sizes, causal effects, diagnosis accuracy, survey representativeness, treatment effects, individual test recovery, measurement savings, or publication-ready clinical validity. Missingness/complete-case selection, sex-age dependence, pregnancy, anemia, medication, fasting adherence, and other unmeasured factors could materially invalidate transport. Those uncertainties are not calibrated.

Parameters are fixed. Different seeds resample fictional individuals and do not quantify source sampling error or model/transport uncertainty. The initial candidate stops at authoring and static metadata validation. The constructor has not executed the generator, written clinical rows, run a source check, frozen the candidate, or manufactured an approval. Actual metadata checks and their limitations are recorded in checks.json with hash-bound auxiliary results. Controller review and permitted execution remain outstanding.
