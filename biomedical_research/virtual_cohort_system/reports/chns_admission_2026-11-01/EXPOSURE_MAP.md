## Classes that may remain available, subject to admission

No specific question, target effect direction or final endpoint is selected. Potentially defensible classes include conditional relationships or interactions outside the exposed relation classes; scientifically justified joint-tail or co-occurrence analyses not recoverable from disclosed summaries; and measurement-agreement analyses using explicitly distinguished assays, if both compatible fields and relevant external evidence exist. These are possibilities, not verified unexposed estimands. Higher dimension, a new threshold, a different subgroup or another wave does not itself establish independence. The broad `glad146` snippet makes system-level proposals particularly uncertain.

For each eventual estimand, record population/wave, variables and roles, selection, transformations, adjustment, event/denominator and units. Compare it with both directly reported and algebraically equivalent exposed quantities. Changing units or reversing a correlation preserves information; a changed adjustment set may define a different estimand but does not automatically make it uncontaminated. Classify exposure as known, unresolved or no documented overlap; distinguish this from scientific identifiability and applicability. Missing historical evidence is not proof of non-exposure.

Only proceed to F2 after compatible schema/access, genuinely external source evidence, approximately three useful scientific analyses, operator support and independent exposure review are established. Mark each analysis directly reported, algebraically implied or assumption-dependent. Keep target-family summaries out of a claimed external-only construction packet; any permitted development use must be disclosed separately. A fresh constructor context limits supplied information but resets neither project exposure nor possible pretraining exposure.
