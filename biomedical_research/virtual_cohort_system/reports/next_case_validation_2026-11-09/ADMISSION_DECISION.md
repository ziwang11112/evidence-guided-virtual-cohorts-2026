# Decision: admit the PNS blood-pressure joint-generation task

Host decision, 9 November 2026. Independent history, task, source and selection reviews are preserved beside this note.

**Admit one new task in the previously used PNS release.** The task tests whether one entirely virtual cohort supports BMI associations with systolic and diastolic BP and their residual dependence. It broadens scientific analyses within existing data, without treating a new variable as an independent cohort. No task effect was computed to select this case.

The fixed selection/readiness pass found 8,352 eligible common records from the 8,952-row release, both recorded sexes and full-rank analysis design. There were no identical required-field rows. These are feasibility counts only; no BP mean, correlation or regression result has been computed or revealed. The 5,278 records with unknown/inapplicable pregnancy status are retained by the prospective rule, not asserted to be confirmed nonpregnant. Readiness results stay outside the author input.

The external packet has two quantitative original study families (INTERSALT and Beijing Longitudinal Study of Aging), plus one methods-only family (INTERMAP). It supports a nontrivial construction but does not identify the target joint distribution. In particular, richer-adjustment source BMI slopes do not equal the target regression, averaged subgroup SDs do not identify pooled SDs, and raw SBP–DBP correlation does not identify adjusted residual correlation. Agents may seek further admitted evidence or state assumptions. The host supplies no generator law or wanted coefficients.

Metadata audit preserves the final-field aggregation uncertainty, baseline versus laboratory timing, absence of full public survey-design fields, and sample-selection/transport limits. Evaluation therefore reports finite weighted descriptive estimates and conditional row-resampling stability, not population truth or national-design confidence. Published national prevalence snippets incidentally seen during methods search are excluded from author inputs and disclosed in the exposure record.

Existing local data are sufficient; no new patient dataset was downloaded. The rejected priority hsCRP branch remains parked because of unresolved legacy/current measurement-unit mapping. No further candidate family is added in this unit.

**Next gate:** the finalized new wrapper/evaluator and source packet must pass independent implementation review and hash freeze before author dispatch. Five fresh authors, three scheduled draws and at most one source-feedback revision each remain the approved allocation. All authors close before reference analyses are revealed. An adverse or incomplete result remains reportable and does not authorize target-informed regeneration.
