# Initial author decision

Choose an executable joint descriptive model for all requested analyses. Two reported INTERSALT adjusted BMI coefficients inform additive conditional BMI terms under explicit transport assumptions. Age, sex and BMI are generated jointly; their target distribution is assumed because it is not supplied. The age surface has separate systolic and diastolic late-age trends and a changing systolic sex contrast. The coefficients and knot are assumptions, not regression estimates inferred from Beijing age-group differences.

Paired finite-variance Student-t residuals share a mixing draw and have assumed correlation0.65; systolic scale increases with age. Beijing SDs and raw covariance provide context only. The raw correlation derived from published marginal SDs is not substituted for the adjusted residual correlation. The regression requested by the task is a linear projection of the complete selected output, so it need not equal every conditional parameter in this nonlinear construction.

The model samples the hypothesized selected distribution directly, then uses explicit rejection for dictionary BP feasibility, the existential BMI range and positive pulse pressure. No clipping is applied. Pregnancy, height, weight, nonresponse and labweight mechanisms are not reconstructed; their joint effects remain unidentified. No restriction based on diabetes, hypertension or treatment is imposed. Treatment is marginalized into the descriptive BP law.

Original INTERSALT pp319,320,322 and Beijing methods/Table1 were inspected. No new search was undertaken because the packet supports a transparent bounded candidate, while the remaining quantities require transport assumptions rather than a nominally exact target match. Sources are two original families. Strict INTERSALT coefficient checks remain unavailable because sodium/potassium/alcohol adjustment and centre pooling are absent. Beijing descriptive checks use generated ages65-79 and disclose source age-range, covariate, selection and device differences. They do not reproduce a source population or certify calibration.

Optional source replay is not scientifically defensible with this packet: INTERSALT lacks the required centre/context and covariates, and Beijing lacks the joint age/sex/BMI distribution and verified full age support. It will not be claimed or run. Individual residual variation is modeled; evidence, parameter and transport uncertainty are not probabilistically quantified. All fixed seeds will be retained, with no favorable-seed selection. The initial artifact is awaiting independent review; no generator execution has occurred.

Actual checks are limited to source reading, static AST and scalar algebra documented in checks.json. One accidental read attempt to nonexistent project-root input_missing returned no content and was immediately disclosed to the host. No reference, evaluator, other author, old generator or clinical data were inspected.
