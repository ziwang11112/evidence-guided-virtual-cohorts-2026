# Source review decision

**Retain.** The final generator and model are byte-identical to their initial files. This was the single source-feedback decision.

At n = 100,000 and seed 4101 the isolated Zhu replay returned 10,338 jointly low records (10.338%, compared with 173/1,671 or 10.35% in Table IV) and a crude OR of 6.7321 (published counts imply 6.65). In ascending Hb band order, ferritin geometric means were 1.27963, 5.84625 and 22.20343 µg/L compared with 1.3, 5.9 and 22.3 in Table III. Depletion fractions of 0.94131, 0.60585 and 0.23601 likewise tracked the published fractions of 0.943, 0.600 and 0.234. The generator targets these quantities in expectation. Refitting to deviations in one finite draw would shift it away from the published constraints.

The decision used the supplied Zhu replay and published source evidence alone. The Cuban study's adjusted-ferritin definition supplies no raw-ferritin calibration. The within-band rank relation and transfer to the US target population remain assumptions.
