# Candidate B. Hb strata with continuous conditional ranks

## Evidence and endpoint mapping

Zhu Table III provides three Hb bands, with 53, 205 and 1,413 women. In g/dL the boundaries are 10 and 12. The ferritin geometric means in ascending Hb bands are 1.3, 5.9 and 22.3 µg/L. The reported percentages below 15 µg/L correspond to 50, 123 and 331 women, consistent with Table IV. Drawing the three bands in their observed proportions and ferritin depletion in these conditional proportions reproduces Table IV in expectation. The source joint-low probability is 173/1,671, and the crude odds ratio is (173 × 1,082)/(85 × 331). S1 Tables III and IV summarize the same sample. The reported adjusted odds ratios describe different models. The overall ferritin geometric mean from Table I serves as an aggregate consistency check. I use its Hb location near 13 g/dL only to check plausibility because the statistic's labeling is ambiguous.

No source provides a numerical Spearman coefficient. The model derives the unadjusted rank analysis from the same two continuous columns as the threshold analyses. Pita-Rodríguez used inflammation-corrected ferritin among Cuban mothers. Its association supports the direction of co-occurrence, but it supplies no quantitative calibration for raw ferritin, the continuous ranks or US population prevalence.

## Continuous reconstruction

Within the lowest Hb band I draw 4 + 6 U^(1/5) g/dL, and in the middle band 10 + 2 U^(1/2), where U is uniform. This puts more mass near the upper limit of each band. Above 12 I draw the excess from a gamma distribution with shape 3 and scale 0.46 g/dL. Together these choices imply a mean near 13 g/dL. The 4 g/dL floor, the Hb shapes and the continuous precision are assumptions. No printed interval was treated as a measure of individual dispersion.

Within each Hb band I begin with a logistic distribution for natural log ferritin and separately reweight the portions below and above log(15) to the observed fractions. I solve for its location to match the band's mean log ferritin. The common log-scale parameter of 0.48 is a modeling choice. It allows approximately eightfold between-person spread from the tenth to the ninetieth percentile of a single unweighted component. Reweighting changes this spread, preserves positive concentrations and accommodates strongly right-skewed ferritin. The conditional concentration distribution can change density at 15, and its rare extreme values are extrapolations.

I link the two within-band percentile ranks with a Farlie–Gumbel–Morgenstern copula. At Hb rank u, the chance of crossing below the ferritin threshold is p + theta p(1−p)(1−2u), where p is the band's marginal depletion fraction. The family permits modest positive within-band association. I choose theta values between 0 and 1 to minimize boundary jumps in this chance. At 10 g/dL even the strongest allowable association in the two lower bands leaves a small jump. At 12 g/dL the third theta, about 0.70, gives continuity. This assumption smooths depletion probability across Hb values while leaving the band evidence intact. The source does not identify the within-band ranks, and another shape or copula could materially change Spearman correlation at the same joint-low prevalence and crude OR.

## Transfer and uncertainty

Both contexts draw from this single joint distribution. The target assumes transfer of the source Hb band frequencies, conditional depletion fractions, ferritin geometric means and continuous reconstruction. The evidence supplies no numerical population or assay correction. Matching units alone cannot establish transport from 2004 Chinese IUD users 2–4 years postpartum to US women aged 20–44. Differences in parity, bleeding, inflammation, age composition and assay methods could alter these quantities and the ranks. Raw ferritin remains in µg/L throughout, with no inflammation adjustment, reference-range clipping or imputation. Hb remains in g/dL, and all endpoints use the requested strict cutoffs.

Repeated generation captures individual dispersion and finite-cohort variation conditional on this specification. Model-form, source sampling, measurement and transport uncertainties do not appear as random cohort shifts. No US survey weights, original records, target statistics, covariate-specific effects or within-person biological trajectories have been supplied. The target deliveries are therefore a source-anchored extrapolation for the stipulated population.
