# Shared source packet: published evidence only

S1. Zhu et al. (2010), https://doi.org/10.1024/0300-9831/a000014 , https://storage.imrpress.com/imr/journal/IJVNR/article/501902/1752835456699.pdf . Xianghe County, China, 2004; 1,671 nonpregnant, nonlactating parous women using IUDs 2–4 years postpartum. Venous HemoCue Hb and immunoturbidimetric plasma ferritin. Measurement/population transfer to US survey women is not established.
- Table I: Hb geometric mean 129.9 g/L, plasma ferritin geometric mean 17.4 µg/L. The intervals are as printed; do not infer dispersion from them. Age mean 30.9 years (SD 4.2); BMI mean 23.9 (SD 3.8).
- Table III: Hb <100, 100–<120, ≥120 g/L: band counts [53,205,1413]; ferritin geometric means [1.3,5.9,22.3] µg/L; % with ferritin <15 by band [94.3,60.0,23.4]. Source uses strict cutoffs. Numeric Hb in g/dL equals g/L divided by ten.
- Table IV: low Hb/low raw ferritin 173; low Hb/other ferritin 85; other Hb/low ferritin 331; other/other 1082. Counts summed over folate/B12 strata. Derived crude OR ~6.65. Distinguish from main text age/BMI/education adjusted 6.5 [4.9,8.7] and Table V further adjusted 6.448 [4.827,8.615]. Table III and IV describe the same people, not independent evidence. Do not infer an individual-level rank coefficient from these tables.
- S2. Pita-Rodríguez et al., https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10049065/ : Cuban mothers, ages 18–40; cross-sectional. Low Hb and low ferritin after survey-specific inflammation correction: OR 3.023 [1.816,5.033]. This is not the same raw-ferritin estimand. Contextual evidence only, not a numeric target for raw-ferritin source replay.
