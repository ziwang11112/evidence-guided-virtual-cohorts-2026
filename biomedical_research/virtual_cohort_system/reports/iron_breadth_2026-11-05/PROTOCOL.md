# Iron-status developmental breadth pilot — analysis plan before generation and reveal

Target fixed a priori: NHANES August 2021–August 2023. Selected sample is women 20–44 with RIDEXPRG=2, both measured biomarkers, and positive phlebotomy weight. Report data-inclusion flow; study family previously exposed elsewhere. No untouched-cohort or clinical-use claim. Do not open the real biomarker files until the final virtual tables are sealed.

Use the shared admissions packet; two independent Agent-authored laws (A, B), source-only context, no manual scientific parameters supplied by experimenters. Each author gets one initial review against Zhu source statistics and may retain or revise once. Source seed 4101 / 100,000 synthetic rows per source check; initial/final artifact separation. Computational execution in the existing WSL/bubblewrap isolation only. Mechanical pre-execution corrections logged; no scientific retries after closure. For each final law, generate three target deliveries (5,000 rows; seeds 5101,5102,5103). Do not select successful draws or replace failed authors. Sources and target are separate contexts. No statistical baseline/superiority claim. This study extends endpoint and input types; it does not reopen Wave A or pool its outcomes.

Primary: crude weighted odds ratio for Hb <12 g/dL by raw ferritin <15 µg/L; assess log OR differences. Secondary: weighted joint prevalence (percentage points), weighted Spearman (midranks, recomputed per replicate). For synthetic tables use equal weights; same table for all three endpoints. Strict < thresholds. No post-hoc thresholding or calibration. All estimates are descriptive: no equivalence threshold, p-value votes or composite pass count. Report subject counts, all law/seed results and source vs target discrepancies by analysis. Display sample reference intervals and between-law/within-law variation separately.

SEQN unique keys (DEMO_L, CBC_L, FERTIN_L); positive WTPH2YR, SDMVSTRA/SDMVPSU; keep the release's detection-limit convention, no clinical imputation. Incomplete or unconfirmed pregnancies excluded from the domain, no CRP requirement. Use stratified delete-one PSU jackknife with the other PSUs in each affected stratum inflated by m/(m−1); retain out-of-domain positive-weight PSUs. For each estimand use variance about full estimate with (m−1)/m weights, t cutoff at sum_h(m_h−1) df; log OR, logit prevalence and Fisher-z rank intervals, back-transformed. If zero cells make log OR undefined, report non-estimability without smoothing; raw cell counts <10 flag imprecision. Constant ranks/boundaries, singleton strata or nonestimable delete-one replicates produce appropriate missing uncertainty and reason; don't switch endpoint. Report sample values as uncertain estimates, not population truth.

Keep patient-level real and generated files local; public outputs limited to original protocol, code, metadata and aggregates. Critical invariants: record hash-locked inputs before constructions, final programs/tables before opening raw outcomes, and independently recompute the three endpoint estimates. One three-panel figure to communicate practical differences. Historical cohorts and the RNA reserve are not extended by this pilot.
