# Completed-result publication boundary

This extends the pre-target whitelist with the reviewed complete report, result review, aggregate summary and its rendering scripts; original PNG/SVG figures and captions; closed allocation metadata and authorization; target disposition and measured-cost summaries; and unchanged primary/independent aggregate evaluation reports, intents and completion receipts.

Only explicit files are copied. The final `PUBLIC_EXPORT_COMPLETE.json` records public paths, hashes and byte counts. No referenced paths are followed automatically. In particular, output-table paths and hashes in an inventory are metadata, not permission to export the tables themselves. No real/generated individual CSV, third-party paper/codebook, credential, live STATE, or running-job record is part of this public completion package.

Primary and independent reports include reference sample summaries, regression estimates/intervals, generated aggregate diagnostics and qualified source statistics. These are aggregate scientific results. Individual tables remain local. The final result review records publication-content inspection separately from numerical agreement.

Pre-execution protocols, historical failure records and the primary report's original `independent_recomputation: pending` field retain their original bytes. The subsequent independent completion and final result review establish current matched status. A source-audit release-file byte digest, a canonical controller release digest and a review-excluding approval digest have distinct scopes; they must not be compared as though they were the same encoding.

The public snapshot is not a portable execution bundle. Its local absolute bindings and omitted individual data preserve an inspectable provenance trail without redistributing participants or third-party full texts. Public visibility does not change the developmental/exposed-reference scope or establish a general method advantage.
