## Three different admission questions

1. **Reported evidence:** was the quantity, population, measurement and statistical meaning recovered faithfully from the source?
2. **Qualified source constraint:** can a named mathematical functional be compared under explicit approximations, with original unknowns retained? Unknown source weighting can permit an approved equal-weight scenario while preventing an exact published-estimator claim. Missing estimator precision need not prevent a point comparison; it prevents justified precision weighting or a manufactured confidence interval.
3. **Strict source replay and target applicability:** do the source implementation/context requirements support exact replay, and is use for the proposed target scientifically defensible? These are separate questions. Correct arithmetic or a good source discrepancy does not establish transport or target utility.

The old CHNS approval is case-specific: it approved 16 item roles and two optional assumption-defined correlation proxies, not an exact conditional source model or a reusable target admission. See the [independent packet review](../chns_admission_2026-11-01/f2/review/SOURCE_PACKAGE_REVIEW.md) and [fixed interpretation boundary](../chns_admission_2026-11-01/f2/evidence/SOURCE_INTERPRETATIONS.md). A new case requires its own applicability and interpretation decision, without rewriting the retained source facts.

## Existing operators and what genuinely needs work

Raw unweighted OLS, Pearson and average-tie Spearman already exist. A qualified Jansen comparison therefore needs a reviewed case/context/unit binding and interpretation policy, not a new regression algorithm. Unknown weighting should remain in source metadata; the declared equal-weight scenario belongs in a separate interpretation record.

The reviewed geometric-mean/proportion utility can perform descriptive arithmetic, and the later source-context machinery can bind common generator specifications to source executions. These capabilities establish procedural linkage, not biological transport. Their existing approval does not automatically activate a new case. See the [operator audit](../chns_admission_2026-11-01/evidence/OPERATOR_AND_ESTIMAND_REVIEW.md) and [source-context review](../scaleup_2026-11-01/SOURCE_CONTEXT_INTEGRATION_REVIEW_2026-11-02.md).

A common-denominator cross-tab operator is necessary only if a joint classification endpoint is selected. Required semantics then include all event variables, units, thresholds/equalities, history/treatment definition, common population and missingness/denominator rules. Code cannot supply an absent OGTT or paired specimen. Do not broaden into ROC optimization, causal effects or interaction machinery without a source-supported scientific question.

If no admissible target or useful qualified evidence basis remains within the ceilings, close this intake with its exact reason and preserve the negative completed results.
