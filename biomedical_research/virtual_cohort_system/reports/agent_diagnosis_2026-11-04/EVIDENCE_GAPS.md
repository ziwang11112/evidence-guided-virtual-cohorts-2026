## 2. Evidence already extracted, but not quantitatively constraining the model

A explicitly transports the unadjusted Wang BMI contrast **26.0−24.8=1.2** and HbA1c contrast **7.7−5.8=1.9** into conditional latent components. Its age/BMI/HbA1c/sex slopes remain declared assumptions. See [A specification](../agent_system_live_2026-11-03/authors/A/candidates/initial/model_spec.json#L39), especially `hba1c_location`, `hba1c_stratum_transport`, and parameter provenance; the [generator](../agent_system_live_2026-11-03/authors/A/candidates/initial/generator.py#L16) implements that factorization.

B already extracts Wang total Spearman rho **0.529**, Bao plasma–HbA1c Pearson r **0.619**, and Wang serum/HbA1c means/SDs by diabetes. It deliberately uses them as context, choosing conditional Gaussian correlations **0.50/0.65**, different component means, and assumed covariate pathways. See [B specification](../agent_system_live_2026-11-03/authors/B/candidates/initial/model_spec.json#L70), `A_transport`, `A_dependence`, and `A_covariate`; [B generator](../agent_system_live_2026-11-03/authors/B/candidates/initial/generator.py#L17).

Thus “more evidence extraction” and “more quantitative evidence use” are separate improvements. The marginal correlation was not missed; the known-history correlation was omitted. A ledger should distinguish **implemented exact constraint**, **explicit transport/approximation**, **qualitative motivation**, and **available but unused**, with a reason for the last category. Merely declaring all source checks unavailable obscures this distinction.

An algebraic reason for the remaining uncertainty is that subgroup mean contrasts supply only the between-group part of covariance:

`Cov(BMI, HbA1c) = E[Cov(BMI, HbA1c | D)] + Cov(E[BMI | D], E[HbA1c | D])`.

The first term is not reported in these tables. Adjusting further for age, sex and glucose needs their joint moments as well. Matching the reported subgroup moments therefore cannot by itself identify the requested regressions.

## 3. Contradiction versus unresolved transport

No new material transcription error or code/specification inconsistency was found in the inspected pathways. The earlier audit's conclusion on this limited point remains supportable. This does **not** certify that either candidate is calibrated to evidence.

The positive age dependence of the latent elevated component, lack of a direct sex term in that component, and B's copula choices are not the same estimands as Wang's observed age/sex-by-diabetes summaries or known-history rank correlation. Consequently their differences are **unverified transports/assumptions**, not established contradictions. Conversely, declaring a free latent state does not turn its parameters into evidence-based estimates.

B's primary **15% meal-affected fraction**, increment **mean 1.5 and SD 0.8 mmol/L**, and independence from other variables have no admitted empirical support. [B `A_fasting`](../agent_system_live_2026-11-03/authors/B/candidates/initial/model_spec.json#L86) states this correctly. Unverified target fasting adherence establishes uncertainty, not that fraction or increment. This is a concrete missing-evidence/scenario issue, but its contribution to any observed analysis difference has not been identified here.

The source-check status `not_computable` is not proof of a scientific contradiction or a failed model. It describes the submitted checking setup. Useful evidence work can proceed by exact extraction, checking units/estimands, auditing parameter mappings and deriving model implications analytically. A source-population simulation would introduce additional composition assumptions and is optional, not an imposed scientific requirement.

## 4. What is genuinely missing in the admitted set

- Numeric conditional effects or sufficient joint covariance information for **HbA1c ~ BMI + age + sex**, and especially **HbA1c ~ BMI + serum glucose + age + sex**, in an applicable population. Neither Bao nor Wang fits those requested regressions. Jansen's abstract names predictors without individual coefficient signs, magnitudes, SEs, coding or a complete fitted-model specification.
- Within-status BMI/HbA1c/glucose covariance, age/sex interactions, and a mapping between diagnosed/treated/OGTT/FPG-defined groups and the submitted latent states. The newly extracted joint counts reduce some uncertainty without filling this gap.
- A target composition and transport rationale sufficiently specified to choose among younger urban no-known-diabetes, older rural treated/untreated, and Dutch nondiabetic source contexts. Age 35–74 and complete measurements do not identify a target joint distribution.
- Evidence about the target serum assay/processing and fasting-adherence distribution, treatment/history and complete-case selection. Source methods are known; their transfer is not.

These are gaps **in this admitted set and model mapping**, not proof that the wider literature lacks the information. Jansen is specifically an access/extraction gap: saved metadata lists a publisher PDF route, but no full text was admitted. That route may or may not work; no access claim is made.
