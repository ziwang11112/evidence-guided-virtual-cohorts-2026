# v0.13 story, contribution and figure roles

The central research question is whether an Agent can convert study-qualified published evidence, a population description and an intended analysis into complete virtual biomedical data with useful analytical properties when individual construction records are unavailable. Missing-variable enrichment remains part of the wider project, but is not substituted for whole-cohort generation in the present main experiments.

The new title is **Evidence-Guided Agents for Analyzable Virtual Biomedical Cohorts**. The paper leads with a practical data gap and the scientific work required to turn heterogeneous summaries into a joint law. It does not lead with Agent count, a new loss, or a universal performance contest.

## Three supported contributions

1. **An implemented evidence-to-cohort procedure.** The Agent chooses sources and their numerical uses, authors a scientific law and creates complete cohorts without individual construction inputs. The new four-author experiment demonstrates repeated bounded execution across two requests with different model families.
2. **Inspectable scientific modeling.** Study populations, measurements and operators remain attached to evidence. The worked projection conversion and missing-dependence sensitivity expose consequential scientific decisions and underidentified assumptions. The statistical identities themselves are established mathematics.
3. **Analysis-centered empirical evaluation.** External-evidence, summary-assisted, published-model and open-retrieval settings demonstrate different uses. Estimates, magnitude, joint relationships, uncertainty context and adverse diagnostics remain visible. No composite success score merges incompatible information settings or units.

## What changed in the manuscript

- The title, abstract and contribution paragraphs now emphasize complete cohorts, substantive Agent authorship and repeated workflow execution.
- Method details distinguish curated packets, the earlier two-stage acquisition/construction example and the new four-author bounded retrieval procedure.
- Q4 contains the full new execution accounting, model-family diversity, all six primary analyses and decisive joint/dispersion limitations. All previous main Tables 2/3 and result Sections 4.2–4.4 retain their numerical content.
- The earlier single-constructor result is condensed into an explicit analysis-sensitivity example; its original full plot and caption remain Figure S1.
- Nine admitted original-study bibliographic records are added, with a twelve-study/sixteen-use inventory in Table S2. There are 24 references, five main figures, three main tables, one supplementary figure and two supplementary tables.
- The discussion separates demonstrated construction/selected usefulness from unestablished broad transfer, uncertainty calibration and feedback benefit. A pre-freeze divergent-moment repair in an earlier live construction remains explicit.
