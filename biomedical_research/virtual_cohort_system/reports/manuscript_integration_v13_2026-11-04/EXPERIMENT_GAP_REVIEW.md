# Claim–evidence map and experimental gap decision for v0.13

4 November 2026. Independent review by `/root/v13_experiment_gap`.

**Decision: GO for an applied-method manuscript centered on complete virtual cohorts and analysis-specific usefulness. No additional experiment is a prerequisite for that bounded claim. STOP before presenting these results as general target-population fidelity, independent clinical confirmation, or a demonstrated benefit of retrieval or revision.** The new four-author batch closes the earlier implementation gap concerning repeated open-retrieval construction under one policy. It does not close the population-transfer or structural-uncertainty gaps.

This assessment reads the v0.12 draft, current scope, actual completed result reports, and the new batch's interpretation, report/figure and evaluator-correction reviews. It does not open participant or generated rows, recalculate their analyses, execute generators, retrieve sources, or alter frozen records. Numerical statements below are reported audited results, not a new numerical audit. The earlier method/rerun audit is used for version identity, not as an instruction to initiate its then-prospective experiment proposals.

## 1. The contribution that the evidence supports

The central object is an analyzable complete cohort constructed from a biomedical population/measurement request, published evidence, optional observations and an intended analysis. In the central experiments, optional individual observations are absent. The Agent makes consequential scientific choices about evidence meaning, model structure, numerical use and unsupported completion; deterministic programs sample the rows. Ordinary Agents are valid implementations. A trained model, several collaborating scientific authors, or superiority over every bespoke statistical generator is not necessary for this contribution.

A defensible paper-level claim is:

> An Agent can translate study-qualified biomedical evidence into executable whole-cohort models, produce complete datasets that preserve selected scientific relationships, and make the assumptions limiting other analyses inspectable. Repeated open-retrieval constructions, controlled information contrasts, summary reconstruction and a longitudinal example provide complementary evidence for this capability.

Here, “useful” means a demonstrated, bounded analytical use: the table supports the intended analysis and selected resulting relationships have useful descriptive agreement. It does not mean that a prospective scientific decision met a validated tolerance, that all downstream analyses are reliable, or that the virtual observations replace measurement. The existing paper can make the first claim without asserting the others.

The new batch should be the clearest demonstration of repeated execution of the current bounded acquisition-to-cohort process. The historical E0/E1 study remains the stronger evidence about the consequences of information availability. The earlier open-search construction remains the clearest example of an explicit missing dependence controlling a conditional answer. These roles are complementary; none should be relabelled as another experiment's version or control arm.

## 3. What remains missing, and whether it blocks the present paper

### Must-have for the present modest applied-method claim

**No new scientific experiment is presently required.** The prior single-constructor implementation limitation has now been addressed by four fresh-context constructions under the unified bounded retrieval/review policy. The reports inspected here identify no unresolved shared implementation failure requiring regeneration. The new scalar-evaluator defect was corrected additively and its results independently audited; HRS has its separate closed parser repair. This is a report-based judgment, not a new complete code/environment certification.

The remaining mandatory work is integration and claim discipline: show all six new endpoints, retain the diagnostic failures, identify the actual implementation and source-input boundaries, and preserve each experiment's denominator. These are manuscript and reproducibility tasks. They do not become missing experiments simply because their accurate reporting weakens a broad claim.

In particular, the negative results are scientifically central:

- Both CHNS laws understate adjusted age; both ENSANUT laws overstate it. Shared sources and an imposed age implication near 0.10 pp/decade explain why author agreement is not independent empirical convergence.
- C01 approximately matches glucose–HbA1c Pearson correlation (0.666 versus 0.661) but not Spearman correlation (0.635 versus 0.361).
- ENSANUT residual HbA1c RMS is approximately 0.295/0.324 versus 0.488, and total SD is 0.362/0.406 versus 0.527. These differences limit uses depending on residual heterogeneity, extreme values, prediction uncertainty or precision.

These findings are compatible with selected relationship preservation. They are incompatible with a blanket claim that the entire target distribution has been recovered. No extra random seeds can resolve that distinction.

### The one most informative bounded extension

The principal unresolved scientific question is **how strongly intended analyses depend on unsupported completion and transport assumptions when the admitted evidence stays fixed**. This is a genuine limitation; it need not block a manuscript that reports it. The earlier BMI–glucose example already demonstrates the phenomenon, but the new rank and dispersion mismatches show that it is not confined to one conditional coefficient.

If one additional method experiment is desired, a small, prespecified assumption-sensitivity study is the most direct extension. It should quantify sensitivity rather than try to repair every exposed-reference discrepancy:

1. Select one of the new task/law pairs by a rule independent of closeness to its target reference. Prefer a rule such as the first eligible constructor by frozen identifier. State one unsupported dependence or residual-allocation decision identified in its evidence ledger.
2. Before any alternative output is inspected, define at most two scientifically interpretable alternatives from the admitted source ranges or explicitly declared assumption bounds. Keep the original law, source packet, population, measurements and analysis definitions fixed. If evidence cannot justify a bound, call it a scenario rather than probabilistic uncertainty.
3. Use analytical law implications where available. Only if those are insufficient, use a separate bounded release for the original and two scenarios with the same small fixed seed set. Preserve all scheduled analyses and the existing diagnostic set. Do not search until a scenario approaches the exposed reference.
4. Report native-unit changes in coefficients, residual variation and the relevant dependence diagnostics. The result is sensitivity to assumptions. It is not a better calibrated generator, generalization, or a causal feedback effect.

This is a proposal, not an initiated run or authorization to change frozen models. A one-law study would deepen a mechanism example; it would not establish robustness across requests. If the paper does not promise robustness, leaving the issue as an explicit limitation is sufficient.

These are conditional requirements, not a work queue for v0.13. Choose a stronger claim only if it is scientifically necessary, then choose its discriminating experiment. The absence of an untuned “general Agent” baseline, training, multi-Agent collaboration, direct token-level row generation, hidden research questions, complete source-population replay, or universal defeat of bespoke statistics is not a gap in the present contribution. End-to-end time/cost savings and arbitrary-request autonomy should remain unclaimed rather than generating a new benchmark program solely to fill them.

## 4. Integration priorities and STOP/GO decision

Use the new batch to answer: **Can the same bounded open-evidence procedure repeatedly deliver complete, analyzable cohorts across two requests?** Show all four authors and six endpoint definitions in the main results. Keep the source-law diversity and two or three decisive joint/distribution failures visible beside the positive directions. They explain why the contribution is analysis-specific.

Use the historical E0/E1 experiment for the information contrast, the L/R study for reconstruction with supplied summaries, HRS for longitudinal capability, and the earlier open-search/sensitivity case for a concrete missing relationship. No pooled “success rate,” cross-version improvement, or combined virtual-row validation count is justified. The summary-assisted and longitudinal settings are legitimate use cases even though their relevant numerical answers are intentionally supplied.

The accounting must stay separate:

| Unit | Construction/table identity to preserve |
|---|---|
| Historical main study | 20 Agent + 4 statistical constructions; 72 final tables; 79 physical executions including 7 exploratory tables. |
| L/R summary study | 6 Agents; 18 distinct final delivery tables; 42 physical executions and 24 distinct tables overall, including trials and duplicated initial/final deliveries. |
| HRS example | 2 constructions; 6 valid comparison tables; 9 physical generations including 3 invalid initial comparator attempts. |
| Earlier open-search construction | 1 fresh constructor using a separately retrieved/reviewed five-paper packet; 3 final tables plus 1 trial. |
| New unified open-retrieval batch | 4 authors; 12 final tables/36,000 final virtual rows; 4 trials/12,000 trial virtual rows; 16 successful physical executions; 36 primary + 330 diagnostic values = 366 audited values; 0 scientific revisions. |

The new batch's 16 source uses are 12 distinct originals, not 16 independent evidence sources. The repeated CHNS/ENSANUT references remain two exposed developmental families. The new figures should make the sample-reference role and distinct CHNS/ENSANUT uncertainty objects clear. This review gives no opinion on a layout that it has not visually inspected.

**GO:** integrate, write and render v0.13 now with the bounded contribution above. No fresh clinical read, new retrieval, generator rerun or larger seed allocation is needed to support that manuscript.

**STOP at stronger claims:** do not describe whole-distribution recovery, clinically interchangeable data, independent confirmation, source-independent convergence, retrieval/revision gains, or calibrated structural uncertainty. The same-evidence sensitivity proposal is the nearest focused methodological extension if the authors elect to do more; an unexposed-reference evaluation becomes necessary only when the paper explicitly claims transport beyond the current developmental examples.

This is a scientific-scope decision, not a guarantee of journal acceptance or a declaration that author declarations, production checks and submission requirements are complete.
