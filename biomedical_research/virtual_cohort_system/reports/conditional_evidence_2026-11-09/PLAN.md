# Conditional-evidence audit for existing scientific uses

Authorized continuation, 9 November 2026. Prior coherence unit is closed and remains unchanged.

## Purpose

Determine whether published external evidence can constrain the three existing uses: continuous BMI coefficients for HDL and total cholesterol adjusted for age/sex, and TC–HDL Pearson residual correlation adjusted for age/sex/BMI. The goal remains evidence-guided analyzable virtual data, including complete generation without individual input. This is an evidence acquisition/admission unit before any new generation.

## Information boundary

Read existing source artifacts before duplicating downloads. Researchers receive source packets and requested estimands, not old target results or generated outputs. Exclude CHNS, ENSANUT and the Turkana target study (Lea et al. 2020; DOI 10.1126/sciadv.abb1430; Dryad 10.5061/dryad.2z34tmpjk). No target-outcome search, cohort download or old-target tuning. No closed bundle, frozen engine, receipt or parameter edits.

Distinguish measured assay outputs from latent concentrations. An evidence item with a measured outcome may inform a measured-data law under population/measurement transfer assumptions; it is not automatically an observation-error-free latent constraint. Preserve population, selection, fasting/assay/units, weighting, transformations, adjustment sets, uncertainty and overlapping cohorts. Normal ranges or diagnostic cutoffs do not identify conditional covariance.

## Work streams

1. Re-read the original methods, tables and supplements behind existing association evidence; determine whether useful continuous coefficients or covariance information were missed.
2. Search external original studies for compatible adjusted BMI–lipid relations and conditional variability.
3. Search joint covariance/correlation evidence or complete compatible summaries from which the requested residual relation is algebraically identifiable; distinguish Pearson from rank statistics and report all assumptions.
4. Independently audit the strongest potentially executable constraint and its exact scientific meaning before admitting it to a generator.

## Search and stopping

Search by missing scientific information, not a fixed paper count. Use primary sources. Hospital/government/manufacturer material may establish definitions and measurement practice, but a numerical population relation requires supporting study data. Record queries, URLs, inspected sections, actual useful information, exclusions and unresolved access problems. Keep source bodies local; no new paid services, training, HPC or applications.

Use two complementary discovery passes (existing-source/supplement follow-up, then broader targeted retrieval). Stop a gap when compatible usable statistics are found and independently verified, or when both passes provide no new executable information. Do not keep searching to select a favorable sign/magnitude. Initial transport ceiling: 24 discovery requests across the team and 100 MiB of new locally saved source material; it is a resource guard, not a required number of papers. Coordinate overlapping candidates and report actual usage, including unmetered browsing separately. Admission can remain partial.

## Outputs and decisions

Produce evidence inventory, explicit use-to-evidence map, source uncertainty/transfer and identifiability assessment, and independent review. If no adequate constraint is found, preserve the negative finding and close this branch without generating more tables. If an executable constraint is found, write a source-supported candidate/replay specification, including whether the requested estimand or a scientifically justified different analysis is supported. A different estimand must remain separately labelled, never silently substituted. Decide a bounded follow-on construction only after the evidence review; a positive source audit alone is not clinical validation.

Public synchronization is limited to reviewed original code/docs, evidence metadata and aggregate calculations. Full source documents and individual tables remain local. No new case or target is admitted in this unit.
