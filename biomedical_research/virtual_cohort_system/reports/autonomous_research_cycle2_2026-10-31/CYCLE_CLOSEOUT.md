# Cycle 2 closeout: evidence-only ENSANUT virtual cohorts

31 October 2026. **Scientific work complete and independently checked. This bounded cycle ends after documentation and private GitHub synchronization; it does not launch another case.**

## Outcome and decision

The useful result is selective analytical feasibility, with a clear quantitative limitation. Six unique fictional cohorts preserve positive age and BMI associations with HbA1c and the descriptive reduction of the BMI coefficient after glucose adjustment. All prespecified primary effect magnitudes disagree materially with the selected real reference: generated age coefficients are higher, and both BMI coefficients are lower. Secondary glucose transfer is closer. Generated biological dispersion is also too narrow.

Both fresh authors retained their initial scientific programs after the fixed source feedback. The feedback mechanism identified a substantive source-age mismatch but did not produce a justified revision. This cycle provides **no evidence of iterative improvement**. The authors independently chose the same linear Gaussian family and residual BMI/glucose correlation 0.30; their agreement is not structural robustness evidence.

Root decision: preserve the complete case as evidence about which analyses the current method can and cannot support. Simplify the claimed contribution to evidence-conditioned virtual-data construction and tested analytical use. Stop tuning this revealed ENSANUT reference and stop treating a feedback loop or multiple Agents as an established benefit. A normal single scientific Agent remains a valid implementation, and full generation with zero individual inputs remains central.

The independent critic proposed a simpler aggregate/projection comparator for a future study. That would help only if claiming added value or improvement; outperforming a direct-analysis Agent is **not** adopted as a prerequisite for the user's data-generation feasibility question. No new fixed baseline, new dataset or additional experiment is automatically imposed by this closeout.

## What the analyses mean

For HbA1c percentage points per five BMI units, the reference age/sex-adjusted BMI coefficient is 0.08364, while generated values range from 0.05032 to 0.05904. With glucose additionally adjusted, the reference is 0.05664 and generated values range from 0.01631 to 0.02811. Direction agreement therefore coexists with quantitative underestimation.

The identity Q3 = Q2 − (Q2 − Q3) shows that about 77%/80% of A/B's arithmetic Q3 shortfall is already present in Q2. Those are descriptive accounting shares, not causal attribution. The result does not isolate covariance as the cause: source projection transfer is already inadequate before the added glucose adjustment. This matters for choosing the next research question rather than adding components to the existing loop.

Reference bands are empirical stability descriptions of the selected finite sample under the declared resampling scheme. They are not known population truth, confidence-coverage guarantees or equivalence thresholds. Six generated outputs across three seeds and two constructions do not supply six independent clinical cohorts. The new result supports qualified scenario/qualitative-analysis use in this case, not recovery of individual measurements, reliable effect sizes, causal inference or substitution for clinical data collection.

## Next-step boundary

The bounded next question, if a new cycle is requested, should be whether more applicable external population/measurement/conditional evidence and explicit model/transport uncertainty improve **prospectively specified analytical use** on another untouched cohort. Freeze the scientific estimands and useful precision before inspecting that cohort. Distinguish source-supported projections from joint/transport assumptions, and preserve incompatible plausible constructions instead of choosing one against the revealed answer.

Do not start a large benchmark, more Agent roles, a new loss, additional training, or another feedback round merely to make the system look more advanced. Any additional analysis of this already exposed target would need an explicit developmental label and would not be fresh confirmation. This closeout itself authorizes no new case.
