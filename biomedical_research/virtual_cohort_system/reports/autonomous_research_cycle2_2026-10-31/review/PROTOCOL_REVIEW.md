## What is already adequate

- **Finite reference and demographic standard.** The estimator is a projection of the complete eligible linked reference, with each of eight age/sex groups assigned mass 1/8. Individual reference weights 1/(8*n_h) define that finite estimand. The 2,000 generated rows, including 250 per group, do not increase clinical evidence. This resolves the original national-weight applicability problem by limiting the question; it does not resolve the population sampling design or complete-case selection for national inference. The first target-definition paragraph should use the same explicit finite-reference wording as the later paragraphs.
- **Module disagreement.** Keeping lab and anthropometry design columns separately, declining to substitute expansion weights, and treating lab-cluster resampling only as a chosen empirical perturbation are defensible. The non-bijective stratum mismatch is a limitation, not a reason this finite-reference projection cannot be calculated. Do not call the resampling survey-design validation or uncertainty about national truth.
- **Paired anthropometry.** First valid same-repeat standing weight/height pair, then the second complete pair, is a reproducible investigator-defined measurement. It avoids assembling an artificial pair from different repeats and does not require recovering the unpublished third-reading replacement process. Discrepancy diagnostics must remain audit-only. The rule must not be represented as the official final ENSANUT BMI algorithm.
- **Eligibility semantics.** Lab P7==2 is a deliberate no-reported-prior-diagnosis/high-blood-sugar subset; it excludes the separately coded pregnancy-related history and does not verify treatment status or absence of clinical diabetes. The 8–12-hour condition is recorded elapsed time since food. Anthropometry P6==4 for women 25–49 is an explicit pregnancy/lactation restriction; routing outside that range does not establish a negative answer. These choices are legitimate prospective restrictions, even though the source studies differ.
- **Source differences.** Unavailable anemia status, the source plasma versus reference serum distinction, assay differences, and source age/selection differences are transfer assumptions. No hemoglobin archive is needed merely to erase these differences. HbA1c cannot supply an anemia exclusion. Keeping pregnancy/lactation and treatment differences visible is also necessary.
- **Three analytical uses.** Q1 is the Pani/NHANES-indexed adjusted age transfer question; Q2 is the Lifelines-indexed BMI projection adjusted for age and sex; Q3 is the BMI coefficient additionally adjusted for glucose and is not a supplied calibration target. These are three valid prospective association estimands. The secondary age/sex-adjusted glucose coefficient preserves the other directly source-indexed association. Lifelines Model III and Liu's univariate equation must not be substituted for Q3. The age coefficients are cross-sectional associations, and Q2 minus Q3 is not mediation.
- **Partial identification and generation.** Marginal summaries and separate projections can constrain, but cannot identify, the five-variable distribution. A defensible generator may therefore choose an explicit dependence structure, residual law, source reconstruction, and transport rule. A well-fitting source reconstruction does not establish those choices as true. All five scientific fields remain generated; fixed synthetic stratum composition is a design constraint, not supplied individual data. No source or target individual records are needed by the builder.

## Required minimal fixes before release

### 1. Finish one literal preparation specification

Freeze a compact field/code table from the already acquired official metadata. It must contain the exact age-routed pairs (PESO1_1 with TALLA4_1, then PESO1_2 with TALLA4_2 below 60; PESO12_1 with TALLA15_1, then PESO12_2 with TALLA15_2 at 60+), P11 route codes including the questionnaire-only code 2, and the meanings of the applicable P3/P5/P14/P16 result codes. The draft names routing and valid readings but does not yet settle how a failed, unknown, or missing result code interacts with a positive numeric pair.

Use an explicit precedence rule: an official route that does not represent standing measurement, or an explicit failed measurement result, cannot be rescued merely by a positive recorded number. State how unknown/missing result codes are handled when the field is asked; distinguish those from structural routing blanks. The custodian must not invent this choice after viewing its impact. Unrecognized codes trigger the existing pre-effect adjudication stop.

Spell out P6==4, laboratory P7==2, numeric finite age and analytes, strictly positive glucose/HbA1c/weight/height, glucose<126, and the existing recorded-duration restriction. Apply the weight sentinel 222.222 only to weight and the height sentinel 222.2 only to height, including the declared extension to second repeats. Exclude a row when neither same-repeat pair is usable; do not cross-pair or average.

The freeze must explicitly supersede the unexecuted averaging proposal in PREFLIGHT_METADATA.json. Keep that preflight artifact as an historical record; do not let its proposed mean-weight/mean-height formula compete with the frozen first-pair/fallback rule. The code review should verify the supersession.

### 2. Make the empirical resampling and descriptive operators exact

A sufficient simple choice is: define clusters by the pair of lab EST_DIS and UPM_DIS among eligible reference rows; within each represented lab stratum with c distinct eligible PSUs, draw c PSUs with replacement and carry every eligible row in each selected cluster, retaining multiplicity. A singleton contributes its sole cluster once. Recompute the eight-group standard in every replicate. An original person's contribution is its cluster multiplicity divided by eight times the total multiplicity in its demographic group. This makes duplicate clusters contribute correctly and preserves each nonempty demographic group's mass of 1/8.

Declare the actual chosen scheme before implementation; if it instead samples the full lab-PSU frame, name that different frame explicitly. Freeze the seed, 399 attempted replicates, the percentile endpoints (for example 2.5% and 97.5%), quantile interpolation convention, and failure reporting. Empty demographic groups or rank failure invalidate a replicate; do not retry until 399 successful draws are obtained. Report attempted and valid counts, failure categories, and that displayed bands use valid replicates only. If no valid replicates remain, report the band unavailable. These are descriptive stability bands conditional on the chosen empirical scheme, with no confidence-coverage claim.

Also freeze the numerical conventions for standardized SDs, weighted quantiles, and residual spread, plus full-rank/numerical-singularity checks. A sufficient finite-reference SD is the square root of the weighted mean squared deviation; it need not invoke a national sampling variance correction. Independently verify the fixed WLS and resampling arithmetic on artificial data, as already required by the draft.

## Recommendations that do not block execution

- Call Q2 minus Q3 the **BMI coefficient change after glucose adjustment**. Use “attenuation” only when that description fits the realized coefficients; the change could show suppression or a sign reversal. Preserve its descriptive, noncausal status.
- Use “recorded 8–12 hours since food” where practical, rather than implying independently verified fasting. Use the full pregnancy/lactation label and never shorten the cohort to universally nonpregnant, anemia-free, untreated, or healthy adults.
- Keep an explicit assumption table distinguishing source-identified quantities from selected joint/transport assumptions. The current construction/stage budget can expose competing assumptions if builders choose them. Do not create a new sensitivity-run budget, exhaustive joint-identification project, archive acquisition, or broader framework merely to satisfy this review. If alternatives are not evaluated, say their sensitivity is unmeasured.
- Treat generated seeds as simulation variability, source uncertainty as separate, and empirical reference bands as stability. Neither source agreement, synthetic significance, band overlap, nor a favorable seed establishes target equivalence or clinical validity.
