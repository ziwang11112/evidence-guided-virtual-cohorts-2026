# Greiner source-packet candidate

[SOURCE_CANDIDATE_GREINER.json](SOURCE_CANDIDATE_GREINER.json) contains six Malawi/Uganda sex/site strata, 24 reported geometric means (BMI, TC, HDL and derived non-HDL), six low-HDL percentages with strict sex-specific thresholds, and 18 age median/quartile records. Table 3's 12 BMI/non-HDL coefficients are contextual only. No other variables become numerical targets, and no generator, loss weights, clinical parameters or success tolerances were chosen.

The candidate retains the original units, DOI/URL/row locators, source eligibility, table-specific subgroup counts and estimation-CI type. Row-specific counts are not invented. The inconsistent table denominators and anomalous Malawi urban female HDL CI remain explicit; CIs are neither individual SDs nor automatic fit weights. Quartile conventions and regression scales remain unresolved, so those records cannot enter numerical calibration.

Geometric-mean and threshold recipes are mathematically explicit. Their equal-weight implementation is a declared operational choice because source weighting is not explicit. The existing semantic collector does not support the geometric operator; this packet does not claim otherwise. All check activation awaits independent review and a declared evaluation context. Evaluating a Turkana table against these numbers is not exact source-population replay: eligibility, population composition, assays and fasting conditions differ.

An Agent could choose a lognormal marginal and use a geometric mean plus threshold proportion to constrain its parameters. The packet states the conditional algebra but computes no parameters. Such a dispersion would be model-derived, not a reported individual SD, and would not identify a conditional residual law or dependence with other variables. No dispersion is obtained from CIs.
