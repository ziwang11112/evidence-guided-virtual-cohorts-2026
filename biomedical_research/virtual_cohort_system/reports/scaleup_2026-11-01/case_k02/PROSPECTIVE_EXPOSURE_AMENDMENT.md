# Prospective K02 exposure classification and Wave A rule

2 November 2026. This is an explicit **pre-outcome amendment** to the scale-up design, following independent review of the already recorded exposure. It preserves the original campaign file and its hash, ceilings, initial/final structure, constructors and seeds. No new target effects were read to make this decision.

## Definite classification

Turkana is a distinct measured target study family from the Greiner Malawi/Uganda construction sources. The reviewed cohort provenance documents no participant overlap; this is not a participant-level identity audit. The coordinating researchers have already seen broad target-study lifestyle findings and a generic residual-dependence summary. The three exact K02 quantitative projections have not been computed or disclosed in the inspected record.

K02 is therefore a **context-exposed, prospective quantitative holdout**, not a fully unseen independent confirmation. It may occupy one of the four bounded case slots and contribute study-family breadth. It must not count toward a claim of four fully unseen independent validations. The maximum design is now described as **up to four total cases, including this qualified case and at most three other independently admitted confirmation cases**. Neither admission nor success of the other three is guaranteed.

Tables must report separate counts for total measured cases, distinct study families, qualified quantitative holdouts and fully unseen confirmations. Changing an adjustment set or withholding exact coefficients does not erase the recorded exposure. K02 remains subject to evidence, task, analysis and implementation release; this classification alone does not admit it or permit a run.

## Fixed consequence for Wave A

K01 must be assigned an independently admitted, unexposed quantitative reference before the existing global freeze. **Only a K01 pass of its prespecified quantitative-use bundle can satisfy the positive scientific continuation condition for Wave B.** K02 success alone cannot unlock Wave B. Material implementation or interpretation defects still pause progression even if K01 appears to pass. If K01 fails admission or its fixed bundle, do not replace it after target revelation or run Wave B merely because K02 looks favorable; save the complete results for a method/claim decision.

K02's complete favorable and unfavorable results remain informative for the paper's qualified analysis-use question and will be reported regardless of the gate. This rule narrows continuation rather than increasing experiments. It does not redefine a pass, add a new threshold, or convert direction-only agreement into quantitative success.

## Constructor information boundary

Fresh scientific constructors receive only the reviewed clinical request, source evidence, measurement metadata and allowed source feedback. They do not receive this exposure note, the target article's outcome narrative, the raw reference, historical target summaries, observed sample demographics, or reference-derived means, slopes or dispersions. Root does not author a desired conditional relation in the generator. Missing target composition and joint relationships remain explicit model assumptions selected by the constructor.

Independent review accepted this classification and gate before target execution. The original campaign inventory remains planning-only; the eventual global release must include and enforce this amendment, not silently evaluate its older gate text.
