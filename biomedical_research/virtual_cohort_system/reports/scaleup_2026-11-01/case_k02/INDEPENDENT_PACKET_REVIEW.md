## Statistical-reference design and information fairness

S01's proposed HDL inversion is algebraically correct under its declared
continuous, unweighted lognormal scenario. With `mu=log(GM)` and threshold
probability p, `p=Phi((log(c)-mu)/sigma)`, so
`sigma=(log(c)-log(GM))/Phi_inverse(p)`, requiring the stated finite positive
domain. The proposal author reports a 6-of-6 source-only domain check; this
review checked the algebra and its assumptions without rerunning a clinical
fit or producing parameters or rows.

The reference's independent within-stratum BMI/HDL/non-HDL draws, common log
dispersion, positive sum `TC=HDL+N`, independent `Uniform(18,80)` age and equal
donor-stratum weights are arbitrary completion assumptions, explicitly labelled
as such. They are acceptable as a simple transparent reference, not as optimal
statistical use of the packet or reconstructed source/target truth. Age summaries
are left unused consistently with the current numerical-calibration prohibition.
The assumed generated age support cannot exclude actual target adults over 80.
The positive-sum model cannot reproduce measured TC<HDL, but that limitation
cannot remove those target records.

S01 analytically uses the six non-HDL means for its internal component without
claiming an implemented non-HDL diagnostic or an extra measured biomarker. TC
geometric means remain unfitted diagnostic evidence. Its target mixture can
induce BMI-lipid association through stratum means; no target regression is
assigned a desired direction or fitted to an answer. Joint-use performance is
strongly assumption-dependent and must be interpreted accordingly.

Every constructor receives the same evidence and request. S01 receives the same
initial source diagnostic packet but precommits retention, with no Monte Carlo
recalibration or family selection afterward. This makes its zero revision gain
honest, while preserving the acknowledged asymmetry of five Agent constructions
versus one fixed reference. Failure of S01 cannot support a claim that richer
statistical approaches fail; best-Agent selection or equal-search superiority
claims remain excluded.

## Finite budget and revision accounting

The unchanged target seeds are 2101, 2102 and 2103, each for 5,000 rows. The six
source contexts each have 5,000 rows and one fixed seed, 3101 through 3106,
reused across constructors and a changed final stage. Source and target budgets

The reservation is 72 source labels / 360,000 scheduled rows and 36 target

alias initial artifacts; unused S01 capacity is not transferable. Failures and

The six initial source reports form one feedback packet. Final diagnostics are
retained without another revision opportunity. Final target stage remains
primary; initial results support paired revision/retention analysis. No target
scientific summaries go to constructors.

The final proposal narrows parameter calculation to at most one finite analytic
job per initial or changed final stage, 120 seconds and 1,024 MiB, with explicit
input/evidence/code/output commitments and an attempt receipt. No numerical
optimizer, quadrature, model grid or fitting-time simulated cohorts are allowed.
It does not require a new generic optimizer counter platform. Ordinary declared
bounded support sampling and RNG rejection remain permitted within scheduled
generator limits; extra complete-cohort redraws or parameter/seed adaptation to
improve diagnostic fit remain forbidden. These distinctions are appropriate.

## Exposure amendment and continuation

The explicit prospective amendment resolves the counting question before
outcomes: K02 is a context-exposed quantitative holdout, not a fully unseen
independent confirmation. Reviewed provenance documents no source-target
participant overlap, but is not a participant identity audit. K02 may occupy
one of at most four total case slots and contribute study-family breadth; the
other at most three cases require independent admission. Reports must separate
these counts rather than claim four fully unseen validations.

The amendment preserves the original campaign file and narrows its future
application. An independently admitted K01 must pass its frozen quantitative
bundle to provide the positive Wave B continuation condition. K02 success alone
cannot unlock Wave B; material invalidity still pauses progression. Failure of
K01 admission or its bundle cannot trigger a post-outcome replacement. The
eventual global freeze and controller must include this amendment explicitly.
