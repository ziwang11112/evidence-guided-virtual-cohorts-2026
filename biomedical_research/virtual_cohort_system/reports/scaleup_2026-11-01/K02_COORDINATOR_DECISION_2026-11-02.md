BMI contrasts are per 5 kg/m²; measured lipids remain in mg/dL for target analysis. No expected direction or magnitude is prescribed.

No trimming, inferred health exclusions, alternative lipid endpoints or outcome-based sample selection. Literal sex codes were checked without frequencies or other variable values. The earlier 397 is still an availability count, not final eligible N.

## What the method implementation must now do

1. **Make the selected analyses executable.** Implement a pure supplied-table adapter, with explicit eligibility accounting and all three estimands. Test it on small invented arrays only. HC3 slope intervals are assumption-conditional sensitivity summaries for a selected sample; residual population uncertainty remains unavailable. No demographic or outcome estimates from the real target are produced in this unit.
2. **Bind additional diagnostics to actual outputs.** Use the existing frozen lifecycle and execution verifier and explicitly retain semantic_feedback_v1. Add the already reviewed geometric-mean/threshold utility as a separately labelled collector component. It must not accept an arbitrary comparison CSV or silently change units, reported statistical kind, uncertainty role or source weights.
3. **Keep the original evidence intact.** Greiner's unknown weighting leaves strict source-estimator checks unavailable. A separately declared, independently reviewed equal-weight scenario may supply a raw proxy discrepancy. That does not certify extraction, population reconstruction or transfer. Geometric means remain geometric means, and reported confidence intervals never become participant SDs.

The collector is not a new source-population generator. Actual cross-context execution will still need the Agent's frozen source/target context definitions and common-model linkage. The historical ENSANUT bridge illustrates active-context switching but uses a legacy collector and fixed units; it is not silently upgraded here. Native source mmol/L and target mg/dL require an explicit reviewed representation or conversion before live checks. Incomplete joint identification remains an assumption the Agent can state; it is not an excuse to search indefinitely.
