## Scientific-use decision

The three proposed outcomes are well-defined finite-sample projections of the same released cleaned-number table: the BMI contrast and age contrast from glucose on age, sex and BMI; and the Female-minus-Male BMI contrast from the model adding sex-by-BMI. The first two share a fitted projection and are not independent replications. The interaction is a conditional reuse item within the three primaries. It is not algebraically determined by the two pooled coefficients alone. The common age term is a projection specification, not a biological claim of common age response.

All three relationships are **joint-assumption dependent** under the selected Ayele/Bahendeka evidence, and remain so if the reviewed MIND marginal summaries are added. None of those inputs supplies the matching continuous-glucose adjusted coefficients or their sex-specific counterparts. Unreported dependence does not establish zero slopes, independence or zero interaction. A constructor can choose those as explicit assumptions; the coordinator must not prescribe desired target relationships or reinterpret them as reported evidence.

The pooled POC choice is defensible if the estimand is stated exactly as proposed: unweighted projections of the released cleaned measurements in a prospectively fixed subset, not universally fasting glucose, latent biological glucose, population causal effects or individual diagnostic accuracy. Age is the documented census age, not an interchangeable interview-age variable. Unknown food/treatment mixtures, assay differences and joint structure limit transport; they do not force an otherwise unnecessary fasting subgroup or a latent-glucose reconstruction.
