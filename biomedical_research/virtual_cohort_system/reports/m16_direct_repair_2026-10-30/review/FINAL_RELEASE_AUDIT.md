## Limits

This is a prospective local procedural audit, not OS isolation or externally witnessed preregistration. Receipts, hashes and timestamps establish the recorded workflow and current integrity; they cannot prove absence of undisclosed activity or model-training exposure. Admission describes a new urate-cycle task, not a proven model-unseen or participant-unseen target.

All constructions declare working location/scale guides, not established conditional moments or a calibrated sampling distribution. Counts beyond two guide scales do not imply an expected normal count without a declared and justified law. Source transport, age shape, interactions, extreme BMI and tails remain assumption-dependent. Disclosure of these limitations is not itself a factual blocker. This release-readiness pass establishes neither biological validity, useful equivalence, national inference nor improvement on the still-unread measured target.
