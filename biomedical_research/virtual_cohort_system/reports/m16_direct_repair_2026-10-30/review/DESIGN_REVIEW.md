# M16 direct-authoring repair: independent design review

30 October 2026. Initial review of the proposed experiment; **not a prelaunch or target-release approval**. Only protocol, contract and interface documents were inspected. No participant rows, hidden outcomes, reference statistics or generated tables were read by this reviewer.

## Proposed scientific unit

Three fresh-context Agent constructions receive the same admitted seed evidence, observed covariates, task and permitted tool budget. Each directly authors the unmeasured column; a sampler or rescaling program must not silently replace this route. Each has an initial candidate and at most two feedback-based revisions. All initial and final results are retained and evaluated on the same separately admitted, locked target. This is a bounded repeated feasibility test, not a method-superiority trial or an estimate based on three independent clinical cohorts.

The added mechanism is diagnostic feedback about whether literal values realize the Agent's own declared construction. This must remain separate from consistency with published evidence and transfer to real target measurements.

## Required safeguards before builders launch

1. **Pre-authoring model commitment.** Each builder submits its evidence, scientific model, machine-readable self-check mean/SD expressions and source-check proposal before any `generated.csv` exists. The controller records their hashes in an exclusive-write receipt. This prevents a good self-consistency score from being manufactured by fitting the specification to already written rows. Expected conditional means are declared model quantities, not inferred clinical truths.
2. **Revisions also preserve chronology.** When changing structure or scale, commit the revised specification before replacement row authorship. Do not overwrite the original spec. A row-only repair explicitly preserves the old spec. A retained table has unchanged numeric content and is not a second independent construction.
3. **No metric substitution through model revision.** Report final-table diagnostics under its current declaration, and where expressions remain evaluable, also under the initial declaration. Label the latter as a comparison to the original target, not a requirement that a legitimately revised model satisfy an obsolete specification. Separate changes in rows from changes in model assumptions; never claim improved realization merely from redefining the expected mean or variance.
4. **Identical starting information.** All builders receive byte-identical seed source artifacts, task, covariates and initial instructions. Give identical independent retrieval budgets. If later retrieval differs, call these equal starting information and access conditions, not identical final information. No prior candidate or old target result is included in any builder's context. Use fresh-context launches, not forks containing this review's historical outcome discussions.
5. **Independent constructions are not independent studies.** Three model invocations are engineering repeats on the same participants and evidence. There is no target-based selection of the best construction or fallback to a favorable seed. Failed or unestimable constructions remain in the record.
6. **Outcome blindness and eligibility.** Commit target cycle, eligibility, group sizes, salted-ID selection, source exclusion and measurement metadata before examining outcome statistics. Accessing outcome availability for selection is not permission to inspect association values. Record all target-data and source-document exposures. A new cycle is not proof that the underlying LLM has never seen related knowledge.
7. **Fixed analysis schedule.** Reuse the scheduled 24 numeric estimands without selecting endpoints after generation. Keep the analysis definitions separate from builder feedback. The builder may know intended analysis families but never target directions, values or score. Source discovery can change an endpoint's evidence-use label, not its scheduled computation.

## Three distinct feedback classes

| Class | Input | Valid interpretation | Invalid interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Construction consistency | Generated table, preserved X and precommitted conditional mean/SD | Literal authoring may differ from the Agent's stated construction | Its construction is biologically correct |
| Source consistency and comparability | Original reported statistic, its study conditions and a declared matching or approximate operator | A matching check constrains a specified quantity; an approximation diagnoses an assumption | Different-population marginal moments must be forced equal |
| Locked target utility | Real withheld measurement and all frozen outputs | Descriptive transfer and scientific-analysis agreement for this task | Feedback for another repair within this run |

Conditional variance and marginal variance must stay distinct. Residual plots or aggregate summaries should distinguish mean drift, scale drift, predictor-dependent drift and rank/nonfinite failures. Finite authored tables need not have exactly zero residual means or correlations. Do not impose zero-sum residuals, deterministic quantile grids or perfect orthogonality to secure a pass. A statement that variation is random or calibrated cannot be inferred from irregular-looking numbers.

A variance or mean guide can be only an assumption. If a mean is not claimed as a conditional expectation, the checker must identify it as a guide; testing zero-mean residuals then has no expectation-level interpretation. If no legitimate scale can be declared, return scale diagnostics as unavailable rather than defaulting to one.

## Source and age safeguards

- Preserve reported units, population selection, adjustment set and regression direction. Never invert a reverse-regression coefficient or treat a standardized path as an unadjusted raw slope without explicit derivation and additional assumptions.
- All accessible applicable source statistics remain usable inputs. Neither a missing statistic nor a nonsignificant group comparison implies a zero effect.
- An age coefficient needs an adjusted relationship or an explicit, unvalidated modeling assumption. Do not guide new builders toward either age sign from old target results. Search terms can name the relation and population, but should not search for evidence of the revealed preferred sign.
- A source check on a target-shaped table is not an exact source replay. If context cannot be matched, its discrepancy is not a direct loss to minimize. A separately authored source-like table also does not prove that the same generation mechanism was reused.
- Overlapping Bogalusa studies/supplements are a shared evidence family. A third original does not necessarily mean three independent cohorts.
- Candidate selection uses permitted diagnostics only. No aggregate target statistic, sign or pass/fail feedback is allowed before the entire construction/revision schedule closes.

## Minimal actionable revision record

For each material finding, require the following fields or equivalent explicit prose:

1. `finding_id` and exact feedback artifact/hash.
2. `class`: implementation error, realization drift, source incomparability, insufficient evidence or structural assumption.
3. `hypothesis`: proposed cause, with unknown causes allowed.
4. `diagnostic`: actually performed check, its result and limits; never a planned check represented as completed.
5. `action`: re-extract, retrieve evidence, revise rows, revise model, evaluate a documented sensitivity scenario, retain with use restriction, or stop.
6. `expected_effect`: which permitted source/self diagnostics could change and which scientific analysis families may be affected, without desired target numbers or signs.
7. `changed_artifacts`: old/new hashes and row/model change counts.
8. `unresolved_uses`: analyses remaining assumption-dependent or unsupported.

Retention remains valid. It should not be used as a generic success state: if an important relation is unresolved, the output must carry that limitation. A revision that worsens a permitted diagnostic is still retained in the history. Do not remove difficult evidence or change thresholds after feedback.

## Prelaunch acceptance checklist

- Fixed protocol and all initial data/source/tool hashes exist, including evaluator code and exposure map.
- Self-check engine passes independent known-answer tests and rejects generated outcome/ID predictors, arbitrary execution, missing group mappings and nonpositive/nonfinite scales.
- Spec and evidence field semantics are documented; input/output preservation is enforced.
- Controller code has no target-reader path in builder feedback; lifecycle slots are permanently reserved before execution.
- Same starting evidence and budgets verified for builders 1–3; run names and full result schedule fixed.
- Source retrieval and source statistics remain available, with exact capability limits disclosed.
- New-target metadata/admission reviewed without inspecting its outcomes.

## Target-release acceptance checklist

- All three initial model commitments precede corresponding row submissions.
- Every revision has appropriate model-before-row chronology and immutable parent linkage.
- All final candidates or explicit construction failures are closed; no pending builder action remains.
- Each feedback report is authentic, binds the correct frozen candidate and contains no hidden target values.
- All candidate inputs, observed cells, registry prefixes and feedback bindings verify.
- Initial/final output and specification hashes, numeric row-change counts, final decision and source exposures are recorded.
- Fixed evaluator accepts the exact hidden-reference hash committed before generation; no changed eligibility or endpoint definition.
- Only now may the controller release the true outcomes for the scheduled analysis, followed by independent recomputation. No further repair under this run.

## What a successful pilot can show

This unit can reveal whether repeated direct authorship is stable, whether explicit consistency diagnostics prompt substantive repairs, and whether those repairs preserve or improve independent downstream analyses. Improved self-consistency alone establishes only closer execution of a declared model. Improved source fit alone establishes only source consistency under its assumptions. Even concordant improvement across three constructions is preliminary, from one target and one source package, with no calibrated claim of broad superiority.

The previously exposed target remains closed. If later used for explicit method development, that would be a separately labeled development branch and cannot be presented as new validation. This review does not initiate such a branch.
