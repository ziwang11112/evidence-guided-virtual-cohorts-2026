# M16 scientific interpretation review

31 October 2026. **Interpretation: selective analytical usefulness, meaningful construction variability, and mixed consequences of a genuine row repair.** The implementation and audit support those observations. They do not establish a generally reliable virtual-data method, a revision benefit across analyses, or superiority over another approach.

This review used the protocol, released evaluation/summary/change aggregates, actual source and self-consistency feedback, source/revision reviews, exposure closure and `ACTUAL_TARGET_AUDIT.md`. No patient-level reference, observed or generated table was opened. No new statistical analysis, experiment, data acquisition or scientific modification was performed.

## What was actually replicated

Three fresh contexts of the available model independently constructed urate values for the **same 200-person NHANES 2017–2018 quota cohort**. All initial/final stages were retained. B1 and b3 retained their tables; b2 changed 12 values with its scientific construction unchanged and then retained that revision. Thus there are six scored stage records, four unique tables and three construction runs—not six independent experiments or three patient cohorts. The independent audit reports 2,863 computational/structural checks, no disagreements beyond its numerical tolerance, and maximum absolute difference 1.794051018855214e-14. This establishes computational agreement, not biological validity.

Starting sources and budgets were equal, but realized evidence was not identical. B1 admitted no additional original after its unsuccessful CARDIA attempt. B2 used an additional Korean health-examination original; b3 used a Japanese original and its supplement. The common three seed originals represent two recruitment populations because Yun and Sun overlap in Bogalusa. The extra Korean and Japanese sources provide separate recruitment contexts, not additional validation cohorts. Between-builder variability therefore combines retrieval, modeling and literal realization choices; it is not an isolated estimate of random authoring variation or a calibrated uncertainty distribution.

## Global relationships and scientific meaning

Values are released final estimates, with b1/b3 identical to initial. BMI and interaction are per 5 kg/m²; age is per 10 years; curvature is the coefficient of scaled BMI squared.

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| BMI | 0.316165 | 0.363935 | 0.334604 | 0.362292 | [0.282537, 0.358771] |
| Age | 0.282147 | 0.169686 | 0.203520 | −0.017153 | [0.225365, 0.323381] |
| BMI curvature | −0.022385 | −0.024097 | −0.010589 | −0.013828 | [−0.031106, −0.009463] |
| BMI × male | −0.066011 | 0.000746 | 0.081255 | −0.057769 | [−0.112339, −0.001001] |

The positive BMI association recurs in all three constructions, with final point discrepancies of approximately 0.018–0.048 mg/dL per 5 kg/m². This supports a limited claim of quantitative transfer for this evidence-supported association in this cohort. No predeclared equivalence margin establishes that these discrepancies are universally acceptable.

Age is not reliably preserved: b1 and b2 reproduce the positive direction but attenuate the reference magnitude; b3 is close to zero on the negative side. This is a practically important failure for an intended age analysis even though some other relations look plausible. The interaction is also construction-dependent. B1 is essentially null at the reported point estimate and should not be described as strong evidence for the opposite interaction. B2 is positive while the reference is negative; b3 reproduces a similar negative point estimate. No construction is uniformly best across these relations, and no candidate should be selected retrospectively on that basis.

All curvature signs are negative, but all constructions already attenuated the high-BMI guide. Agreement therefore assesses transfer of a chosen shape assumption; it is not evidence of unconstrained mechanism discovery. Source-informed age heuristics, marginal-to-conditional conversions and subgroup assumptions likewise remain hypotheses. Published marginal age summaries do not uniquely identify the adjusted target age coefficient.

The reference has 29 joint (stratum, PSU) groups. All 116 deletion fits succeed and retain each full-fit sign. The reference interaction nevertheless approaches zero in one deletion. These are **descriptive reference sensitivity ranges, not confidence intervals, equivalence bands, significance tests or robustness intervals for generated tables**. A candidate's position inside or outside a range is not a scientific pass/fail criterion. The test remains unweighted and finite-cohort; it supports no national or causal inference.

## Reverse regressions and all 16 group summaries

Every target reverse regression is positive, but magnitude discrepancies remain substantial, especially in the White groups:

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| White male | 1.483388 | 3.283567 | 2.807460 | 2.704077 |
| White female | 1.992203 | 4.637984 | 3.011488 | 4.575788 |
| Black male | 2.485399 | 2.709673 | 2.647079 | 2.992084 |
| Black female | 3.845699 | 3.459787 | 2.840129 | 3.763624 |

Units are (kg/m²)/(mg/dL). Correct forward BMI direction does not ensure a useful reverse regression; dispersion and joint structure matter. These regressions are related views of the same joint distribution, not four independent biological confirmations.

Review of the four means, SDs, medians and Q90s in each construction shows:

- Male group means are above the reference in all three final constructions. Female means are nearer, but this does not establish correct conditional relationships.
- All group medians are above their respective measured medians in all three final constructions. This common asymmetry deserves reporting alongside mean agreement.
- White-female SD is underestimated in all three finals: 1.246245, 1.293041 and 1.306313 versus measured 1.593898 mg/dL. Thus relatively similar means and some Q90s conceal a dispersion error.
- Other SDs and Q90s have mixed errors; tails cannot be described as uniformly calibrated. For example, the b2 Black-male Q90 is 8.710 versus measured 8.020 mg/dL, while its Black-female Q90 is 7.440 versus 7.400. These are examples within the complete 16-summary record, not a post hoc endpoint subset for claiming success.

## What b2's repair shows

The repair was substantive but bounded: 12 literal values changed after a precommitted repair plan, with evidence, model and construction held fixed. Its overall guide-standardized deviation SD changed from 0.876580536 to 0.992976473; absolute deviations exceeding two guide scales changed from 0 to 10. These describe closer realization of the chosen scale guide, **not** evidence of a correct normal tail, conditional variance or biological distribution. No such sampling law was identified.

Downstream consequences are mixed. BMI coefficient absolute discrepancy decreased from 0.048314 to 0.018438, while age increased from 0.074811 to 0.078626, curvature from 0.008902 to 0.011797, and interaction from 0.125079 to 0.147266. Three target reverse-regression discrepancies decreased; Black-female reverse discrepancy increased from 0.300292 to 1.005569. All four target SD discrepancies decreased. Across all 24 records, 12 decreased, 9 increased and 3 stayed unchanged. The endpoints differ in units and scientific purpose and are correlated; these counts are not a net-benefit score or a test of revision efficacy.

One particularly informative observation is that **source fit, own-guide consistency and target utility did not move together**:

- All four absolute Yun source-SD discrepancies increased after repair, yet all four measured-target SD discrepancies decreased.
- All four Sun source reverse-slope discrepancies decreased, yet the measured-target Black-female reverse discrepancy increased.
- Improved realization of the assumed scale guide coincided with worse age, curvature and interaction agreement.

This is a supported within-run dissociation, not a universal law, causal estimate of a repair policy, or evidence that source matching should generally be avoided. Differing source/target populations and unidentified joint structure make it a useful warning against optimizing a single feedback proxy. Repeated stages and self-check values must not be counted as independent replications of this observation.

## Supported and unsupported paper claims

| Supported by this unit | Not established by this unit |
|---|---|
| An Agent can retrieve/use full reported evidence, declare a construction, literally enrich a fixed cohort and respond to source/self diagnostics under an auditable workflow. | That the architecture, direct authorship or extra retrieval is superior to another generation method. |
| Some downstream associations transfer usefully in this benchmark; failures depend on the analysis and construction. | General analytical equivalence, interchangeable patient data, accurate individual measurements, calibrated uncertainty or reduced clinical collection requirements. |
| A target-blind, precommitted limited row repair occurred and had mixed measured consequences. | A reliable, causal or consistently beneficial repair strategy; a gain from retaining unchanged tables. |
| Source fit and construction consistency are insufficient substitutes for target analytical validation in the observed cases. | A validated universal reliability metric, calibrated endpoint admissibility rule or fully identified biological mechanism. |

These results can support a feasibility/results section and an honest failure analysis. A methods-paper contribution still needs a clearly delimited scientific-use claim and evidence that the reusable procedure delivers it beyond a small single-target demonstration. Engineering auditability is valuable, but computational correctness alone does not supply method novelty or biological validation. No inference of method gain is justified by differences between the previous M15 target and this separately cycled target; target populations, constructions and evidence differ.

## One bounded next test, if continuing

**Separate literal-realization variation from model-choice variation.** Before inspecting any new target outcome, freeze one source-only evidence set and one explicit construction, then let three fresh contexts each produce one direct initial table and at most one precommitted diagnostic row repair. Hold the evidence, construction and feedback definitions fixed across those runs; report every initial/final result on one newly admitted locked cohort using the same 24 analyses, with no best-run selection or overall score. This single controlled repeatability test would ask whether repair trade-offs persist when the scientific model is held constant. It would not establish that the frozen model is true, but would identify whether row realization/repair is itself a reproducible bottleneck. The current M16 reference is exposed and cannot serve as that new confirmation target. No next run is started by this review.
