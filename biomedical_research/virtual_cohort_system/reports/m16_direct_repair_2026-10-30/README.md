# M16 direct authorship, diagnostic repair and repeatability

This selected archive preserves three fresh Agent constructions on one 200-person NHANES 2017–2018 finite quota benchmark. Six scheduled initial/final stages represent four unique authored tables. B1 and b3 retained their initial tables; b2 repaired 12 outcome cells once and retained that revision. The results remain relation-specific: b2's target discrepancies improved for some endpoints and worsened for others, while b3 retained an age-direction failure. There is no overall winner, general replacement claim or demonstrated universal revision benefit. See the [reviewed milestone](../../MILESTONE_16_RESULTS.md) and [complete 24-analysis appendix](results/analysis_tables.md).

The new [self-consistency checker](../../docs/SELF_CONSISTENCY.md) computes aggregate deviations from a predeclared construction. It does not create outcomes, choose biological coefficients or establish scientific accuracy. All three constructions used the weaker working-guide interpretation. Source fit, guide consistency and measured-reference analytical performance remain separate checks.

`results/` contains released aggregate JSON, the 144-row six-stage estimand ledger, the 72-row initial/final endpoint-change ledger, the complete appendix and the four-panel aggregate figure. `independent_target_audit/actual_audit.json` preserves the released independent computation checks. Its agreement corroborates the same scheduled analyses; it is not a new cohort, experiment or source of builder feedback. The reference joint-PSU ranges are descriptive sensitivity, not confidence intervals or national inference.

Protocol, builder instructions, seed provenance, controller/setup/release scripts, frozen estimator implementations, tests and independently reviewed reports are retained as selected exact files. `review/REVIEW_SUMMARY.json` is an explicitly curated export of closure metadata and counts with the original detailed source hashes. The detailed closure snapshots, candidate trees, changed-row inventories, full-source content and unlisted working data are omitted. Other selected provenance records retain original paths, hashes and procedural flags. Historical reviews and preparation-stage statements record their time of writing; the final milestone and released outputs govern the completed findings. `CONTINUATION.json` is the preserved pre-export snapshot: its completed target is exposed and closed, while its Git synchronization status was still pending. The bounded export receipt and final Git commit record the subsequent synchronization; the pending snapshot is not a request for further biomedical work.

No observed, reference or generated participant table is included. Frozen release/prelaunch bindings are audit metadata for omitted inputs, not a complete executable cohort bundle. Original machine-local paths are retained in unchanged commitments. Actual acquisition, controller and target-analysis scripts require the omitted local data/source files and original layout; do not interpret their presence as a turnkey patient-data replay. The fresh export test report covers only the published invented-data suites; no real target or source check was rerun during synchronization.

`manifest.json` is the exact archive payload allowlist and SHA-256 inventory, excluding itself. It records every source/export hash and named transformation. The [bounded export receipt](../../../../sync/M16_EXPORT_RECEIPT.json) also binds the six project code/doc files and verifies unchanged historical report hashes. Only work-to-report link prefixes are rewritten in the exported milestone and current index documents; the reviewed local scientific files remain unchanged.

Pre-existing `sync/EXPORT_AUDIT.json`, `sync/EXPORT_MANIFEST.json`, `sync/source_allowlist.json`, `sync/FILE_INVENTORY.tsv` and earlier milestone receipts are historical evidence and remain unchanged. They do not claim to audit this new archive or all current checkout files. The M16 manifest, bounded receipt and independent export review provide the new exact-scope checks.
