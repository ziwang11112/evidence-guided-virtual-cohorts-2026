# M16: direct authorship, diagnostic repair and repeatability

30 October 2026. Prospective local protocol; not externally preregistered.

## Question and scope

Can three fresh-context scientific Agent constructions use full published evidence to directly enrich the same unseen-outcome cohort, diagnose deviations from their own declared construction, and make justified revisions that support downstream scientific analyses? This is a small feasibility and repeatability experiment, not a test of superiority over an ordinary Agent, not a power calculation, and not an estimate of clinical-data replacement savings.

The completed M15-B1 target and all prior freezes remain closed and unchanged. No previous target outcome, result, fitted coefficient or recommended biological parameter is supplied to the builders. Root knows historical findings and therefore delegates scientific construction to fresh contexts with a restricted file-reading contract. Separation is procedural, not OS isolation; prior model-training exposure is unknown.

## Target and information

The independent custodian admits an available, separately cycled NHANES urate task using metadata and prior-use records before opening outcomes. Preferred cycle is 2017–2018; any alternative must be admitted before selection and without outcome inspection. The fixed cohort has 200 adults, 50 in each recorded sex by non-Hispanic White/Black group, aged20–56, examined, with no self-reported professional diabetes diagnosis and valid BMI, survey design/appropriate weight and available urate. Exact native columns, availability conditions, salt and selection are committed in admission/custody records before download. No changes to selection after outcome inspection. This quota subset is not a national probability sample.

Builders see only sample_id,race,sex,age,bmi, target metadata, full admitted sources and their own feedback. Hidden urate, SI aliases, original participant IDs, design variables, other builders and historical results are excluded. Existing observations and row order must be preserved exactly. No sampler/optimizer/rescaling program may populate urate in the direct-authoring route. Programs may calculate deterministic expressions, validate preservation, calculate diagnostics and format declared metadata; generated outcome cells must be literal Agent-authored values.

Identical curator-seeded full originals are Yun2021 (PMC8236402), Sun2014 (PMC4208749), Bonaccorsi2019 (PMC6941025), and Yun's linked supplement/figures. Yun and Sun overlap in recruitment and are not independent confirmations. All reported source statistics may be used. Incidental NHANES citations in the originals are disclosed; NHANES-derived numerical findings may not become independent generation evidence. Each builder has up to8 retrieval query charges and4 source acquisition attempts, plus no more than4 new primary originals. Existing seed reads do not consume new retrieval budget. Failed attempts count. New artifacts are admitted by the supplied gateway or a logged controller action within the same budget. Available starting information and budgets are equal; acquired information may differ and will be reported.

## Three constructions and chronology

Builders b1,b2,b3 use fresh contexts, the same task, observations, seed evidence and instructions. They do not exchange findings or inspect one another. These are three construction repeats from the available model, not three independent patient cohorts, not distinct model families, and not evidence of statistical independence of LLM behavior.

For each initial or revised candidate, before writing any new outcome rows:

1. Submit evidence.json, model_spec.json, audit/construction.json, and audit/revision_plan.json (initial plan allowed). Exact source-check definitions excluding only the later table hash are committed inside model_spec.parameters.source_check_plan. The construction file contains safe declarative mean/location and optional SD/scale expressions, chosen by the Agent, with conditional_moments versus working_guides distinguished. It cannot depend on IDs or generated outcomes.
2. Controller validates the evidence/specification, reviews reported/derived/assumed status, source comparability and mappings, then records hashes and whether a generated.csv exists. A proposed revised specification is committed before replacement-row authorship. Technical corrections are preserved and must be committed before generation; no scientific field may be silently repaired by the controller.
3. Agent literally writes the200 rows and completes decision.md, checks.json and source_checks.json. The table is validated and frozen with the committed construction and evidence. No alternative table may be selected after feedback without using a revision slot.
4. Controller computes the actual source checks and the new self-consistency report. Original and current construction diagnostics are both retained for revised tables, separating row repair from changes to the declared model.
5. Agent responds to each material finding with cause, observable diagnostic, proposed action, predicted analysis impact and remaining uncertainty. Actions may be literal row repair, evidence-supported model revision, targeted retrieval, retain with a use limitation, or stop. A no-op is permitted and is never called improvement.

Initial feedback and, if a revision is made, one further feedback pass are allowed. There are at most two scientific revisions after initial (initial,revision1,final). Early retain copies scientific files unchanged to final with actual feedback and rationale bound; it does not create a second independent table. A final repair is scored by the same diagnostics after freeze but receives no further editing opportunity. Preserve all intermediate candidates and failures. No forced change, zero-residual target, favorable-draw selection, or threshold relaxation is allowed.

## Meaning of feedback

Self-consistency evaluates the authored table against the Agent's declared conditional construction. It diagnoses finite-table deviations; it does not certify that the construction is biologically true. A nonzero finite-sample residual correlation is not automatically an error. Working guides are not claimed conditional expectations. Changing a model to describe the existing table is not row repair; both old/current construction diagnostics are reported.

Source feedback classifies each statistic as comparable, explicitly approximate or not computable. Target-shaped means/SDs and reverse regressions from differing source populations are approximate transport diagnostics, not equality targets. Missing covariates, source selection, assay changes, reported uncertainty and overlapping cohorts remain explicit. No target outcome value, metric, sign or pass/fail is returned. Evidence gaps may remain unresolved; absence of a reported relation does not imply zero or independence. Alternative assumptions are sensitivity scenarios, not calibrated uncertainty distributions.

## Fixed target analysis

Use the established24 estimands without choosing favorable endpoints: four global coefficients, four group reverse regressions, and four summaries in each of four groups. All analyses are unweighted on the identical200 participants. Define b=(BMI-28)/5, a=(age-40)/10, m=male1, r=Black1. The primary reported associations are BMI and age from urate~1+b+a+m+r; reuse analyses include BMI curvature from separately adding b*b, and male-minus-female BMI slope from separately adding b*m. In each group fit BMI~1+urate+age; summaries are mean, sample SD(ddof1), median and Q90(linear interpolation). All four global reference coefficients also receive leave-one-joint-(stratum,PSU)-out descriptive sensitivity ranges. These ranges are not CIs or national survey inference. Rank failures remain failures.

Initial and final tables from all three builders are scored. Report per-construction effects, generated-minus-measured discrepancies and changes in absolute discrepancy, plus all degradations. Direction agreement and source fit alone do not establish useful equivalence; no unregistered binary equivalence tolerance or new overall score is introduced. Intermediate tables and terminal self-check reports are retained; they cannot be selected using target performance. If a builder fails, report that scheduled failure rather than replace it with an extra successful run.

The final source/exposure record marks which target analyses were directly constrained, indirectly informed or assumption-dependent for each builder. Adding external evidence about a relationship is allowed; such an endpoint remains transfer validation rather than an unprovided-relation claim. These labels describe exposure, not independent causal discovery.

## Release and reproducibility

Before any builder starts, hash-bind protocol, target commitment, shared observed bytes, seed registry/files, builder instructions, self-check engine and evaluator. Prior to target release, independent review checks all three initial/final freezes, pre-authorship construction commitments, actual feedback/revision lineage, preservation, budgets and missing failures. Source feedback engines never read the reference. Target execution requires a new explicit release artifact and exclusive output destination. Independently recompute the real aggregate results after release; do not send them to builders or tune this target.

Historical artifacts remain unchanged. Real/generated rows and full articles/supplements stay local. Only reviewed code, protocol, provenance and aggregate reports may sync to the existing private repository. No training, HPC, new paid API or recurring automation is part of this unit. Completion or a documented scientific/operational stop ends this bounded run.
