# Initial scientific candidate: mixref_v1

This candidate uses the explicitly supplied CHNS 2009 development summary (selected complete cases, n=6631) as a set of sample estimates. It generates all five variables for the required adult age range. It is a statistical reconstruction scenario, not an independently validated population model.

Age uses an assumed maximum-entropy shape over integer ages 35-74 constrained by the supplied mean, SD and quantile-compatible bin masses. Female is a Bernoulli threshold variable. BMI uses a positive lognormal marginal fitted to mean and SD; its implied 10th/50th/90th percentiles are close but not forced to the supplied quantiles. Serum glucose and HbA1c use positive two-lognormal mixtures fitted to their five supplied marginal summaries. All moments are finite. No rows were generated during fitting.

The difference between supplied Pearson (about 0.661) and Spearman (about 0.361) associations motivates component-dependent glycemic association. Four joint mixture cells have a fitted joint probability and normal correlation, with an explicit assumed 0.90 glucose-HbA1c latent correlation in the broad/broad cell. Other cells share a lower fitted correlation. Components do not label diabetes, treatment, or disease states. Mixture indicators are independent of age, female and BMI, an important structural limitation. Nonzero within-component age/BMI/sex dependencies remain possible and are represented. Unknown nuisance correlations are listed as assumptions rather than inferred from missing evidence.

Three latent correlations are fitted jointly to analytic covariance projections for the three supplied regression analyses. This preserves their different adjustment sets. The supplied cluster-robust intervals remain evidence uncertainty, not fitting bands or p-value targets; IID generated rows do not reproduce the source's community sampling. The close deterministic fit is development fit by construction, not evidence of population recovery.

External literature is retained in its actual context. Bao's plasma-glucose Pearson association, MIND's older rural serum-glucose Spearman association, and Jansen's differently selected plasma-glucose projections are neither interchangeable nor pooled as additional CHNS constraints. Jansen's restored age rows and Pani's weighted age result show available age evidence, while differing selections and adjustments prevent assigning them directly as this cohort's coefficients. Pani's stratum SEs are not individual SDs. NGSP scale conversion does not establish assay or serum/plasma equivalence. The source audit and repeated rows from a study are not independent replications.

The retained deterministic producer is derive_spec.py. It fits analytic moments/CDFs, analytic covariance projections, and 64x64 Gaussian quadrature for a rank integral; a 96x96 calculation checks numerical stability. Integration nodes are not synthetic participants. An earlier common-correlation algebraic model could not jointly match the two supplied glycemic correlations; its script and report are retained as derive_common_rho_diagnostic.py and fit_report_common_rho_diagnostic.json. That was mathematical model assessment before any generated-table trial, not an additional cohort attempt.
