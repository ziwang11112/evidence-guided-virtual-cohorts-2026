# Initial scientific decision

I use the explicitly supplied CHNS 2009 complete-case summaries as development inputs. They describe a selected 6,631-person sample, not population truth. The generator produces 3,000 new rows for adults aged 35–74 with no observed participant inputs. There is no disease, treatment, or pregnancy exclusion.

The BMI mean, SD, and quantiles are compatible with a simple lognormal approximation. Age uses a maximum-entropy discrete distribution on the required integer support. Its mean and variance use the reference summaries; three CDF anchors near the supplied quantiles are explicit interpolation assumptions. Female probability uses the supplied count fraction. Independence of female from the other generated fields is a disclosed simplification, not inferred from missing sex-specific evidence.

Glucose and HbA1c use a common two-component lognormal mixture. The much larger Pearson than Spearman association in the supplied sample motivates a separate upper-tail component with its own latent correlation. The components are mathematical devices, not diagnosed diabetes groups, and their prevalence is not a diabetes prevalence estimate. A single shared component fraction and two component margins approximate each biomarker's five supplied marginal statistics. Covariate correlations reproduce the three supplied adjusted projection coefficients and the two distinct glucose–HbA1c associations at the model-distribution level. Missing covariance information is not independence evidence: age–BMI and BMI–glucose latent correlations are explicit assumptions; component membership is also assumed independent of age and BMI. These choices are underidentified by the supplied summaries.

All calibration is deterministic. The retained producer uses closed-form lognormal moments, normal-threshold expectations, nonlinear solvers, and two-dimensional Gauss–Hermite integration of a rank-correlation expectation. It does not sample, import the generator, generate five-variable integration rows, or execute any cohort. The order-48/order-80 rank-integral comparison differs by about 0.0000024. Distribution-level fitting is neither finite-sample agreement nor independent validation. The objective's scales are numerical calibration weights, not empirical SEs.

The external evidence is retained in its named populations and measurement contexts. Jansen's separately adjusted projections cannot be combined into a recovered conditional equation; Bao's plasma Pearson correlation is not MIND's serum Spearman correlation. The restored Jansen and Pani age evidence makes a positive age association plausible in those selected populations, but the target age projection here comes from the explicitly supplied CHNS summary. NGSP scaling does not establish assay or serum/plasma equivalence. No external source was pooled into the reference-fitting objective, and repeated or converted summaries are not counted as independent studies. No new literature query or document retrieval was used.

Lognormal components have positive support and finite moments of every fixed order. Bounded age and Bernoulli female also have finite moments. No Student-t exponentiation or unsupported hard glycemia cap is used. Random rows and repeated seeds represent residual sampling variation under this fitted model; they do not propagate uncertainty in the evidence, selection, structure, or population transport.

Declared-input summary inspection is supported. Causal effects, individual predictions, sex differences, clinical thresholds, unreported tail probabilities, disease prevalence, spatial clustering, uncertainty calibration, and transport to national Chinese adults are unassessed. External source replay is not computable because no source reconstruction is supplied or run. First request: freeze this candidate and execute the authorized trial seed 4201. Do not interpret its fit as population recovery.
