# Information provision and one diagnostic revision: bounded CHNS development experiment

Prospective new unit, authorized by the user's continuation on 3 November 2026. Earlier pilots and their remaining allocations stay closed and are not pooled into this experiment. The scientific product is useful whole-cohort data; questions are visible and task-specific generation is allowed.

## Factors and allocation

Two explicit-information conditions: L receives the research request, target-population/measurement definitions and external literature evidence; R receives the same plus the complete existing CHNS developmental summary package (three adjusted regression estimates and 27 distribution/joint diagnostics). Each condition has three new, independent-context scientific authors. Each author produces an initial delivery and, after all permitted diagnostic feedback, one revised or retained final delivery. These yield L0/L1/R0/R1.

The revision factor is **one additional allowed-information diagnostic/revision stage**, including additional reasoning and computation. It is not a claim of identical source-fit feedback, a pure feedback-content intervention, or multi-Agent superiority. L sees generated statistics, implementation/shape diagnostics and admitted external evidence; R can also see reference comparisons. L's generated task-population statistics are never labelled numerical source-population replay. Genuine source-context calculations, if supplied and reviewed, consume the ordinary trial budget. An unavailable adapter or non-identifiable source operator is stated, not simulated by comparing unlike populations.

All six scientific authors use fresh contexts and the same inherited model configuration/service, subject to the model identity actually observable in tool records. No old generator or result is supplied. Balanced launch blocks are L01/R01, R02/L02, L03/R03; execution may overlap after independent tasks are launched. Ordering is not selected using outcomes. Science is chosen by each author; the coordinator does not prescribe coefficients, signs, families or a preferred model.

Each author has two initial and two revision trial-table slots, at most 3,000 rows each, and initial/final deliveries at seeds 4101, 4102, 4103 with exactly 3,000 rows. Thus the whole unit has at most 36 delivery and 24 trial attempts (60 total). Failures consume slots; no hidden retries or extra seeds. Six constructors on one case are the independent author replications. Seeds and generated rows are not additional scientific constructions. Same seeds within an author do not guarantee common random numbers if the code changes. Retained identical outputs are reported as no change and deduplicated in inventories.

Up to two literature queries and one newly retrieved original document per author across both phases (12 queries/six documents overall). Reuse of supplied evidence and deterministic parameter calculations does not generate additional synthetic cohorts. All row-producing simulations go through the isolated execution tool and count, including any additional simulated source context. No training, paid API, HPC, new cohort collection or patient-level reference read is allowed. All tables remain local.

## Information boundary

L's task, profile, admitted evidence and feedback contain no project-derived CHNS development-reference values not explicitly supplied to L, intervals, discrepancies, thresholds, source-selection hints based on those values, previous models, or paths to R/evaluation-reference artifacts. Its numerical provider computes generated statistics without opening any reference summary. A separate allowlist strips non-permitted fields; injected reference-sentinel tests verify this boundary. The ordinary author's general tools remain procedurally scoped, not securely sandboxed by this policy. Generator execution is isolated.

Both conditions can retrieve ordinary original literature. Equivalent published facts found by L are retained and logged, not discarded or blocked to enforce ignorance. Prior project reports or candidate files are not original literature and are outside author inputs. Do not claim absent prior model knowledge. Each author reports the actual information used, including retrieved overlapping CHNS studies where applicable. If information enters an arm unintentionally, record the exposure and qualify the contrast rather than conceal it.

The same three analysis definitions and complete 27 diagnostic definitions are public to both conditions. R's summaries are designated developmental inputs. CHNS was already exposed to the research team; withholding values from a fresh L context does not create an untouched clinical test.

## Frozen evaluation and interpretation

All six final decisions and delivery attempts close before one common reference-evaluation report is computed for this experiment. The root may operate R feedback because it is permitted, but does not use it to steer later scientific authors. The final common comparator is the existing unmodified CHNS aggregate reference; no new patient computation is needed.

Report all three adjusted coefficients, all 27 diagnostics, invalid/missing rows, code/parameter changes, output uniqueness, trial/author/retrieval cost and failures. Match uncertainty types: the retained CHNS intervals concern adjusted regression estimates, not means or equivalence. Seed ranges represent conditional generation variation. No post-hoc pass threshold, global accuracy score, best-author selection or pooled row-level significance test will be used.

For every analysis/statistic, summarize each author's three fixed-seed results, then show all three constructor summaries per condition. Report paired initial/final changes per author, including no-ops and deterioration. Cross-condition differences measure explicit information provision under this procedure; they are not superiority over conventional statistical synthesis. Three authors per condition provide descriptive feasibility/variation evidence, not a conclusive treatment-effect estimate. The revision contrast includes extra computation; no isolated causal feedback mechanism claim is made.

Stop this unit after all six bounded constructions close and independent numerical review, or preserve a concrete runtime/interface blocker and incomplete slots. Do not add candidates, seeds, information arms or datasets to obtain favorable results. If the mechanism has no stable benefit, retain that outcome and simplify the method. Independent population utility, unsupported joint relations, subgroup/causal uses and model/transport uncertainty remain separate limitations.

Implementation scope fixed before dispatch: this unit supplies isolated target-population numerical trials and analytic source checks, but does not supply a new source-context execution adapter. Source-population numerical replay is therefore unavailable in both conditions and is labelled so. The revision label remains allowed-information diagnostics. This limitation does not forbid original-literature retrieval or analytic use of its evidence; a future source-replay extension is a separate experiment.
