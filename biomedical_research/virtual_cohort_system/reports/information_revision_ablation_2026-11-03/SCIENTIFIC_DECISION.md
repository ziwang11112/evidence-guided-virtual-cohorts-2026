# Scientific decision after the information × revision experiment

## What this unit establishes

Six fresh scientific Agent contexts constructed whole five-variable cohorts without participant input. The coordinator supplied the same task and external evidence, attached fixed measurement operators, and executed the submitted programs; it did not choose scientific parameters. Three authors additionally received all thirty existing CHNS development-reference statistics. Every author used one initial numerical trial, delivered three fixed seeds, then retained the same scientific law after the additional review stage.

There were 42 successful 3,000-row executions: six trials and 36 initial/final deliveries. Eighteen final delivery files exactly duplicate their paired initial files, leaving 24 distinct tables overall and eighteen distinct delivery tables. These are six constructions on one exposed clinical case, not 42 cohorts or additional patient evidence. No retrieval query, new source document, paid API, training, HPC job or raw patient-data read is recorded in this unit. This is a retained-record statement, not host-level access attestation.

All three prespecified regression coefficients remained positive in every distinct delivery table. This preserves the broad directions on this case, but direction alone hides consequential differences in strength and joint structure.

The full report retains all 27 distribution/joint diagnostics, all three coefficients, all seed values and all construction decisions. No overall quality score, favorable-author selection or post-hoc equivalence threshold is applied. Existing CHNS regression intervals describe sampling uncertainty in the reference coefficients; they are neither population truths nor acceptance bands for the generated data. Corresponding uncertainty intervals for the marginal and correlation references are not available in this package.

Reference-assisted authors showed substantially closer construction means for the requested relations in this case. They had those same quantities as inputs, so this is transparent calibration consistency. It does not establish independent predictive validity, discovery of the supplied associations, transport to a new population, or an advantage over an equally informed statistical synthesizer.

External-literature authors reproduced directions but consistently attenuated the BMI projection and overstated the rank association. Source selection, measurement and adjustment sets differ; missing joint and transport information was filled with declared assumptions. The evidence supports limited analysis-specific feasibility, not reliable reconstruction of the target joint distribution.

## What the revision contrast says

All six authors chose retention. Generator and specification bytes were unchanged, and all eighteen matched final delivery tables were byte-identical to initial delivery. Therefore every paired revision contrast is exactly zero. The author reviews identified assumptions and limitations, but this experiment provides no numerical improvement from the additional review stage.

Initial delivery was already allowed feedback-bearing trials and deterministic analytic fitting. This result does not show that all feedback is useless or that revisions can never help. It shows that another optional stage did not change these six constructions under this budget and information regime. The earlier single-author rank-improvement pilot remains a separate result and is not pooled to manufacture a positive revision effect here.

## Method decision

Continue the evidence-to-data construction core. Keep the review/revision step optional and retain the ability to stop with an unchanged model. Do not use generic iteration as the current main contribution or add rounds to this exposed case seeking improvement.

The central unresolved issue is the strength and applicability of evidence for conditional and joint relations. A future bounded experiment should focus on partial, study-qualified quantitative evidence and analyses that were not numerical calibration targets, using a separately declared information boundary and independent reference. This unit neither authorizes new cohort intake nor turns CHNS into an untouched test. Previous cases, unused slots and old budgets remain closed.

Methods and implementation can be drafted now around the actual workflow, information regimes and explicit assumptions. Strong claims about independent scientific reuse, transport, comparative method advantage, clinical equivalence or measurement-cost replacement remain unproved. The broad project still includes whole-cohort synthesis, indicator augmentation and missing-cell completion; this unit directly tests whole-cohort synthesis only.
