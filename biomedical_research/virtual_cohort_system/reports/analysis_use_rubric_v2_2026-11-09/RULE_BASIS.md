# Basis and scope of the TC/HDL consistency rule

Independent source and experiment-design review, 2026-11-09. This review uses three official sources for definitions and analytical limitations; it does not introduce a new cohort, clinical thresholds, target statistics, or a desired correlation. Source bodies are not redistributed.

## Supported rule and its interpretation

CDC defines serum total cholesterol as including cholesterol in HDL, LDL and VLDL. NHLBI defines non-HDL cholesterol as total cholesterol minus HDL cholesterol. These definitions imply **HDL-C <= total cholesterol** for paired, coherent concentrations of the same biological material at the same time, expressed in comparable units. Equivalently, non-HDL cholesterol is nonnegative. This is a component relation, not a statement about a population regression slope or correlation. [CDC definition](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/hus/sources-definitions/cholesterol.htm); [NHLBI diagnosis](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/blood-cholesterol/diagnosis).

The PTS professional lipid-panel insert describes distinct TC and HDL analytical reactions and documents imprecision, interference and rounding. Thus recorded assay outputs need a different interpretation from perfectly coherent latent concentrations. In an explicitly declared observation model, TC_obs = TC_latent + error_TC and HDL_obs = HDL_latent + error_HDL, so an observed negative TC-minus-HDL difference is possible even when the latent components are coherent. This is a mathematical possibility, **not evidence that a particular generated violation represents assay error**. Distinct assays do not establish independent errors or the joint error distribution. The insert is also not evidence identifying the historical assay used by an unrelated dataset. [PTS insert, principles, limitations and performance, pages 1–2](https://ptsdiagnostics.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/PS-002676-E-Pkg-Insert-Lipid-Panel-HX-Pro-English-Rev_1.pdf).

Apply the rule as follows:

- For declared latent or coherent concentration outputs, use the nonnegative-component requirement as a hard consistency check after explicit unit harmonization.
- For explicitly modelled noisy measurements, check the latent components and report observed violations separately, with the observation model and its evidence limitations. Marginal source SDs alone do not validate a joint measurement-error model.
- If the measurement interpretation or pairing is unknown, report a diagnostic requiring interpretation; neither automatic rejection nor automatic exemption is justified. Do not compare different specimens, times, or transformed variables as if they were paired concentrations.

Report usable and excluded/missing pairs, violation count/fraction, and the magnitude of positive HDL-minus-TC excess in the declared unit. A numerical tolerance must be explicit and separate from biological tolerance; any rounding allowance needs a declared measurement resolution. Manufacturer analytical ranges are separate instrument diagnostics, not biological bounds. No universal clipping, deletion, generic normal range, or target-derived tolerance follows from these sources. A prospectively declared constrained generator or resampling design is possible, but its effects on the joint distribution must remain visible.

Paired seeds aid finite-draw comparison but do not establish clinical uncertainty or general efficacy. If a revision changes random-number consumption, common seeds do not imply matched individuals or common latent draws.

This unit plans no real-target comparison. More generally, previously exposed aggregate references cannot become new independent validation merely by withholding them from a fresh author. No overall gain should be inferred solely from fewer component violations.

**Permitted claim:** in this bounded case, a declared source-grounded consistency/use rule informed a revision, with its effect on component coherence and prespecified analyses fully reported. If supported by the measured results, this may be narrowed to a reduction in component inconsistency under the declared interpretation. It does not establish recovered target covariance, a desired scientific correlation, universal data-cleaning rules, clinical equivalence, or causal/general effectiveness of agent feedback.
