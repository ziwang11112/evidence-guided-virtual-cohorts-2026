# Existing data first: prospective benchmark-reuse amendment

2 November 2026, America/Chicago. The user challenged the repeated search for new data despite substantial existing public datasets. **Pause acquisition and use the existing collection for method experiments.** The earlier requirement for a fresh cohort was too broad: it belongs to a new independent-confirmation claim, not every developmental or controlled benchmark experiment.

This is a new planning amendment, not a rewritten historical protocol or an execution release. No reference results, frozen candidates, scientific decisions or failed gates from the earlier studies change. The one-family Okayama access attempt remains unsuccessful. Its failure does not block a properly labelled existing-data experiment.

## What can be reused

| Purpose | Existing exposed data | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Examine errors, develop or revise a method | Permitted with declared developmental use | Results influenced by that feedback are development performance. |
| Fixed new evidence-information comparison | Permitted with a new protocol and fresh constructors receiving only permitted source/task inputs | A reused-benchmark comparison; researcher exposure remains disclosed. |
| Additional analyses of old frozen virtual tables | Permitted as separately labelled exploratory work | Does not change the original prespecified result or its primary denominator. |
| Claim a newly untouched independent confirmation | Previously exposed data cannot supply that status | A new folder, split, endpoint or Agent does not erase exposure. |

The immediate study adopts the second row. It does not permit numerical target feedback to the constructors, coordinator insertion of target parameters, or repeated repairs on the reference until a favorable result appears. Data reuse and target-answer injection are different actions.

## Minimal controlled comparison

- **E0:** three source contexts and the 15 approved grouped marginal records.
- **E1:** identical base information plus the four reviewed relationships: two separate conditional Jansen projections, Bao Pearson and MIND Spearman.
- Five fresh independent constructors per arm, one frozen scientific candidate each, three common target seeds, 3,000 generated rows per table: **at most 30 new target table attempts**. No participant measurements are supplied to constructors; all five variables are generated.
- One-pass construction isolates the supplied-information comparison. No source-feedback revision, simulated source calibration, automatic retrieval during construction or extra target trial is included in this bounded profile. These are optional capabilities of the broader system, not required components of this information experiment. No approximate OLS collector is needed here.
- A conventional statistical comparator is not required to claim an effect of extra information. Any later claim of superiority over another generation method would require its own capable same-information comparison. Do not label old CHNS C1/C2 as E0 controls: their inputs and procedures differ.

This supersedes the unreleased fresh-case 126-attempt proposal **for the next experiment only**, with fewer scheduled executions. It does not retroactively simplify the historical method or imply that feedback was tested successfully. No additional family, original-reading or download budget is created.

## Analysis and stopping rules to bind before construction

For each endpoint, summarize each constructor by the mean absolute discrepancy across its three prescribed seed estimates from the fixed reference point. Report all constructor values, the two arm means and E1 minus E0. Show directions and raw scientific units. Source uncertainty, measured-reference uncertainty, constructor variation and simulation variation remain distinct. No synthetic-row confidence interval supplies clinical evidence.

A claim of improvement on both evidence-addressed projections requires both mean-discrepancy contrasts to favor E1. The comparison is descriptive on this reused cohort; it does not establish clinical adequacy or unseen-cohort generalization. Report the complete constructor spread and discrepancies against the existing reference-uncertainty endpoints as sensitivity, without treating them as equivalence margins or joint confidence regions. The third projection remains reportable whether better or worse.

Every constructor and attempted draw stays in the denominator. If a constructor or prescribed endpoint is incomplete, report available results and failure counts, but do not issue the complete-arm positive claim based on survivors. A concrete finite technical-repair policy and exact common construction instructions must be fixed before execution; scientific replacements after comparison are prohibited. Complete all outputs before the new comparison report, then independently recompute the arithmetic. New results do not replace the old CHNS results.
