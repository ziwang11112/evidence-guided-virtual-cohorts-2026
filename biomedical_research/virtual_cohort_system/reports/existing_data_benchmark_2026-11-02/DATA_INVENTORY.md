# Existing local biomedical data inventory

2 November 2026. Original synthesis of existing authored acquisition audits, manifests, preparation records and completed results. No participant rows or new literature were read, no network request or acquisition was made, and no experiment was run. Counts below distinguish released file records from the selected samples actually analysed. They are not a pooled participant total.

**CHNS and ENSANUT already have prepared, evaluated joint references containing age, sex, BMI, glucose and HbA1c.** Existing exposure limits a claim of untouched confirmation; it does not make these data unusable. Historical protocols, outputs and negative results remain unchanged. This inventory records resources and prior roles; it does not release a new experiment.

## Interpretation and record precedence

The ready CHNS/ENSANUT variable sets mean their five-variable references have already been prepared and evaluated. This is not a claim that every other field, assay, source comparison or new use is ready. PNS offers a different measurement-timing setting; NHANES requires cycle-specific component and weight handling; HAALSI and Turkana do not substitute for HbA1c datasets.

National survey programs, longitudinal cohort releases, selected health-examination deposits and trial exports are different resources. A curated analytical reference is a further subset or transformation of one such release. Neither its row count nor the original download count establishes a population-representative sample or a new independent study.

The information regimes also differ. CHNS, ENSANUT, M17-B and Wave A generated complete cohorts from published evidence without participant inputs to constructors. M10/M11 enrichment supplied observed covariates. The Rich/NHANES-to-NAGALA comparison used **source-data-derived aggregate packets**, not evidence recovered solely from publications. Zero participant input to an Agent does not make those evidence origins equivalent.

All completed target results retain their exposed status and original interpretation. Existing files remain resources for transparently labelled reuse; changing a folder, endpoint or split does not restore untouched-confirmation status. The earlier atlas's prospective/P2 Turkana entry is superseded by the completed Wave A report. Older admission/preparation documents linked above establish provenance and definitions; later completed results determine actual execution status and analysed sample sizes.

This inventory adds no new eligibility rule, data access, benchmark arm, tuning permission, performance claim or experiment release. It preserves the audited metadata so continued work can start from existing resources rather than assume another acquisition is necessary.
