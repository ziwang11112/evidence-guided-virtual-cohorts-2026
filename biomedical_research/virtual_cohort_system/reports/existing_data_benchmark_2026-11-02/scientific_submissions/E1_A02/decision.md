# Initial constructor decision

This is one fixed whole-cohort scenario for 3,000 synthetic adults. It draws integer age from four strata, female membership conditional on age, positive BMI conditional on age and sex, and positive glucose and HbA1c conditional on those covariates. Correlated normal log residuals represent shared glycemic variation. A single latent elevated component adds shared gamma-distributed severity and creates a right tail. That component is not a diabetes diagnosis or treatment indicator. All scientific settings are in `model_spec.json`; no settings change by seed.

The Shanghai margins inform the general scale of BMI and glycemic measurements, while the older rural serum study informs the decision to allow broader glycemic heterogeneity. Their different ages, selection, calendar years and specimens are preserved. The Western-European source is less directly transferable and its separate adjusted associations serve only as qualitative motivation for dependence. Neither its BMI slope nor its FPG slope is installed in an HbA1c equation. Bao's Pearson coefficient and MIND's Spearman coefficient motivate positive glucose-HbA1c dependence; neither is assigned as a target coefficient, converted into the other, or matched. The chosen conditional correlation of log residuals is an assumption and does not equal the output's marginal Pearson or Spearman correlation.

Age probabilities, the sex-age association, every conditional covariate effect, mixture probabilities, tail shape and residual scales remain assumptions. Reference medians are conditional model coordinates, not asserted target medians. Source quartiles are not estimator intervals or normal-theory SDs. Source individual SDs are not estimator SEs; no source count or synthetic sample size is used to manufacture precision. No inverse-variance weighting or pooled cross-source loss is defined.

The output glucose denotes the requested released central-serum measurement. Source fasting plasma measurements and MIND local fasting serum are contextual evidence, not interchangeable measurements or hidden generated fields. No serum/plasma or fasting-protocol correction is asserted. HbA1c is generated directly in percent. The supplied NGSP affine relation would require its offset for levels and only its multiplier for slopes/SDs, but no IFCC value or coefficient is converted or used here. A unit relation cannot establish assay equivalence.

Female=1 and male=0 preserve the task's documented categories. The source's unresolved original numerical gender coding is not inferred. Source female fractions are not copied as target composition. The target's complete-case selection, treatment, pregnancy, laboratory differences and missingness process are not modeled mechanistically. The generator does not apply source diabetes exclusions, create missing values, or reproduce source imputation. Conditional independence of remaining innovations, lognormal central shapes and gamma tail severity are explicit restrictions; the admitted evidence does not identify the full joint distribution.

Support is discrete 35-74 for age and 0/1 for female, and strictly positive for BMI, glucose and HbA1c through exponentiation. Continuous outputs are not rounded or empirically clipped; the gamma severity is untruncated. This permits rare extreme values and does not establish physiological range fidelity. The implementation has no file, environment, process, network or external-data access. One local seeded NumPy random generator supplies every draw, and the function returns a DataFrame with the required column order and deterministic unique IDs.

No generator execution, generated table, source diagnostic, calibration, sweep, target evaluation or external retrieval was performed. Runtime behavior and numerical distributions remain untested; static acceptance is not scientific validation. The data would not support clinical thresholds, treatment inference, causal effects, population prevalence estimates, source-study reconstruction or fresh independent confirmation of the existing target study. Source agreement, even if later observed, would not establish target utility. Work stops after this single initial submission because the construction contract authorizes one scientific candidate and assigns validation, freeze and fixed-seed execution to the controller.
