# One initial whole-cohort model

The submitted law draws age from a discrete Gaussian-shaped mass on 35-74, female membership from an age-dependent Bernoulli law, and BMI from an age/sex-dependent lognormal law. An unobserved two-component glycemic state varies with age, BMI and sex. Within each state, central-serum glucose and percent HbA1c are conditionally lognormal with correlated log residuals. The high component is a distributional device, not diagnosed diabetes. All scientific numbers are in `model_spec.json`; the seed changes random draws only.

For precise reconstruction, let `a=(age-53)/10`, `f=female-0.56`, and `b=(BMI-24)/3.5`. Age probabilities normalize the declared kernel on the task's integer support. Log BMI uses its declared reference median, linear and quadratic age terms, female shift and Gaussian residual. State probability is the logistic equation declared in the specification. Each laboratory log measurement is its state-specific log median plus declared linear terms in `a,b,f` and its state-specific log SD times a standard-normal residual. The laboratory residuals have correlation `0.85*2*sin(pi*0.529/6)`. Independent innovations generate BMI, state and the laboratory residual pair conditional on the stated predecessors. There is no glucose predictor in the HbA1c equation.

## Evidence use and derivation

The external samples describe different selected populations. Bao primarily contextualizes central BMI and percent HbA1c, while MIND contextualizes serum glucose and broader laboratory dispersion with known diabetes included. These facts motivate a central component and a dispersed upper component but do not identify mixture parameters. Composition, component membership, all conditional medians, log SDs and covariate effects remain explicit assumptions, even when numerically near source summaries. No source moments were pooled or fitted, and no quartiles were treated as confidence intervals or estimator SEs.

MIND's 0.529 is retained as a Spearman statistic. The equation `2*sin(pi*s/6)` is an algebraic latent-correlation relation conditional on an assumed continuous Gaussian copula, not a conversion to raw-measurement Pearson correlation. Multiplying it by 0.85 and assigning it to conditional residuals are additional transport assumptions. The mixture and covariates alter overall dependence; no output correlation is guaranteed to reproduce MIND. Bao's Pearson coefficient supports the broad choice of positive dependence without being averaged with a rank statistic. The two Jansen projections provide qualitative evidence for positive HbA1c associations; they are separate age/gender-adjusted regressions and are never pasted into one equation. Their printed intervals have no asserted confidence level or derived precision here.

HbA1c is generated directly on the percent scale. No converted Jansen coefficient is installed. If an IFCC representation were used, the supplied NGSP relation would require slope/SD multiplication by 0.09148 and level conversion with the additional 2.152 offset; this bookkeeping establishes no assay equivalence. Jansen's unresolved gender coding does not identify a gender coefficient or target prevalence. Category membership is preserved independently of original numeric coding.

## Transfer and unresolved dependence

MIND serum is closer in specimen to target glucose, but its older selected rural 2019 population, prior-year BMI, local assay, transport timing and reported imputation differ from the target central-serum complete-case analytical sample. Bao's 2007-2008 Shanghai population excludes prior diagnosed diabetes but retains screen-detected cases. Jansen excludes diabetes and anemia and is western European. Their fasting plasma fields remain distinct from serum throughout. The model uses no universal serum/plasma multiplier and does not assert the target field follows any source fasting protocol. The target has no claimed diabetes, treatment or pregnancy exclusion; mixture modeling acknowledges heterogeneity without reconstructing those unobserved attributes.

Age/sex composition, joint predictor dependence, treatment, nonglycemic HbA1c mechanisms, selection, geography, clustering and tail behavior are unidentified. Conditional independence and shared-state assumptions close that gap by choice, not because missing source relationships imply zero effects. Parameters are fixed; simulation variation across seeds does not represent scientific or transport uncertainty. The supplied source individual SDs and synthetic row count are never used to manufacture source precision.

## Implementation and stopping point

Age is normalized on its integer support rather than clipped. Continuous outputs have positive lognormal supports without empirical truncation or rounding. This yields model-implied tails and can omit laboratory discreteness, unusual assay values, differential missingness and clinical subgroups. The function requires `observed=None`, reads only its supplied specification, returns exactly the requested columns and writes no files. Row identifiers are unique within each cohort and may repeat across seeds as row labels.

No source-context generation, generator execution, generated-row inspection, numerical diagnostic, calibration, target access or performance feedback occurred. All numerical checks are therefore recorded as not computable/not performed. The controller retains responsibility for static validation, freezing, the prescribed isolated executions and later evaluation. The model is a method-benchmark candidate, not an independent confirmation of the existing study population. It is unsuitable for clinical inference, prevalence estimation, treatment decisions, causal claims or claims of target predictive utility. I stop after this one initial specification because the assignment permits no scientific revision, alternate candidate or feedback-guided tuning.
