# Single initial scientific submission

This is one fixed-parameter whole-cohort scenario for the supplied 3,000-row task. Age is drawn from a bounded discrete normal kernel; female membership varies weakly with age. BMI is lognormal with modest age curvature and a small sex shift. Serum glucose and whole-blood HbA1c have correlated lognormal baseline components. A covariate-dependent latent elevated-burden state adds concordant positive increments with shared severity and separate noise. This state is not a diabetes diagnosis or a treatment assignment.

The model uses the supplied sources to choose a biologically coherent structure and contextual scales, with every transported quantity explicitly assumed. Bao's BMI and percent HbA1c summaries provide broad adult context, while MIND's serum glucose and broader glycemic dispersion provide a distinct older-population context. Jansen is a selected western-European population excluding diabetes and anemia. These cohorts are not pooled, and their marginal summaries are not installed as target moments. The exact task age support and row count are representation requirements only. No source count ratio establishes the target female proportion; source numeric gender coding remains unresolved.

The source relationships motivate shared metabolic variation. Jansen's separately adjusted BMI and FPG coefficients remain separate source projections; neither is inserted into the generator. The conditional baseline log-residual correlation of 0.45 is an explicit new assumption. Bao's 0.619 marginal raw Pearson association and MIND's 0.529 marginal rank association are distinct, and neither is converted or assigned to that residual parameter. Age, sex, adiposity, mixture frequency, severity distribution and all conditional shifts are unverified assumptions. Absence of additional supplied relationships does not establish independence; independence of the model's primitive innovations is a declared construction choice.

The released central-serum glucose is generated directly in mmol/L. Source fasting plasma is not renamed serum, and MIND's serum measurement is not assumed to reproduce the target laboratory or fasting protocol. HbA1c is generated directly in percent using percent-scale contextual evidence. The supplied NGSP affine relation preserves the distinction between IFCC and NGSP scales, but no IFCC conversion is needed or applied here. It supplies no serum/plasma conversion, assay equivalence or estimated-average-glucose equation.

There is no empirical truncation of BMI or glycemic outputs. Positive lognormal components and positive additions impose their supports; age is generated only on the required integer support. Continuous variables are unrounded. All draws use the supplied seed; no model setting varies by seed. The shared residual loadings are algebraically derived from the declared variance fraction. Source quartiles are not treated as confidence intervals or converted into reported SDs, and individual SDs are never used as estimator SEs. Synthetic sample size does not increase the precision of the underlying source evidence.

This observational generator does not separately model medication, diagnosis history, red-cell turnover, pregnancy, kidney disease, fasting duration, assay error or complete-case selection. It assumes their combined effects can be represented by the component distributions and discordance noise. Tail frequency is a modelling choice, not estimated disease prevalence. The selected analytical sample is not claimed nationally representative. Causal interpretation, clinical decision rules, source reconstruction, source interval membership, target coefficient recovery and fresh independent confirmation are unsupported uses.
