# Single initial scientific submission

This is one fixed, assumption-dependent whole-cohort generator for the supplied existing-population benchmark. It does not claim fresh independent confirmation. The E0 packet contains source marginal summaries and no relationship records. The joint distribution therefore remains unidentified by the supplied evidence; the dependence choices below are explicit assumptions, not recovered associations.

The law draws integer age from a discrete Gaussian-shaped kernel on 35-74 and documented female membership from a weakly age-dependent Bernoulli distribution. These working composition choices are not target estimates. The source female fractions reflect different selection processes; the unresolved Lifelines numeric gender code is never used. The output follows the task's female=1, male=0 convention.

BMI follows a conditional lognormal law with a modest curved age trend and a small female shift. Bao's median and quartile endpoints provide the direct numeric inputs. The log SD is derived from the quartile ratio under an expressly assumed lognormal approximation, and the reference median is shifted by 0.3 kg/m^2. This approximation transports a marginal source spread into a conditional target law. It does not treat quartiles as an SD or confidence interval, and it does not guarantee reproduction of the rounded source quartiles. MIND and Lifelines BMI summaries remain context rather than pooled constraints.

A latent elevated glycemic component has an assumed reference probability of 0.08, with assumed age, BMI and sex dependence. This is a distributional component, not a diabetes diagnosis or a recovered prevalence. Within each component, glucose and HbA1c have correlated Gaussian log residuals and separate conditional medians and dispersions. Shared state and covariates create further dependence. Residual correlations of 0.60 and 0.75 are assumed on log scales; they are neither observed-scale correlations nor converted rank associations. The residual construction yields a valid covariance law algebraically for each declared correlation, but no numerical covariance diagnostic has been run.

The glucose levels use the separately identified source FPG and MIND FSG margins as contextual scale information. Target glucose remains the released central-serum measurement. No plasma-to-serum conversion is applied, no source value is relabelled as a target observation, and no exact source moment matching is intended. Source differences include exclusions of diagnosed or all diabetes, treatment eligibility, age restrictions, country and locality, fasting protocol, assay, sample handling, and MIND's reported imputation and prior-year BMI assessment. The chosen mixture is an assumption about how an unrestricted complete-case population could differ. These sources do not identify that transport.

HbA1c is generated directly in percent using Bao and MIND percent summaries for context. Lifelines IFCC HbA1c is not used numerically. The supplied NGSP affine scale relation does not establish assay equivalence or a glucose model. No converted HbA1c observation or coefficient is installed. Individual SDs are never interpreted as estimator SEs; source sample sizes and synthetic n are not used to manufacture uncertainty.

All scientific constants, centering coordinates and transport terms are in model_spec.json. The program uses one seeded NumPy random generator, makes no file, process or network calls, accepts observed=None and returns only the six requested columns. Age has the task support; positive continuous outputs use lognormal support without clipping or an assumed empirical upper limit. The lognormal tails may be unrealistic at extremes. Rows and model innovations have the stated conditional independence assumptions; those assumptions are not inferred from absent relationship evidence. No post-generation moment correction, alternate model, seed-specific tuning or fallback is provided.

The model does not identify treatment, diagnostic status, pregnancy, familial or geographic clustering, actual complete-case selection, assay error components, or transport uncertainty. It is unsuitable for clinical decisions, causal inference, prevalence claims, or inferential confirmation from the synthetic sample. A source fit, even if later demonstrated, would not establish target utility.

No generator execution, generated table, numerical source diagnostic, dry run, calibration or target comparison was performed. checks.json records only unperformed numerical checks with empty artifacts. The fixed evidence.json was not changed. Construction stops with this single submission because the contract allows one scientific stage; static review, freeze and the three prescribed isolated executions belong to the controller. Runtime, numerical support, realized distributions and reproducibility remain untested by the author.
