# Initial scientific submission

This is one fixed hierarchical probability model for 3,000 whole-cohort records. Age follows a bounded discrete bell-shaped law; documented female membership depends weakly on age; BMI is conditionally lognormal with age curvature and a sex shift. A latent high-glycemia component depends on age, BMI and sex. Separate lognormal serum-glucose and HbA1c measurements share that component and a continuous normal factor, with additional independent residual variation. Component membership is a distributional device, not a diabetes diagnosis or treatment state.

The E0 packet contains marginal summaries and no relationships. Every joint dependence, coefficient, residual loading, component weight and conditional independence is therefore declared as an assumption. This factorization imposes no causal interpretation. The product of the two factor loadings is a conditional correlation of standardized log residuals by algebra; it is not an imported correlation or the final raw-scale association. The shared state creates additional dependence. No rank-to-Pearson conversion or combination of separately adjusted coefficients occurs.

Bao's BMI median and quartiles inform a plausible central scale; MIND and Jansen describe distinct dispersion and selection contexts. The selected conditional BMI median and spread are author choices, not fitted source moments. Source age summaries suggest a broad adult distribution, but the chosen bounded law and its sex composition are unverified transport assumptions. Female coding follows the named category, not unresolved original gender codes. The normal-kernel location and scale are not claimed as the age mean and SD after restriction.

For glucose, MIND's fasting serum is the nearest supplied specimen context. Its older rural follow-up, local assay, timing and reported imputation differ from the target's released central-serum complete-case field. Bao and Jansen fasting plasma remain distinct fields and serve only as scale and selection context. The generator does not produce source plasma values and relabel them as serum. No universal specimen or fasting conversion is used. A high-value component allows variation beyond the selected diabetes-free source context, but its weight and shape are assumed and do not estimate diabetes prevalence.

HbA1c is generated directly in percent using the percent-scale sources as context. The common NGSP guidance was read: it is a deterministic scale relation and does not establish assay equivalence. No IFCC conversion is needed in this program, no estimated-average-glucose formula is used, and Jansen's IFCC summaries are not combined with percent summaries as independent constraints. Individual SDs remain individual dispersion; quartiles remain endpoints. No estimator SE, confidence interval, source precision or inferential certainty is manufactured from these summaries or synthetic sample size.

All scientific settings are in `model_spec.json`; the generator reads only the supplied specification and seed. Age support is 35-74 by construction. Positive lognormal variables need no empirical clipping; their tails are assumptions. The BMI driver alone is smoothly saturated to avoid unbounded covariate shifts. IDs are synthetic sequence strings, and output contains only the six required columns. All stochastic draws use the one seeded random generator, with the same scientific law for every seed.

The selected target complete cases need not resemble any source population. Region, survey clustering, selection weights, missingness, assay errors, treatment and nonglycemic HbA1c processes are not separately identified. Separate residuals permit glucose-HbA1c discordance without establishing its real frequency. The model cannot support causal claims, clinical decisions, validated prevalence estimates, national representativeness, recovered target coefficients, or claims of fresh independent confirmation. Source plausibility would not establish target utility.

No generator execution, generated table, pilot, calibration, source diagnostic or target comparison has been performed. Numerical checks are recorded as not computable in `checks.json`; runtime, generated bounds, empirical dependence and source compatibility remain untested. The fixed evidence artifact has not been edited. Work stops at this single initial submission because the assignment reserves static validation, freeze and the three isolated seed executions to the controller and permits no scientific revision or additional candidate.
