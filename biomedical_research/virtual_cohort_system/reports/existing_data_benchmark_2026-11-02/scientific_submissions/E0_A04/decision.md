# Initial scientific submission

This single generator uses a hierarchical distribution for age, sex, BMI, serum glucose and whole-blood HbA1c. A discretized beta distribution supplies age within 35-74; a logistic age model supplies the documented female category; BMI follows a conditional lognormal law with modest age curvature and a sex shift. An assumed high-glycemia component changes both laboratory centers and residual dispersions. A shared Gaussian factor couples the laboratory residuals within each component. This describes plausible positive dependence and upper tails while allowing discordant glucose and HbA1c. The component is not an observed diagnosis or treatment label.

The supplied E0 packet contains marginal context and no relationship records. Shanghai's BMI and percent HbA1c summaries chiefly inform the chosen central scales, while MIND's serum glucose and broader laboratory dispersion inform allowing a wider component. Every conditional effect, mixture probability, factor loading and distributional shape is explicitly assumed in the specification. No reported quartile is treated as an SD; no individual SD is treated as an estimator SE. The conditional log-residual correlation is algebraically the product of the two factor loadings, and exponentiation creates positive measurements; neither fact supplies an empirical association. Source summaries are not combined through sample-size weighting or treated as independent constraints after compilation.

Age and sex composition of the target are unknown. Their chosen distributions are assumptions, not recovered source or target composition. Female=1 and male=0 follow the task's category convention, without importing Lifelines' unresolved original numeric coding. Source age selections are heterogeneous, and the target common-complete-case release can differ from every source. Lifelines excludes diabetes and anemia; Shanghai excludes previously diagnosed diabetes but retains screen-detected cases; MIND includes known diabetes in an older selected rural population and reports imputation and measurements from different years. The model applies none of these source exclusions to the target and does not estimate their selection effects.

Glucose is generated directly as the requested central-serum field in mmol/L. MIND serum FSG is the nearest specimen context but has different laboratory, population and processing conditions. Shanghai/Lifelines fasting plasma FPG remain separate contextual measurements; there is no serum/plasma conversion. HbA1c is generated directly in percent. NGSP guidance was read and retained in the fixed evidence, but no IFCC conversion is needed by this implementation. No estimated-average-glucose equation, assay equivalence or fasting equivalence is inferred from the scale relation.

All scientific constants are in model_spec.json and are consumed through spec['parameters']; the same law applies to every prescribed seed. Independent innovations conditional on the stated hierarchy and exchangeable rows are simplifying assumptions. Important unresolved dependence includes treatment, erythrocyte biology, disease duration, geography, selection, diet and laboratory processing. These omissions prevent causal interpretation of the generated associations. The fixed settings represent one construction, not parameter uncertainty. The logarithmic tails are unbounded and no clinical range clipping, empirical moment correction or hidden calibration is performed. Only the required age support is bounded; continuous variables are not rounded.

This submission is a method-benchmark candidate for an existing study population. It does not establish national representativeness, prevalence accuracy, clinical thresholds, transportable prediction, causal effects or independent confirmation. No generator execution, simulated table, source-fit statistic, target evaluation, pilot, sweep or scientific revision was performed. Runtime validity and finite numerical output remain untested. checks.json records numerical diagnostics as not_computable with no auxiliary artifacts. The controller owns static validation, freeze and the three fixed-seed executions. Work stops at the one initial five-file submission because the assigned information boundary supplies no authorized numerical feedback or revision stage.
