This submission specifies one fixed joint law for 3,000 complete records. Age uses a discrete Gaussian-shaped kernel on the required 35-74 support. Female membership follows an age-dependent Bernoulli model, and BMI is conditionally lognormal with a weak concave age pattern and a modest female shift. The equations use fixed reference values rather than sample moments. Laboratory and component-membership equations use a logarithmic relative-BMI predictor, with one unit between BMI 23.5 and 28.5; their BMI coefficients are not linear per-5 slopes.

The two laboratory outputs have positive lognormal cores, correlated through a shared standard-normal factor, and a shared additive gamma-severity tail. Tail membership depends on age, BMI and female membership. This allows both continuous variation and occasional joint elevations without treating a simulated component as a diabetes diagnosis. All dependence coefficients, component probabilities and tail parameters are assumptions. E0 supplies no relationship records; omitted relationships are not interpreted as empirical zeros. The residual conditional independences are explicit simplifications of this one joint law.

Bao's BMI median and quartile endpoints supply provisional scale anchors. The expression log(Q3/Q1)/(2*Phi_inverse(0.75)) is a log-SD derivation under the declared lognormal assumption, not a claim that quartiles are reported SDs. Source BMI means and individual SDs from MIND and Jansen provide population context rather than additional fitted constraints. The synthetic marginal BMI quartiles need not equal Bao's after age and sex mixing. The chosen age kernel and sex fraction are assumptions informed by differing source selection; source sample sizes are not weights or measures of target precision.

Glucose is generated directly as the released central-serum field. MIND fasting serum measurements provide the nearest specimen-scale context, but its older rural follow-up, local laboratory processing, later year and mean filling do not establish equivalence to the target. Bao and Jansen fasting plasma are retained as distinct source facts and are not renamed or converted into serum. HbA1c is generated directly in percent, using Bao and MIND percent summaries as context while acknowledging assay and population differences. Jansen's diabetes/anemia exclusions and IFCC immunoassay limit transport; no IFCC numeric setting is installed. NGSP guidance is used to preserve scale distinctions; no HbA1c-to-glucose relationship or serum/plasma correction follows from it.

Reference laboratory levels describe conditional core medians at zero centered covariates and zero log error, not unconditional means. The elevated component is a judgemental response to the target's absence of a claimed diabetes or treatment exclusion. The source margins cannot identify its frequency or severity, treatment effects, assay bias, selection mechanism or the joint dependence structure. No source coefficients were concatenated, no correlation conversion was made, and no estimator SE was inferred from individual SD or synthetic sample size.

Age support is imposed by a normalized finite categorical distribution. Positive links and nonnegative increments maintain BMI and laboratory support without clipping or unsupported upper limits. Standard numerical constants express distribution definitions; every scientific setting is in model_spec.json. Randomness is local to the supplied seed and all three prescribed seeds use the same law. Records are independent, so household/community clustering and survey design effects are not reproduced. There are no participant inputs, missingness, diagnosis labels, medication fields or latent-state outputs.
