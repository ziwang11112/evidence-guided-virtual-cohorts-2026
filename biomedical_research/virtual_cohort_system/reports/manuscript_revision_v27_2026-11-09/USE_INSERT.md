## Optional surrounding prose

## Suggested inference paragraph

Regression intervals calculated from a synthetic table condition on the selected generating law and fitting assumptions. They do not automatically include uncertainty in source estimates, structural choices or population transfer. Consequently, agreement of a slope does not validate its synthetic standard error, a power calculation or a hypothesis test about the target population. More generated rows reduce sampling variation under the same law; they add no measured evidence. The dispersion discrepancies reported here also matter for these inferential uses, beyond studies of individual variability or tails.
