## New material and internal consistency

S16 makes the reference populations and estimands easier to interpret. In particular, the selected, equal-cell ENSANUT analysis is distinguished from national survey inference, the iron reference retains its complete-case limitation, and HRS remains supplied-model reconstruction. The table separates reference uncertainty from uncertainty in source-to-target transfer. These additions provide definitions; they do not purport to add new validation. This review checked their coherence with the manuscript, not the participant-level selection calculations.

S17 distinguishes replaying a frozen program from reproducing the authoring session, documents model-metadata gaps and the review process, and explicitly labels the reproduction recipe as unexecuted. The statement that review roles need not use a different model family or be unexposed to project results prevents overinterpretation of “independent review.” These disclosures clarify the workflow without denying the reproducibility of its executable artifacts.

Table references are internally correct after renumbering: Table 1 is the operational sequence; Tables 2–3 are the iron attribution and evidence tables; Table 4 is the evaluation design; Table 5 is the observed-property/use summary. Existing supplementary references remain consistent. S16 and S17 contain Tables S9 and S10 respectively. The reference to an older draft's Tables 2–6 is explicitly scoped to that archived draft and should not be renumbered.

The added summaries increase length but have distinct roles: operational attribution in Table 1, interpretation in Table 5, reference definitions in S16 and reproducibility conditions in S17. They do not introduce a competing headline or obscure the positive result sequence. No further expansion is needed to resolve this review's concerns.
