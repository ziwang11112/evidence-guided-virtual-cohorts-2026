# Rubric/skill prototype and behavioral forward check

Date: 9 November 2026 (America/Chicago). User requested exploration of rubrics and skills as a method improvement.

## Boundary

Create a project-local experimental skill and exercise its decision behavior on a small fictional source packet. Do not change the installed cohort skill, frozen candidates, clinical data or previous completed exact-moment experiments. No new patient or artificial participant tables, clinical comparisons, model training, paid API calls or external downloads are part of this unit. A new-context Agent may read the supplied fixture and skill, perform deterministic algebra and write its own response in this unit only.

This is skill forward-testing, not a multi-agent or clinical efficacy experiment. No baseline arm is run and no improvement claim can follow from success. The previous complete-summary trial remains closed; the present check concerns task relevance, missing dependence and responses to informative versus irrelevant evidence, without sampling any table.

## Frozen visible packet

The fixture is fictional and contains a proposed Gaussian law, two visible scientific analyses and three source-information conditions. Give the test Agent only the packet, the experimental skill and its linked references. Do not supply the adjudication criteria or the research project's prior target results.

The Agent should produce a task rubric, the check or limitation for each requested analysis, and the recommended construction/revision decision under each information condition. Any suggested alternative must preserve the information admitted in that condition. Calculations are permitted; clinical source retrieval and generated rows are not.

## Predeclared behavioral checks

Before dispatch, bind packet and skill bytes in DESIGN_LOCK.json. Root will assess the response against these criteria:

1. Both requested analyses remain represented; no hidden scientific question is invented.
2. The first packet supports the marginal association while the candidate's conditional result depends on its additional independence assumption. Missing links are not asserted to be zero evidence.
3. Additional marginal summaries do not identify the missing conditional relationship.
4. The matching joint-correlation update is used to derive a coherent conditional relationship; no preferred point sign is retained by fiat.
5. Proposed matrices are mathematically admissible, with unchanged supplied margins and marginal association. Numerical identities can be independently recomputed.
6. The response separates checked algebra from unexecuted data analysis, and does not claim clinical validity, calibrated rubric scores or skill superiority.

These checks assess behavior and mathematical consistency only. A rubric declaring something unresolved does not demonstrate final-data usability. Record failures and then either narrowly revise the skill for an observed defect or retain it. Do not rerun merely to obtain a successful answer.
