# Evidence use across two scientific requests

5 November 2026. Status: preparation; no constructions or executions released by this document.

## Purpose

Test whether one Agent workflow can turn published evidence and a population description into useful full virtual tables for two different biomedical requests. The new method instruction is to choose how each important source quantity is used: constrain a relationship, constrain dispersion, check without fitting, support an alternative context, or leave an explicit assumption. A coefficient, correlation, scale ratio and absolute residual variance are distinct possible transport choices. None is automatically invariant across populations.

The completed source-increment E01 experiment remains closed. Its mixed result motivates an explicit source-use decision; it supplies no desired coefficient, scale or direction to new constructors. We do not carry over its generator or parameter values. This new pilot evaluates capability and final data utility, not a causal improvement over a baseline policy.

## Selected requests, before reference inspection

1. ENSANUT2018 analytical-use HbA1c request: retain the exact existing population, variables, three requested adjusted effects and complete diagnostic set. Target population metadata are allowed; prior generators, patient observations and reference estimates are not author inputs.
2. Turkana measured TC/HDL request: use the existing age/sex/BMI/TC/HDL metadata and prior measurement mapping. Proposed requested analyses are HDL-on-BMI adjusted for age and sex, TC-on-BMI adjusted for age and sex, and residual TC-HDL association after age, sex and BMI. Exact formulas and units require source/schema admission and independent review before freeze. Do not select endpoints using old target estimates. Failure of source/schema admission closes preparation without substituting another dataset in this unit.

Both reference families have already been exposed at project level. Fresh constructor contexts provide procedural separation, not new clinical confirmation or filesystem isolation. Any historical summary encountered during metadata inspection is logged; it is not added to construction inputs.

## Common construction procedure

Provide each fresh Agent the full research questions, approved task/population metadata, reviewed external source artifacts and existing harness documentation. The Agent chooses structure, parameters, distributions and which source quantities it regards as transferable. No desired biological sign or target fit is prescribed. External evidence cannot come from either target cohort. Missing conditional information remains an explicit assumption rather than independence by default.

Before a candidate freezes, require a brief evidence-use decision for each requested analysis. Reuse the existing evidence and analysis-evidence objects. Bind numerical uses to actual model-specification paths, check-only uses to actual checks, and assumptions to their existing IDs. A new optional validator checks references and bytes; scientific review checks meaning, unit conversions, source applicability and consistency between code and specification. Neither structural validation nor a review pass establishes population validity.

The same Agent may inspect one initial isolated trial and qualified source feedback, then retain or revise once. A no-op is valid. Any revision must state which new evidence or source check supports the changed mechanism and preserve unsupported assumptions. It must not copy target values or optimize held reference discrepancies. Alternative contexts may be discussed, but no extra scenario execution is authorized outside the stated budget.

## Proposed fixed resource bounds

* Two requests, three independent fresh constructions each, three final seeds: at most 18 delivery tables. These are six constructions, not 18 independent scientific replications.
* 3,000 fictional rows per delivery, subject to each frozen task's population constraints.
* One initial trial and at most one revised trial per constructor; at most five execution attempts per constructor, 30 total. Failures consume attempts; no replacement author, favorable seed selection or silent retry.
* Initial/revised trial seeds 9100/9110; delivery seeds 9101, 9102, 9103. Final model chosen before delivery draws.
* Existing admitted sources are reused with provenance. Optional gap-directed acquisition through the existing task-bound literature gateway: at most four query credits and two original downloads per constructor, failures charged; acquisition closes at initial freeze. One invocation may consume both metadata and download credits. No new patient data, paid API, training or HPC.
* At most two prefreeze scientific/mechanical review rounds per stage. Terminal invalid candidates are retained as failures. No new components or budget extension in response to unfavorable results.

The current admission review may inspect up to two additional primary papers for the lipid task. They are reported separately from per-constructor retrieval and may be provided equally to all lipid constructors after approval. These bounds are pending independent protocol review, not a new inherited quota reset.

## Evaluation and interpretation

After all constructor choices and final attempts close, compute all frozen primary analyses and diagnostics. Show every construction and seed; distinguish direction, native-unit effect magnitude, joint dependence and conditional/marginal dispersion. Do not require exact matching to one real sample or define success by p-value agreement alone. Do not choose acceptance margins from the observed generated results. A lack of prespecified scientific equivalence margins means descriptive agreement, not equivalence certification.

Reuse audited generated-only analysis functions where their formula matches the request. Where a new lipid estimator is required, implement and independently check it using controlled arrays before clinical-reference use. Use existing aggregate references when definitions match. If a new reference functional is needed, freeze its estimator before reading the existing local participant data; report it as a new descriptive analysis of an exposed cohort. No target summary enters author feedback.

Source-feedback v1 is explicit. Task-context comparisons retain transport approximations and are not renamed source-population replay. Unsupported weighted/log/geometric or otherwise undefined source operators remain unavailable. Directly reported, algebraically derived and assumption-dependent quantities stay separate.

Independent numerical reconstruction checks every delivered primary estimate and relevant diagnostic. Preserve failures, missing operators, mixed outcomes and disagreement. Final conclusions concern which analyses these delivered data support under these information conditions. This pilot does not identify a causal retrieval or feedback benefit, guarantee all biomedical relationships, or demonstrate replacement of clinical measurement.

## Completion gate

Preparation is complete when both task/estimator packets are admissible, the optional decision interface and controller are reviewed, constructor inputs exclude prior results, and the protocol is frozen. The subsequent execution unit completes with all scheduled attempts accounted for, independent numerical review and a continue/simplify/stop decision. Old frozen files and manuscript results remain unchanged.
