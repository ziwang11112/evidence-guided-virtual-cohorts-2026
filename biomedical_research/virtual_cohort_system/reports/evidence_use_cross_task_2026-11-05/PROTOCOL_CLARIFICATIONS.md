# Accepted independent clarifications before release

5 November 2026. The six clarifications in review/REVIEW.md are accepted without expanding the study. This document supplements PLAN.md; final machine-readable task and estimator bindings take precedence over its proposed wording.

* The HbA1c task and all 31 endpoints retain the existing definitions. Lipid endpoints retain the prior K02 model: unweighted OLS with intercept, `(age-50)/10`, literal `I(sex == 'F')` (M reference), and `(BMI-25)/5`; report the two lipid BMI coefficients in mg/dL per5 kg/m² and Pearson correlation of the two full-model residual vectors. The generated lipids are in mmol/L and converted by38.67. The F/M biological legend is not inferred beyond the admitted coding; the composition diagnostic is `sex.F_fraction`.
* Lipid participant-reference criteria remain common finite complete age/sex/BMI/TC/HDL, age>=18 and strictly positive BMI/TC/HDL, with no added disease, fasting, outlier or TC>=HDL restriction. Generated data must satisfy the entire table contract; no row exclusion silently repairs a delivery. Rank-deficient/degenerate analyses remain unavailable. No source instrument is declared equivalent merely because units match.
* Use the existing cached K02 reference for exactly its three primary endpoints and two sample SDs. The additional generated-only diagnostic quantities are reported without invented reference values or new clinical fits. HbA1c uses the unchanged cached 31-endpoint reference. All reference parsing waits for six constructor closures. Their historical exposure is disclosed.
* Five attempt slots per author are initial9100, optional revised/repair9110, then delivery9101/9102/9103. Failed calls consume slots. A failed revision may fall back to the already valid initial candidate, with an explicit decision binding both feedback histories before final draws. If neither candidate is valid, close the author as failed. No final rerun or replacement seed; skipped trials do not expand delivery. Preflight/static review is not a generator call and remains within two review rounds per stage.
* Revision grounds are a concrete admitted-source finding, qualified source discrepancy, extraction/definition error, or implementation/contract defect. Assumptions may change with a recorded justification; their visibility and historical versions must be preserved. No new retrieval after first freeze, and no second scientific revision after the revised trial. Final candidate bytes are fixed for every delivery.
* Six fresh-context constructions share a model service, workflow and sources; they are not independent clinical studies. Seeds only probe sampling variability. Report per-analysis native-unit performance and relevant dependence/dispersion, without seed selection, inferred equivalence margins or an overall superiority claim.
* Constructor packets exclude result-bearing histories and old models. Source-use decisions may use evidence, assumptions or both, including retained numerical choices and shared parameters affecting several analyses. The pure gate establishes actual parameter-path/byte bookkeeping. Separate scientific review checks code use, source meaning and finite moments. Neither proves scientific applicability.
* Program sampling is the route for this bounded pilot. Direct Agent-authored rows remain a valid separate project route. Only the generator process is isolated; fresh author tools are separated procedurally.

The source reviewers explicitly retain unresolved weighting, regression scales, assay differences and unmeasured adjustments. In semantic feedback v1, unknown weighting/unsupported transform remains not computable. An equal-weight simulation assumption does not rewrite an unreported source method into a reported one. This restricts that check, not the Agent's ability to construct an explicitly assumption-dependent model.
