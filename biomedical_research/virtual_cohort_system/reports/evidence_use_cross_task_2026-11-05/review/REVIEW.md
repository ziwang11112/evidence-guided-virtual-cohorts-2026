## Assessment

The purpose is scientifically coherent: evaluate the usefulness of complete generated tables for the two stated requests, using the same evidence-to-construction workflow. A useful model may depend on explicit assumptions even when the joint law is not identified by the sources. Identification is not a prerequisite for constructing or inspecting such a model; it limits what the result can guarantee. Conversely, matching a source statistic, passing a schema, or making an evidence-use declaration cannot establish target validity, causal correctness, or improved utility.

Six fresh constructions with three final seeds each are a bounded capability pilot. No baseline, superiority claim, new clinical cohort, additional constructor, extra scenario execution, or extra acquisition quota is needed. Existing source artifacts may be enough; optional retrieval should pursue a stated missing definition or relationship rather than a preferred sign or fit.

## Necessary clarifications before freeze

1. **Freeze complete estimators and measurement definitions in the task packets.** Preserve the existing HbA1c request IDs, population, formulas and entire diagnostic list by exact version/hash; do not reconstruct them from historical results. For lipids, explicitly choose the two unstandardized BMI coefficients from `HDL ~ 1 + BMI + age + sex` and `TC ~ 1 + BMI + age + sex`, if those are the intended requests, and define the third quantity precisely. A suitable interpretation is Pearson correlation of the two residual vectors after separately regressing TC and HDL on the same `1 + age + sex + BMI` design and same complete-case rows. Freeze units, sex reference/coding, transformations, intercept, inclusion/missingness rules, weighting and singular-design handling. The coefficient units are lipid-unit per BMI-unit; residual correlation is dimensionless. Covariance or a residual regression coefficient is a different request. If uncertainty or p-values are reported, freeze their estimator and degrees-of-freedom conventions too.

2. **Use reference values only when the full estimator definition matches.** Matching column names or a familiar coefficient label is insufficient. Check direction of regression, adjustment, units, measurement mapping, weighting, selection and missingness against the frozen functional. An unmatched aggregate remains unavailable for that functional; it is not silently substituted. The plan's route of freezing a new estimator before computing it on the already exposed local cohort is adequate. Record that reference as a new descriptive analysis of an exposed cohort, and keep it out of constructor feedback. Controlled-array numerical checks and independent reconstruction already in the plan are sufficient; this does not require another experiment.

3. **Resolve the five-attempt accounting and repair boundary.** Two possible trial attempts plus three delivery attempts exhaust five. A minimal rule is one initial trial slot at 9100, at most one revised/repair trial slot at 9110, and one slot for each of 9101/9102/9103. Failures consume their slot; skipped trials do not authorize new delivery seeds or repeat draws. Count an invoked generator adapter attempt even when the backend fails, and retain any pre-launch error in the attempt ledger. Declaration-only validation/preflight does not consume an execution slot but remains within the stated review-round cap. Any post-trial mechanical fix creates a new immutable candidate and uses the optional second-stage slot; a fix changing a transformation, distribution, parameter or dependence law is scientific, whatever its label. Freeze the final candidate before delivery and use its identical bytes for every final seed. State whether a failed revised stage retains the already-valid initial candidate or terminates that constructor; choose this rule before any run. No post-delivery repair or seed replacement is needed. Eighteen is a maximum number of valid delivery tables, not a completion guarantee.

4. **Clarify what can justify the single revision.** Replace the requirement that every revision cite "new evidence or source check" with a requirement for a concrete documented finding: admitted evidence, qualified source discrepancy, extraction/definition error, or implementation/contract error. Retrieval closes at initial freeze, but rereading an already admitted source remains possible. "Preserve unsupported assumptions" should mean preserve their visibility and history, not forbid changing an assumption with a stated reason. Any remaining, replaced or newly introduced assumption must stay explicit. A justified no-op is valid. A second trial may verify the chosen revision, but does not open another scientific feedback-revision cycle.

5. **Keep the final-data utility assessment primary and qualified.** The final report should address each requested analysis using all delivered estimates and the prespecified dependence/dispersion diagnostics, then explain which uses appear useful, limited or unsupported under the declared assumptions. Without scientific equivalence margins, these are descriptive scientific judgments, not equivalence or validity certificates. Do not select the best seed, pool the 18 outputs as independent studies, or turn source-fit improvement into target utility. Describe the six runs as separately authored fresh-context constructions: shared sources, model family and workflow can produce dependent scientific choices. Three seeds estimate draw variability under one chosen law; they do not calibrate evidence, transport or model uncertainty. Record the common prompt/workflow, author configuration and admitted source packet; do not force constructors to choose different models.

## Evidence-use checks to retain

The new interface should bind each required analysis to actual evidence IDs, assumptions, numeric specification locations and check IDs where relevant. The scientific review must separately confirm that the source quantity has the stated meaning and the generator actually uses the referenced parameter. A parameter-path validator cannot infer those facts or prove that a changed declaration changes the generated law. An evidence-use choice can materially change that law when implemented, but this design does not identify the choice's causal benefit.

Do not conflate a marginal SD, individual residual SD, coefficient SE, scale ratio, correlation, standardized slope or adjusted slope. For example, `beta = r * SD(Y) / SD(X)` is the simple unweighted OLS identity with an intercept; an adjusted relationship requires the corresponding residualized variables and matching rows/design. A source's overall multiple-regression R-squared does not identify an individual adjusted coefficient or an absolute target residual variance. Such quantities may still be constructed under named assumptions; they are not thereby source-identified. No specific transport choice is mandatory.

Keep semantic-feedback v1's preflight, verified-output linkage, unavailable operators and raw-discrepancy interpretation. Author its semantic bindings from the actual source, not by duplicating the proposed recipe. Whole-cohort task-context checks remain approximate transport checks rather than source-population replay. Matching units in JSON is necessary, but does not itself verify a conversion or assay correspondence.
