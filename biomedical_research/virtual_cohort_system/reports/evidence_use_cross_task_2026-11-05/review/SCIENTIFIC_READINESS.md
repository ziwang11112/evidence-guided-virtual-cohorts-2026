# Final scientific protocol and source-input readiness

5 November 2026. **PASS for the bounded source-and-assumption-dependent capability experiment.** This decision covers the scientific protocol and the prepared input definitions represented by H01 and L01. It does not approve the whole controller, an individual construction, generator execution, or the resulting data. The current source-proposal release still explicitly has `generation_authorized: false`.

The accepted clarifications resolve the earlier scientific protocol findings. HbA1c retains its three adjusted effects and complete 31-endpoint contract, including the stated equal-age/sex-cell analytical design. Lipid definitions retain the three K02 functionals, common eligibility, unweighted models with intercept and literal F coding, effects per 5 kg/m², and explicit mmol/L-to-mg/dL mapping. Its 23 diagnostics include `sex.F_fraction`; unknown biological coding or assay equivalence is not inferred. Only definition-matching cached references are eligible; unmatched lipid diagnostic references remain unavailable.

The resource and revision rules are now sufficiently concrete: six separately authored constructions, 3,000 rows per table, initial/revised trial slots 9100/9110, and fixed delivery slots 9101/9102/9103; at most five attempts per constructor and eighteen valid delivery tables overall. Failures consume their slots. The optional second trial covers either scientific revision or repair; the explicit failed-revision fallback selects an already valid initial candidate before delivery. Final bytes remain fixed. Documented extraction, definition or implementation findings can justify revision without inventing new evidence, while assumption history is preserved.

The reviewed source inputs permit construction with explicit population, measurement, conditional and joint-law assumptions. They do not identify all target relationships. The earlier qualified lipid source admission remains in force. Existing HbA1c approved source artifacts are reused without a fresh re-extraction in this pass. Unknown weighting, unresolved scales and unsupported operators remain unavailable under semantic-feedback v1; an equal-weight simulation assumption is not a reported source method. Generic source-packet language about possible source-scenario computation is subordinate to that explicit adapter rule.
