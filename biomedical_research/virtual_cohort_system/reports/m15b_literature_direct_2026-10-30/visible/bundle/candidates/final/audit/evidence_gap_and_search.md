## Evidence gaps identified before search

The seeds establish positive BMI-urate dependence, subgroup marginal levels and variation. They do not fully identify the conditional urate distribution given the four available covariates. Important gaps are an adjusted adult age association (particularly across the menopause transition), possible BMI curvature at extreme BMI, residual rather than marginal dispersion, and transport across selection criteria and assay platforms. The two papers draw on the same community and overlapping survey periods, so they are not independent replications.

Observed-only summaries reinforce the extrapolation gap. In White male, White female, Black male, Black female order, BMI means are 28.874, 30.398, 29.916, 35.078 and sample SDs are 6.9622, 9.9769, 9.0955, 9.4421 kg/m^2. Maximum BMI is respectively 54.2, 69.9, 69.1, 64.0. Mean ages are 38.46, 37.64, 39.64, 38.20 years. Each stratum contains 50 fixed observations. These are covariate summaries, not outcome information.

## Proposed construction and remaining uncertainty

Use source-centered subgroup urate location anchors with a positive BMI component. A pooled descriptive calculation from Yun Table2 yields urate SD1.5980016 and BMI SD7.3937403. Under an explicitly assumed usual full-variable standardization, 0.322 times their ratio is0.0695935 mg/dL per kg/m^2. This is a derived approximation, not an extracted raw coefficient; use a rounded0.07 working BMI slope. Even under exact standardization, that converted slope would still be smoking/alcohol-adjusted. Carrying its scale into a model without those variables therefore requires the separate, unverified A_omitted_covariate_transport assumption; reduced-model equality is not established. The source-based SD ratio calculation uses source subgroup sample sizes, not the equal target allocation. In evidence.json the mean items' uncertainty.kind=sd fields hold reported participant spread, not precision of the mean, SE or CI.

Above BMI40, halve the working slope as an explicit conservative extrapolation assumption. The location40 and factor0.5 are not literature findings and will not be presented as validated curvature. Use a common BMI slope by sex initially; the sex interaction remains uncertain and will be estimated descriptively in the fictional table rather than constrained to a published target. No deterministic row sorting or symmetry is proposed.

Use working age slopes0.005 mg/dL/year for men and0.020 for women; these are assumptions motivated by broad source context, not extracted adjusted effects or menopause-status imputation. Use conditional residual-scale guides WM1.20/WF1.10/BM1.40/BF1.55 mg/dL. They are deliberately below the corresponding marginal SD anchors to allow covariate variation; no marginal SD is identified with residual SD. The unobserved covariance decomposition, distribution tails and the age/BMI dependence of residual variation remain unidentified. These scale guides are not claims of fitted or calibrated residual variances.

Direct literal authorship will permit irregular deviations on both sides of the conditional location, with no artificial pairing, exact zero-sum requirement, rank ordering or deterministic BMI-urate monotonicity. No participant values have yet been chosen. Observed rows/cells will be preserved exactly. All source comparisons will be explicitly approximate transport diagnostics or noncomputable; there is no source-context generator or verified execution replay.

Review requested: source extraction and comparator semantics, plus whether controller-supported provenance review of PMC6941025 or admission of the unsupported Yun supplement is worthwhile before freezing the specification. The controller has subsequently initiated these two access/admission reviews; this builder will await their result before finalizing the specification or authoring rows. No reviewer requested a numeric parameter adjustment and no target feedback was supplied. Imposed age/hinge/interaction choices are modeling priors, never new scientific discoveries. The provisional specification makes no evidence-completeness claim.

The literal choices include departures above and below the location guide at both low and high BMI. No matched positive/negative pairs, exact zero-sum residual requirement or monotonic sorting rule was used. Residual-scale guides and the working formula are descriptive construction choices, not claims that the authored finite table realizes a calibrated stochastic model.
