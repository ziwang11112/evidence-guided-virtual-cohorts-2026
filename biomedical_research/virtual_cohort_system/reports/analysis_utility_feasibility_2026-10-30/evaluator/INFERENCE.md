## NHANES Taylor adapter

The design universe is every record with finite positive official `WTSAF2YR` in GLU_I, joined by SEQN to DEMO_I's masked strata and PSU. The reference's retained INS fasting weights and design identifiers must agree exactly. The established preparation XPORT reader fixes only exact IBM zero encodings. All other eligible-domain exclusions remain in the complete design with zero influence, including records excluded by age, pregnancy ascertainment, insulin use, covariate availability and target availability. PSU identifiers are nested within strata. There is no finite-population correction, in accordance with the first-stage with-replacement NHANES convention.

For weighted least squares, B = sum(w*x*x'), residual e = transformed_y - x'beta, and the row influence is B^(-1)*w*x*e. Normalizing all weights by their mean changes neither beta nor influence. For a domain weighted mean, row influence is w*(y-mean)/sum_domain(w). Out-of-domain row influences are zero. For stratum h and its **full-design** m_h PSUs, sum influences within each PSU to U_hj, then compute sum_h [m_h/(m_h-1)] * sum_j [(U_hj-mean_h)(U_hj-mean_h)']. This produces covariance for all coefficients; contrast variance is c'Vc, including the curvature multiplier squared. No ordinary residual n/(n-p) correction is added. A singleton **full-design** stratum fails; a domain represented in one PSU retains the other full-design PSU's zero influence rather than dropping the stratum.

Confidence limits are descriptive two-sided 95% t intervals. Domain degrees of freedom equal the number of represented PSU/stratum pairs minus the number of represented strata. Means use domain df. Regression uses domain df minus (model rank minus one), the conservative residual-df convention documented by survey::svyglm; this may be conservative for person-level predictors. Full-design counts and eligible-domain counts are reported separately. Nonpositive df retains the point and SE but produces no CI. A zero-variance interval is marked degenerate and cannot support a zero-null screen.

References inspected before target parsing: [CDC NHANES variance tutorial](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/nchs/nhanes/tutorials/varianceestimation.aspx) (design, domain retention and subgroup df); [survey package primary documentation](https://cran.r-project.org/web/packages/survey/survey.pdf), svyCprod and svyglm sections (PSU-total covariance and conservative regression residual df). These sources justify the prespecified methods; no effect direction was obtained from them.

## Interpretation and PNS limits

For primary contrasts, exact point signs are compared and magnitude errors remain in the endpoint's scientific units. No success tolerance, universal mixed-unit score, absent-effect conclusion or equivalence region is invented. NHANES records may say that a descriptive reference interval excludes zero or includes zero/inconclusive. This is a zero-null screen and never a clinical importance judgment, causal effect claim, multiplicity-adjusted discovery, or evidence that generated data independently support a scientific conclusion. Positive arithmetic means do not earn direction-agreement credit. The HOMA1 result is derived use, not independent confirmation.

PNS has no released PSU/stratum design in this task. It receives provided-weight point estimates and the same complete endpoint family using weights of one. There are no invented independent-row survey CIs. The supplied-weight lab-complete participant estimand, unresolved national calibration and baseline-to-laboratory time lag remain limitations.

All three residual seeds are retained for each of the four independent constructions. Their minima/medians/maxima are labelled residual Monte Carlo only, not clinical uncertainty or independent task replication. Failed draws and nonestimable endpoints are preserved. No construction is selected using the reserved reference. All four primary endpoint families are descriptive, not a confirmatory superiority test.
