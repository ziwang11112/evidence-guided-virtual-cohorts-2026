This independent construction uses a conditional lognormal generator. It derives marginal log variance from Taiwan raw insulin moments and conditional coefficients only after declaring a predictor covariance model and an assumed logBMI association. MACAD transformed BMI R informs that assumption; it is not copied as a conditional coefficient. Sex shift and bounded glucose extrapolation are explicit assumptions. Every scientific setting is in model_spec.json with provenance.

The source selection rule was written before any diagnostic. Two approximate source contexts will test Taiwan raw mean and Pearson associations, plus MACAD sex-specific medians, at three fixed seeds. MACAD IQR widths and transformed R remain unreplayed rather than converted into unsupported operators. Both source populations are non-diabetic; target diabetes, severe obesity, age/sex interactions and subgroup transport are unassessed. Within-source statistics overlap and are not independent evidence. Residual draws are individual variation, not source or model uncertainty.

The intended use is inspectable exploratory synthetic-data feasibility for associations and nonlinear/subgroup analyses. No target analysis, causal effect, individual prediction, measurement saving, calibrated uncertainty or publication readiness is established.
