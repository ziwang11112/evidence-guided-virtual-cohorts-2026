# Initial scientific decision

This is one fully specified, assumption-dependent virtual cohort for the requested primary and secondary linear projections. It is not an estimate of a uniquely identified Chinese population law.

## Construction and evidence choices

Generate 3000 independent people, with Hainan's selected-sample female fraction. Within sex, use a Gaussian copula for age, BMI, glucose and HbA1c. Age is a rounded normal restricted to 35-74, with Hainan's mean/SD as pre-restriction parameters. BMI is lognormal with the JDPP median and log-quartile spread. HbA1c and glucose are shifted lognormal with Hainan means/SDs; the assumed lower locations 3.5 percent and 2 mmol/L regularize low tails without truncating high observations. Single skewed marginals stand in for the aggregate disease/treatment mixture: no identifiable diabetes prevalence, treatment status or mechanism is claimed.

Hainan is geographically relevant and retains diabetes/treatment, making its descriptive glycaemic margins preferable here to nondiabetic European margins. Its frame/date/age uncertainties remain. JDPP provides explicit BMI margins and BMI-HbA1c Pearson correlations but is Japanese IGT-selected; transferring them unchanged is a substantial, visible modeling choice. The two correlations remain sex-specific 0.29/0.08. They are converted using the chosen lognormal margins. Hainan's Spearman 0.44/0.50 is converted via 2*sin(pi*rho/6), never equated with Pearson or latent correlation. The BMI median and log-quartile ratio fit cannot preserve both asymmetric source quartile endpoints, and this is recorded in the static report.

The missing age-BMI, age-glucose and BMI-glucose latent correlations are assumed 0.10, 0.20 and 0.25. They are not estimates from the packet. A normal exponential-tilting identity calculates the exact covariance of bounded integer age with each shifted lognormal. The age-HbA1c latent correlation is solved so the within-sex population OLS age term equals 0.010 percent/year, borrowing the magnitude from Pani NHANES under an explicit source-to-target and sex-homogeneity assumption. Weighted US nondiabetic evidence does not validate that target law. Neither additional source precision nor confidence weights are invented.

LifeLines ModelIII has a continuous BMI term 0.05 mmol/mol per kg/m^2 (algebraically 0.0045746 percent per kg/m^2), while its age/gender-only term is 0.13 mmol/mol per kg/m^2. These are different adjustments in the same cohort. Its CI levels and gender coding are unresolved. Hoorn Q4-versus-Q1 effects -0.135 and -0.022 percent condition on waist, hip, 2h glucose and other variables; they are not linear slopes. These conflicting conditional findings remain in evidence and unavailable feedback entries. No sign averaging or substitution is performed, and omitted-covariate independence is not assumed. JDPP's unresolved Beta and Hainan's ambiguous regression scale/orientation are withheld.

## What the joint model implies

For centered X=(age,BMI,glucose), the primary within-sex coefficient is Var(X)^-1 Cov(X,HbA1c). With an intercept and female indicator but no sex interactions, the requested pooled slope uses the sex-probability-weighted within-sex covariance matrices. The female coefficient is the difference of sex means after applying those common slopes. Thus the BMI coefficient emerges from the full covariance law; it is not set to any source adjusted coefficient. The secondary projection removes glucose from that matrix rather than reusing the primary BMI term.

Deterministic unrounded-law primary coefficients in order intercept,BMI,glucose,age,female are [3.307861869885501, 0.0129857915722276, 0.29177804630537374, 0.010241200742187545, -0.055679461253359847]. Secondary coefficients in order intercept,BMI,age,female are [3.745012159802132, 0.045549420566018815, 0.01755478326546027, -0.04125612152379585]. These are algebraic consequences before sampling, not generated-data or target results. The static report also changes only the unreported BMI-glucose latent correlation to 0.10 and 0.40: this demonstrates structural sensitivity without selecting a favorable realization or assigning scenario probabilities.

Age and sex are bounded; all other variables are shifted exponentials of a positive-definite Gaussian vector. Gaussian moment generating functions give finite polynomial joint moments of every order. Nondegenerate predictor variation gives unique population linear projections. Integer-age covariances are evaluated by finite sums, not a continuous-age approximation. Double precision and deterministic bisection are the numerical approximations; final 8-decimal table rounding is at most 5e-9 per cell. Lognormal tails are assumptions and need empirical scrutiny.

## Applicability and checks

The construction supports inspecting distributions, Pearson/Spearman associations and the two declared regressions under its law. These uses remain assumption-dependent, especially glucose-adjusted BMI. It cannot establish causal BMI effects, individual recovery, clinical decision validity, national representation, treatment response or calibrated target uncertainty. Target fasting adherence and serum/plasma equivalence are unverified; the baseline assumes no extra contamination, without claiming none exists.

Source feedback compares only the actual generated target table, not a relabeled source sample. Hainan means, sex fraction and Spearman plus JDPP Pearson are approximate raw comparisons. Unknown SD denominator/quantile convention and incompatible conditional estimands remain unavailable. Source SD denotes individual dispersion. Exact source replay is not attempted because unreported joint populations and omitted variables would require further unsupported detail.

The construction used the five admitted originals and reviewed packet only, with zero new searches/downloads and no generator execution. Analytical/static calculations are preserved. Contract validation, semantic v1 preflight and lifecycle static source/check-artifact validation passed; their actual results are bound in checks.json. Initial acquisition is closed; at most one controller feedback revision/retain decision remains. No seed is chosen here.
