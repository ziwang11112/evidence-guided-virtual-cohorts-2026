# Initial construction decision

Use one conditional lognormal construction with a positive arithmetic-scale conditional mean. Source-sex anchors are142/97 mg/dL, common BMI slope6.8 mg/dL per kg/m^2, a smooth female midlife rise, a modest male age decline, and residual log SD.65/.48. These are deliberately rounded evidence-informed assumptions, not a demand that generated samples equal paper numbers. BMI effects bend above35; age response is flat outside20-80. All numerical settings and source-context recipes are in model_spec.json. No target TG, target regression result or desired p-value was inspected or supplied.

The BMI coefficient is chosen from Oh's age/sex-adjusted Table3 because its adjustment set is closest to the observed variables. It is not a correlation, co-twin causal estimate, or the heavily adjusted Table5 coefficient. Oh age strata guide a sex interaction, although transferring marginal age patterns into conditional effects assumes away unidentified age-BMI dependence. Bansal provides US fasting-female distribution shape, qualitative rank-association evidence, and distinct checks of TG category proportions and covariate means within TG categories. Its Spearman coefficients are never treated as Pearson; median/IQR and rank correlations remain explicitly unreplayed. Neither paper supplies a complete conditional distribution.

Oh's table mean/SD labels and narrative mg/dL changes favor an arithmetic interpretation, but its general log/geometric-average sentence is unresolved. Table2 is provisionally checked as arithmetic central tendency with an explicit assumption. Methods specify EDTA plasma while Oh repeatedly says serum. Recruitment dates and minimum-age statements conflict. We selected PMC6911291.1 and the table BMI23.0 for women, preserving the narrative23.1 discrepancy. Bansal uses plasma, >=8-hour fasting and women>=45. No fixed conversion from source plasma to target serum is justified. All tables within each cohort overlap.

Source covariates are sampled only from published marginal summaries: independent truncated normals within Oh sex, and shifted-gamma age/lognormal BMI for WHS. Synthetic source rows reduce Monte Carlo error only. Their marginal checks expose recipe discrepancies; they do not validate TG. Unknown joint distributions, family clustering, omitted metabolic variables, medication/hormone effects, and source selection remain unresolved. Both contexts run the identical generator and specification and accept arbitrary row counts. The generator never recenters to the target or generated sample.
