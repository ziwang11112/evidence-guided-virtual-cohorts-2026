# Initial scientific decision — fictional fixture only

Use a conditional lognormal model with arithmetic mean m=(2+x)*1.25**group and individual SD s=0.5*m. The six source strata support those point-specific first and second moments after converting source B from z to y. A lognormal distribution provides nonnegative values without clipping, which would alter the fitted moments. The multiplicative group term means the additive x slope is 1 u/x_unit in group 0 and 1.25 u/x_unit in group 1; it is not assumed to be the same additive slope across groups.

Every scientific parameter is read from model_spec.json. For CV c, the implementation uses log variance ln(1+c^2) and log location ln(m)-ln(1+c^2)/2. No local generator execution, generated outcomes, observed target outcome, or external medical source is used during construction.

The source B records retain the reported z statistics in du and separate derived y statistics in u. Their conversion is E[y]=E[z]/10 and SD(y)=SD(z)/abs(10), using the stated exact calibration z=10*y. Derived and original records are not independent confirmations. Source A and B are explicitly nonoverlapping fictional sources; repeated statistics within either source share that source's records.

Replay uses two covariate-only CSVs, each with 20000 artificial replication rows at x=0,1,2 for the relevant group. All six mean and six sample-SD checks are specified. These replication counts are numerical design choices, not claimed source sample sizes. Equal replication across strata does not assert equal source prevalence because each statistic is selected within its own stratum. The same frozen generator/specification is to be executed in both contexts with identity measurement in task units. The check status is approximate because the unreported source samples are represented by declared artificial covariate replications. The seed is supplied by the controller; no seed search or sample moment forcing is performed.

Unresolved assumptions are lognormal family and tails (including the absence of exact zeros), linear mean interpolation and constant CV between source strata, exchangeability/transport to target rows, conditional residual independence, and treating the fictional moments as fixed despite unknown sampling/rounding uncertainty. The model does not identify individual outcomes, causal effects, unreported covariate relationships, or predictive calibration. Different seeds reflect residual variation only, not uncertainty about evidence, structure, or transport.

Static checks inspect source hashes, contracts, input preservation structure in the AST, complete replay-stratum coverage, operator metadata, conversion arithmetic, and covariate-only CSV integrity. They neither execute the generator nor prove generated-table validity or source numerical agreement. The controller may execute the frozen candidate and provide one source-feedback round; the subsequent decision may revise or retain. Initial construction stops at freeze, before any such feedback.
