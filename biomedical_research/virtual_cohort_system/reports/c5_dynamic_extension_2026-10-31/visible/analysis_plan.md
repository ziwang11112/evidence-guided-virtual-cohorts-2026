# C5 analysis plan fixed before generator authoring

This is one deliberately artificial implementation and mechanism sanity check. It is not biomedical validation, a claim to have invented conditional-probability theory, a test of unidentifiable associations, or a causal comparison of algorithms or Agents. The complete final Gaussian evidence identifies all declared population answers. Numerical analytical answers are held in the custodian artifact until the controller commits every final output. A capable author could derive the answers from the visible evidence; this is outcome-file separation, not concealment of identifiable mathematics.

## Three prespecified statistical analysis definitions

1. **A1, old-pair association:** fit ordinary least squares with intercept, `x2 ~ 1 + x1`. Report the slope in u2/u1 and the intercept in u2 for each parent, joint, and extended table. The slope is the primary A1 endpoint. Check that the parent and its extension give exactly the same old-pair estimates.
2. **A2, new partial association:** fit ordinary least squares with intercept, `x3 ~ 1 + x1 + x2`. Report both slopes (x1 in u3/u1 and x2 in u3/u2) and the intercept in u3 for each joint and extended table. Both slopes are retained endpoints; neither will be selected after viewing results.
3. **A3, conditional residual variation:** from the A2 fitted model report the residual variance `SSE/(n-3)` in u3^2 and its square root in u3 for every joint and extended table. Residual variance is the primary A3 endpoint; SD is its companion presentation. Compare against the Gaussian population conditional residual variance. Individual residual variation is not uncertainty in the exact source constraints.

## Additional prespecified checks

For every available variable report sample mean, sample SD (denominator n-1), sample covariance (n-1), Pearson correlations, and linear-interpolated 0.05, 0.50, and 0.95 sample quantiles. Show numerical deviations from corresponding analytical population values. For the full tables also compute residuals against the population conditional mean and report their mean, sample SD, and Pearson correlations with x1 and x2. These are descriptive mechanism checks, not additional hypotheses selected by the outputs. No normality p-value, clinical tolerance, or invented acceptance threshold will be used.

Check exact column order, row count, finite numeric values, unique nonempty identifiers, and each final output's precommitted SHA256. For each extension, compare identifiers, row order, and every old x1/x2 cell to its paired parent after round-trip-safe CSV parsing. Report lexical CSV equality separately because serialization may change the last printed digit while preserving the exact parsed floating-point cell. Also report maximum absolute cell differences; no positive cell-change tolerance is permitted for the numeric preservation check.

## Fixed runs and comparison

Use n=2000 for every table and exactly three replicates. Parent seeds: 25101, 25103, 25107. One-shot joint seeds: 25201, 25203, 25207. Conditional-extension seeds: 25301, 25303, 25307. Replicate indices pair the corresponding parent and extension. All nine generator runs are retained. No calibration, seed search, output rejection, or feedback-driven scientific revision is allowed.

Report every per-run endpoint and signed/absolute analytical deviation. For each arm, summarize endpoint means, between-seed sample SDs, and ranges. Report joint-minus-extension differences by prespecified replicate index descriptively; the equal index is bookkeeping and does not make the joint draw a paired observation from the parent. Each extension shares all old cells with its parent. The three draws in an arm are repeated synthetic samples from one model/evidence construction, not new evidence. One fresh scientific author may author all three generators; arms are not independent Agent implementations and cannot identify an algorithm or authorship effect. Do not pool the rows across arms as independent observations.

Monte Carlo variation is interpreted descriptively. Correct compatible joint and conditional constructions target the same distribution but need not produce equal finite tables or equal estimates. All discrepancies, including unfavorable ones, remain visible. Structural failures are recorded separately from numerical Monte Carlo deviations; no closeness threshold converts this exercise into clinical validation. If all runs succeed, the strongest possible conclusion is that this implementation behaves consistently with this chosen, fully specified artificial example.

## Custody and release

Initial source release precedes final source release. The controller must commit initial scientific artifacts before making the final packet available to the author. All scientific artifacts are frozen before their execution. The custodian does not author generator decisions, execute generator candidates, or manufacture target rows. Evaluation code and analytical answer files remain unread by the controller and scientific author until all final output paths and hashes are committed in `final_output_manifest.json` and the controller writes `reveal_authorization.json` binding that manifest hash. The evaluation gate checks those artifacts before reading analytical answers or output tables. The evaluator then preflights table structure and old-cell preservation before opening analytical truth; if fewer than nine valid scheduled tables are available, it records the failure and stops without truth disclosure. The controller likewise retains failed executions and stops instead of forcing a nine-table manifest. All source and evaluator hashes are recorded in `source_commitment.json` before authoring starts.
