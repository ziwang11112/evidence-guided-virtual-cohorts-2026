**Figure 3 | Analysis-specific utility of repeated open-evidence cohort construction.** Four Agent authors independently selected external evidence and constructed complete virtual cohorts for CHNS 2009 (C01, C02; a-c) and ENSANUT 2018 (E01, E02; d-f) requests. All six prespecified analyses and all 36 estimates from 12 final tables of 3,000 virtual rows are shown. Open circles are the three fixed seed draws per law (8101-8103, top to bottom); diamonds and colored lines show their means and ranges. Gray squares show the existing sample estimates. Solid reference bars in a-c are community-cluster CR1-t 95% confidence intervals for the selected, unweighted CHNS analytical sample; dashed bars in d-f are empirical cluster-resampling stability bands from the 399-attempt ENSANUT procedure. These intervals have different interpretations and are not equivalence or acceptance margins. HbA1c effects are in percentage points (pp), with predictor units and adjustment sets shown on each panel. All generated estimates retain the positive reference direction, and both ENSANUT constructions retain BMI-coefficient attenuation after glucose adjustment. Effect magnitudes remain mixed: both CHNS age effects are smaller, and both ENSANUT age effects larger, than their sample references. Seed ranges describe sampling under fixed laws, not uncertainty in evidence or population transfer. The previously exposed references make this developmental replication, not independent clinical validation; all constructions and their additional dispersion and rank-dependence discrepancies remain in the complete evaluation.
