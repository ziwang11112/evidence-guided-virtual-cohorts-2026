9 November 2026. Editorial integration of existing frozen results; no new clinical evaluation.

## Core question and result order

Can a scientific Agent convert incomplete published biomedical evidence and a research request into virtual individual-level data that support the intended analyses?

The first paper foregrounds complete-cohort construction and multivariable analytical utility. Optional observed inputs permit enrichment and completion; those modes remain within the system scope. The final scientific product is the delivered data and its reproducible construction. Neither a multi-Agent architecture nor an improvement after every feedback round is required.

The narrative order is: (1) the practical data gap; (2) the Agent's actual scientific choices; (3) resulting analysis utility; (4) what relationship evidence changes; (5) useful and weak properties across other requests; (6) versioned implementation and validity boundaries.

## What is frozen, and what still needs work

- The research question and application scope can be fixed for this draft.
- Original experimental protocols, generators and references remain frozen. This revision only changes narrative and displays.
- The common scientific procedure is supported by several versioned implementations; [method coverage](METHOD_COVERAGE.md) specifies which capabilities were actually exercised.
- The manuscript may describe a common procedure. It must not describe all historical results as a multi-case test of the newest controller or one known backend model.
- Complete primary endpoints, fixed comparator values and negative prospective results stay in the paper or supplement.
- The latest adaptive controller's reliable cross-request performance is a stronger, uncovered claim. It is optional for the current paper framing; if pursued, it needs its own fixed release and protocol rather than a relabeling of the archive.
