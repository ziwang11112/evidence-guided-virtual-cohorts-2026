# Pre-fit specification: bounded maximum-entropy tail-patchwork model

Status: implementation specification for independent review; no clinical fit or clinical rows have been run by the builder. The family and every allocation below are fixed before release. This is a traditional statistical construction adapted to supplied summaries, not a reproduction of Filshtein/SynthIPD.

## Inputs, output and population

`statistical_fit.fit(summary)` takes the openly supplied R30 summary object and returns a JSON-serializable dictionary with `parameters`, `fit_diagnostics`, and a 30-row `usage_ledger`. Import does not run fitting. No patient matrix, old candidate, old generated cohort, retrieval, learned model, or final evaluation enters fitting. Authorized original task/analysis/source metadata determine interpretation. The coordinator binds the input hash and original packet audit separately.

`generator.generate(observed, spec, seed)` is the isolated harness interface. The whole-cohort harness supplies `observed=None` and `spec['parameters']` contains the fitted dictionary, including n_rows=3000. A row_id-only observed DataFrame and a raw parameters dictionary are also accepted for the pure interface. `generator.generate_rows(parameters,n,seed)` is the equivalent direct interface. It uses NumPy, Pandas and Python math; SciPy is required only by the host fitter. All final rows are iid and are returned once, without rejection, moment forcing, row selection or replacement. Generator output is row_id, integer age, female, bmi, glucose, hba1c. Three final draws are n=3000 with seeds4101–4103; the coordinator enforces this.

The population is the original CHNS2009 linked complete-case analytical population, ages35–74 inclusive, documented male/female category, central-serum glucose and whole-blood HbA1c, BMI derived from released height/weight. No diabetes, treatment or pregnancy exclusion is added. It is not a nationally representative population. Age is years; female is1 for female,0 for male; BMI is kg/m²; glucose is mmol/L; HbA1c is percentage points. This law contains no sampling clusters or measurement-error correction.

## Marginal laws

Female is Bernoulli with p equal to the supplied female mean. Its SD and three quantiles are redundant diagnostics, not four independent fitting degrees of freedom. The other26 summaries are constraints, implemented by the stages below. All30 are retained in the usage ledger and reported after fitting.

Age has a discrete maximum-entropy PMF on integers35,…,74. The five fitted expectations are mean, second raw moment, and three midpoint CDF values: P(A<q)+0.5P(A=q)=p for supplied q10, median and q90 and p=.1,.5,.9. Positive mass at every integer guarantees that the inverse-CDF population quantiles equal those supplied integers. This explicit midpoint convention selects one member of the nonidentified set of PMFs having those quantiles. It is not a claim to recover the real age histogram. The mean and SD are computed directly from the actual integer values, never from a latent normal approximation.

Each positive continuous variable has four bins split at supplied q10, median, q90. Their probabilities are exactly .1,.4,.4,.1. Each bin contains256 equal-width cells, and density is constant in each cell. Lower support is max(.01,q10−4×(median−q10)); upper support is q90+10×SD. These broad finite bounds are explicit modeling assumptions derived from the supplied summaries, not observed minima/maxima or clinical thresholds. They imply positive values and all finite moments. The q90 summary does not identify the upper tail; the bounded tail shape is not claimed as recovered evidence.

Within each bin, the tilted cell probability is proportional to cell width times exp(λ1 f1+λ2 f2), where f1 is the cell's exact standardized mean and f2 is its exact standardized second moment. Each bin reserves a fixed10^−10 share of its probability as uniform density to keep all cells numerically resolvable; the remaining share is the maximum-entropy tilted component. The two multipliers enforce the supplied overall mean and variance of this complete mixture, while fixed bin totals enforce the three quantiles. Thus the entropy component is maximized subject to the moments remaining after the fixed uniform reserve. Stored CDF/edges define the actual inverse-CDF sampler. Marginal means and second moments are evaluated by exact uniform-cell formulas. Supplied sample SDs are treated as population plug-in SDs; they are not corrected to force a finite n=3000 sample SD. Generated sample quantiles use the original evaluator's linear interpolation, which need not equal population quantiles exactly.

All marginal fits use SciPy least_squares, analytic Jacobian, one fixed initial value (age fivezeros; continuous[0,−.5]), maximum300 function evaluations, ftol=xtol=gtol=1e−12. Age features are scaled to (age−54.5)/20 and its square; continuous features are centered/scaled by the supplied mean/SD. Numerical zero-probability or nonmonotone-CDF cells fail rather than being silently removed.

## Ordered latent factorization and dependence

Draw R~Bernoulli(τ), followed by mutually independent X,Y,εA,εB~N(0,1), and an independent sex uniform. R=1 means the common upper-rank regime. For R=r, set c_r=sqrt((1+ρ_r)/2), d_r=sqrt((1−ρ_r)/2), ZG=c_r X+d_r Y and ZH=c_r X−d_r Y. The fixed tail correlation isρ1=.8; the body correlationρ0 and tail massτ are fitted.

For R=0 set UG=(1−τ)Φ(ZG), UH=(1−τ)Φ(ZH). For R=1 set UG=1−τ+τΦ(ZG), UH=1−τ+τΦ(ZH). Both ranks are exactly uniform marginally. Apply the fitted glucose/HbA1c quantiles. Thus membership in the topτ rank fraction is shared exactly, an explicit unmeasured structural assumption. This two-block copula permits a relatively weak association within the body and a stronger contribution from shared upper-tail membership, so it is substantively different from one Gaussian copula. It does not establish clinical latent classes.

Set ZA=aX X+aY Y+sqrt(1−aX²−aY²)εA and ZB=bX X+sqrt(1−bX²)εB. Age=QA(Φ(ZA)) and BMI=QB(Φ(ZB)). These latent scores remain standard normal within both regimes, preserving the fitted age/BMI marginals and independence from regime membership. Age may relate to both glycemic common and difference scores; BMI relates only to the common score. Residual age and BMI noises are independent. The three fitted covariate parameters are encoded as aX=.97tanh(t1), aY=sqrt(.97²−aX²)tanh(t2), bX=.97tanh(t3), with each t in[−2,2]. Age–BMI association is induced by aX bX. Sex is independent of all four variables, hence its population regression coefficient iszero. These unreported associations, sex independence, absence of cluster effects, and age/BMI independence of tail membership are assumptions, not supplied evidence.

## Actual-law expectations and regression projections

The observed covariance matrix uses exact marginal variances. Its off-diagonal entries are expectations under the above factorization, integrated deterministically with normalized Gauss–Hermite nodes. Continuous pairs use a two-dimensional integral. For age/continuous pairs the age contribution is evaluated analytically conditional on the continuous latent score: E[A|Z=z]=35+Σ(k=35…73)Φ((rz−Φ⁻¹(FA(k)))/sqrt(1−r²)). This one-dimensional integral uses the actual integer age. Pairwise latent correlations follow directly from c_r,d_r,aX,aY,bX. Regime contributions are weighted by1−τ andτ; the glucose–HbA1c calculation includes between-regime covariance through centering on the overall means.

Pearson is the actual glucose–HbA1c covariance divided by exact marginal SDs. Spearman is analytical because the glycemic marginals are continuous: 3τ(1−τ)+(1−τ)³×(6/π)asin(ρ0/2)+τ³×(6/π)asin(.8/2). There is no target-space proxy correlation. E1, E2 and E3 are distinct observed-variable OLS projections ΣXX⁻¹ΣXY: E1 is the BMI coefficient in HbA1c~age+female+BMI; E2 is glucose in HbA1c~age+female+glucose; E3 is age in HbA1c~female+BMI+glucose+age. Intercepts are accounted for by centering; no common coefficient vector is substituted across the models.

## Fixed fitting allocation and objective

After the marginal stage, fit(τ,ρ0) using the Pearson and Spearman residuals divided by .02. Bounds areτ∈[.002,.25], ρ0∈[−.85,.95]. Three fixed starts are(.03,.25),(.08,.10),(.15,−.10); each uses least_squares with max_nfev80. Select the smallest squared objective, breaking exact ties lexicographically by parameters. No final rows are drawn.

Holding that fitted glycemic law fixed, fit(t1,t2,t3) against E1,E2,E3. Residual denominators are max(|target coefficient|,.02), max(|target coefficient|,.1), max(|target coefficient|,.005), respectively. Three fixed starts are(.25,0,.25),(.5,.25,.5),(0,−.3,.6); each uses least_squares with max_nfev120 and the same objective/tie selection. Both dependence stages use ftol=xtol=gtol=1e−9, finite-difference relative step1e−4, and128-point quadrature per dimension. Every start logs SciPy nfev and a separate objective-call count that includes finite-difference evaluations. Stage totals also include the one selected-parameter replay in each stage. Marginal stages log objective, Jacobian and probability-evaluation counts. Integration calls and cached-pair requests are counted. Numerical derivatives can require additional residual calls beyond nfev. The wall-clock ceiling is900seconds over the complete fit, checked during residual evaluation and between stages; timeout terminates without generation or another solver.

Once fitting ends, all parameters are fixed. Diagnostics are recomputed at quadrature orders128,256,512. Both adjacent comparisons must differ by no more than .003 Pearson, .001 E1, .002 E2, .0003 E3, in native coefficient units. Spearman and marginal summaries are analytical. These are numerical integration checks, not new target-fitting rounds. No parameter changes, extra starts or refinement are allowed after this check. The final512-order diagnostics and all discrepancies are retained. Quadrature approximates mathematical integrals; reports must not call the numerical answers exact to machine precision.

## Failure handling and reporting

An exception, timeout, invalid/nonpositive covariance, support/CDF failure, any marginal constraint error>1e−6 in its fitted standardized equations, or either failed integration comparison prohibits generation. A valid law with optimization nonconvergence or nonzero coefficient/correlation residuals is retained and may be sampled once with all failures and residuals disclosed. Calibration success is never a condition for retaining a final seed. No additional family, ad hoc target changes, iterative sample matching, seed retries or post-evaluation tuning are permitted.

The three regression interval/SE/cluster-count/rank/condition-number fields are contextual metadata only; they neither change objectives nor become synthetic cluster targets. The reference n and cleaning metadata describe the analytical population, not generated row allocations. Original literature/NGSP source materials remain scientific context; the richer same-population R30 summaries take numerical priority, and no original-source replay is claimed. The source audit must record that role explicitly. None of the30 scalar targets is concealed or held out, so final agreement describes calibration and finite-sample behavior only.

Pre-fit tests are restricted to invented summaries and invented laws; numerical integration nodes do not constitute synthetic clinical patient matrices. The specification, code, tests and independent review are hashed before fitting. Fitted parameters freeze before the three clinical draws. Final aggregate evaluator feedback cannot alter this model.
