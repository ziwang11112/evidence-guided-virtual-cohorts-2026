The Agent received the complete authenticated feedback and chose **retain**. No target table, participant-data access, revised generator or comparative method result was produced.

This completes the bounded engineering integration. It supports running the evidence-to-generation-to-analysis path, not source equivalence, biomedical transport, a revision advantage, or freezing the final paper method.

## What the Agent actually chose

This candidate generates all five biomedical/demographic variables, with no participant input. It uses Gaussian latent dependence and positive lognormal biomarker margins, plus bounded integer age distributions and binary female membership. It does not directly emit rows through language-model text; its Agent-authored program produces seeded rows.

- Jansen's two separate age/sex-adjusted projections were translated into separate pairwise constraints under the declared marginal and age/sex assumptions. They were not inserted as coefficients of one simultaneous structural regression.
- An assumed BMI–glucose latent correlation combines those constraints into a shared residual BMI loading. Independent review verified an actual dependency path into the dormant target model.
- Bao Pearson and MIND Spearman use their respective lognormal-covariance and Gaussian-copula-rank identities. Their distinct source anchors inform a shared dependence rule; its population transfer is explicitly assumed.
- Age and sex are independent of biomarkers by assumption. In particular, this model does not recover an evidence-supported age effect. The age analysis remains unassessed as a scientific use.

## Feedback decision and unresolved science

The Agent's [retained decision](AGENT_DECISION.md) binds the complete feedback packet. It did not change the frozen model or fit a parameter to the one observed source draw. A retain decision is a valid completed feedback path; it supplies no evidence of improvement from revision.

Independent review and the author retained three substantive limitations:

1. Positive lognormal HbA1c percentages can lie below the NGSP/IFCC affine intercept in an extreme lower tail, yielding negative native IFCC values after conversion. Finite moments do not certify physiological support.
2. Jansen glucose clipping enforces the declared observed range but creates a point mass and changes the projection relative to the unclipped identity used during construction. It is not equivalent to conditioning the full joint population on source eligibility.
3. Age/sex independence, the BMI–glucose association, hypothetical target margins and dispersion-based sharing are assumptions. Good source statistics do not identify those assumptions or establish target usefulness.

The reviewed law has finite second moments for all three planned point analyses in each of four declared contexts (12 checks), and independent review found nondegenerate population predictor designs. Those conclusions are conditional on code/declaration correspondence and the stated laws. They are not a universal generator-validity certificate or an approved uncertainty procedure.
