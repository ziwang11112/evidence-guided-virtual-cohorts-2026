# Request B initial scientific decision

The program creates a developmental scenario of3000 complete adult records, ages35-74, for descriptive analysis `hba1c ~ 1 + bmi + glucose + age + female`. No requested coefficient value, sign, p-value or target data was supplied. The equations and parameter choices are authored independently here.

The source base is two gateway-fetched original articles: Bao2010 SHDS II (PMC2871988 v2) and Wang2024 MIND-China (PMC10810428 v1), plus the registered original Jansen2013 Lifelines abstract. These are distinct cohorts; publications are not pooled as repeated independent estimates of one parameter. The PMC gateway could not supply Jansen full text, and no outside or old curated summary was used.

I chose a shifted lognormal BMI and two correlated lognormal glycaemia components. Source subgroup moments show substantial glycaemic heterogeneity and support considering a broad upper tail; a component is not a diagnosis. Age/BMI influence component probability and continuous means; small assumed nonglycaemic age/BMI/sex effects affect HbA1c. The model therefore produces a nonlinear joint distribution, and OLS is a descriptive projection, not a set of implanted source regression estimates. All scientific settings are in model_spec.json, with source/assumption links and explicit moment conversion. No post hoc calibration or rejection of seeds is specified.

Glucose is serum mmol/L. MIND measured fasting serum locally and fasting plasma centrally; Shanghai measured fasting plasma. I do not treat these as identical target assays. The primary scenario adds an explicitly assumed15% meal-affected subset because target fasting adherence is not verified. Its prevalence and size are unidentified; alternative0% and30% contamination would be sensitivity scenarios, not validated models. Component SDs already include individual/measurement variability, so no additional assay noise is silently added.

Target age composition, sex composition, region, diabetes/treatment mix, fasting adherence and joint distribution are not supplied. Uniform age composition and female probability0.55 are design choices. BMI shape, latent prevalence, conditional copula correlations and residual covariate slopes are assumptions rather than reported adjusted estimates. Source values are recorded in their original selection and statistic definitions. Spearman0.529 in MIND is not Pearson0.619 in Shanghai, and neither is equated to conditional copula rho.

No source population can be reconstructed from the target output. Eight semantic checks are consequently declared not_computable, including source quantile/OGTT and full-model R-squared limitations. Generated-only trial diagnostics may identify coding errors or extreme tails, but comparing a target coefficient to these sources would not be valid replay. SDs represent individual dispersion; parameter and transport uncertainty are uncalibrated. Repeated delivery seeds only quantify conditional simulation variation.

Supported use: inspection and execution of the stated regression and investigation of an explicit developmental scenario. Assumption-dependent use: interpreting signs, magnitudes, adjusted associations, tail prevalence and sex/age patterns. Unassessed use: target-population inference, causal effects, diabetes diagnosis, clinical prediction, measurement replacement and publication claims. Successful contracts/execution will not validate any of those uses.

Preparation stops at an executable submission. No generator was locally imported or run. Coordinator freeze and one isolated trial are required before any generated-data diagnostic exists.

## Pre-execution mathematical review

The coordinator correctly identified divergent moments from exp(k*BMI) with unbounded shifted-lognormal BMI. I preserved the pre-review candidate in bundle/draft_history/pre_tail_review and replaced the BMI terms in both glycaemia mean links by positive linear multipliers. At the centered BMI24.5 the original chosen local derivatives are retained; because BMI>16 the lower multiplier limits are0.898 and0.983. Age and sex factors are bounded. Products of finite-moment lognormal factors with linear BMI and bounded component indicators have finite moments. This author decision preceded all generated or target feedback and does not claim evidence-based improvement.
