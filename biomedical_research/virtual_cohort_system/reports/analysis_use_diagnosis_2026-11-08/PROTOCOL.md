# Existing-case scientific-use diagnosis — 8 November 2026

The user's request is to identify which analyses the existing virtual data support, distinguish evidence shortfalls from generation problems, and make targeted improvements. The research product remains analyzable virtual biomedical data, including whole cohorts without observed individual inputs.

This unit is a retrospective diagnosis of exposed cases. It is not an independent validation, a new cohort or a reclassification of the frozen stopping rules. Earlier source packets, programs and results remain unchanged. The preceding exact-moment artificial batch is closed.

## Scope and decisions

Audit the existing iron, CHNS/ENSANUT glycemia, Turkana lipids, HAALSI and HRS/supplied-summary examples. Keep evidence conditions and historical method versions separate. Include unfavorable analyses and all relevant constructors, not only the best model. No new target-data acquisition, clinical fitting or old-target tuning is part of this audit.

For each analysis family, record the research use, actual quantitative results, source definition, directly reported versus derived versus assumed inputs, and the inferential boundary. A direction agreement, source reconstruction or broad interval inclusion alone does not certify general usability. No new post-hoc numerical pass margin is introduced. Existing limits and uncertainty labels remain in force.

Distinguish these causal hypotheses:

1. **Verified implementation defect:** code contradicts its declared mathematical model, an operator is computed incorrectly, or a deterministic source translation is wrong. Require a reproducible counterexample independent of a target result.
2. **Model/measurement approximation:** code implements its stated choice, but that choice does not preserve a relevant source statistic or measurement. Describe the specific approximation; do not call it a software bug.
3. **Insufficient joint evidence:** the needed relationship or dispersion is not supplied. Demonstrate what remains unconstrained where possible.
4. **Population/selection transfer:** a source-supported relation has been transported under an assumption. Target disagreement alone does not isolate this as the cause.
5. **Sampling/reference uncertainty:** distinguish generated finite-draw variation, reference estimation uncertainty and structural uncertainty. Larger synthetic tables reduce only the first.
6. **Mixed or unresolved:** preserve competing explanations when the existing records cannot distinguish them.

These are retrospective reviewer judgments, not a validated automated diagnostic classifier.

## Targeted supplementary work

Three parallel audits may run deterministic mathematical checks of existing source statistics and declared laws. Such checks must bind input hashes, state approximation versus exactness and avoid executing authored generators on the host. No patient-level reads, new generated tables, sampling or target-effect refits are authorized by this diagnostic protocol. A fixed sensitivity calculation is an assumption demonstration, not new biological evidence.

Only a verified source/implementation defect can justify a corrective specification in this unit. Unconstrained parameters may not be set to known target values. Any proposed generation experiment must follow from a specific unresolved diagnosis and state what observation would distinguish competing explanations; it must not reopen the previous artificial batch or present a reused target as new confirmation.

## Deliverables and stop

Deliver separate case audits, source-only mathematical checks where useful, one consolidated scientific-use map, an independent review and a prioritized action plan. The map must distinguish demonstrated reconstruction, partial external-reference agreement, unvalidated uses and actual failures. Stop the unit after these judgments and targeted checks are reviewed. Do not add cases or components to obtain a favorable result.

Public export is an explicit allowlist of original code, metadata and aggregate findings. Individual real/generated records, third-party originals and private reviews remain local. No new paid API, training, HPC or automation is needed.
