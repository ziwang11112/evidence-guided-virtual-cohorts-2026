LA explicitly limits its unshifted lognormal identities after adding covariate trends and a very small-probability latent cap.
- LC was checked independently using the full covariance matrix of age, female and BMI, rather than merely repeating its simplified projection formula. Solving that system reproduces its recorded BMI contrasts, residual RMS values and adjusted residual correlation (approximately 0.1320653) to floating-point precision. Its lipid log variances are positive and its imposed marginal moments are recovered.
