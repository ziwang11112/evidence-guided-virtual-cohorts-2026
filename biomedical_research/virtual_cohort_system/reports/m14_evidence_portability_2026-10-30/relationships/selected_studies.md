No participant overlap with Gallagher2000 or Thai2005 is identified in these three papers, but person-level independence is not verified. Geographical/cohort labels are not proofs of complete disjointness. Publications using the same GOS or Navarra participants must not be counted as new independent cohorts. No cited earlier primary paper was opened as an additional full study.

## Access note for R3

Main XML provides the formula, methods, comparison/validation SEE, pooled Bland-Altman dispersion and prior derivation range. Image-only main tables, figure pixels and Supplementary Table1 were not recovered; claims requiring them are therefore unavailable. In particular, do not transfer the prior derivation BMI extremes to the two current validation/comparison cohorts. Numerical results explicitly present in main text are usable with that locator. No coefficient covariance/standard errors or conditional residual law are reported in the accessed main text.

## Screen not admitted

Jackson et al.2002, DOI[10.1038/sj.ijo.0802006](https://doi.org/10.1038/sj.ijo.0802006), remained metadata/abstract-only. Publisher fulltext/PDF identity redirect was inaccessible. Its reported HERITAGE sample and hydrodensitometry methods were screening information; no coefficient or uncertainty estimate from it is used. Replacement with R2 was declared before full reading and endorsed by root.
