# M14-A: bounded evidence-portability audit before another experiment

30 October 2026. This is the next authorized research unit after M13-B, not a new outcome evaluation or another parameter-search campaign. The user authorized continuing the research autonomously. M13-B is complete and exposed; its outcome cannot again serve as independent confirmation.

## Question

Does accessible external evidence identify a defensible change that can improve a particular analytical use of the virtual data? M13-B found low primary means, excessive BMI slopes, mixed sex-specific spread changes and overly certain generated male-age intervals. These observations motivate investigation; they do not identify the cause or supply permissible new calibration values.

## Four evidence gaps to inspect

1. Conditional BMI/age association: extract the exact population, BMI support, outcome/transform and adjustment set. Does external evidence supply a conditional relation closer to the intended population, rather than only marginal correlation or a group mean?
2. Conditional spread: distinguish reported individual residual dispersion from marginal outcome SD, coefficient SE and prediction error containing other uncertainty. Look for sex/age/BMI-dependent residual evidence; do not infer a residual law from coarse group IQR alone.
3. Measurement mapping: look for actual same-person or otherwise defensible scanner/software comparisons relevant to source and target DXA systems. A generic DXA statement or the NHANES BCA adjustment is not a validated Lunar-to-Hologic equation. Prefer primary methods and validation papers.
4. Uncertainty: identify which source coefficients/covariances, calibration uncertainty or structural alternatives are genuinely available. More residual draws do not quantify uncertain transport or create clinical evidence. Alternative scenarios may be reported as sensitivities when probability weights are unsupported.

## Bounded work and admission

Read the full relevant methods/results/tables of at most six additional primary studies, selected by relevance to these gaps. Official technical measurement documents can support definitions but are not new biological cohorts. Keep actual search queries, exclusions, source copies/locators, reported quantities, uncertainty and source-cohort overlap. Avoid unscreened target-result browsing. Exclude NHANES-derived studies from generator evidence in this body-fat case; literature does not become independent merely because it is another paper.

Prefer two independent source-retrieval/review subtasks: conditional relationships/spread and measurement/uncertainty. Root resolves evidence compatibility and records a single decision. No more general harness modules, model training, HPC jobs or paid external API calls are needed for this audit. No new real-target acquisition, candidate calibration, generation or target analysis is scheduled in M14-A.

## Required output and stopping rule

Produce one compact evidence-to-analysis table with four outcomes: usable reported constraint, defensible derived constraint, unresolved assumption, or incompatible/unavailable. For every proposed change, state which analysis it can affect and which errors it cannot fix. Preserve contradictory evidence and zero useful constraints. Do not treat a different study estimate as proof that it applies to the target.

If there is an actionable quantitative constraint or measurement bridge, write one source-supported revision specification and name the minimum separate reserved target required for later testing; do not execute that later phase automatically within this unit. A future builder may use disclosed development failures, but the new confirmation protocol and candidate must be frozen before accessing a new outcome.

If the six-study audit yields no defensible change, close this body-fat calibration branch and record the missing information. The broader virtual-data idea remains open; a new case requires its own admission. Do not invent target-derived intercepts, slope multipliers, sex SDs or an omnibus utility score to create a favorable continuation.

Finish the evidence audit, independent source check and go/no-go record, then pause this bounded follow-up. The output is a scientific decision and reproducible evidence record; it is not a promise of methodological novelty or publication.
