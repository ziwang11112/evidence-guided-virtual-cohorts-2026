# Source-conditioned execution — proposed manuscript insert

This is a completed developmental capability result for the next manuscript version. It does not revise the earlier clinical comparisons or upgrade their claims.

## Methods

We extended the execution harness to replay study-specific analyses in explicitly declared source populations. A scientific Agent constructs a shared generator and records which mechanisms and parameters transfer to the requested population. Source tasks expose the variables required by the reported adjustment model, while the delivered cohort can have a different output schema. The harness executes the same frozen program and parameter specification in both settings. It separately records the original reported estimator, the implemented source projection, and any assumptions connecting them. Unresolved source definitions remain unavailable in strict semantic checking; a predeclared approximate projection can still be computed and labelled assumption-dependent. Source feedback is disclosed before the author chooses to retain or revise. The final choice is sealed before downstream target-scenario analyses are released.

## Results

In one lipid-generation case with no individual input, the Agent used all eight sex/site-stratified BMI coefficients from Soares2019 to define conditional lipid means. Four source contexts and three fixed seeds produced twelve 8,000-row tables. All 24 numerical projections were computable, retained the reported direction, and matched an independent QR/FWL calculation to within 5.9×10⁻¹⁵. Source weighting and BMI standardization remained unresolved; these projections therefore establish consistency under declared conventions rather than exact source recovery. The author retained the initial model without using its optional revision. After sealing, three 3,000-row whole-cohort outputs supported all nine predeclared analysis calculations and 69 descriptive calculations. Independent recomputation confirmed the nine primary results. No generated records were excluded and no violations of the declared positive-component construction were observed.

The two BMI association requests retained declared partial source support. The adjusted lipid correlation had no direct supporting evidence links and remained unassessed. A frozen analytic sensitivity calculation varied residual component coupling while preserving the mean slopes, producing markedly different fixed-covariate lipid correlations. This separates successful implementation of reported relations from identification of a joint distribution.

## Interpretation

The contribution illustrated here is executable, traceable use of heterogeneous study conditions in an Agent-authored whole-cohort generator. Source-coefficient agreement is largely expected under the selected conditional-mean model; it does not establish superiority over statistical generators. The three target-like draws were not compared with a new clinical reference. Their variation describes sampling from one law, not source or transport uncertainty. No feedback-revision gain is claimed. Clinical applicability remains governed by the earlier independent-reference results and their limitations.
