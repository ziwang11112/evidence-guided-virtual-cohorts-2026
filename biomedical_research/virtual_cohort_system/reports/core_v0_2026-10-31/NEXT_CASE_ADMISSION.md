# Next real-case admission contract — not yet an admitted case

The next case must evaluate the actual scientific use of generated data. This document does not authorize treating the artificial integration as real validation or reopening an exposed reference. Finish the compact method's implementation review first.

**Bounded search:** at most three candidate dataset families and six newly audited original studies. Existing audited sources may be reused with their overlap and applicability reevaluated. Screen metadata, protocols and availability first, before inspecting target biomarker relationships. Check the cross-project exposure registry and dataset aliases/file identities. A changed filename or subgroup of an already inspected cohort does not automatically create a fresh reference. If no candidate qualifies within the limit, close admission with reasons.

**Question before dataset:** choose a concrete scientific use, population and estimand. A suitable case has a small connected set of variables, accessible individual reference measurements, and genuinely external evidence that constrains important conditional or joint structure. Default to generating the full population without individual inputs. Choose enrichment only if the intended use and available source information justify that regime; dynamic extension is optional. Do not require an impressive disease label, large row count, or positive outcome to admit a case.

**Required source table:** original study locator; population/eligibility; recruitment and measurement timing; units/transforms/assays; reported statistic, selection and adjustment set; uncertainty; dependence or cohort overlap; relation to the target; and status as reported, derived or assumed. Separate applicability from statistical precision. A required source-context or measurement operator unsupported by v0 must be implemented and independently checked before target disclosure, or the case must be declared unsupported. An approximate same-table check cannot be described as exact replay of a different source study.

**Required evaluation table:** approximately three analyses defined from the scientific use, with the variable roles, transformations, estimand and interpretation rule fixed in advance. Suitable categories include an adjusted relationship, a subgroup comparison and a scientifically motivated shape/contrast if the data and source evidence can resolve it. These are categories, not a mandate to test a quadratic term. Mark whether each relation is fitted, algebraically implied, assumption-dependent or unassessed. Include at least one analytical use beyond a directly fitted statistic, without pretending that hiding its name removes equivalent evidence.

**Reference uncertainty:** distinguish a finite-cohort estimand from a survey or broader population quantity. Prespecify survey weights or other sampling structure where relevant, exclusions/missingness, transformations and uncertainty calculation. Define practical agreement only with a scientific justification; otherwise report discrepancies and uncertainty without claiming equivalence. The reference's uncertainty cannot erase a clear mismatch.

**Execution accounting before launch:** at most two independent constructions, one source-feedback/retrieval-and-revision opportunity per construction, and three fixed seeds per initial/final stage (up to twelve scheduled target-output labels). Retained outputs must not be counted twice as independent data. The case contract must additionally fix total source executions, optimizer trials if any, query/download budgets and one explicit failure policy. No hidden-target feedback, seed replacement or post-reveal scientific repair is confirmation.

**Decision:** freeze F2 only when access, independence, source applicability, critical operators, reference precision, interpretation rules and budgets are reviewable. Report all eligible outcomes. Use an information-matched generation comparator if an algorithmic superiority claim is planned; no such claim or mandatory direct-analysis Agent comparison is currently imposed. After one full case, decide whether the method supports a limited feasibility paper, needs a substantively different version, or should stop. Do not automatically expand cases to obtain a favorable result.
