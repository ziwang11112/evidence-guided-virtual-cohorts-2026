# Initial scientific decision

Use independent gamma draws within each of the three exact x strata, with group=0. Source A reports arithmetic means (2, 3, 4) u and within-stratum individual SDs (1, 1.5, 2) u. Setting shape k=(mean/SD)^2 gives (4, 4, 4), and scale theta=SD^2/mean gives (0.5, 0.75, 1) u. These are analytic population-moment identities; no empirical moments are forced. Each stratum has exactly 256 rows because the task prescribes a balanced design, not because the source reports prevalence.

The gamma family supplies nonnegative-compatible strictly positive draws and a simple finite-moment distribution. Its continuous support, absence of a point mass at zero, unbounded exponentially decaying tail and conditional independence of separate rows are assumptions. The source does not identify a distribution family, tail shape, within-stratum dependence or zero frequency. No interpolation, extrapolation, group comparison or causal law is supplied or inferred. All scientific parameters and implementation choices have evidence or named-assumption provenance in model_spec.json.

Reported source SDs describe individual variation, not SEs. The source provides no sample sizes or uncertainty for its six moments. Treating them as fixed process moments is a plug-in assumption; variation among generated individuals or seeds does not quantify source, model or transport uncertainty. The prescribed sample SD diagnostic uses ddof=1; the source's original divisor is unknown. The six approximate checks retain the original mean/SD values, exact x and group selections, y unit u and no adjustment. They impose no acceptance thresholds.

This initial candidate supports inspection of synthetic, within-stratum descriptive means and individual spread under the declared gamma law. Tail probabilities, ranks and other distribution-sensitive analyses depend on unverified family assumptions. Pooled x comparisons also depend on the task quotas, and group effects are unassessable because group is constant. The fixture supports no clinical, mechanistic, causal, real-population or publication-readiness claim.
