# Reuse of existing evidence: bounded reporting unit

2 November 2026, America/Chicago. The user requested reasonable, thorough use of the acquired data to demonstrate the method's strengths. This unit produces a descriptive atlas from already completed, frozen aggregate results. It creates no new participant analysis, candidate, generation, source search or target-feedback opportunity. It does not amend the four-cohort prospective campaign or its budget.

**Claim:** literature-informed whole-cohort construction can produce one joint virtual release that supports multiple ordinary scientific analyses without participant inputs.

**Existing evidence to use:** NHANES M17-B, ENSANUT cycle 2 and CHNS F2, with all six unique analytical tables per case. Their 36 initial/final labels represent 18 unique tables; the retained copies will not be plotted twice. They used different historical implementation versions, populations, evidence packets, variables and estimators. The atlas is a version-labelled retrospective synthesis, not a common-protocol confirmation or a pooled accuracy estimate.

**Fixed display scope:** all eight locked M17-B association/contrast endpoints; all three ENSANUT primary questions plus their prespecified Q2-minus-Q3 companion; all three CHNS primary associations. This yields 15 distinct case-specific analytical quantities and 90 generated point estimates from 18 tables. Preserve exact endpoint definitions and original units. Do not select endpoints, constructors or seeds according to favourable agreement. Complete original diagnostics remain linked, including dispersion failures and the ENSANUT secondary glucose analysis.

**Minimal action:** read only four explicit aggregate/inventory JSON files, check their hashes, extract already evaluated estimates, verify initial/final retention from hashes, and generate three publication-style PNG/SVG displays plus a machine-readable table. Never follow embedded patient-file paths. No regressions, resampling, p-values, success thresholds, effect-ratio ranking or synthetic intervals are recomputed.

**Decision informed:** which existing results support the paper's whole-cohort and analysis-reuse claims, where evidence must remain qualified, and which old datasets belong in development or optional-regime appendices. This reporting unit cannot identify a better generator or establish an advantage of evidence interpretation.

**Stop rule:** after complete extraction, independent metadata/numeric review, visual QA and public aggregate/code synchronization. If inputs disagree or endpoints are missing, report the mismatch rather than silently dropping records. No old target is reopened for tuning and no extra experiment is launched to improve the figure.

Reference bands must retain their actual meaning: M17-B HC3 95% intervals; ENSANUT empirical cluster-resampling stability bands, explicitly not confidence intervals; CHNS community-clustered CR1/t 95% intervals. Seed variation is model-conditional Monte Carlo variation. No band is a clinical equivalence region or known population truth.
