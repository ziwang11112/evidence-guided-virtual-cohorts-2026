# v0.11 visual redesign

4 November 2026. The user requested a more developed Agent-framework overview, less repetitive/dispersed results and removal of green. This unit changes the graphical explanation and presentation, not the research outcomes.

## Design decisions

The overview now separates research context, Agent scientific work and the virtual-data/analysis product. Original vector document and table symbols clarify the inputs and outputs. A model-dependence sketch and the actual projection identity explain what the Agent must decide. Routed connectors stay in gutters. Source/reference information and optional revision retain their different roles.

Results use different, complementary encodings. Figure 2 connects native-unit effects to six endpoint-specific evidence changes, grouped by cohort. Figure 3 aligns all analyses of the same data product in a matrix, printing native means while displaying all seed statistics. Figure 4 joins the actual visit schedule to the two longitudinal interaction terms. Figure 5 aligns the seven analysis effects and locates the two pre-existing sensitivity scenarios alongside them. This layout gives each experiment a visual purpose rather than repeating one small-panel template.

The palette is navy `#182647`, blue `#4169B1`, violet `#8264B5`, coral `#D97762` and slate `#778394`, with restrained pale backgrounds. E0/L and E1/R keep consistent violet/blue encoding. The overview uses the same palette for scientific roles. There is no green/teal. Native numbers remain in labels/bindings, and all normalized coordinates have explicit definitions.

## Source and result integrity

The supplied reference image was viewed as an aesthetic example. No artwork, curve or method component was reused. There is no PCSD, GRPO, reward, new training objective or invented iterative gain in our figure.

The main scientific body, all three tables and all fifteen references are identical to v0.10. Only the version note, five image links and their captions changed. Figure 2 adds arithmetic summaries of frozen discrepancies; Figures 3/5 use declared display transformations. These are graphical transformations of existing results, not new clinical experiments. Negative findings remain visible. Older figures, drafts and frozen results are preserved.

The supplied-data reconstruction, exposed developmental references and published-model reconstruction remain separate settings. Gray intervals in different figures have their original meanings. No common graphic scale establishes equivalence or a cross-study quality ranking.

## Production

The five figures are provided as vector SVG/PDF and preview PNG. Figure 1 also embeds editable draw.io XML in its exports. The manuscript is rebuilt as a venue-neutral two-column reading PDF with the new figures. Figure 5 is placed at 92% of text width to keep the final paragraph together; its standalone vector remains 7 inches wide. All actual final figures and the compiled pages receive independent inspection.

This unit requires no patient-table access, new data acquisition, model training or scientific rerun. It produces a revised manuscript and visual package for discussion, not an official conference template or submission-ready paper.
