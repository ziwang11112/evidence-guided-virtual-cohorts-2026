## Concrete numerical operation

The observed main-study A01 construction interpreted separately age/sex-adjusted Jansen BMI and glucose projections, cB=0.13 and cG=2.29, in IFCC response units. It did not copy both into one fully adjusted equation. Under residual SDs sB=4.07611 and sG=0.49694 and an explicitly assumed correlation r=0.25, it solved:

`cB = thetaB + thetaG*r*sG/sB`

`cG = thetaG + thetaB*r*sB/sG`

This yields thetaB=0.0642173 and thetaG=2.1583154 in IFCC response units. Multiplication by 0.09148 converts response slopes to HbA1c percentage-point units. The graph may use a short inset (“separate adjusted projections → stated covariance assumption → joint parameters”) with equations in the caption/manuscript when visual space is limited. The correlation is assumed, the solved coefficients are derived, and the published projections are reported. Unit conversion supplies no population-transport evidence.
