## What this batch establishes

The defensible contribution is repeated execution of an Agent evidence-to-whole-cohort workflow: four fresh-context scientific authors selected accessible published originals under the same acquisition policy, translated different evidence into explicit joint models, received source/generated feedback, and delivered all twelve scheduled 3,000-row final tables. The sixteen charged executions comprise four initial trials and twelve final executions. All authors retained their initial model, with zero feedback revisions. All final table hashes differ. This supports operational repeatability across two existing task contracts and demonstrates that the workflow can produce analyzable whole cohorts with useful selected scientific relationships.

The acquisition is open in query and study choice within a fixed Europe PMC/PMC gateway and small budget, not an unrestricted or exhaustive literature review. Sixteen author-source selections contain twelve distinct PMC identifiers. Some authors selected overlapping studies; fresh author contexts do not imply independent empirical evidence. C01 and C02 used different four-source sets, whereas E01 and E02 both used Pani and Huang.

Scientific usefulness is partial and analysis-specific. Every scheduled coefficient has the same positive direction as its existing reference: 36 slot-level comparisons, arising from four authors, three analyses per author and three seeds. These are not 36 independent scientific conclusions, nor two new clinical replications. There are six endpoint definitions within two related biomedical requests, four constructed laws, and three scheduled outputs per fixed law. Distinct table hashes do not certify independent draws, and this review does not estimate uncertainty over scientific structures or over future Agents.

The references were historically exposed developmental samples. This batch made no new clinical reads or fits. Authors' fresh task contexts and final-choice closure before the final comparisons strengthen the local workflow trace; they do not transform the project into a prospectively hidden clinical validation. Likewise, approximate comparisons on the generated target population are not replay of source populations.

## Scientifically useful patterns and persistent discrepancies

**Different complete-cohort laws can preserve selected relationships, with clear trade-offs.** In CHNS, C02 has a smaller mean absolute reference difference for BMI and adjusted age; C01 has a smaller difference for glucose. C01's glucose mean coefficient is 0.3775 versus 0.4021, whereas C02's is 0.4924. C02's BMI coefficient is 0.0534 versus 0.0549, whereas C01's is 0.0399. This is evidence for endpoint-specific usefulness and author/model variation; it supplies no single winning law. The larger CHNS scientific differences between authors on these endpoints are not resolved by generating three samples from each already chosen law.

**Adjusted age is persistently displaced in opposite directions across the two tasks.** Both CHNS authors underestimate the existing age point estimate, while both ENSANUT authors overestimate theirs. Each displacement persists across all three final seeds. E01 and E02 explicitly constructed an age implication of 0.100 pp/decade from transported evidence; their near-agreement is therefore understandable from shared evidence and modelling decisions. It is not two independent empirical discoveries of the Mexican relationship. The pattern prioritizes examination of conditioning, eligibility and transport assumptions rather than merely adding random seeds. This is an interpretation of the recorded laws and results, not a causal attribution established by an experiment.

**The change in BMI association after glucose adjustment is recovered qualitatively in ENSANUT.** Q2 exceeds Q3 in all six ENSANUT final outputs. The mean Q2–Q3 difference is 0.016562 for E01 and 0.024074 for E02, versus 0.027005 in the existing reference, in pp per 5 kg/m². E01 is numerically closer on Q2, and E02 is closer on Q3 and on the attenuation contrast. This is useful for demonstrating that a complete joint model can support more than one conditioning set. It is not evidence that glucose causally mediates a BMI effect: the models impose particular dependencies and the analyses are associational projections.

**Agreement in a moment or coefficient does not recover the full joint distribution.** C01's mean glucose–HbA1c Pearson correlation is 0.6662 versus 0.6610, but its Spearman correlation is 0.6351 versus 0.3615. C02's corresponding correlations are 0.8375 and 0.7686. Thus even C01's useful Pearson agreement leaves a large rank-dependence discrepancy. C02's HbA1c SD is numerically close to the reference (0.9435 versus 0.9536 pp), yet its lower and upper deciles are displaced (4.4932 and 6.8111 versus 5.0 and 6.3). Correct aggregate scale does not establish correct tail shape.

**Both ENSANUT laws underrepresent unexplained and total HbA1c variability.** Their Q1 residual RMS values are 0.2950 and 0.3238 versus 0.4885 pp. Their HbA1c SDs are 0.3625 and 0.4057 versus 0.5274 pp. Both also have higher mean HbA1c (5.4203 and 5.4724 versus 5.3141 pp). These persistent discrepancies matter to scientific uses involving residual heterogeneity, individual prediction, extreme values, power or precision. Selected regression-sign recovery does not validate those uses. Fixed ENSANUT age/sex composition and the identical age summaries across the two authors also caution against presenting design-imposed demographic agreement as independently learned scientific fidelity.

**Marginal fidelity varies alongside projection fidelity.** C01's CHNS BMI mean is 23.9150 versus 23.6999 kg/m², while C02's is 26.2104. Conversely, C02's HbA1c SD is closer than C01's 0.6949. ENSANUT E01's BMI SD is 3.7686 versus 5.5211 kg/m²; E02's is 4.9774. These are illustrative diagnostics, not a selected replacement endpoint set. All declared diagnostics remain in the aggregate results and should be available beside the six main outcomes.

## What the source reviews add to the scientific interpretation

The reviews support traceability and mathematical coherence: quoted quantities were checked against originals, transformations distinguished from reported values, candidate moment algebra examined, finite moments supported, and implementation caveats documented. This is part of the core scientific contribution because the whole-cohort law must combine incomplete and incompatible evidence, rather than merely copy a single table of coefficients.

The same reviews expose the central unresolved empirical issue: published evidence rarely identifies the requested joint law. C01 uses ecological age mappings, assumed BMI dependencies and a newly detected diabetes distribution as a surrogate for another clinical component. C02 transfers coefficients from a richer adjustment set and assumes a zero direct BMI term. E01 transfers marginal moments to residual quantities, assigns signs to R-squared-derived magnitudes, and combines evidence with different age coding and eligibility. E02 uses an ecological HGI guide and an untruncated surrogate to inform a glucose-restricted target law. These are documented assumptions, not source mistakes that the present output comparison automatically repairs.

The reviewers found and preserved actual source-reporting limitations and small transcription/context corrections without supplying target-directed scientific parameters. Passing review therefore means the specified construction is traceable and coherent within its stated assumptions; it does not mean those assumptions are identified, transported correctly, or clinically validated. The source feedback was correspondingly limited: final-decision reviews retain 7/9/6/7 approximate comparisons and 13/4/14/8 unavailable checks for C01/C02/E01/E02. Unavailable operators cannot be silently counted as passed checks.

## A meaningful next bounded question

The next useful scientific question is: **with the same retrieved evidence held fixed, how sensitive are the requested regression relationships and residual variability to the explicitly unsupported transport and dependence assumptions?** This follows directly from persistent age displacement, compressed ENSANUT residual variation and the Pearson–Spearman discrepancy. More draws of the same laws would principally refine conditional sampling behavior and would not identify these assumptions.

A suitable bounded follow-up, if separately authorized, would predeclare a small assumption-sensitivity analysis on copies of the existing laws, with a fixed number of alternatives per task chosen from source-conditioning/eligibility limitations before inspecting alternative outputs. Preserve all six endpoint definitions and all diagnostic outputs; report native-unit movement and trade-offs, retain failures, and do not select a revised winner against the exposed reference. This would answer robustness to modelling assumptions. It would not establish search benefit, feedback benefit or fresh clinical validity. No follow-up is initiated or required to close this batch. A later claim of generalization beyond exposed development cases would require a separately frozen, previously unexposed reference evaluation.

## Claims to avoid

- Do not convert 36 positive slot-level comparisons, 12 tables, 36,000 generated rows, four authors or twelve unique selected papers into independent clinical evidence counts.
- Do not claim accurate reproduction of both cohorts, equivalence, population truth or general usefulness from signs, one close coefficient, interval inclusion or a pooled cross-unit score.
- Do not call the target-row source feedback source-population replay, or treat unsupported feedback operators as successful checks.
- Do not claim benefit from autonomous retrieval, feedback or revision: there is no randomized information contrast here and all four authors retained their initial law.
- Do not equate model-implication checks with independent validation, or interpret BMI attenuation as causal mediation.
- Do not claim comprehensive uncertainty from three seeds, infer independence from unique table hashes, or treat overlap in sources and fixed demographic construction as independent scientific convergence.
- Do not claim superiority over a bespoke generator or a general Agent. Neither is required by the contribution or established by this batch.
- Do not claim end-to-end cost savings from short execution-adapter times; those exclude scientific authoring and literature acquisition, and the records do not provide full process/Agent costs.

Suggested main result wording: “Under a shared bounded retrieval and review policy, four fresh-context Agents independently authored executable whole-cohort models for two existing tasks and completed all twelve scheduled final outputs. All prespecified coefficient directions agreed with the exposed sample references, while coefficient magnitudes, rank dependence and residual variability showed persistent, task-specific discrepancies. These developmental results support repeated evidence-to-cohort construction and selected analytical usefulness, with scientific transport assumptions remaining a central limitation.”
