# E01 initial candidate

The candidate implements the phase1 proposed bounded-BMI, conditional-beta glucose and conditional-gamma HbA1c law, with unchanged scientific values. It produces exactly375 individuals in each of eight task age/sex cells. Within-cell age is uniform integer; BMI is independent of age/sex by assumption. All scientific settings used at runtime reside in model_spec.json. The generator needs no auxiliary file, network or source access.

The source review permitted implementation with a context correction: Finnish weighted R-squared and predictive margins adjust age as **30–44 versus45–64**, not continuous years. Candidate evidence and assumptions now state this and label continuous-age transfer. The weighted source numbers are unavailable checks. The Taiwanese reported male coefficient is also unavailable because feedback cannot implement its conversion to the task female variable. Six Kerman descriptive/Pearson recipes retain original units and statistics and are explicitly approximate: they use target rows within the reported source age18–75 and glucose<=126 ranges, not a recreated source population or a residual-correlation check.

Cross-population transfer remains substantial. No Mexican source was admitted. NHANES used plasma glucose and survey weights; Taiwan did not require universal fasting or FPG<126; Finland excluded HbA1c>=6.5 and ages outside30–64 and allowed4–17h fasting; Kerman included30 known-diabetes cases among525 low-FBS participants, with unspecified exact fasting duration, specimen matrix and calibration. The original studies do not identify the combined residual covariance matrix. Positive signs from R-squared, raw-to-residual Pearson transfer, marginal-to-conditional SD transfer, normal-quantile scale, omitted interactions and distribution/tail choices are assumptions. Source SE/CI is never treated as individual SD; residual variation is not uncertainty about evidence, structure or transport.

The source-centered means and assumed residual covariance imply population projections Q1=.1000 per10years, Q2=.09356887 per5kg/m² and Q3=.07628033 per5kg/m². These are algebraic consequences of the proposed law, not target findings or an optimization goal. Their contrast does not establish mediation. Source feedback cannot validate target causal effects, clinical classifications, individual recovery, measurement savings or publication readiness.

Bounded age/BMI/glucose and bounded positive gamma shape/scale yield finite polynomial moments of every fixed order, including second/fourth cross-moments. Phase1 constant-only checks confirmed positive beta/gamma parameters throughout the predictor rectangle.
