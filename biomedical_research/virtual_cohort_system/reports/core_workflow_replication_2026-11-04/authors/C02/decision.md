# C02 initial candidate

Implemented the source-reviewed proposal without changing its scientific law or parameter values. The program draws age, BMI and glucose from a specified Gaussian copula; age is discrete uniform 35–74, BMI lognormal and glucose shifted lognormal. Female is independent Bernoulli. HbA1c has the reviewed affine conditional mean in glucose, age and female, a structural zero direct BMI term, and independent bounded uniform residual. All scientific settings and derivations are in `model_spec.json`; generator operation needs only the specification, NumPy, pandas and math. It reads no auxiliary files or network resources.

The P1 Pinggu sample retained known diabetes, motivating its use for pooled BMI/glucose/HbA1c arithmetic moments and sex proportion. P2 supplies the Chinese-reference glucose coefficient, common age/sex coefficients, and unadjusted Pearson associations with age and **log BMI**. P3 Changchun and P4 Hoorn remain context rather than fitted evidence. No source effect is averaged or assigned an LLM confidence weight. Distinct study settings are documented, but unreported participant overlap cannot be disproved.

The Singapore paper's participant-flow inconsistency is retained: 5,157 minus stated exclusions of 325, 932 and 2 equals 3,898, whereas reported analytical N and ethnic counts total 3,895. The candidate keeps reported N=3,895, flags the unexplained difference of three, and invents no additional exclusion. This note does not alter any parameter.

P2's original regressions include waist, lipid measures, log HOMA-IR, ethnicity and glucose interactions. Transporting their coefficients to the task's reduced model is an explicit omitted-covariate assumption; it does not make the coefficients source-equivalent estimates of E1–E3. The zero direct BMI term is a structural choice allocating the transported marginal association through glucose, not a reported null or causal mediation. P2 excludes known diabetes while the task does not. Identity mapping of fasting plasma glucose to central-serum glucose and fasting comparability are also unverified assumptions.

Pooled source moments derive from rounded inputs; additional digits are arithmetic only. Uniform target age composition, predictor/sex independence, zero age–BMI dependence, positive glucose shift, distribution tails and a single homoscedastic conditional HbA1c law are unsupported assumptions. P3 sex differences specifically warn against treating predictor/sex independence as a demonstrated null. Source sampling uncertainty, transport/model uncertainty and individual residual variation are separate; source coefficient SEs are never individual SDs.

The retained deterministic algebra gives a positive-definite latent matrix (determinant about 0.891777405), positive residual variance 0.260315284 and conservative HbA1c lower bound 2.903034%. Thus the law requires no clinical clipping. Bounded age/sex/residual and finite-order lognormal moments give finite second/fourth mixed moments for OLS. Population designs are nonsingular; finite-draw rank and numerical validity await execution. Analytic E1/E2/E3 implications are consequences of model assumptions, not independent target findings or selection criteria.

The feedback recipe contains nine approximate checks: pooled BMI/glucose/HbA1c means and SDs, pooled female proportion, and the source Pearson age–HbA1c and glucose–HbA1c associations. The latter glucose correlation is context only, explicitly not fitted. All checks concern the actual task-design simulation; no source-population reconstruction, weighting or measurement conversion is claimed. Four checks are unavailable: log-BMI Pearson requires an unsupported transform, and all three original adjusted coefficients require absent covariates/transforms. They are not replaced with raw BMI or reduced regressions. P4 Spearman values remain context and are never relabelled Pearson.

Source acquisition ended after six of eight query credits and all four original reads. No new source was acquired during implementation. No participant rows, generator import, local generator execution, freeze, hidden-target analysis or numerical source-feedback calculation was performed. `checks.json` binds the actual source review and retained analytic calculation. Contract validation and semantic preflight are declaration-only; their actual reports are retained by the author outside this candidate. This initial implementation is ready for controller static review, then controller freeze/execution if accepted.
