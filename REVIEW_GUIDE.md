# Reviewer guide to manuscript v0.18

The manuscript asks whether published, study-specific statistics can support the construction of complete virtual records for a biomedical analysis, including when the researcher supplies no individual observations. This guide follows the three research questions in the paper. The [v0.18 manuscript release](https://github.com/ziwang11112/evidence-guided-virtual-cohorts-2026/commit/0dd16840fd7ab175d3f5aa61f125cefbed62908b) fixes the scientific content. Open a file using the commit selected for review to keep the guide and its links on one snapshot.

### Start with the paper and a generator

1. Read the [seven-page main paper](biomedical_research/virtual_cohort_system/manuscript/conference_draft_v18_2026-11-06/paper.pdf) or the [searchable Markdown with supplements](biomedical_research/virtual_cohort_system/manuscript/DRAFT_v0_18.md). Figure 1 follows a published statistic into a joint model. Figures 2 and 3 share page 5 and Figure 4 is on page 6.
2. Follow a [published-regression derivation](biomedical_research/virtual_cohort_system/manuscript/SUPPLEMENT_SOURCE_AND_ASSUMPTIONS_v05.md). It identifies a reported pair of projections, the assumed residual correlation and the numerical coefficients that enter the equation.
3. Inspect the independent iron-status constructions. Both received the same [source packet](biomedical_research/virtual_cohort_system/reports/iron_breadth_2026-11-05/admission/SOURCE_PACKET.md). Read the choices and the actual sampling programs for [A: model](biomedical_research/virtual_cohort_system/reports/iron_breadth_2026-11-05/candidates/A/final/model.md), [A: code](biomedical_research/virtual_cohort_system/reports/iron_breadth_2026-11-05/candidates/A/final/generator.py), [B: model](biomedical_research/virtual_cohort_system/reports/iron_breadth_2026-11-05/candidates/B/final/model.md) and [B: code](biomedical_research/virtual_cohort_system/reports/iron_breadth_2026-11-05/candidates/B/final/generator.py). The source paper's counts constrained the threshold association. How dependence would transfer to US women remained a modeling choice.

### Questions for a review

Which scientific analyses are supported by the source evidence, which depend on an unmeasured joint relationship, and where does independent target evidence change that judgment? The manuscript reports errors in native units and distinguishes reference sampling uncertainty from variation across draws of a frozen model. The earlier workflow and the current workflow used different protocols. A prospective transport claim for the later workflow, causal benefit from revision and measured human time savings each require additional evidence.
