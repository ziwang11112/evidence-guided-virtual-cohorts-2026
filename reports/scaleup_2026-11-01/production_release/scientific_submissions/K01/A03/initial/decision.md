# A03 initial scientific construction

Route: **inline_deterministic**. This is one predeclared whole-cohort scenario. Source-based distribution parameters are derived by finite algebra inside the generator before random generation. No optimizer, source fitting job, trial sample, target inspection, or sample-dependent rescaling is used.

The three contexts share a conditional Gaussian latent construction. Age and BMI have distinct marginal families, and glucose is positive with a lognormal baseline. Age and BMI dependence is context-specific. Glucose loads on the age latent and the independent BMI residual, with different male/female BMI loadings. These choices make the requested age, BMI, and sex-by-BMI projections computable but entirely assumption-dependent. They are not extracted effects, causal effects, or re-expressions of composite-diabetes odds ratios.

For MIND-China, Table 1 age 69.5 (4.2), BMI 25 (3.6), glucose 5.2 (1.4), and female count ratio 2049/3547 define marginal anchors. Individual SDs remain total participant dispersion: sex mean separation is explicitly allocated from total variance before deriving within-sex parameters. Age uses beta distributions on 61–80; BMI and baseline glucose use lognormals. Unreported sex differences, distribution shapes, correlations and interactions are assumptions. The glucose baseline is the recorded source serum distribution; its zero additional device-noise setting does not assert a noiseless biological assay. Source selection, imputation, unreported weights/SD conventions, and the 2018 demographics/BMI versus 2019 laboratory timing prevent strict source reproduction claims. No age increment is added.
