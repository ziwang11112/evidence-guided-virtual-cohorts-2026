# Initial scientific construction

Route: **inline_deterministic**. Fixed scientific assumptions and reported source anchors are committed in the specification. The generator derives each source HDL log-scale marginal dispersion by one normal inverse-CDF equation, then computes finite weighted log moments before making any random draw. There is no optimizer, generated-sample calibration, host parameter fitting, or trial execution.

The construction is a Gaussian copula with positive lognormal BMI, measured total cholesterol (TC), and measured HDL. Each source stratum uses its own reported geometric means. Under the explicit lognormal and unweighted-description assumptions, `mu_H = log(G_H)` and `sigma_H = (log(c) - log(G_H))/Phi_inverse(p)` use the strict sex-specific low-HDL threshold and percentage divided by 100. This is a model-implied marginal log SD, not an observed SD, a residual SD, or a CI-derived SD. BMI and TC dispersions are unreported and explicitly assumed. Finite-sample source summaries will fluctuate rather than being forced to equal publication values.

Four independent normal innovations define age rank, BMI residual variation, shared lipid variation, and remaining TC variation. BMI has a small positive age loading; HDL has a small positive age loading and negative loading on the BMI innovation; TC has positive age and BMI-innovation loadings and shares an HDL innovation. These are independent scientific scenario choices, including their signs and magnitudes. No Table 3 coefficient is inserted, reinterpreted, inverted, or rescaled. Conditional innovation independence and the absence of extra interactions are simplifying assumptions, not deductions from missing evidence. Raw-scale regressions and residual correlation are consequences of this nonlinear joint distribution; no requested regression contrast or residual correlation is imposed.

Age is a beta distribution on the assumed support 18-95 years. This upper endpoint is a generative support assumption, not a verified maximum or an eligibility exclusion applied to records. Shapes vary across source scenarios to represent broadly different age structures; they are not fitted to the unresolved source quantiles. Target F/M proportions are assumed equal, target age shapes are assumed equal across these recorded categories, and the source male-to-M/female-to-F convention is explicitly extended to the target as an unverified assumption.

For the target, equal weights on the three same-code source reference profiles define a single Gaussian approximation to their first two marginal log moments. This is a neutral reference-profile pooling scenario; it does not assert that the target comprises Malawi rural, Malawi urban, or Uganda subpopulations, or that these labels are target lifestyle groups. Each source weight is 1 before normalization; reported sample sizes and precision do not determine weights. Source profiles from the same paper and the overlapping MEIRU cohort are not independent replications. Zero log-location transport shifts and small additional independent lipid log errors (TC 0.05; HDL 0.08) are target assumptions. They neither estimate a CardioChek assay bridge nor establish fasting equivalence. Extra log errors preserve geometric means but alter arithmetic moments and dependence.

The direct source scenarios represent distributions conditional on the reported adult, complete-lipid, sex-observed, pregnancy/HIV/diabetes exclusions; the absent health variables are not fabricated. Their finer sampling and selection mechanisms remain unknown. Source selection is not applied to the target, which has no such additional exclusions. The descriptor strings retain Malawi Beckman Coulter laboratory measurements, qualified fasting attempts, Uganda Cobas laboratory measurements and the reported fasted-sample qualification. The target is measured venous-EDTA CardioChek Plus with fasting unresolved. All generation uses mmol/L; no unit conversion is treated as measurement harmonization.

Proposed source descriptive proxies are equal-weight geometric means and the original strict threshold percentages. They are explicitly assumption-defined comparisons, with no claimed approval or strict match to an unknown published weighting estimator. Confidence intervals retain their estimation-uncertainty role; they supply no individual dispersions, fit weights, or acceptance bands. Rounded/collapsed intervals and the anomalous Malawi urban women's HDL interval remain unresolved. Published Table 1/Table 2 denominators are neither harmonized nor used as row counts. Source age quantiles and pooled regressions stay contextual. Non-HDL geometric means remain evidence only: no subtraction of geometric means, extra output column, or derived diagnostic is introduced.

The model does not force TC to exceed HDL: these are separately modeled measured values, with occasional ordering reversals possible under the assumed tails. It therefore does not support physiological component decomposition or a non-HDL positive-domain claim. The unbounded lognormal tails, bounded age support, conditional dependence, pooling, sex mapping, selection transfer, and assay offsets are substantial uncertainties. Seed variation represents individual scenario variation only; it does not propagate evidence, structural, or transport uncertainty. Source numerical agreement would not validate any target analysis or causal effect.

The same specification and generator serve six source scenarios and the target, with only `active_context` changed mechanically. Every context requests 5,000 complete rows in the task order. This candidate supports inspection of the three prespecified descriptive analyses conditional on the assumptions, not a claim of target validity, clinical decision utility, national representativeness, measurement replacement, or publication readiness. Only static parsing is authorized at this stage; execution and source diagnostics await the independent controller schedule.
