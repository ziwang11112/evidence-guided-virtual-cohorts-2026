# K02/A01 initial scientific construction

Route: **inline_deterministic**. All selected source point estimates and scientific assumptions are frozen in `model_spec.json`. Only finite logarithm, inverse-normal-CDF, weighted-average and square-root equations are evaluated inside the isolated generator before any randomness. No host fit, optimization, sample calibration or generator trial has been performed.

The model generates a complete 5,000-person cohort for each of six source contexts and the target. It uses a Gaussian factor joint structure with positive lognormal BMI, total cholesterol (TC) and HDL marginals and a bounded logistic-normal adult age distribution. The shared factors create age/BMI dependence, a modest inverse BMI-specific HDL association, a positive BMI-specific TC association and positive residual lipid dependence. These relationships and all factor magnitudes are assumptions; missing joint evidence was not interpreted as independence. The requested raw-scale OLS contrasts and Pearson residual correlation are descriptive consequences of the full generated cohort, not fitted inputs or causal effects.

For each source subgroup, reported BMI, TC and HDL geometric means set log-locations. Under the declared lognormal marginal family, HDL log-SD is derived as `sigma_H = [log(c)-log(G_H)] / Phi_inverse(p/100)`, using the original strict source low-HDL threshold (1.03 mmol/L for source men and 1.29 for source women) and reported percentage. This is a model-derived marginal dispersion; the conditional residual scale additionally depends on the factor loadings. BMI and TC log-SDs are fixed assumptions. No estimation CI is converted into individual/residual variation. The near-50% rural-men prevalence makes its HDL inversion sensitive to rounding; its derived scale is not an independently observed variance.

The proposed source diagnostics are explicitly unweighted descriptive proxies for geometric means and low-HDL percentages. Published weights and row-specific denominators remain unknown, and proxy approval is reserved for independent review. Table-specific count disagreements are preserved in copied context descriptors. The Malawi urban-women anomalous HDL interval and rounded/collapsed intervals remain in the fixed evidence; none becomes an acceptance band, SD, exact-equality tolerance or precision weight. Non-HDL geometric means are retained as evidence but are not calibrated or checked. Age quartiles and all pooled Table 3 coefficients remain context only; no coefficient, transform, adjustment set or quantile convention is substituted into the generator.

The target is one explicit transport scenario: P(F)=0.5 and equal weights across the three external source anchors within each source-coded sex, averaging log-locations and derived HDL log-SDs. This is not an estimate of target demographic or lifestyle mixture, and source strata are not interpreted as target lifestyle categories. Source counts do not supply mixture or precision weights. These strata come from one paper and overlapping cohort programs; they are not independent confirmations. Source men-to-M/women-to-F encoding is internal, while carrying that mapping into target literal codes is an unverified assumption because the target biological-label legend was not established.

Population and measurement descriptors are copied mechanically. Source templates omit n_rows, so the released task's 5,000 applies to all source contexts. Source health-selection rules are represented as conditions of the marginal source scenarios, not reconstructed selection mechanisms. Target rows receive no source health exclusion. Transport from those selected samples remains vulnerable to selection bias. The shared age law is illustrative and does not reconstruct the different published source age distributions; its upper support of 100 is a modeling choice, not an eligibility filter.

Source assays and fasting qualifications differ from the target CardioChek venous-EDTA protocol with unknown fasting. The target uses a zero net log-location offset plus independent additional row-level log-error SDs of 0.06 for TC and 0.08 for HDL, explicitly assumed rather than estimated assay performance. Canonical mmol/L units do not establish measurement equivalence. The target assay-offset and extra-scatter choices need external validation; they do not model epistemic transport uncertainty.

Measured TC and HDL have no deterministic ordering constraint. A TC<HDL realization is retained; no row rejection, floor, clipping or derived non-HDL correction changes the cohort. Positive marginals and complete output are construction requirements, while the absence of a compositional lipid model limits mechanistic interpretation.

This single scenario supports inspection of coherent cohort generation and the prespecified descriptive analyses under its assumptions. Marginal source agreement cannot validate unreported slopes, residual correlation or target transport. Random seeds represent individual/model sampling variation, not evidence uncertainty, and no target accuracy, causal validity, measurement savings or publication readiness is claimed. Only Python/JSON syntax and signature inspection are permitted before freeze; source numerical findings await the fixed independent execution schedule.
