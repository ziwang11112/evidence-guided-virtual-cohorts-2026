# A04 initial scientific construction

Route: **inline_deterministic**. The chosen scientific structure and coefficients are fixed. Finite algebraic log-moment calculations and a normal inverse CDF derive marginal parameters inside the generator before any random draw. There is no fitting, optimizer, trial table, generated-sample adjustment, rejection sampling or target feedback.

The cohort follows one Gaussian latent-factor model in every context. A bounded logistic-normal age law supplies adult ages. BMI, measured total cholesterol and measured HDL are positive lognormal variables, with nonzero age and BMI-related dependence and a shared lipid innovation. These loadings are explicit biological scenario assumptions, not recovered regression coefficients. BMI and total-cholesterol marginal log SDs are assumed. HDL marginal log SD is derived from the subgroup geometric mean and strict low-HDL proportion under a lognormal assumption: sigma = [log(threshold) - log(G)] / Phi_inverse(p). The percent is converted to a fraction. This identifies a family-specific marginal dispersion, not an observed or conditional residual SD. The joint factor construction preserves that marginal dispersion.

Each source context takes its own reported BMI, TC and HDL geometric means and low-HDL percentage. The six strata come from one Greiner paper and two African cohorts; they are overlapping evidence rather than six independent replications. Source male/M and female/F are internal encoding conventions. Each source scenario represents the stated adult, complete-lipid, nonpregnant, HIV-negative and nondiabetic selected population directly, without pretending to reconstruct unavailable health indicators. Source weights, exact row denominators, covariate missingness and assay details remain incompletely known. All source geometric-mean and prevalence comparisons are proposed equal-weight descriptive proxies, not verified reproductions of the source estimator.

For the target, coded F and M each have probability one half. Within each code, the three corresponding external source distributions receive equal assumed transport weights. A single lognormal distribution matches the pooled first two log moments: mu = sum(w*log G); variance = sum(w*[sigma^2 + (log G-mu)^2]). This smooth projection is a declared target scenario, not a discovered Turkana composition or an identification of source site strata with target lifestyles. Matching source sex labels to target F/M is explicitly unverified. Additional independent zero-mean log errors of 0.06 for TC and 0.08 for HDL represent an assumed target-protocol dispersion increment. Their values do not validate an assay bridge; unknown assay bias is left unresolved. No source health exclusion is applied to the target.

The source age quartiles remain distributional context and are not numerically calibrated or checked. A common age law with support 18 to 100 and chosen logit location/scale is an additional modeling assumption; 100 is model support, not target eligibility. The six non-HDL geometric means are retained in controller-owned evidence but do not constrain this measured-TC/HDL model. TC is not constructed by subtracting geometric means or by imposing TC >= HDL. There is no derived non-HDL diagnostic in this submission.

Reported confidence intervals remain estimator uncertainty. None is converted to individual SD, inverse-variance weight, zero variance, or an acceptance band. This includes the anomalous Malawi urban women HDL interval and collapsed printed endpoints.
