## Reproduction limits

`python -B sync/audit_export.py` checks the exact export inventory, aggregate schemas, source-to-export hash mappings, retained commitments, original audit statuses and scheduled-output accounting. It does not rerun tests, generation, target analyses or the independent numerical audit. The recorded 12,918 numerical and 24,919 exact/provenance checks are outcomes of the original audit, not new calculations performed in the sanitized repository.

Source URLs and download hashes remain in `custodian/source_manifest.json`; `acquire_sources.py`, `build_packet.py`, `audit_packet.py`, eligibility metadata and the evaluator describe the local pipeline. Full replay requires separately obtained public CDC inputs and screened literature, plus a new versioned preparation/generation/evaluation run. Original randomly assigned identities and bitwise output commitments require the omitted original packet and files. This checkout alone is not a self-contained replay package.

The source and review check scripts can reference omitted article artifacts or participant tables. Their presence records the procedure; it does not mean all such scripts run from this checkout without those inputs. Source-ledger URLs and bibliographic metadata permit independent retrieval, but cached copyrighted content is not included. Re-running builders also requires LLM construction sessions; the controller is not a standalone provider-API launcher.

The study used procedural isolation on a shared filesystem, not enforced OS access control. Exact reasoning-token use was not available, and equal tool caps do not establish exact compute equality. Computational audit success does not establish biological transport, calibrated synthetic uncertainty, individual recovery or policy superiority.
