Paths below are relative to `biomedical_research/virtual_cohort_system/`: harness filenames mean `cohort_harness/`; `core/` means `reports/core_v0_2026-10-31/`.

- **F1 — documented capability limitation; qualify the population wording.** `whole_cohort_feedback.py:43–52,87–108` requires the feedback-plan population to equal the execution task and uses identity measurement on its existing output. It does not require the evidence source's population to equal that task. Heterogeneous-source comparisons require explicit approximation or unavailability; general whole-cohort source reconstruction is expressly unsupported (`core/CORE_METHOD_V0.md:30`). Case-specific bridges do not establish that capability in the frozen reference integration.

- **F2 — confirmed estimator limitation, not an arithmetic defect.** `source_checks.py:106–119,163,170,191` excludes weighting/design/transform fields and implements unweighted mean, sample SD and OLS. `core/CORE_METHOD_V0.md:32` discloses this. Whether a particular Pani statistic was improperly mapped requires the primary-source/operator pairing, not inference from this limitation alone. An `approximate` label does not by itself justify a mismatched estimand.

- **F3 — confirmed schema gap; misuse remains a risk.** `contracts.py:209–224,262–264,306–308` permits SD uncertainty without a linked source-sample-size field or statistic-specific SD/SE interpretation. However, SD and SE remain distinct labels; feedback preserves them verbatim (`whole_cohort_feedback.py:97`). No automatic conversion of individual SD into estimation SE was identified. `source_checks.py:198–199` reports generated-row counts, not source-study sample size. Missing source n obstructs some calculations, but reported SE/CI may suffice; n alone cannot resolve survey, clustering or adjusted-estimator uncertainty.

- **F4 — confirmed missing Monte Carlo diagnostic; same-seed interpretation needs correction.** Feedback supplies raw differences, not MC SE/z (`whole_cohort_feedback.py:97–108`). Both reference runs use seed 26301 (`core/run_integration.py:16,60,97–99`). Common random numbers can support paired model comparisons; they do not establish independent-seed performance or prevent selection on that seed. Identical output also requires retained deterministic construction, not merely identical seeds. This artificial integration claims linkage, not revision efficacy (`core/CORE_METHOD_V0.md:28,47`). Keep simulation, source-estimation and transport uncertainty separate (`:40`).

- **F5 — confirmed generic retention boundary, guarded in F1.** `_same_model` compares only specification and route bytes (`lifecycle.py:280–285`). Each candidate's evidence and recipe remain hash-frozen (`:304–308`); the gap concerns what may differ between parent and retained child. F1 independently requires unchanged evidence/recipe and authentic feedback (`core/run_integration.py:64–75`), checks lineage, prohibits retries and caps executions (`:87–99`). The generic task revision budget is enforced (`lifecycle.py:327`); a global generator-call budget is controller policy, explicitly documented (`core/CORE_METHOD_V0.md:38–39`).

- **F6 — seed-specific fitting concern, with existing held-out replay.** `reports/c3s/PROTOCOL.md:22,25–27,36` fixes one fitting seed, freezes selection, then compares initial/selected models on two unused seeds; fitting loss alone is insufficient. Worse unused-seed results are consistent with poor generalization, but do not prove noise fitting caused it.

- **F12 — confirmed semantic-validation gaps.** Source selection/applicability are prose (`contracts.py:262–266`); `_check_metadata` checks kind, units and variable/adjustment sets, not equivalent filters or regression roles (`source_replay.py:36–57`). Priorities are explicit source-estimator semantics, source/MC uncertainty accounting, reviewed population mappings and independently assessed release performance—not relabelling every documented v0 boundary as an exposed failure.
