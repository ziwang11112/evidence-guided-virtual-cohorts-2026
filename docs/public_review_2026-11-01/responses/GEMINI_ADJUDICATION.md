## Recommendations worth retaining

Adopt transparent mixed-result reporting, distinguish directly constrained from assumption-dependent analyses, consider defensible compatible-model sensitivity analyses, and use competent same-information comparators when claiming advantage. Retain whole-cohort generation as central; bounded useful-data feasibility need not become exclusively a failure/identifiability paper.

Portability is a genuine limitation: fixed WSL/Windows paths impede external replay. General recipe validation, parameter-to-program review and stronger Agent/custodian separation are worthwhile engineering candidates, without retroactively invalidating guarded runs.
