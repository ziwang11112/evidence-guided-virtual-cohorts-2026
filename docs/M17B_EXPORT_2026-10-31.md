Start with the [results](../biomedical_research/virtual_cohort_system/MILESTONE_17B_RESULTS.md), [prospective protocol](../biomedical_research/virtual_cohort_system/docs/M17B_WHOLE_COHORT_PROTOCOL.md), and [independent audit](../biomedical_research/virtual_cohort_system/reports/m17b_whole_cohort_2026-10-31/independent_audit/FINAL_AUDIT.md).

## Included

The exact receipt binds our own source summaries and aggregate extractions, source admission and provenance, prospective protocol, original builder programs and model specifications, decisions and freezes, source-only feedback, custodian and independent-checker code, aggregate diagnostics and comparison tables, an aggregate figure, and results documentation. The CSVs in the two `results` directories contain endpoint-level estimates or realization ranges. They do not contain participants or generated individuals.

Two fresh Agent constructions generated complete tables without any individual records as input. Each retained its initial program after source feedback; six actual generated tables underlie twelve scheduled initial/final entries. The eight analyses are all reported. These are mixed-transfer feasibility results in one reference case, not a demonstrated repair benefit, national-population inference, model superiority, or generally valid replacement for measurements.

## Omitted and referenced local artifacts

- All of `custodian/private`, including downloaded source data, selected records, participant identifiers and the measured reference table.
- Every generated individual table and every execution input/output bundle, runtime description, log and repeated runtime copy.
- Full third-party article XML/HTML/PDF, figures and implementation copies. Our factual extractions and their original-source identifiers remain available.
- Duplicate `builders/*/sources` packets, environment files, caches and continuation machinery.

Frozen JSON/code, numerical comparison CSVs and aggregate figures retain their source bytes. Several receipts contain **local provenance paths and hashes referring to artifacts intentionally omitted here**. Those strings document the original local audit; they are not GitHub navigation links, promises that the referenced payload is included, or instructions to expose private files. The `source_packet` originals and builder source-copy hashes are verified in the local research workspace; a curated checkout alone cannot reconstruct all those checks without separately acquiring permitted sources and recreating the original layout. The exported programs also retain their original workspace-path assumptions rather than pretending this selective export is a turnkey full runtime.

Markdown navigation links from the local `work/m17b_whole_cohort_2026-10-31` directory are mapped to `reports/m17b_whole_cohort_2026-10-31`. Any additional link transport is listed in the receipt. This changes navigation only; original source and exported byte hashes are both retained.
