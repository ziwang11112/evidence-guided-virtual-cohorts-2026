## Exact scope and omitted dependencies

The checkpoint includes original source/test code, authored documentation, the public v2 specification, and the normalized evidence packet with four short authored source summaries. The packet's small preparation ledger describes evidence preparation and a bounded citation recheck; it is not the raw-data download ledger. Its schema receipt is historical metadata, not a new test run. Pending labels inside the original packet remain unchanged; the separately included admission receipt records the later decision.

No builder bundles or candidate artifacts, private directories, real or generated individual tables, full papers or PDF/XML bodies, raw download ledgers, STATE/history files, execution bundles, output fixtures or temporary run logs are added. Preparation releases/receipts, protocol-freeze JSON, original public-v1 package and private data dependencies remain local. Their names and hashes in authored documents/code are provenance references; they do not imply that those payloads are included. Historical tracked artifacts from earlier exports remain unchanged.

The source-context unit test imports the local-only `prepare_artificial_source_contexts.py` fixture-construction helper, which is outside this checkpoint's selected source list. Its generated fixture directories are also omitted. The exported test source therefore does not by itself form a complete runnable test package. Other entry points depend on the original harness, local releases, frozen builder artifacts and/or private reference inputs. This is a reviewable implementation checkpoint, not a claim of self-contained reproduction or authorization to execute it.
